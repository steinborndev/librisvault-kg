# ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice

A family and mouse study from Xuanwu Hospital, Capital Medical University (Beijing). Exome sequencing of a Han Chinese pedigree with early-onset [[Familial Alzheimer disease]] and no mutation in [[APP]], [[PSEN1]] or [[PSEN2]] found a novel heterozygous missense change, [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]], in the palmitoyltransferase gene [[ZDHHC21]]. Knock-in mice homozygous for the change (see [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]]) learned and remembered poorly, had hyperpalmitoylated [[FYN]] and [[APP]], more [[Amyloid-beta]] and phosphorylated tau, lost synapses and neurons, and showed [[GRIN2B]]-dependent calcium overload that palmitoylation inhibitors reduced. The authors read this as a new, palmitoylation-based mechanism of Alzheimer disease.

## The family

- Recruited through the [[Chinese Familial Alzheimer's Disease Network]]: eight members in two generations, three with Alzheimer disease (the proband, a brother and the mother). The proband, II-6, presented in 2015 at 55 with about five years of progressive memory loss; her Mini-Mental State Examination score was 5/30 and her Clinical Dementia Rating 3 ([[Cognitive impairment]]).
- 11C-PIB PET showed marked amyloid retention in bilateral frontal, parietal and lateral temporal cortex ([[Amyloid PET]]); CT showed severe atrophy, a medial temporal atrophy score of 3 in both hippocampi ([[Hippocampal atrophy]]).
- APOE ε4 was present in two affected members (II-6 and I-2) and two unaffected ones (II-1, II-3), and absent in affected II-2, so [[APOE]] does not explain the pedigree.
- [[Whole-exome sequencing]] of two affected and two unaffected members (coverage above 70x) removed variants that were absent from affected members, present in unaffected ones, synonymous, or above 0.5% frequency, then applied score cut-offs (CADD above 20, SIFT below 0.05, PolyPhen-2 above 0.8, MetaLR above 0.5, M-CAP above 0.025). No known APP, PSEN1 or PSEN2 pathogenic variant was found. One novel heterozygous change remained, c.999A>T in exon 6 of ZDHHC21, confirmed by Sanger sequencing, present in all affected and no unaffected members, absent from gnomAD, DisGeNET, the AD and frontotemporal dementia mutation database and HGMD, and at a highly conserved residue.
- For context, in the Chinese network 13.12% of 404 pedigrees carried PSEN or APP missense changes, 3.71% synonymous or untranslated-region changes and 83.17% neither.

## Knock-in mouse findings

- Behaviour: at 9 months homozygous mice had longer escape latency, fewer platform crossings and less time in the target quadrant in the [[Morris water maze]], with normal swimming speed.
- Enzyme activity: ZDHHC21 mRNA and protein were unchanged, and ZDHHC21 co-precipitated with APP and FYN in both genotypes. By the [[Acyl-biotin exchange assay]], hippocampal palmitoylation was 47.6% higher for APP and 25.2% higher for FYN; FYN in the membrane fraction was 15.5% higher. The authors conclude the variant is gain-of-function ([[Protein palmitoylation]]).
- Pathology at 6 to 9 months: soluble Aβ40 up 53.0%, Aβ42 up 129.7% and the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio up 56.7% by ELISA; [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]] at Thr181, Thr217, Thr231 and Ser396; widespread intraneuronal Aβ and phospho-tau staining but no plaques.
- Cell and synapse loss: more TUNEL-positive CA1 neurons ([[Apoptosis]], [[Neuronal loss]]); fewer synapses by electron microscopy; fewer dendritic branches at 60 to 120 µm from the soma and fewer spines by Golgi staining; smaller field potentials after tetanus in [[Long-term potentiation]] recordings at 3 months ([[Synapse loss]]).
- Pathway: [[GRIN2B]] (NMDAR2B) phosphorylation was higher. Viability after 100 µM NMDA plus 10 µM glycine fell 13.8% in wild-type and 30.0% in mutant cortical neurons ([[Excitotoxicity]]); 1 µM [[2-Bromopalmitate]] or [[Cerulenin]] brought mutant neurons back to wild-type survival. In GCaMP3 calcium imaging ([[Calcium signalling]]), mutant neurons had larger spontaneous calcium transients and spread; [[Ro 25-6981]] had almost no effect on wild-type cells but normalised mutant cells, and 2-bromopalmitate brought them close to wild-type.
- Discussion: the authors propose that raised APP palmitoylation shifts APP to lipid rafts where [[BACE1]] cuts it more readily, so the effect acts before secretase cleavage, unlike PSEN or APP mutations; and that FYN hyperpalmitoylation raises NMDAR2B phosphorylation, as reported for loss of the depalmitoylating enzyme PPT1.

## Methods summary

Exome capture with Agilent SureSelect V6 and Illumina NovaSeq, hg19 alignment with BWA and GATK, Ensembl VEP annotation, ACMG classification. Mice were made on C57BL/6J by CRISPR/Cas9 with a single-stranded donor oligonucleotide (Shanghai Model Organisms Center), both sexes used. Statistics were t tests, ANOVA or Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney tests in SPSS 26, with blinded analysis.

## Assessment

- Human evidence is one small pedigree: three affected members, two of them sequenced. "Cosegregation" is stated without a LOD score, so the genetic case is suggestive rather than conclusive. No ACMG class is reported in the results.
- The pedigree is described inconsistently: methods name I-2, II-2 and II-6 as affected, results name I-1 (mother) and II-2, and the APOE passage refers to I-2.
- Mice are homozygous, whereas the patients are heterozygous; heterozygous knock-in animals were not characterised. Group sizes are small (3 to 4 mice for several biochemical and imaging panels), and the ages differ between assays (3, 6 and 9 months).
- The model lacks amyloid plaques, which the authors concede; the title and conclusions therefore rest on soluble Aβ, phospho-tau, synaptic and behavioural changes.
- 2-bromopalmitate and cerulenin were tested only in cultured neurons at one dose and inhibit palmitoylation broadly, so the data do not show that rescue is specific to FYN or APP. No in vivo inhibitor treatment was done.
- A text slip gives the dendritic branch distance in mm instead of µm.

## Open Questions

- Does ZDHHC21 p.T209S segregate with Alzheimer disease in other pedigrees, or occur in unrelated sporadic patients (only one Han Chinese family and no population cohort were studied)?
- Do heterozygous ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mice show the same palmitoylation, Aβ and memory phenotypes as homozygotes (only homozygous animals were reported)?
- Does lowering palmitoylation in vivo, with a more selective inhibitor than 2-bromopalmitate or cerulenin, rescue memory and synapse loss in the knock-in mice (inhibitors were tested only in cultured neurons)?
- Does the T209S substitution raise ZDHHC21 catalytic activity directly in a purified enzyme assay (activity was inferred from substrate palmitoylation in tissue)?
- Do plaques form when the ZDHHC21 variant is combined with human APP or other familial Alzheimer mutations in mice (the authors suspect so but did not test it)?

## Why This Source Matters

It proposes protein palmitoylation as a mechanism upstream of amyloid and tau change, separate from the secretase-centred mechanism of the three classical familial genes, and it supplies a candidate gene for the large share of Chinese familial pedigrees without APP or presenilin mutations. It also links the palmitoylation of one kinase ([[FYN]]) to NMDA receptor overactivity, giving a concrete drug-target hypothesis, though the human genetic evidence is a single small family.
