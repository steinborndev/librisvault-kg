# Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia

Primary mouse microglia kept 2 days in vitro (2 DIV, activated by the isolation) or 16 days in vitro (16 DIV, "aged") were given 1000 nM of pre-aggregated [[Amyloid-beta]] 1-42 (a mix of oligomers and fibrils) for 24 hours. The two ages reacted differently on almost every readout, which the authors read as a loss of reactivity with in vitro aging. The model is described on [[Primary microglia in vitro aging model]].

## Design

- Microglia from 1 to 2 day-old CD1 mouse cortex, purified from mixed glial cultures after 21 days (astrocyte contamination under 2%, neurons 0%), then kept 2 or 16 DIV before treatment.
- Viability by annexin V and 7-AAD flow cytometry; morphology by Iba1 staining and ImageJ; [[MMP2]] and [[MMP9]] activity by gelatin zymography; autophagy by [[MAP1LC3B]] puncta and [[BECN1]] blots; senescence by SA-β-galactosidase; migration in a Boyden chamber (Aβ or 10 μM ATP as attractant, 6 h); phagocytosis by 1 μm latex bead uptake over 75 min.
- mRNA by qRT-PCR for phagocytosis receptors, cytokines, inflammasome parts, pattern-recognition receptors and M1/M2 markers; three microRNAs by qRT-PCR; CD11b, CD86 and CD45 subsets by flow cytometry.
- At least four independent cultures per group, t-test for treatment and two-way ANOVA for age by Aβ.

## Findings

- **Viability:** no significant change with age or Aβ, so the later differences are not explained by cell death.
- **Morphology:** 2 DIV cells were mostly amoeboid and Aβ enlarged their area 1.9-fold; 16 DIV cells were ramified, rod-like or bipolar and, with Aβ, shortened (perimeter 0.7-fold, circularity 1.2-fold).
- **Matrix metalloproteinases:** Aβ raised MMP9 in both ages (1.7-fold at 2 DIV, 1.5-fold at 16 DIV) and MMP2 only in the aged cells (2.4-fold).
- **Autophagy and senescence:** [[BECN1]] rose 1.6-fold with Aβ in 2 DIV cells and was low in 16 DIV cells; LC3 puncta, low in aged cells, rose to near the young level with Aβ. Aβ doubled SA-β-gal positive 2 DIV cells (2.1-fold) toward the 16 DIV level, and raised [[MIR146A]] in 2 DIV cells without reaching the aged level; ferritin rose in both ages, less in aged cells (data not shown). See [[Cellular senescence]].
- **Migration:** 2 DIV cells migrated toward Aβ (1.7-fold) and ATP (2.0-fold); 16 DIV cells did not move.
- **Phagocytosis:** Aβ halved bead uptake per cell in 2 DIV microglia and cut the share of cells with more than 10 beads 10-fold, bringing them to the level of aged cells, which were low with or without Aβ.
- **Phagocytosis genes:** [[MFGE8]] fell more than 2-fold with Aβ at 2 DIV and was already low at 16 DIV; [[TREM2]] mRNA rose 5.2-fold at 2 DIV and did not change at 16 DIV; [[CD33]] rose 1.7-fold at 2 DIV and 2.8-fold at 16 DIV. Lysates of 2 DIV cells held more Aβ (monomers and dimers), whereas most Aβ in 16 DIV cultures lay outside the cells.
- **MicroRNAs:** Aβ lowered [[MIR155]] (0.7-fold) and [[MIR124-1]] (0.6-fold) in 2 DIV cells; both were already about half as high in 16 DIV cells and did not respond.
- **Cytokines and inflammasome:** at 2 DIV Aβ raised [[TNF]], [[IL1B]], [[IL6]], [[HMGB1]] (1.6-fold) and [[IL18]] (2.1-fold) but not [[NLRP3]]; 16 DIV cells had about 10-fold lower baseline cytokine mRNA and raised only TNF and IL1B.
- **Receptors:** Aβ raised [[TLR2]], [[TLR4]] (2.4- and 2.0-fold) and [[CX3CR1]] (2.6-fold) at 2 DIV only.
- **Polarization markers:** [[NOS2]] rose in both ages (4.7-fold at 2 DIV, 5.4-fold at 16 DIV); MHC class II rose 11.4-fold at 2 DIV; [[ARG1]] rose only at 2 DIV; [[TGFB1]] rose in both. The authors read 2 DIV cells as mainly M1 and 16 DIV cells as a mix of M1 and M2 features (see [[Microglial cell activation]]).
- **Surface subsets:** aged cells had fewer [[ITGAM]] (CD11b) positive cells; Aβ lowered CD11b-positive cells at both ages and raised [[CD86]] single-positive cells 4-fold at 16 DIV (about 25% of cells), which the authors take as a possible M2b-like subset able to co-stimulate T cells.

## Why This Source Matters

It tests, in one primary-cell system, the idea that microglia respond to amyloid-beta differently early and late in Alzheimer disease. Young, already-activated cells migrate, make cytokines and raise TREM2 but lose phagocytosis and drift toward a senescent state; aged cells barely respond, carry high CD33 and leave Aβ outside the cell. The authors infer that anti-inflammatory therapy such as NSAIDs may only help early, but this is a hypothesis from cell culture.

## Assessment

- Aging is modelled only by time in culture (2 versus 16 days) and the 2 DIV cells are activated by the isolation itself, so "young" and "aged" are culture states, not cells from young and old animals. The authors say translation to humans needs further work.
- Cells come from neonatal mouse cortex; one Aβ dose (1000 nM; 50 nM was tried and dropped for less consistent results) and one 24-hour time point were used.
- Most gene readouts are mRNA only; ferritin and the Aβ lysate blots are shown as supplementary or "data not shown".
- The flow cytometry paragraph labels CD11b as the co-stimulatory ligand and CD86 as the integrin αM, which is the wrong way round; the surrounding text treats them correctly (CD11b as integrin αM, CD86 as co-stimulatory).
- The abstract says TREM2 was reduced in 16 DIV cells, but the Results show TREM2 rising with Aβ at 2 DIV and unchanged at 16 DIV; the comparison is to the Aβ-treated young cells.

## Open Questions

- Does the 2 DIV versus 16 DIV difference in the response to amyloid-beta hold in microglia isolated from young and old animals or from human post-mortem brain (only neonatal mouse cultures were used)?
- Do aged cultured microglia reduce phagocytosis of amyloid-beta because of raised CD33, or only in parallel with it (CD33 was measured as mRNA and not manipulated)?
- Which route releases interleukin-18 in Aβ-treated young microglia when NLRP3 mRNA did not change (NLRP1 is proposed in the discussion but not tested)?
- Do the CD86-positive, CD11b-negative cells that expand in aged Aβ-treated microglia co-stimulate T cells (the T-cell link is a hypothesis and no co-culture was done)?
