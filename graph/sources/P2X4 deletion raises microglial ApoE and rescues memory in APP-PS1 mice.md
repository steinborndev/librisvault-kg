# P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice

A Cellular and Molecular Life Sciences study from the groups of François Rassendren and Lauriane Ulmann that asks what the ATP-gated channel [[P2RX4]] does in microglia in [[Alzheimer disease]]. Using mouse bone-marrow-derived macrophages, transfected COS-7 cells, 12-month-old [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] animals crossed to a [[P2rx4 knockout mouse]], and post-mortem human cortex, the authors link microglial P2X4 to lysosomal degradation of [[APOE]], soluble [[Amyloid-beta]] and memory performance.

## Design

- Interaction screen: native P2X4 complexes were immunoprecipitated from membrane-enriched fractions of wild-type and P2X4-null [[Bone marrow-derived macrophages]] and read by tandem mass spectrometry ([[Affinity purification mass spectrometry]]). ApoE was the only specific partner with significant coverage in two independent experiments; co-immunoprecipitation with either antibody confirmed it.
- Cell models: ApoE and P2X4 levels in macrophage lysates and supernatants, primary microglia, and [[COS-7]] cells co-transfected with mouse ApoE and P2X4, [[P2RX2]] as a specificity control, or the ATP-binding-dead P2X4-K69A mutant.
- Protease dissection: overnight [[E-64]] (10 µM), calpain inhibitor III (10 µM) and [[Z-Phe-Ala-FMK]] (20 µM); cathepsin B activity by the Z-RR-AMC substrate and the Magic Red assay; macrophages from cathepsin B-deficient mice.
- In vivo: 12-month-old APP/PS1, APP/PS1 crossed to P2X4 knockout (APP/PS1xKO), knockout and wild-type mice. ApoE was measured by Western blot in FACS-sorted CD11b-positive cortical microglia, soluble and insoluble Aβ1-42 by ELISA, plaque size by Thioflavine T and microglial coverage around plaques with Amylo-Glo and [[AIF1]] (Iba1) staining. Memory was tested in the [[Hamlet test]] and the [[Morris water maze]], with open-field locomotion as a control.
- Human tissue: cortical sections from Alzheimer patients and controls (3 to 6 per group) from a Paris brain bank, stained for P2X4, ApoE, Iba1 and plaques.

## Findings

- P2X4 and ApoE co-immunoprecipitated and sat in CD68-positive endo-lysosomes of macrophages, although their intracellular distributions only partly overlapped and ApoE localisation did not change without P2X4.
- P2X4-null macrophages held far more ApoE than wild-type, in lysate (9.87 ± 6.81 vs 1.53 ± 0.46) and supernatant (7.54 ± 2.91 vs 1.1 ± 0.27), with identical ApoE mRNA; primary microglia behaved the same. Co-expression of P2X4, but not P2X2, lowered ApoE in COS-7 cells, and the K69A mutant lowered it as much as wild-type P2X4, so ATP binding and channel activity were not required.
- E-64 and Z-Phe-Ala-FMK raised ApoE in wild-type but not in P2X4-null macrophages; calpain inhibition did nothing; cathepsin L and S inhibitors had no effect (data not shown). Cathepsin B activity was lower in P2X4-null cells (Magic Red 19,969 ± 2,357 vs 47,301 ± 9,238 arbitrary units) although cathepsin B protein was equal, and cathepsin B-deficient macrophages held more ApoE. [[CTSB]] is therefore the protease through which P2X4 promotes ApoE loss, by a mechanism the authors say is unclear.
- In 12-month APP/PS1 cortex, P2X4 was present only in microglia clustered on plaques (co-localised with CD68 and ApoE) and absent from parenchymal microglia away from plaques and from neurons. Sorted microglia from APP/PS1xKO mice had more ApoE than APP/PS1 (0.36 ± 0.001 vs 0.13 ± 0.02).
- Soluble Aβ1-42 was lower in APP/PS1xKO cortex (0.937 ± 0.083 vs 1.216 ± 0.066 ng/mg) and small soluble Aβ on Western blot was about halved, while insoluble Aβ, plaque number and size and microglial coverage around plaques did not differ.
- In the Hamlet test, water-deprived APP/PS1 mice did not locate the drink house faster than non-deprived ones (73.5 vs 87.1 s) whereas APP/PS1xKO mice did (39 vs 112 s), as did wild-type and knockout animals; the water maze showed memory retention loss only in APP/PS1. Open-field locomotion was if anything higher in the transgenic groups. A supplementary blot showed a hippocampal [[GRIN1]] (GluN1) reduction in APP/PS1 synaptosomes that was absent in APP/PS1xKO.
- In human cortex, P2X4 and ApoE staining were strong in Iba1-positive microglia around plaques in Alzheimer brain and faint in controls.

## Why This Source Matters

It ties a microglial ion channel to the best-known Alzheimer risk gene by a concrete biochemical route (P2X4, cathepsin B, lysosomal ApoE turnover) and shows that removing the channel in an amyloid mouse leaves plaques untouched but lowers soluble Aβ and restores memory. The result that more microglial ApoE accompanies less soluble Aβ and better cognition runs against the view of mouse ApoE as purely amyloidogenic, and supports the authors' reading that microglial ApoE protects synapses more than it shapes plaques. It also adds [[P2RX4]] to the genes that mark plaque-associated, [[Disease-associated microglia]]-like cells at protein level even though its RNA is rarely flagged in transcriptomic studies.

## Assessment

- The link from higher microglial ApoE to lower soluble Aβ and better memory is correlative: the knockout is global, P2X4 is also expressed at low levels in neurons, and the authors state their data do not directly show the ApoE-Aβ-memory chain.
- ApoE Western blots were so variable between experiments that values were normalised to wild-type or untreated controls; the sorted-microglia comparison rests on two experiments with two mice per group, and the soluble Aβ ELISA on two experiments with six to seven mice per group.
- The P2X4 knockout carries a P2X7 passenger mutation from the 129 background (P2X7 451L in the knockout, 451P in APP/PS1), a confound for any microglial phenotype; no sex breakdown or blinding is described.
- Lysosomal pH, cathepsin L/S inhibition and the plaque-associated mRNA increase are stated as data not shown or cited from earlier work. The Results report cortical soluble Aβ while the Discussion speaks of hippocampal soluble Aβ.
- Human data are qualitative immunostaining of 3 to 6 donors per group, and mouse and human ApoE differ in disease contribution (no ε4 genotype was considered).

## Open Questions

- Through what mechanism does P2X4 raise cathepsin B activity in lysosomes when ATP binding is not required (not resolved by the pH, K69A and protein-level controls used)?
- Does microglia-specific deletion of P2rx4 reproduce the lower soluble Aβ and the memory rescue seen with the global knockout (only a global knockout with a P2X7 passenger allele was tested)?
- Is P2X4 protein also raised in plaque-associated microglia of human Alzheimer brain by APOE genotype (only 3 to 6 donors per group were stained, without ε4 status)?
