# ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models

A preclinical drug-discovery paper (Theranostics) that screens plant-derived compounds for [[Mitophagy]] induction, optimises an isoquinolinium hit into [[ALT001]], shows that ALT001 acts through the [[ULK1]]-[[RAB9A]] [[Alternative mitophagy]] pathway rather than the canonical [[PINK1]]-[[PRKN]] route, and tests it in an APP-mutant cell model and in three mouse models of [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Why This Source Matters

Mitophagy-boosting compounds such as urolithin A and [[Nicotinamide riboside]] already improve Alzheimer models, but the usual experimental inducer [[Carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenyl hydrazone]] is toxic to mitochondria and the mechanism of most candidates is unclear. This paper offers a non-uncoupling inducer with a defined pathway and tests the pathway dependence in vivo, by knocking down Rab9 in the hippocampus and abolishing the benefit. It also proposes that the alternative pathway, previously described in heart, is a drug target for brain. The in vivo mechanism rests on one Rab9 knockdown experiment with six animals per group, and the direct binding target of ALT001 is unknown (see Assessment).

## Design

- Screen: 61 single compounds from medicinal plants in [[BEAS-2B]] cells carrying mitochondria-targeted Keima, read by flow cytometry ([[mt-Keima mitophagy assay]]), with [[Carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenyl hydrazone]] (10 μM) as positive control.
- Chemistry: six derivatives (1-6) made from [[Palmatine]] and [[Berberine]] by selective demethylation and BCl3 or BBr3 ether cleavage; compound 3, with four hydroxyl groups, became ALT001.
- Mechanism: ATG7 knockout [[HeLa]] cells expressing Parkin, PINK1 and ULK1 and Rab9 shRNA, [[Brefeldin A]] and [[Bafilomycin A1]] inhibitors, with [[HEK293]] used to repeat the knockdowns.
- Disease models: [[SH-SY5Y]] cells expressing APP Swedish/Indiana mutant [[APP]]; 5×FAD ([[5xFAD mouse]]), PS2APP ([[PS2APP mouse]]) and [[Tg2576 mouse]] animals; mt-Keima reporter mice (also crossed to 5×FAD) and primary cortical neurons from them.
- Dosing: intranasal ALT001 at 40 mg/kg for 4 days or 1 mg/kg for 7 days (mitophagy), and 1 mg/kg daily for 4 weeks (behaviour and electrophysiology, starting at 4, 10 and 9 months of age in 5×FAD, PS2APP and Tg2576).
- Readouts: mitophagy (mt-Keima), TMRM membrane potential, MitoSOX superoxide, ATP, Seahorse respiration, electron microscopy, [[Morris water maze]] and hippocampal CA1 [[Long-term potentiation]]. Statistics were Student's t test or ANOVA with Sidak correction, P below 0.05.

## Findings

- Potency: berberine, epiberberine, palmatine and coptisine (all isoquinolium cores) induced mitophagy in the screen; ALT001 was about 25-fold more active than palmatine, matched CCCP in confocal mt-Keima signal, and acted in a dose- and time-dependent way and in cell lines from several tissues.
- Low toxicity: unlike CCCP, ALT001 left the mitochondrial membrane potential intact up to 24 h, caused no OPA1 cleavage or PINK1 accumulation, kept inducing mitophagy under the antioxidant [[Acetylcysteine]], and did not trigger cell death.
- Selectivity: ALT001 raised mitochondria-targeted but not cytosolic Keima puncta (so no general macroautophagy), and lowered a mitochondrial marker but not ER, Golgi or peroxisome markers. Mitochondrial mass fell first and then recovered, with [[PPARGC1A]], [[TFAM]] and [[NRF1]] induced ([[Mitochondrial biogenesis]]).
- Pathway: [[MAP1LC3B]]-II and GFP-LC3 puncta did not rise, and mitophagy persisted in [[ATG7]] knockout and PINK1 knockdown cells while CCCP-induced mitophagy was lost. ULK1 phosphorylation at Ser555 and Ser317 rose, Rab9 puncta ringed mitochondria, and brefeldin A or ULK1 or Rab9 knockdown suppressed mitophagy (repeated in HEK293).
- Cell AD model: APP Swe/Ind expression raised mitochondrial ROS, lowered ATP and respiration and increased damaged mitochondria; 15 μM ALT001 for 12 h reversed all of these (basal, ATP-linked and maximal respiration back to normal), and bafilomycin A1 or Rab9 knockdown removed the benefit ([[Mitochondrial dysfunction]]).
- In vivo mitophagy: intranasal ALT001 raised hippocampal mitophagy by about 53% (40 mg/kg, 4 days) and about 42% (1 mg/kg, 7 days), raised ULK1 Ser317 phosphorylation, lowered [[MFN2]] and raised biogenesis factors; autophagosomes rose about 36%. Hippocampal mitophagy in 4-month 5×FAD mice was about 27% below wild type, and 1 mg/kg ALT001 restored it with ULK1 phosphorylation and biogenesis factors.
- Function: in 10-month PS2APP mice four weeks of ALT001 restored time and distance in the target quadrant of the water maze to wild-type levels and reversed hippocampal mitochondrial ROS, membrane potential and ATP changes. In 5×FAD slices LTP was 108 ± 8% (n = 5) versus 150 ± 5% in wild type (n = 6) and 148 ± 7% (n = 6) after ALT001; rescue was also seen in Tg2576.
- Causality in vivo: hippocampal AAV Rab9 shRNA blunted ALT001-induced mitophagy and left LTP at 114 ± 5% (n = 6) versus 155 ± 6% (n = 6) with control shRNA; Rab9 knockdown did not change mitophagy in untreated mice.

## Assessment

- The central causal claim in the brain depends on a single AAV knockdown experiment with n = 6 per group (the text does not say whether this counts slices or animals), and only LTP, not the water maze, was tested for Rab9 dependence.
- ALT001 binding partners are unknown; the authors note that ULK1 protein level did not change, unlike other stress inducers of this pathway, so the activation route is open.
- Effects on amyloid, Tau and neuroinflammation were not measured, so it is unclear whether ALT001 acts downstream of or independently from classical pathology.
- No pharmacokinetics or brain exposure are shown for intranasal dosing, and a second hit, actinonin, was dropped for toxicity without data.
- Behaviour was tested in one model (PS2APP) and electrophysiology in 5×FAD and Tg2576; animal numbers are small and group sizes differ between panels.

## Open Questions

- What is the direct binding target of ALT001 that leads to ULK1 Ser317 and Ser555 phosphorylation (the authors say it is under investigation, not reported in this paper)?
- Does ALT001 lower amyloid-beta, Tau hyperphosphorylation or neuroinflammation in 5×FAD and PS2APP mice (these readouts were not measured)?
- Does chronic intranasal ALT001 dosing remain free of toxicity over longer treatment and reach brain at pharmacologically relevant concentrations (no pharmacokinetic or long-term safety data given)?
