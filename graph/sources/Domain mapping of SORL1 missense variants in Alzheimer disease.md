# Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease

A sequencing case-control study of [[SORL1]] in 18,959 [[Alzheimer disease]] cases and 21,893 non-demented controls (40,852 genetically unrelated people from the ADES, ADSP, StEP-AD, Knight-ADRC and UCSF/NYGC/UAB cohorts; 80.6% European, 12.9% African). It asks which rare missense variants carry a risk and onset-age effect as large as protein-truncating variants (PTVs), by prioritising them with [[Domain mapping of disease mutations]].

## Design

- Exome and genome sequences were processed against GRCh37 on a uniform pipeline; carriers of pathogenic variants in [[PSEN1]], [[PSEN2]], [[APP]] or other Mendelian dementia genes were excluded, as were relatives up to the third degree. Exon 1 (signal peptide, pro-domain and the first 10 residues of the VPS10p domain) was dropped for differential coverage.
- After quality control there were 637 unique coding variants: 585 with minor allele frequency below 0.05% (74 PTVs and 511 missense) and 52 more common ones. The rare missense variants were sorted by a stated scheme into 107 high-priority (HPV), 65 moderate-priority (MPV), 72 low-priority (LPV) and 267 no-priority variants (NPV). HPVs were chosen by the domain-mapping analysis regardless of REVEL score; in the VPS10p domain, where no homologous disease data exist, a REVEL score above 0.5 was used, apart from cysteines in loops L1 and L2.
- Association used Fisher's exact test (Bonferroni corrected) and, for larger groups, logistic regression adjusted for six ancestry principal components and APOE genotype. Age at onset (AAO) was compared case-only by Kaplan-Meier curves and log-rank tests, and the [[APOE]] interaction was tested in the ADES cohort alone, where APOE was not part of selection.

## Findings

### Risk and onset age by variant class

- 74 PTVs (89 cases, 6 controls) gave a 17.2-fold risk of overall disease (95% CI 7.5-39.3), 35.3-fold for [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] and 8.6-fold for [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]. Median AAO was 62 years against 72 for SORL1 wild-type cases.
- 107 HPVs (151 cases, 29 controls) gave 6.1-fold overall risk (95% CI 4.1-9.0), 9.9-fold for early-onset and 4.2-fold for late-onset disease; median AAO 64 years, 8 years earlier than wild-type. Of the HPVs, 72% were singletons.
- MPVs, LPVs and NPVs had no or negligible effect on risk or AAO. The common variants [[SORL1 p.A528T]] and [[SORL1 p.E270K]] (sample frequencies 3.6% and 1.9%) imputed in a GWAS gave odds ratios of 1.11 and 1.02.
- Combining REVEL or the AlphaMissense score above 0.9 gave smaller aggregate effects (3.9-fold and 3.5-fold) than the 107 HPVs (6.1-fold), which the authors read as an added value of expert annotation over prediction tools alone.

### Domain by domain

- VPS10p domain: 27 HPVs, 8.8-fold overall risk. [[SORL1 p.Y391C]], a cysteine gained in loop L1, occurred in 12 unrelated cases and no controls with median AAO 67.5 years (range 60-86).
- YWTD domain: [[SORL1 p.R953H]] in five cases (AAO 46-78) and one control; carrying any YWTD variant gave a 4.6-fold risk.
- CR cluster: 12 calcium-cage variants in 13 cases and no controls, median AAO 12 years earlier than wild-type; variants giving an odd number of cysteines (34 cases, 8 controls) gave 4.9-fold risk, including [[SORL1 p.R1490C]]. A calcium-cage variant [[SORL1 p.D1108N]] appeared in three unrelated cases.
- 3Fn domain: [[SORL1 p.Y1816C]] at the "tyrosine corner" in six unrelated cases (mean AAO 60.2) and [[SORL1 p.G1732A]] in one case. No variants were prioritised in the transmembrane and tail domains.
- Truncating nonsense variants [[SORL1 p.R744X]] (five cases) and [[SORL1 p.R866X]] (four cases) spanned broad AAO ranges.
- [[SORL1 p.D2065V]] showed a 1.36-fold GWAS risk but a non-significant opposite direction in the exome sample, which the authors attribute to limited power.

### APOE

- Each extra APOE ε4 allele raised risk about 3-fold. Median AAO in ε4/ε4 cases was 64 (wild-type), 60 (PTV) and 58.5 (HPV) years; in ε4-negative cases 75, 69 and 66 years. PTV or HPV shortened AAO by 6 and 9 years in ε4-negative cases and 4 and 5.5 years in ε4/ε4 cases, an additive effect; interaction evidence was weak (p = 0.04 and 0.06) with only 18 variant-carrying controls of known genotype.

## Proposed mechanisms

The authors suggest that variants in calcium-cage residues, the YWTD motif and the VPS10p domain may be dominant negative: misfolded SORL1 keeps its 3Fn-domain, dimerises with wild-type SORL1 in the endoplasmic reticulum and traps it there, so less than half reaches the endosome for [[Retrograde transport from endosome to Golgi]] with retromer. Tyrosine-corner variants such as p.Y1816C instead appear to act by haploinsufficiency, with reduced soluble SORL1 in a carrier, and p.Y391C is speculated to impair amyloid-beta delivery to the lysosome. These modes rest on prior functional work cited by the authors and are not tested here.

## Why This Source Matters

[[SORL1]] truncating variants are labelled variants of unknown significance in ClinVar and are not routinely reported. This study puts numbers on a missense subset (the HPVs) whose risk and onset age approach those of truncations and overlap those of carriers of known [[PSEN2]] and some [[PSEN1]] variants, and it shows APOE genotype shifts onset in carriers. It closes with the argument that these variants merit a debate on clinical counselling, as moderate- and high-risk genes do in hereditary breast cancer and as NOTCH3 variants do in CADASIL.

## Assessment

- Cases and controls are assembled rather than population-based, with many early-onset cases and index patients, and about half of controls were younger than 65, so effect sizes are likely conservative and AAO distributions are not population representative.
- Many HPVs are singletons; the differences between individual variant groups rest on few carriers, and the dominant negative interpretation is not statistically established. APOE genotype was missing for 29% of controls.
- Founder effects for recurrent variants (p.Y391C, p.R1490C, p.Y1816C, p.R953H, p.R744X, p.R866X) were not excluded.

## Open Questions

- Do the high-priority missense variants in the VPS10p domain, YWTD motif and calcium-cage residues act by dominant negative retention in the endoplasmic reticulum, as proposed (only few carriers and no functional test in this study)?
- Are the recurrent SORL1 variants p.Y391C, p.R1490C, p.Y1816C, p.R953H, p.R744X and p.R866X founder mutations or independent de novo hotspots (relatedness was excluded but haplotypes were not compared)?
- What is the age-specific penetrance of high-priority SORL1 variants in population-based samples (this assembled sample is enriched for early-onset cases and index patients)?
- Do SORL1 variants interact synergistically with APOE ε4 on age at onset (the case-control design lacked power, with only 18 variant-carrying controls of known APOE genotype)?
