# PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome

A preclinical study in [[Drosophila melanogaster]] that compares three neurotoxic transgenes relevant to [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Down syndrome]] (human [[MAPT]] tau 0N4R, a secreted tandem human [[Amyloid-beta]] 42, and the fly [[DYRK1A]] orthologue minibrain, mnb) across five readouts, and asks whether the oral, brain-penetrant DYRK1A inhibitor [[PST-001]] corrects them.

## Design

- Fly lines: GAL4-driven overexpression of human tau 0N4R, tandem Aβ42 (two Aβ42 copies joined by a 22 amino acid linker, secreted) or mnb isoform H, with GAL4/+ flies as controls. Drivers differed by assay: GMR-GAL4 (eye), elav-GAL4 (all neurons: lifespan, climbing, Western blot), tim-GAL4 (clock neurons: sleep) and OK107-GAL4 (mushroom body: memory).
- Drug: PST-001 at 334 μM in the food (100 mg per litre in DMSO), given from egg laying through adulthood; vehicle food for controls. The dose was taken from the level that suppressed associative memory deficits in [[Ts65Dn mouse]] animals.
- Readouts: Western blot of fly heads with total and phospho-specific tau antibodies (S262, S356, T231; S396 was also listed); eye surface area (n = 7); Mantel-Cox survival of mated females (n = 50); negative geotaxis climbing in 2 to 5 day old flies (50 flies per genotype); five-day sleep in males by Drosophila Activity Monitor; and 1 h aversive olfactory memory with odour and shock avoidance controls. Statistics were two-way ANOVA with Dunnett's and Bonferroni post hoc tests, log-rank for survival and a t-test for the blot.

## Findings

- Tau phosphorylation: PST-001 left total human tau unchanged but cut phosphorylation at S262, a [[DYRK1A]] site, by more than 50% (p < 0.001); S356 and T231 did not change significantly. The authors read this as in vivo inhibition of the fly kinase mnb.
- Degeneration: tau, Aβ42 and mnb in the eye each produced a rough eye with smaller surface area (about 60%, 50% and 25% smaller). PST-001 did nothing to control eyes, enlarged all three by roughly 20 to 30% over the untreated genotype, brought the mnb eye back to wild-type size and only partly rescued tau and Aβ42.
- Lifespan: control flies lived 56 days; tau shortened life by 38%, Aβ42 by 50% and mnb by 13%. PST-001 lengthened life by 30% in tau flies and 25% in Aβ42 flies but not in mnb flies or controls.
- Climbing: about 75% of control flies climbed past the line in 10 s. All three transgenes lowered this (p < 0.001). PST-001 restored tau and mnb flies to control level and only partly restored Aβ42 flies, whose deficit was the largest (60 to 70%) and whose gain was about 40%.
- Sleep: all three transgenes in clock neurons reduced total, day and night sleep. PST-001 restored total sleep in tau and mnb flies (night sleep only in tau flies; day and night in mnb flies) and did not raise sleep in Aβ42 flies.
- Memory: tau, Aβ42 and mnb in the mushroom body lowered 1 h memory (p < 0.05). PST-001 brought all three back to control level, and flies on drug or carrying any transgene avoided octanol, 4-methylcyclohexanol and shock normally, so the memory change was not a sensory or reinforcement defect.
- Controls on drug showed no deficit in any assay, which the authors take to mean enough endogenous mnb activity is left for normal function.

## Why This Source Matters

It gives a side-by-side, behaviourally broad comparison of tau, Aβ42 and DYRK1A dosage in one organism and shows a single kinase inhibitor correcting most of their phenotypes, with the fly offering a fast screen for sleep and cognition drugs in [[Down syndrome]]-linked Alzheimer disease. It also adds an in vivo test of the idea, reviewed in [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]], that DYRK1A-driven [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]] contributes to disease. See [[Drosophila models of Alzheimer disease and Down syndrome]] for the model itself.

## Assessment

- Target engagement is shown only as lower tau S262 phosphorylation in tau flies; kinase activity was not measured in the Aβ42 or mnb flies, so the claim that the drug acts through mnb in those lines is inferred. Rescue of Aβ42 flies is explained by the authors as mnb inhibition limiting Aβ42 toxicity, which was not tested.
- One drug dose and one vehicle were used, so there is no dose-response; exposure ran through development and adulthood, which cannot separate a developmental from an adult effect.
- Each assay used a different driver, sex and cell population (males for sleep, mated females for lifespan, mixed sex for memory), so the "differential sensitivity" to the drug across models may reflect the driver as much as the transgene; the authors raise selective neuronal vulnerability and a floor effect for Aβ42 climbing.
- The abstract says the inhibitor improved lifespan, locomotion, sleep and memory in all three models, but mnb flies did not live longer and Aβ42 flies slept no more.
- Selectivity (GINI index 0.936, lack of GSK3β activity, off-target DYRK family members and CLK2) is cited from earlier profiling and not re-tested; the compound was supplied by its manufacturer, Pharmasum Therapeutics, and the paper's own competing-interest statement was not part of the text read.
- The climbing tube is described as 7.5 cm in Methods and 10 cm in Results.
- The Aβ42 and mnb flies overexpress a transgene, so the model tests inhibition of excess kinase activity, not the effect of trisomy 21 as a whole.

## Open Questions

- Does PST-001 lower DYRK1A-type kinase activity directly in the Aβ42 and mnb overexpressing flies (only tau S262 phosphorylation was measured, in tau flies)?
- Does PST-001 correct sleep, memory and lifespan when given only in adulthood, after development (drug exposure covered development and adulthood together)?
- Why does PST-001 not rescue sleep in Aβ42 flies or lifespan in mnb flies although it rescues other phenotypes in the same lines (tested only with one dose and different drivers per assay)?
- Does PST-001 reduce tau or amyloid pathology and cognitive deficit in a mammalian model of Alzheimer disease with Down syndrome (rescue was shown in flies here and in Ts65Dn mice only for associative memory in earlier work)?
