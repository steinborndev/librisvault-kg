# PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia

A functional study of the rare protective coding variant [[PLCG2 p.P522R]] (rs72824905, first linked to [[Alzheimer disease]] by an exome-chip association with P = 5.38e-10). The authors ask whether the arginine allele (R522) changes signalling and function in macrophages and microglia expressing [[PLCG2]] at normal endogenous levels, alongside the other phospholipase C isoforms. They built two new models: CRISPR-edited human iPSC clones ([[Kolf2 PLCG2 R522 hiPSC clones]]) and a knock-in line ([[Plcg2 R522 knock-in mouse]]), and compared R522 with the common P522 allele in conditionally immortalized macrophages, primary neonatal mouse microglia and iPSC-derived microglia.

## Design

- Structure: the HoPE web server for the residue swap, then 300 ns [[Molecular dynamics simulation]] in triplicate (GROMACS, Amber03).
- Signalling: Fura-2 and Fluo-8 [[Calcium signalling]] readouts after Fc-receptor cross-linking (anti-FcγRII/III in mouse, anti-CD32 in human), lipopolysaccharide, amyloid-beta 1-42 oligomers and phosphatidylserine liposomes (a TREM2 ligand). Specificity checks used the PLC inhibitors edelfosine and U71322, the IP3-receptor blocker xestospongin C and GapmeR knockdown of Plcg2.
- Function: pHrodo-labelled E. coli and zymosan for [[Phagocytosis]]; fluorescent amyloid-beta oligomers and 10 kDa dextran for [[Endocytosis]], with cytochalasin D, [[Chlorpromazine]] and methyl-beta-cyclodextrin to split uptake routes.
- Lipids: [[Phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate]] (PIP2) by immunofluorescence, plate reader and mass ELISA, plus PI(3,4)P2, PIP3 and a live-cell diacylglycerol (DAG) sensor; PIP2 was also read in situ in microglia (Iba1-positive) of 2-, 6- and 9-month-old mice.

## Findings

- Structure model: the proline-to-arginine swap makes the residue larger, positively charged and less hydrophobic. Overall flexibility was unchanged, but flexibility rose at residue 522 and across most of the SH2 domains. P522 made hydrogen bonds with one neighbouring residue only (two bonds), R522 with seventeen residues; a previously coiled stretch became a helix and the SH2 domain shifted position. The authors read this as a plausible loosening of autoinhibition, not a tested mechanism.
- Hyperfunction: R522 cells gave a larger calcium rise to anti-Fc receptor antibody in macrophages, primary microglia (cortex and hippocampus) and iPSC-derived microglia, with equal ionomycin responses. Responses to LPS and TREM2-ligand liposomes were also larger. Amyloid oligomers at 0.5 µM did not separate the alleles significantly; 40 µM did. Edelfosine, U71322, xestospongin C and Plcg2 knockdown abolished the response, so it runs through PLCγ and IP3.
- Substrate and product: after stimulation R522 macrophages and microglia lost more PIP2 and gained more DAG than P522 cells, and PIP2 took longer to recover. Mass ELISA showed the extra loss is specific to PI(4,5)P2: PI(3,4)P2 and PIP3 did not differ between alleles, and inhibitors of SHIP1, PI3-kinase or PTEN did not prevent the PI(4,5)P2 drop, placing it upstream of those enzymes.
- In vivo: microglial PIP2 was about 60% lower in cortex and hippocampus of 2-month-old R522 mice than in P522 littermates, and the pattern held at 9 months.
- Phagocytosis fell: E. coli and zymosan uptake were lower with R522 in all three cell systems. A PIP2 shuttle raised uptake more in R522 cells, so PIP2 may be rate-limiting; at low bioparticle doses the deficit disappeared, in line with another group's trend toward higher uptake at low doses.
- Endocytosis rose: uptake of amyloid-beta 1-42 oligomers and dextran was higher with R522 in all three systems. Chlorpromazine cut oligomer and dextran uptake by about 80-90% and methyl-beta-cyclodextrin by 50-70%, equally for both alleles, so both clathrin-dependent and clathrin-independent routes are used. Adding PIP2 did not raise this uptake, and Plcg2 knockdown reduced it only partly (about 30% in P522 and 50% in R522 for oligomers), so PLCγ2 is a partial contributor here, perhaps through the larger calcium signal.

## Interpretation by the authors

PLCγ2 sits downstream of [[TREM2]] and [[CSF1R]], and LPS and amyloid act through [[TLR4]], so a hyperactive enzyme changes signalling through several Alzheimer-linked receptors. Short bursts of activity are allowed, but a chronically depleted PIP2 pool may limit sustained activation. Lower uptake of stressed but viable neurons and synapses, together with faster clearance of soluble [[Amyloid-beta]], is proposed as the route to protection. Because PLCγ2 is an enzyme, it is the first classically druggable target to come from a late-onset Alzheimer genetic study.

## Assessment

- The structure section reports no difference in overall flexibility between alleles with "P = 1.83e-09", which reads as a significant difference; the wording and the number contradict each other.
- Calcium and lipid assays are mostly three independent experiments per condition, and the in vivo PIP2 result rests on three mice per genotype; the figure legend describes them as experiments.
- The phagocytosis deficit depended on bioparticle dose, so it may not describe the low, continuous challenge in a living brain.
- Cytokine release, inflammatory state and any effect on plaque load or cognition were not measured; no data were deposited in a repository.

## Open Questions

- Does the R522 allele alter cytokine release and inflammatory activation of microglia, as one cited study hints for high-dose LPS plus interferon gamma (not measured in this work)?
- Does the reduced basal PIP2 in R522 microglia change plaque-associated microglial behaviour in an amyloid mouse model (only young mice without amyloid pathology were studied)?
- Why does R522 raise endocytosis of amyloid oligomers and dextran when added PIP2 does not (the authors suggest the larger calcium signal but did not test it)?

## Why This Source Matters

It gives the first cell-level account, at endogenous expression levels in human and mouse microglia, of how a protective Alzheimer variant in PLCG2 changes the enzyme's output: more calcium and DAG, a lower PIP2 pool, less phagocytosis and more endocytosis of soluble amyloid-beta. That links a genetic association to a measurable lipid-signalling phenotype and to a drug target.
