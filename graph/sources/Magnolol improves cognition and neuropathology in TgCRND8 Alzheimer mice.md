# Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice

A preclinical study in which male [[TgCRND8 mouse]] animals (human [[APP]] with Swedish and Indiana mutations) were given [[Magnolol]], the main neolignan of Magnolia officinalis bark, by daily oral gavage for four months and compared with untreated transgenic mice, wild-type littermates and a [[Donepezil]] positive control. Magnolol improved recognition and spatial memory, lowered brain [[Amyloid-beta]] and inflammatory cytokines, shifted the secretase and Aβ-degrading enzyme balance, calmed microglia and astrocytes, restored synaptic proteins and moved the Akt/GSK-3β and NF-κB phosphorylation ratios.

## Design

- Five groups of 10 three-month-old male mice: wild type, TgCRND8 plus vehicle (0.5% carboxymethyl cellulose), TgCRND8 plus magnolol 20 mg/kg, plus magnolol 40 mg/kg, and plus donepezil 5 mg/kg; gavage once daily for 4 months. Doses came from an unshown pilot.
- Behaviour: open-field test (line crossings and rearings), novel object recognition test (recognition index) and an 8-day radial arm maze with 4 baited arms (working memory errors, reference memory errors, total entries); observers were blinded to group.
- Tissue: ELISA for Aβ40, Aβ42 and four cytokines (n = 6 per group), western blots for secretase, degrading-enzyme, synaptic and signalling proteins (n = 3), Iba-1 and GFAP immunofluorescence in hippocampus and cortex (n = 4). One-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post hoc test, p < 0.05.

## Findings

- Behaviour: transgenic mice had fewer rearings (crossings unchanged) and neither magnolol nor donepezil changed locomotion. The recognition index was lower in transgenic mice (p < 0.01) and higher with both magnolol doses (p < 0.05). In the maze, total entries, working memory errors and reference memory errors were raised in transgenic mice and lowered by magnolol (20 mg/kg p < 0.05; 40 mg/kg p < 0.001) and by donepezil.
- Amyloid: brain Aβ40 and Aβ42 were far above wild type; magnolol lowered Aβ40 at both doses (p < 0.001) and Aβ42 (p < 0.05 at 20, p < 0.001 at 40 mg/kg); donepezil lowered Aβ42 only.
- Amyloid processing: [[BACE1]], phosphorylated APP (Thr668), [[APH1A]] and [[PSEN1]] were raised and [[MME]] (neprilysin) and [[IDE]] lowered in transgenic brain. Magnolol suppressed the four production-side proteins at both doses; only 40 mg/kg raised neprilysin and IDE. Donepezil had similar effects.
- Glia and cytokines: Iba-1 microglial and GFAP astrocyte densities were raised in hippocampus and cortex and reduced by magnolol and donepezil. [[TNF]], [[IL1B]] and [[IL6]] were raised and [[IL10]] lowered in transgenic brain; magnolol reversed all four (p < 0.001 at both doses).
- Synapse: [[DLG2]] (PSD93), [[DLG4]] (PSD95), [[SYN1]], [[SYT1]] and [[SYP]] were lowered in transgenic brain. Magnolol 40 mg/kg restored all five; 20 mg/kg raised only synapsin-1.
- Signalling: phosphorylated [[GSK3B]] (Ser9) and [[AKT1]] (Ser473) were lowered and the phosphorylated-to-total [[RELA]] (NF-κB p65) ratio raised in transgenic brain; magnolol raised the first two ratios and lowered the third, as did donepezil.
- The authors argue magnolol at 40 mg/kg outperformed donepezil on several readouts, cite oral LD50 values of about 2200 mg/kg for magnolol and 45.2 mg/kg for donepezil in mice (about 50-fold lower toxicity), and judge the synaptic effect probably the dominant one.

## Why This Source Matters

It is a one-compound, multi-readout test of a plant polyphenol in an aggressive amyloid model, and it ties a behavioural rescue to a coherent set of molecular changes: less Aβ production machinery, more Aβ-degrading enzymes, less glial activation and cytokine release, more synaptic proteins, and a shift of the [[PI3K-Akt-GSK3B axis]] and [[NF-kappaB signaling]] in the protective direction. The matched donepezil arm gives a benchmark against a licensed drug, and the work extends earlier magnolol results in Aβ-treated cells, scopolamine-treated mice and [[SAMP8 mouse]] animals to a genetic amyloid model.

## Assessment

- Mechanism is inferred from correlation: protein levels were measured, no inhibitor, knockout or pathway rescue was used, so the Akt/GSK-3β and NF-κB route is a proposal. Western blots used three mice per group.
- The abstract says magnolol "increased" the phosphorylated NF-κB p65 ratio, but the results and discussion report that it reduced it; the results text is the consistent one. In several results paragraphs magnolol effects on secretase proteins are phrased as "compared to the WT control", where the Tg vehicle group is evidently meant. Phosphorylated APP is written both Thr668 and Thr688.
- There is no wild-type plus magnolol group, only males were used, and treatment started at 3 months when plaques, gliosis and memory deficits already exist in this model, so "preventive" in the discussion overstates a treatment design.
- Amyloid was measured only by guanidine-extracted ELISA with kits labelled for mouse Aβ; plaque histology was not shown, and the paper does not discuss how well those kits detect the human-sequence Aβ made by the transgene.
- The superiority over donepezil is stated from group means with no direct magnolol-versus-donepezil test, and the toxicity comparison rests on LD50 values from other work. Pilot data for dose choice were not shown. Brain exposure to magnolol was not measured here.

## Open Questions

- Does magnolol lower amyloid plaque burden by histology in TgCRND8 mice, as opposed to guanidine-extractable Aβ40 and Aβ42 (the paper reports ELISA only)?
- Does blocking Akt or activating GSK-3β abolish the cognitive and synaptic benefit of magnolol in TgCRND8 mice (the pathway link rests on phosphorylation ratios from three mice per group)?
- Does magnolol work when started after plaques are established, or in female mice or other amyloid models (only males, treated from 3 months, were studied)?
- What brain concentration does oral magnolol at 20 and 40 mg/kg reach in TgCRND8 mice (the paper cites blood-brain barrier penetration from earlier work and did not measure it)?
- Does magnolol alter tau phosphorylation in a model with tau pathology, given its effect on GSK-3β (TgCRND8 mice carry amyloid but not tangle pathology and tau was not measured)?
