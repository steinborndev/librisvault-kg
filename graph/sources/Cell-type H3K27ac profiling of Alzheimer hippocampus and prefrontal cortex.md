# Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex

A 2022 ChIP-seq resource that maps [[Histone H3K27 acetylation]] in three sorted nuclear populations (neurons, microglia and [[Oligodendrocyte-enriched glia]]) from the hippocampus and dorsolateral prefrontal cortex of 19 ROSMAP donors with and without high amyloid-beta load, and uses the maps to place late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] risk variants in cell types and to find amyloid- and age-associated acetylation changes.

## Design

- Donors: 19 participants of the Religious Orders Study and Rush Memory and Aging Project ([[AMP-AD brain RNAseq cohorts]]), mean age 87.8 years (74.8 to 101.9); 10 prefrontal and 16 hippocampal samples (7 donors had both), 15 labelled high amyloid and the rest amyloid-free, matched for age, education and postmortem interval. Hippocampal comparison of women alone was 5 high-amyloid against 3 amyloid-free samples.
- [[Fluorescence-activated nuclei sorting]] into NeuN-positive (neurons), Pu.1-positive (microglia) and NeuN-negative, Pu.1-negative nuclei, 400,000 each, then H3K27ac [[ChIP-seq]] in duplicate; 20 of 159 libraries failed quality control. Peaks were called with the ENCODE-style AQUAS pipeline on hg19 and merged into a consensus set of 352,012 peaks (17.7% of the genome; 59.2% intronic, 31.7% intergenic) that ranked highest against brain samples among 98 Roadmap Epigenomics tissues.
- Sorting was checked by acetylation near RBFOX3 (NeuN) and SPI1 (Pu.1), by agreement with single-nucleus RNA-seq and snATAC-seq clusters from non-diseased brain, and in every sample, region and sex separately. The third population matched oligodendrocyte clusters only and was named oligodendrocyte-enriched glia.
- Analysis: DESeq2 for cell type-specific, amyloid-associated and age-associated differentially acetylated regions (DARs); [[Stratified LD score regression]] plus a permutation test on two large Alzheimer GWAS; GREAT gene ontology enrichment, HOMER motif enrichment and [[STRING protein interaction network analysis]] for DAR sets; RT-qPCR in Olig2-positive hippocampal nuclei from an independent ROSMAP cohort (12 high- versus 12 low or mid-amyloid); comparison with published dorsolateral prefrontal single-nucleus and microglia RNA-seq.

## Findings

- Risk variants sit in microglia peaks. Microglia-specific peaks were significantly enriched for Alzheimer SNP heritability in both GWAS (FDR 1.6e-4 and 0.011) and by permutation (FDR 3e-6); neuron- and oligodendrocyte-specific peaks were not. A schizophrenia GWAS gave the expected opposite result, enriched only in neuron peaks.
- Locus examples. [[INPP5D rs10933431]] overlaps a peak acetylated only in microglia; [[BIN1 rs4663105]] with [[BIN1 rs6733839]], and [[PICALM rs10792832]] with [[PICALM rs3851179]], overlap peaks enriched in both microglia and oligodendrocyte-enriched glia. Across the sentinel SNPs, most overlapped microglia-enriched peaks, eight loci (including BIN1, CLU, ADAM10, NYAP1, CR1) overlapped peaks enriched in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia, and two (CLU and [[KAT8]]) overlapped neuron-enriched peaks. Peaks at the [[APP]] and [[PSEN1]] promoters were enriched in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia, the [[PSEN2]] peak in microglia.
- Amyloid-associated changes are mostly oligodendrocyte-enriched. Of 3,598 amyloid-associated DARs, 2,991 (80.3%) came from oligodendrocyte-enriched glia and only 85 (2.4%) from microglia. They form two sets: 1,962 hypoacetylated peaks in the hippocampus of women (81.7% within 5 kb of a transcription start) and 1,029 hyperacetylated peaks in the prefrontal cortex of both sexes (49.1% distal). Both sets were enriched for central nervous system [[Myelination]], oligodendrocyte development and differentiation, even against oligodendrocyte-specific backgrounds; the hippocampal set also touched the KEGG Alzheimer pathway and oxidative phosphorylation genes.
- Early-onset and late-onset genes in the hippocampal set. Promoters of APP (log2 fold change -0.38), PSEN1 (-0.41), PSEN2 (-0.42), [[BACE1]] (-0.49), [[ADAM10]] (-0.44), [[BIN1]] (-0.46), [[PICALM]] (-0.40), [[ADAMTS4]] (-0.46) and [[FERMT2]] (-0.47) were hypoacetylated, as were [[MYRF]] (-0.48) and the strongest peak of the set, at [[STMN4]] (-1.12). The Sox family motifs were enriched, and peaks near [[SOX2]], [[SOX4]] and [[SOX5]] were in the set.
- Prefrontal set. Distal PSEN2 peaks (four), a distal BIN1 peak, promoter peaks at [[CLU]], ADAMTS4 and [[SORL1]], and three distal peaks at [[MAPT]] were hyperacetylated, along with [[ADAMTS18]]; peaks near [[SOX8]] and [[SOX10]] were in the set. Genes near promoter-proximal DARs (500 genes) had slightly higher oligodendrocyte transcription in Alzheimer prefrontal single-nucleus data (mean log2 fold change 0.036, FDR 0.016); CLU, BIN1 and ADAMTS18 were significantly up, SORL1 up without significance, PSEN2 and MAPT down without significance. Neither DAR set was enriched for GWAS heritability.
- Transcript check. In Olig2-positive hippocampal nuclei, ADAMTS4 (Hedges g 1.35), PICALM (1.24), STMN4 (1.23) and MYRF (1.57) were significantly lower in high-amyloid donors (q 0.018 to 0.027), matching the acetylation direction; the other five genes tested were not significant after correction.
- Sex. The hippocampal DARs were not seen in men, but men had less amyloid (mean 4.3 versus 8.5), a sex-by-amyloid interaction model found only 3 DARs, and RT-qPCR changes appeared in both sexes, so the authors judge the effect unlikely to be sex-specific (see [[Sex differences in Alzheimer disease]]).
- Age. Microglia alone showed age-associated acetylation: 444 DARs (391 hypoacetylated), against 9 in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia and none in neurons. They included 2 hypoacetylated peaks at APP, 6 at [[LRRTM3]] and 3 distal hyperacetylated peaks at [[FKBP4]]; hypoacetylated peaks matched lower microglial transcription in a published aged-versus-middle-aged comparison (307 genes, mean log2 fold change -0.69, p = 1.2e-10). These DARs were not enriched for GWAS heritability.

## Assessment

- Strengths: sorted populations with several independent checks of purity, quality filtering on ENCODE metrics, post hoc tests against age, education, postmortem interval and library strand cross-correlation (RSC), oligodendrocyte-specific backgrounds for the ontology enrichments, and a positive-control GWAS. Data and code are public.
- Small and uneven cohort: 5 versus 3 women in the hippocampal contrast, and women carried more amyloid than men. Only 6 of the 1,962 and 5 of the 1,029 DARs survive correction across all 13.4 million tests of every contrast.
- The two regional DAR sets disagree: 1,804 of the 1,962 hippocampal peaks gain acetylation in female prefrontal samples, and 815 of the 1,029 prefrontal peaks lose it in hippocampus, so the cross-region story is either real heterogeneity or noise. Library RSC correlated with 94% of the prefrontal DARs (effect sizes stayed correlated, r = 0.77).
- The NeuN-negative, Pu.1-negative population is a mixture and no astrocyte-positive selection was done, so oligodendrocyte attribution is inferred. RT-qPCR confirmed four of nine genes; transcript changes in the prefrontal single-nucleus comparison were tiny in size.
- Microglia showed few amyloid DARs despite the strongest GWAS enrichment, but microglial subtypes were not separated, so a state-specific amyloid response cannot be excluded. Donors were all 75 or older, which limits the age finding. One passage speaks of one of "three" SOX candidates after naming two.

## Open Questions

- Do the promoter hypoacetylation of APP, PSEN1, PSEN2 and BACE1 in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia replicate in a sex-balanced hippocampal cohort larger than five high-amyloid against three amyloid-free women (only 6 of 1,962 peaks passed the strictest correction)?
- Why do hippocampal and prefrontal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia change acetylation in opposite directions at the same Alzheimer risk loci (regional biology, differing pathology stage, or low power)?
- Which Sox factor (SOX2, SOX4, SOX5, SOX8 or SOX10) drives the acetylation changes near myelination genes (motif similarity prevents deciding and no knockdown or reporter assay was run)?
- Do the BIN1, PICALM and CLU risk SNPs in oligodendrocyte-enriched peaks change enhancer activity in oligodendrocytes (no reporter or genome-editing test in oligodendrocytes was done)?
- What share of the NeuN-negative, Pu.1-negative nuclei are astrocytes rather than oligodendrocytes, and do the amyloid-associated DARs persist after positive oligodendrocyte selection (no oligodendrocyte-positive ChIP-seq was run)?
- Are the oligodendrocyte acetylation changes a cause or a consequence of amyloid toxicity to myelin (the cross-sectional postmortem design cannot order them)?

## Why This Source Matters

It supplies the first sorted-nucleus H3K27ac maps of Alzheimer brain for neurons, microglia and an oligodendrocyte-enriched population, confirms that common Alzheimer risk variants fall in microglia regulatory elements, and shows that the amyloid-linked acetylation signal sits instead in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia near both early-onset and late-onset risk genes and myelination genes. That separates where genetic risk acts (microglia) from where amyloid load leaves an epigenomic mark (oligodendrocyte lineage) and frames oligodendrocyte gene regulation as a testable mechanism.
