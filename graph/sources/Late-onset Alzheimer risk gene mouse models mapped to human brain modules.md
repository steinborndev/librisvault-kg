# Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules

A Molecular Neurodegeneration (2019) study from The Jackson Laboratory. The authors profiled whole-brain RNA-seq from six-month-old female mice that carry a perturbation in one late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] risk gene, and asked which human brain processes each perturbation resembles. The mice were an [[Apoe knockout mouse]], a [[Clu knockout mouse]], a [[GFAP-APOE4 mouse]], a [[Bin1 heterozygous mouse]] and a [[Cd2ap heterozygous mouse]], with the [[APP-PS1 mouse]] as an early-onset comparison. Mouse co-expression modules from [[Weighted gene co-expression network analysis]] were compared with 30 human post-mortem modules of the Accelerating Medicines Partnership for Alzheimer's Disease, grouped as [[AMP-AD consensus clusters]]; the comparison logic is in [[Mouse-human module comparison]].

## Design

- Mice came from The Jackson Laboratory on a [[C57BL-6 mouse]] background. Left hemispheres were taken at 6 months from six females per late-onset strain and six C57BL/6 controls; five APP/PS1 females and seven further controls were added, giving 48 samples in three batches (36 late-onset models, 8 with one APP/PS1 and seven controls, and 4 more APP/PS1). Bin1 and Cd2ap were hemizygous because homozygous loss is lethal.
- Poly(A) libraries were sequenced on an Illumina HiSeq 2000 (125 bp paired-end, 35 to 40 million reads), aligned with STAR to mouse genome build 38, and quantified as TPM (RSEM) and read counts (HTSeq-count). DESeq2 with batch as covariate gave differential expression; ComBat removed batch effects before network construction.
- WGCNA used an unsigned network, soft-thresholding power 8 and a minimum module size of 30 on 10,704 genes. Module eigengenes were tested for strain effects with ANOVA and Tukey HSD. Enrichment used clusterProfiler (Gene Ontology and KEGG).
- Mouse-human module overlap used Jaccard indices with 10,000 permutations. Direction was tested by correlating human log2 fold change (Alzheimer versus control) with each mouse perturbation's regression coefficient over mouse orthologues within each human module. Human modules came from the [[AMP-AD brain RNAseq cohorts]] (about 700 ROSMAP, 300 Mount Sinai and 270 Mayo samples, seven brain regions).
- Transcription factors for module genes were predicted with iRegulon (normalized enrichment score of at least 4) and Enrichr.

## Findings

- **Target gene knock-down.** Mouse Apoe fell in the knockout (p < 1e-60) and in GFAP-APOE4 mice (p < 1e-258, on an Apoe-null background); Clu fell in the Clu knockout (p < 1e-30); Bin1 fell only slightly in hemizygous mice (log2 fold change -0.3, p = 8.7e-12); Cd2ap did not change (log2 fold change -0.07, p = 0.7).
- **Overall transcriptome.** The first principal component (13% of variance) separated APP/PS1 from the late-onset models; the second (10%) separated Clu knockouts. GFAP-APOE4 and Apoe knockout mice were most alike, and Bin1 and Cd2ap hemizygotes grouped together, nearest to controls.
- **Differentially expressed genes** (p < 0.05): 120 in GFAP-APOE4 (57 up, 63 down; no pathway enrichment), 219 in Apoe knockout (154 up, enriched for inflammation and immune response and for osteoclast differentiation, linked to [[TREM2]] and [[TYROBP]]), 1,759 in Clu knockout (762 up with Notch signalling; 997 down with spliceosome, RNA transport and ubiquitin-mediated proteolysis), 16 in Bin1 and 34 in Cd2ap hemizygotes (down genes enriched for [[Endocytosis]] and phagosome terms), and 250 in APP/PS1 (67 up, 183 down; ribosome, oxidative phosphorylation, Alzheimer pathway).
- **Modules.** WGCNA gave 26 modules, 13 of them driven by the Apoe knockout, GFAP-APOE4, Clu knockout or APP/PS1 strains. The Apoe knockout drove the ivory module (64 genes, microglia and inflammation terms such as osteoclast differentiation, phagosome and endocytosis); the skyblue3 module (80 genes) was driven by both Apoe knockout and GFAP-APOE4. The Clu knockout drove five modules (brown, lightcyan1, black, plum1, brown4) enriched in synaptic function, endocytosis and RNA transport, and shared the steelblue module with the Cd2ap hemizygote. APP/PS1 drove darkorange2, darkorange, orange, lightgreen and lightyellow, with lipid-processing and metabolism terms. No module was driven by Bin1 or Cd2ap alone.
- **Overlap with human modules.** The ivory module overlapped the inflammation and microglia modules of consensus cluster B and ranked among the ten strongest mouse-human overlaps; 22 genes were shared by the ivory module and every cluster B microglial module. Nine of these (TREM2, [[CSF1R]], [[C1QA]], [[C1QB]], [[C1QC]], [[PTGS1]], [[AIF1]], [[LAPTM5]], [[LY86]]) were raised in the Apoe knockout, and [[TYROBP]] was lowered in the Clu knockout. The Clu-driven brown, lightcyan1 and plum1 modules overlapped neuronal-system modules (cluster C), with the brown module giving the five strongest overlaps; 122 genes were shared with all cluster C modules, 35 of them differentially expressed only in Clu knockouts and [[SYT7]] in both Clu knockout and APP/PS1. The skyblue3 module overlapped no human module. APP/PS1 modules overlapped lipid and metabolism modules (cluster E) and immune modules (cluster B).
- **Direction of effect.** Apoe knockout and GFAP-APOE4 mice correlated positively with immune cluster B (r = 0.1 to 0.3) and slightly negatively with neuronal cluster C (r about -0.05). Clu knockout and Cd2ap hemizygous mice showed the reverse (r about 0.1 with cluster C, about -0.15 with cluster B). Bin1 hemizygous and APP/PS1 mice correlated positively with both (r 0.1 to 0.2). Cell-cycle modules (cluster D) correlated negatively (about -0.2) with most strains, and most organelle-biogenesis modules (cluster E) negatively with all strains except the Apoe knockout.
- **Regulators.** Targets of [[SPI1]] were enriched in the Apoe-driven ivory and skyblue3 modules, and ETS-family factors in the Clu-driven modules; the authors stress this is bioinformatic only.
- **Authors' reading.** At 6 months the Apoe and Clu knockouts already resemble human Alzheimer brain, but through different processes (inflammation and microglia versus neuronal signalling, lipid transport and endocytosis); the opposite-signed correlations are read as evidence that the genes act through distinct pathways, and the Clu result as consistent with a protective role. The APP/PS1 signature is distinct from the late-onset models, with some shared neuroimmune change in the absence of amyloid in the late-onset ones.

## Why This Source Matters

It is one of few attempts to place several [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]] GWAS genes side by side in one mouse panel, one age and one pipeline, and to read the results against human post-mortem co-expression modules. It supports the idea that [[APOE]] and [[CLU]] perturbations pull the brain transcriptome toward different human Alzheimer processes, and that early-onset transgenic and late-onset gene models are not interchangeable, which bears on [[Mouse model validity for Alzheimer disease]], [[Heterogeneity of late-onset Alzheimer disease]] and [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]].

## Assessment

- Groups were small (six per late-onset strain), female only, at a single age, and bulk whole hemisphere, so cell-type and region-specific effects are diluted; the authors say so for the weak Bin1, Cd2ap and GFAP-APOE4 results.
- The mice are loss-of-function or heterozygous models, and the APOE4 line is a human transgene driven by the astrocyte promoter GFAP on an Apoe-null background, so it does not test the common risk variant in its natural expression pattern. GWAS risk alleles at CLU, BIN1 and CD2AP are not known to be simple null alleles.
- Cd2ap expression did not fall in the Cd2ap hemizygotes and Bin1 fell only slightly, so those strains are weakly validated as perturbations; the authors still interpret their module correlations.
- The correlations are small (absolute values mostly 0.05 to 0.3) and the text reports one cluster D result for Bin1 as significant while giving p > 0.05. The text labels the Bin1 strain once as Bin1-/- in the correlation section, although the mice are hemizygous. Differential expression is stated as adjusted p = 0.05 in the methods and p < 0.05 in the results.
- APP/PS1 samples were spread over two batches that also held most controls, so batch correction carries weight for the early-onset versus late-onset contrast.
- The transcription factor results and the protective-role reading of CLU are inference from enrichment and anti-correlation, not tested.

## Open Questions

- Do the Apoe knockout and Clu knockout transcriptional signatures persist or strengthen in mice aged beyond 6 months (only 6-month-old females were profiled)?
- Which cell types carry the module changes in the Bin1 and Cd2ap hemizygous mice (bulk whole-brain RNA-seq may have diluted cell-specific signals)?
- Does a human APOE4 allele expressed from the endogenous locus reproduce the Apoe knockout immune signature (the astrocyte-driven GFAP transgene did not drive its own modules)?
- Do double perturbations such as combined Apoe and Clu loss shift the human-module correlations beyond either alone (only single-gene models were profiled)?
