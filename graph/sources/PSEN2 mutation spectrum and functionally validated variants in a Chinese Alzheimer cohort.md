# PSEN2 mutation spectrum and functionally validated variants in a Chinese Alzheimer cohort

A retrospective genetic study of 702 patients with probable [[Alzheimer disease]] from the dementia cohort of Peking Union Medical College Hospital (PUMCH). Nine rare [[PSEN2]] variants were found, seven of them novel; transfection of [[HEK293]] cells showed that three raise the Aβ42 level and the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio, giving the first functional validation of [[PSEN2 p.N141S]], [[PSEN2 p.M239T]] and [[PSEN2 p.I368F]]. All three carriers had familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]].

## Cohort and methods

- 702 patients aged 41 to 84 (mean 67.2), 62.1% female; 52.3% had onset before 65 years and 43.6% a family history of dementia. APOE genotypes ranged from ε2/ε2 (0.7%) to ε4/ε4 (8.1%), with ε3/ε3 the commonest (49.0%).
- 558 had whole-exome sequencing and 144 targeted sequencing of 278 dementia-related genes (see [[Whole-exome sequencing]]); reads were aligned to hg19, variants called with GATK and annotated with Annovar, classed by the ACMG criteria ([[ACMG variant classification]]) and confirmed by Sanger sequencing.
- Function was tested by transfecting [[HEK293]] cells carrying Swedish-mutant [[APP]] with wild-type or mutant PSEN2 and measuring Aβ40 and Aβ42 in the medium at 48 hours by ELISA, compared by Student's t test. Eight of nine variants were tested ([[Functional validation of presenilin variants in transfected cells]]).

## Findings

- Rare variants in the three classical genes were found in 4.0% (28/702): [[APP]] 7, [[PSEN1]] 12, PSEN2 9. Fourteen were pathogenic or likely pathogenic and fourteen of uncertain significance.
- The nine PSEN2 variants were [[PSEN2 p.R62C]] (published), [[PSEN2 p.M239T]] (reported by the same group in 2021) and seven novel ones: [[PSEN2 p.N141S]], [[PSEN2 p.I368F]], [[PSEN2 p.L396I]], [[PSEN2 p.G117X]], [[PSEN2 p.I146T]], [[PSEN2 p.S147N]] and [[PSEN2 p.H220Y]]. Eight were missense and one a stop-gain. All lay in the presenilin domain except R62C (intracellular, next to the N-terminus); seven were in transmembrane regions and G117X in the extracellular loop between the first and second.
- Aβ42 in the medium (pg/ml) was 71.5 for wild-type PSEN2 against 140.3 for N141S, 144.7 for M239T and 380.9 for I368F; the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio rose from 0.031 to 0.067, 0.067 and 0.156. Aβ40 did not differ. L396I, G117X, S147N, H220Y and R62C changed neither peptide nor ratio; I146T was not tested.
- ACMG classes: N141S and M239T pathogenic, I368F likely pathogenic, the other six uncertain. Residues 141 and 239 already carried causative changes (N141I, M239V); residue 368 had none before.
- Frequency: 0.4% (3/702) carried a pathogenic or likely pathogenic PSEN2 variant, 0.8% (3/367) of early-onset and 1.0% (3/306) of family-history patients, and none of the sporadic or late-onset patients. The authors compare this with about 1% in early-onset disease in the literature and 1.7% (7/404) of pedigrees in a Chinese familial series.
- The three validated mutations sit in the second, fifth and seventh transmembrane regions; with earlier S175C, Y231C and T430M (third, fifth, ninth) the authors call the transmembrane domains a PSEN2 mutation hotspot.

## The three validated carriers

- N141S (c.422A>G): a woman of 54 with two years of quarrelling, repeated questions and trouble learning a dance; her father had memory loss in his 60s. At first MMSE 28 with near-normal memory tests and normal MRI; five years later MMSE 20, severe verbal and non-verbal memory deficit, delusions of theft and infidelity. She lacked insight early on.
- M239T (c.716T>C): a woman of 48 with a year of difficulty dressing, writing and calculating, restlessness and sleep-talking; mother, uncle and aunt had cognitive decline in their 70s. MMSE 20, severe visuospatial and executive deficit, left-predominant parietal atrophy, CSF Aβ42 439 pg/ml, phosphorylated tau-181 66 and total tau 242 pg/ml, diffuse cortical uptake on 18F-AV45 PET. Features were [[Apraxia]] (dressing), visuospatial agraphia, [[Dyscalculia]] and [[Visual mislocalization]]; after four years she had bradykinesia and rigidity of the right upper limb and trunk and MMSE 16. Her sister tested negative for the variant.
- I368F (c.1102A>T): a man of 60 with two years of [[Memory impairment]], getting lost, suspicion of theft and word-finding trouble; his mother had memory loss in her 70s. MMSE 7 on admission, near-normal MRI; lost to follow-up.
- Six carriers of uncertain variants (onset 56 to 70, five familial) all began with memory deficit and mental symptoms; three had parietal or temporal atrophy and four had white matter lesions of Fazekas grade 1 to 2. The G117X carrier, 63, began at 56 and had bilateral parietal atrophy and MMSE 8.

## Assessment

- The functional evidence is overexpression in a non-neuronal cell line with a co-expressed APP mutation; Aβ measurements in medium say nothing about aggregation, and the authors note the APP E693G mutation also leaves Aβ levels unchanged, so the five null results cannot clear those variants.
- The abstract gives 1.9% (13/702) for rare PSEN2 variants while the results give 1.3% (9/702), the number that matches the nine variants listed.
- Only one of the three validated carriers had CSF biomarkers and none had neuropathology, which the authors list as the main limitation; each mutation rests on a single carrier with segregation data limited to one mutation-negative sibling, so the pathogenicity calls rest mostly on the cell assay and ACMG criteria.
- The idea that a higher in vitro Aβ42 level predicts faster progression rests on one observation: the I368F carrier had the highest in vitro Aβ42 and the lowest MMSE (7), but with three patients and different disease durations this is a hypothesis.
- The first description of visual mislocalization with a PSEN2 mutation rests on one patient.

## Open Questions

- Do PSEN2 N141S, M239T and I368F raise the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio in neurons derived from patient or knock-in stem cells (the work used HEK293 overexpression only)?
- Do the five PSEN2 variants that left Aβ unchanged (L396I, G117X, S147N, H220Y, R62C) alter Aβ aggregation, tau, or other presenilin functions (only secreted Aβ levels were measured)?
- Does the in vitro Aβ42 or Aβ42/Aβ40 level of a presenilin variant predict the rate of cognitive decline in carriers (suggested from three patients, with CSF data in one)?
- What is the effect of PSEN2 p.I146T on Aβ production (it was not tested in cells)?

## Why This Source Matters

It supplies functional data for a gene where most reported variants have none: the authors note that most of the 87 known PSEN2 variants lack functional evidence. For the vault's familial material it gives a Chinese early-onset cohort with an explicit PSEN2 prevalence (0.4% pathogenic, 1.0% of familial cases), against a Taiwanese referral series where none was found, and it shows how ACMG classes and a cell assay combine to call a variant.
