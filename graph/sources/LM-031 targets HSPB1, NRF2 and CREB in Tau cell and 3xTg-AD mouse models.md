# LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models

A preclinical study of [[Licochalcone A]], a licorice-root chalcone, and five synthetic derivatives (LM-004, LM-006, LM-016, LM-026 and [[LM-031]], 3-benzoyl-5-hydroxychromen-2-one) as multi-target candidates for [[Alzheimer disease]] and other tauopathies. The compounds were first screened in human cells that express the pro-aggregant [[MAPT p.K280del]] Tau repeat domain fused to DsRed, and the best one was then tested in streptozocin-treated 3xTg-AD mice.

## Design

- Cell model: Tet-On [[MAPT p.K280del]] TauRD-DsRed lines in [[HEK293]] (3 days) and retinoic-acid-differentiated [[SH-SY5Y]] (7 days); see [[TauRD-DsRed cell model]]. [[Congo red]] was the comparator.
- Read-outs: DsRed and thioflavin S fluorescence for [[Tau aggregation]], ROS, caspase 3 activity, neurite length (TUBB3 staining), immunoblots, lentiviral shRNA knockdown of [[NFE2L2]] or [[CREB1]], and a cell-free thioflavin T and electron microscopy aggregation assay with recombinant TauRD.
- Permeability: parallel artificial membrane permeability assay for the blood-brain barrier (PAMPA-BBB), with Lipinski and polar-surface-area predictions; see [[Blood-brain barrier permeability prediction]].
- Mouse model: 6-month-old male homozygous [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals, n = 10 per group, given [[Streptozocin]] (100 mg/kg i.p., four doses) to induce [[Hyperglycemia]], then [[LM-031]] 40 mg/kg i.p. daily (days 15-36); see [[Streptozocin-induced hyperglycemia model]]. Open field, Y-maze and [[Morris water maze]] testing, then immunohistochemistry and immunoblot of hippocampus and cortex.

## Findings

- All six compounds were soluble to 100 μM and met Lipinski criteria and the polar-surface-area rule for brain entry. In uninduced HEK293 / SH-SY5Y cells the IC50 for cytotoxicity of [[LM-031]] was 55.9 / 6.8 μM, the lowest toxicity in HEK293 among the derivatives (licochalcone A: 7.0 / 5.8 μM).
- In HEK293 cells, 1 μM [[Licochalcone A]] or [[LM-031]] raised DsRed fluorescence (105%; LM-031 105-108% at 1-10 μM), lowered thioflavin S signal (130% to 106-109%), ROS (109% to 96-98%) and caspase 3 activity (121% to 75-83%). In the cell-free assay, [[Congo red]] and LM-004 (not LM-031) cut ΔK280 TauRD aggregation (16,856 to 4,774 and 6,758 arbitrary units).
- In SH-SY5Y cells, ΔK280 TauRD shortened neurites (63 to 56 μm); licochalcone A and LM-031 restored them (62-63 μm) and lowered caspase 3 activity (117% to 90-102%).
- Mechanism: LM-031 raised [[HSPB1]] (88% to 112-113%) and soluble TauRD-DsRed (100% to 129-132%) with no change in its RNA, supporting a folding rather than transcriptional effect. It also restored [[NFE2L2]], [[NQO1]] (not significant, p = .063), [[GCLC]], [[CREB1]] and phospho-CREB (S133), [[BDNF]], phospho-[[AKT1]] and phospho-ERK1/2 ([[MAPK3]] and [[MAPK1]]), [[BCL2]] and [[GADD45B]], and lowered [[BAX]].
- Knockdown of NFE2L2 or CREB1 was modest (mRNA 71% to 60%; 81% to 67%, neither significant), yet LM-031 still rescued neurite length in both knockdown lines (42 to 46 μm and 46 to 51 μm), which the authors read as these pathways mediating the benefit. The Tau solubility gain in knockdown cells did not reach significance.
- PAMPA-BBB effective permeability of LM-031 was 4.80 ± 0.12 × 10-6 cm/s, above the 4 × 10-6 cut-off for high permeability.
- In streptozocin-treated 3xTg-AD mice, blood glucose rose to 220-314 mg/dl; LM-031 lowered it only partly (183-215 mg/dl, still above the 107-112 mg/dl of untreated controls). It raised Y-maze alternation (53% to 72%), shortened water-maze latency on the test day (39 to 26 s), and lengthened time in the target quadrant (17 to 24 s; p = .047-.060, borderline).
- Streptozocin lowered hippocampal [[RBFOX3]] (NeuN) to 89-91% of control in dentate gyrus, CA1 and CA3 and raised [[Amyloid-beta]] and [[MAPT]] immunoreactivity; LM-031 reversed these (NeuN 95-97%) and reduced Tau staining area (e.g. 202% to 141%) and Aβ area. NRF2 fell to 53% and phospho-CREB to 68% with streptozocin and returned to 92% and 110% with LM-031, while total CREB did not change.

## Why This Source Matters

It is a worked example of a synthetic chalcone acting on several protective routes at once: a chaperone ([[HSPB1]]), the [[NFE2L2]] antioxidant axis and CREB-dependent survival signalling, against both Tau misfolding and the [[Oxidative stress]] and [[Apoptosis]] that follow it. The mouse work adds a metabolic-risk angle, because the compound improved memory in a diabetes-accelerated Alzheimer model, though it lowered glucose only in part.

## Assessment

- The cell effects are small in absolute terms (DsRed fluorescence up about 5-8%, neurite length up about 7 μm) and several key comparisons carry p values of .05 to .24, including the Tau solubility gain in knockdown cells and the probe-trial memory result.
- shRNA knockdown only partly silenced NFE2L2 and CREB1, so the "mediates the effect" conclusion rests on rescue persisting in weakly knocked-down cells rather than on a loss of effect.
- Only male mice were used, with a hyperglycemia protocol chosen to accelerate the phenotype; because LM-031 itself lowered glucose, part of the cognitive and pathology benefit may be metabolic. No independent group tested glucose control alone.
- The cell-free assay did not show LM-031 binding or disaggregating Tau directly, so the claim that it targets HSPB1 comes from expression changes, not target engagement. The authors state further preclinical studies are needed.

## Open Questions

- Does LM-031 still improve memory and lower Aβ and Tau in 3xTg-AD mice without streptozocin-induced hyperglycemia (all animals here were hyperglycemic or normoglycemic controls without the drug-only comparison)?
- Is the cognitive benefit of LM-031 independent of its partial blood-glucose lowering (no glucose-matched control group was included)?
- Does LM-031 bind HSPB1 or an upstream regulator of its expression (the study measured expression only)?
- Does LM-031 work in female mice and in older mice with established Tau pathology (only 6-month-old males were treated)?
