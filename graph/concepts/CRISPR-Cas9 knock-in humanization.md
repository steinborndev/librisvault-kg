# CRISPR-Cas9 knock-in humanization

Replacing a few codons of an endogenous rodent gene with the human sequence by injecting Cas9, guide RNAs and a single-stranded repair template into embryos or zygotes, so the gene stays under its own promoter. Silent changes in the template (to stop re-cutting and to create a genotyping restriction site) keep the edit scarless. It avoids the overexpression and random-insertion artefacts of transgenic Alzheimer models, but needs a humanized wild-type line as control for any added disease mutation.
