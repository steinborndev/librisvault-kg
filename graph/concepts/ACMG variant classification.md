# ACMG variant classification

The American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics (ACMG) criteria sort a sequence variant into pathogenic, likely pathogenic, variant of uncertain significance (VUS), likely benign or benign by weighing evidence such as population frequency, computational predictions, segregation in families and functional assays. Pathogenic and likely pathogenic calls are often pooled as "PLP".
