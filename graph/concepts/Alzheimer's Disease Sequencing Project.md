# Alzheimer's Disease Sequencing Project

The Alzheimer's Disease Sequencing Project (ADSP) is a United States consortium that generated whole-exome and later whole-genome sequence for thousands of people with and without Alzheimer disease. Its exome discovery sample was chosen by a risk score from age, sex and APOE ε4 status, to enrich cases for risk variants and controls for protective ones, and it includes European-ancestry and Caribbean Hispanic participants.
