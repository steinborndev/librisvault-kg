# Cytochrome P450 metabolizer phenotypes

People are grouped by how fast a cytochrome P450 enzyme clears its drug substrates, inferred from the alleles they carry: poor metabolizers (two inactive alleles), intermediate metabolizers (one reduced or inactive allele), normal or extensive metabolizers, and ultra-rapid metabolizers (gene duplication or a high-expression allele). Poor metabolizers risk drug accumulation and adverse reactions; ultra-rapid metabolizers risk under-treatment. Combining several enzymes gives a multigenic profile: for CYP2D6, CYP2C9 and CYP2C19 together there are 134 genotypes and 33 phenotypes.
