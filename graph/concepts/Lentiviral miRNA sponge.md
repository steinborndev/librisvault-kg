# Lentiviral miRNA sponge

A lentiviral miRNA sponge is a viral vector that expresses a transcript carrying several binding sites for one or more microRNAs. The decoy soaks up the endogenous microRNAs (see [[miRNA binding]]), so their messenger RNA targets are released and the targets that respond in vivo can be read from the transcriptome. Compared with overexpressing a microRNA in cultured cells, it tests the microRNAs at their natural levels in the target tissue.
