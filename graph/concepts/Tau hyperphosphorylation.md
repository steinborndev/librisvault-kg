# Tau hyperphosphorylation

Tau hyperphosphorylation is the shift of the kinase-phosphatase balance that leaves [[MAPT]] tau over-phosphorylated, detaching it from microtubules and letting it form filaments and [[Neurofibrillary tangle]] in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Reviewed as the primary event of tau dysfunction; early post-mortem work found tau phosphorylation three to fourfold higher in Alzheimer than healthy brain. Kinases covered: [[PRKACB]], [[FYN]], [[GSK3B]], [[CDK5]] and [[DYRK1A]]; phosphatases: [[PPM1B]] and [[Protein phosphatase 2A]] (activated by [[Prostaglandin A1]]). A high-salt diet raised hippocampal and neocortical tau phosphorylation in mice through calpain-2, p35 and CDK5, and tau ablation reversed the cognitive harm. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Downstream: [[Tau propagation]]; upstream modulators: [[Amyloid-beta]]
- Antibody arrays showed raised [[GSK3B]] S9, [[MAPK14]], [[RPS6KB1]] and [[CDK5]] signalling and several tau phosphosites in hippocampus of 3xTg-AD mice overexpressing [[PTK2]]. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Raised at Thr181, Thr217, Thr231 and Ser396 in 6-month-old [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]] hippocampus, alongside hyperpalmitoylated [[FYN]]. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- In fly neurons overexpressing human tau 0N4R, the DYRK1A inhibitor [[PST-001]] lowered phosphorylation at S262 by more than 50% without changing total tau or significantly changing S356 and T231, alongside gains in lifespan, climbing, sleep and memory. Study: [[PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome]]
- Phospho-Tau at T205, T231 and S396 rose in hippocampus and cortex of [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals given [[D-ribose]] and fell with [[Berberine]], returning when mitophagy was blocked with [[Mdivi-1]]. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- AT8 (Ser202/Thr205) tau phosphorylation fell in 5xFAD hippocampus after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]] although it was not raised at baseline; AT100 and total tau did not change, and the p35 to [[CDK5]] ratio fell. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
