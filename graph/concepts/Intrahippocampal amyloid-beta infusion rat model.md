# Intrahippocampal amyloid-beta infusion rat model

A non-transgenic model of early sporadic [[Alzheimer disease]] in which pre-aggregated Aβ1-42 peptide is infused stereotaxically into the hippocampus (CA1) of an adult rat. It avoids the familial-mutation backgrounds of transgenic lines and exposes tissue to a defined Aβ load at a known time.

## Method

- Aβ1-42 stock in DMSO is diluted in PBS and incubated 5 days at 37 °C to form fibrils; 3 µL per hemisphere is infused over 6 minutes (coordinates 2.6 mm lateral, 3.0 mm back, 3.0 mm deep from Bregma), with PBS for controls.
- Read-outs used: Morris water maze at day 14 (path length, escape latency, probe trial), GFAP immunofluorescence, serum at days 7, 14 and 21 and CSF at day 14 for miRNA qRT-PCR with spiked-in synthetic normalisers.

## Limits

- It is a partial model that shows acute, direct exposure rather than chronic accumulation; the serum miRNA pattern differs from that of the [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] for miR-146a-5p.
