# ChIP-seq

Chromatin immunoprecipitation followed by sequencing: an antibody pulls down a DNA-binding protein or histone modification together with its bound DNA, and sequencing the DNA maps where in the genome the protein or mark sits. Differences between groups are scored as changes in binding affinity at each site.
