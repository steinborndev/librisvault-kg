# Histone H3K27 acetylation

Acetylation of lysine 27 on histone H3, a chromatin mark found at active enhancers and promoters and used as a readout of regulatory activity.

## Connections

- Genes with reduced CTCF binding in Alzheimer brain significantly overlapped genes with reduced H3K27ac, and the shared genes were enriched in synaptic organization. Study: [[Diminished CTCF binding at synaptic genes in Alzheimer frontal cortex]]
- Related: [[CTCF]], [[Chromatin looping]], [[ChIP-seq]]
- Profiled in sorted neuronal, microglial and oligodendrocyte-enriched nuclei from Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex: a consensus of 352,012 peaks, microglia peaks enriched for Alzheimer heritability, and 3,598 amyloid-associated and 444 age-associated differentially acetylated regions. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
