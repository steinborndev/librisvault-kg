# Notch signaling

Notch signaling is a cell-contact pathway in which a ligand of the Delta or Jagged family ([[JAG1]]) binds a Notch receptor such as [[NOTCH3]], and successive cleavage by [[ADAM10]] and the presenilin-containing gamma-secretase complex ([[PSEN1]], [[PSEN2]]) releases an intracellular domain that acts on transcription. It shares the secretase machinery with [[APP]] processing, so changes in it are discussed alongside amyloid beta production.

## Connections

- Protein interaction network analysis of NOTCH3 and JAG1 gave enrichment in this pathway (P = 6.48 x 10^-49), angiogenesis and two secretase pathways. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Related: [[Amyloid-beta formation]], [[BACE1]], [[Alzheimer disease]], [[CADASIL]]
- Proposed as a possible non-APP gamma-secretase pathway whose disturbance by transmembrane PSEN1 variants might add to neurodegeneration (speculative). Study: [[PSEN1 transmembrane versus cytoplasmic variants and progression in DIAN]]
