# Alternative splicing

Alternative splicing lets one gene give several mature transcripts by choosing different transcription start or end sites, skipping or including exons, using other 5' or 3' splice sites, or retaining introns. The isoforms can differ in protein domains, signal peptides, disordered regions and sensitivity to nonsense-mediated decay.
