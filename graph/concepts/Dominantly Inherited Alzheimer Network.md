# Dominantly Inherited Alzheimer Network

The Dominantly Inherited Alzheimer Network observational study (DIAN-Obs) enrols members of families that carry a pathogenic variant in [[PSEN1]], [[PSEN2]] or [[APP]], together with unaffected sibling non-carriers as controls. Participants have clinical and cognitive assessment, MRI, amyloid PET with Pittsburgh compound B and CSF collection (following the biofluid protocol of the [[Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative]]), and are placed on a common time axis by [[Expected years to symptom onset]]. Data are available to investigators on request.
