# PSEN1 transmembrane and cytoplasmic domain grouping

A way to stratify carriers of pathogenic [[PSEN1]] variants by the topological region of the protein that the changed residue lies in, taken from UniProt annotation: one of the nine transmembrane helices (TM) or a cytoplasmic segment (CY). The rationale is that the helices contribute to the intramembrane catalytic site of the [[Gamma-secretase complex]] whereas cytoplasmic regions help recruit and hold the substrate [[APP]] for successive cleavages (processivity). It is coarser than a per-domain split across APP, PSEN1 and PSEN2, which explained amyloid PET variation but not clinical progression, and it is easier to apply in small trials than per-variant analysis. It also differs from the older split at codon 200, which did not separate MMSE, hippocampal volume or CSF phospho-tau 181 in the same cohort.
