# SERT and TSPO radioligand autoradiography and uptake assays

A paired toolset for reading serotonergic terminal integrity and microglial activation in rodent brain. Tritiated DASB labels the serotonin transporter ([[SLC6A4]]) on frozen sagittal sections, tritiated PK11195 labels [[TSPO]] on adjacent sections, and specific binding is the total signal minus the signal in the presence of excess paroxetine (SERT) or unlabelled PK11195 (TSPO), quantified against tritium microscales and the Allen Mouse Brain Atlas.

Transporter function is measured separately as [3H]serotonin uptake into freshly prepared cortical membranes at six concentrations, fitted with the Michaelis-Menten equation to give Vmax (capacity, which tracks transporter number) and Km (affinity). Frozen tissue gave no usable specific uptake in the source study. Competition with DASB distinguishes inhibitors that bind the ligand site from those that act elsewhere.
