# Amyloid-beta formation

Amyloid-beta (Aβ) is made when [[APP]] is cut first by β-secretase (releasing sAPPβ and the C99 fragment) and then by γ-secretase (releasing Aβ and the APP intracellular domain). The competing non-amyloidogenic route cuts APP with α-secretase ([[ADAM10]] is the main constitutive one), releasing sAPPα and C83 and precluding Aβ. The balance between the two routes, and where in the cell they happen, determines how much Aβ forms in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Compound [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]] shifts the balance toward sAPPα and lowers secreted Aβ in cell and mouse models, with part of the effect depending on [[ADAM10]] and part on Golgi trafficking ([[Brefeldin A]], [[Golgicide A]]).
- The β-cut happens mainly in Rab5-positive early endosomes where [[APP]] meets [[BACE1]]; in [[Tg2576 mouse]] mice the tau antibody [[12A12 monoclonal antibody]] lowered APP and BACE1 and shifted the endosomal adaptors [[BIN1]] and [[RIN3]] and lactate-linked energy metabolism ([[Endocytosis]], [[Glycolysis]]).
- Studies: [[DAPG lowers beta-amyloid secretion in Alzheimer disease models]], [[Tau antibody 12A12 and amyloid-beta in Tg2576 mouse hippocampus and retina]]
- E2-domain variant [[APP p.V551M]] and presenilin variant [[PSEN2 p.H169N]], found together in one patient, are proposed to change amyloid beta production through APP dimerisation and gamma-secretase helix structure; unproven functionally. Study: [[Double APP and PSEN2 mutations in a Korean patient with early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Upstream receptor control of the β-cut: δ-opioid receptor ([[OPRD1]]) signalling restrained [[BACE1]] expression and activity and Aβ42 under amyloid injury in [[PC12]] cells, whereas μ-opioid receptor ([[OPRM1]]) activation promoted them. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
- Species difference in the β-cut: three human-specific residues in the Aβ sequence make [[APP]] a better [[BACE1]] substrate. The mutation [[PSEN1 p.M139T]] leaves total Aβ unchanged but shifts γ-secretase output toward Aβ42 and away from Aβ43 in knock-in rats. Study: [[Humanized APP knock-in mouse and rat with PSEN1 M139T for Alzheimer disease]]
- Silencing the β-cut at its source: [[BACE1]] siRNA in [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]] cut brain Aβ1-42 by 52% and plaque load in [[FAD4T mouse]] animals. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- In isogenic hiPSC neurons, APOE2 lowered the sAPPβ/sAPPα and βCTF to total APP ratios with unchanged total APP in APP duplication and [[PSEN1 p.A246E]] lines (a smaller trend with [[APP p.V717I]]), the basis for proposing that APOE2 shifts APP to α-secretase cleavage; the effect persisted without astrocytes. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]; clearance counterpart: [[Amyloid-beta clearance]]
- Pharmacological cut of the β-step: the [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitors]] ([[FAH65]]) lowered sAPPβ, βCTF and Aβ and raised sAPPα, shifting APP towards the α-secretase route ([[ADAM10]]). Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Both steps of gamma-secretase processing were lower in a 20% subpopulation of wild-type mouse neurons: the first cleavage of C99 (FRET sensor) and the later trimming (higher Aβ42/38). Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]. Method: [[FRET biosensors of gamma-secretase activity]]
- Loss of ADAM17 maturation ([[ADAM17 p.R215I]]) raised Aβ40 and Aβ42 secreted by APP-expressing SH-SY5Y cells relative to wild-type ADAM17, partly through higher APP expression. Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
