# Tau aggregation

Tau aggregation is the conversion of soluble [[MAPT]] Tau into misfolded oligomers and insoluble filaments, the core of [[Neurofibrillary tangle]] formation in [[Alzheimer disease]] and other tauopathies; hyperphosphorylation promotes it (see [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]]).

## Connections

- Misfolded ΔK280 TauRD raised ROS and caspase 3 activity in human cells, and [[LM-031]] and [[Licochalcone A]] reduced thioflavin S signal while raising DsRed fluorescence and chaperone [[HSPB1]]. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Cell model: [[TauRD-DsRed cell model]]; variant: [[MAPT p.K280del]]
- Downstream effects: [[Oxidative stress]], [[Apoptosis]]
