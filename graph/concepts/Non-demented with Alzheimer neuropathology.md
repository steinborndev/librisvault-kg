# Non-demented with Alzheimer neuropathology

Non-demented with Alzheimer neuropathology (NDAN) describes people who keep cognitive function despite amyloid-beta species and tau pathology in the brain.

## Connections

- Hippocampal proteomics of NDAN humans showed a post-synaptic proteome distinct from both Alzheimer and normal brain; three upstream regulators (miR-149, miR-485, miR-4723) were differentially expressed in NDAN post-synapses, and giving them to mice altered synaptic genes and reduced amyloid-beta binding to synaptic terminals and oligomer toxicity.
