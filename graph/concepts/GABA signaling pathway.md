# GABA signaling pathway

Signalling by gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), the main inhibitory neurotransmitter of brain, through ionotropic GABA-A receptors and metabotropic GABA-B receptors.

## Connections

- GABA receptor genes [[GABRG3]], [[GABBR1]] and [[GABRR2]] were downregulated at different ages in the hippocampus of [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals, and the authors propose altered GABAergic neurotransmission from early to end stage of disease. Study: [[GABA-related gene changes in 3xTg-AD mouse hippocampus from early to end stage]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- GABAergic synapse was among the terms enriched in the 244 genes that differ between two Alzheimer brain subtypes defined by [[CDK5R1]] level. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
- The GABAergic synapse pathway was downregulated in men with Alzheimer disease in hippocampal CA1 and CA3 but not in women; in the whole-hippocampus dataset GSE48350 it was instead upregulated in women with the disease, so the direction is not consistent across datasets. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
