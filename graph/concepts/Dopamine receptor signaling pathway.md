# Dopamine receptor signaling pathway

Signalling by dopamine through its G protein-coupled receptors, which in hippocampus supports memory and whose loss in Alzheimer disease has been linked to neuropsychiatric symptoms such as depression.

## Connections

- The KEGG dopaminergic synapse pathway was downregulated in women with Alzheimer disease in both CA1 and CA3 and not in men, which the authors tie to depressive symptoms. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
