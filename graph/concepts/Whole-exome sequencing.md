# Whole-exome sequencing

Whole-exome sequencing reads the protein-coding exons of the genome, about 1 to 2 percent of its length, and so finds rare coding variants cheaply across many people. Its yield for rare variants is limited by sample size, because a variant seen in a handful of people cannot reach statistical significance in a conventional association test.
