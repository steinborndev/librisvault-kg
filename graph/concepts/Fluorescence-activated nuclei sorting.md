# Fluorescence-activated nuclei sorting

Flow-cytometric sorting of fixed nuclei from frozen tissue by antibodies against nuclear antigens, which lets cell type-specific chromatin or RNA be profiled from postmortem brain.

## Connections

- Used with NeuN for neurons and Pu.1 for microglia to collect 400,000 nuclei per population before [[ChIP-seq]]; in a separate cohort Olig2, GFAP and NeuN gates sorted oligodendrocyte nuclei for RT-qPCR. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Related: [[Nuclei depletion for microglia enrichment]], [[Oligodendrocyte-enriched glia]]
