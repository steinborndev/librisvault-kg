# Chromatin looping

The folding of a chromosome so that two distant stretches of DNA, such as an enhancer or silencer and a promoter, come into contact. Loops let regulatory elements act on genes far away along the sequence and can cluster co-regulated genes; they are detected by [[Chromosome conformation capture assay]] methods and genome-wide by [[Hi-C chromatin interaction analysis]].

## Connections

- At the [[APOE]] locus, loops join the APOE promoter to [[TOMM40]] introns 2 to 3 (glia-specific in cell lines) and the APOE exon 4 CpG island to the [[APOC1]] promoter (in all cell lines tested and all brain samples); the latter was stronger in Alzheimer brain and in ε3/ε4 carriers. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
- Modulator examined: [[DNA methylation]]
- Genome-wide, binding of the loop organiser [[CTCF]] was reduced at synaptic genes in Alzheimer frontal cortex (binding data only, loops not measured). Study: [[Diminished CTCF binding at synaptic genes in Alzheimer frontal cortex]]
