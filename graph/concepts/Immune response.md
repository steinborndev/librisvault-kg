# Immune response

The reaction of the immune system to a stimulus such as a pathogen or tissue damage; in brain it overlaps with [[Neuroinflammation]].

## Connections

- Among the biological process terms enriched in genes upregulated in the hippocampus of [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals from 12 to 52 weeks of age. Study: [[GABA-related gene changes in 3xTg-AD mouse hippocampus from early to end stage]]
- Related process: [[Neuroinflammation]]; disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]

- Immune-related processes were enriched among CSF proteins altered in Alzheimer disease, dementia with Lewy bodies and frontotemporal dementia. Study: [[CSF proteomics of Alzheimer, Lewy body and frontotemporal dementia]]
- Immune pathways (B cell receptor, Toll-like receptor, complement, innate immune response) scored lower in one of two Alzheimer brain subtypes defined by [[CDK5R1]] level, with different immune cell fractions between them. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
