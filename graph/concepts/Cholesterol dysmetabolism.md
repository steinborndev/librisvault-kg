# Cholesterol dysmetabolism

Cholesterol dysmetabolism is disturbed synthesis, transport, oxidation and export of cholesterol in the brain, proposed to raise amyloid-beta by lowering [[LRP1]] and raising [[AGER]] at the blood-brain barrier.
