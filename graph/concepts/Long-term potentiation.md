# Long-term potentiation

Long-term potentiation (LTP) is a lasting increase in synaptic strength after brief high-frequency activity, mediated by NMDA receptor calcium influx and the insertion of AMPA receptors ([[AMPA receptor trafficking]]); its counterpart, long-term depression, follows repetitive low-frequency stimulation. It is the standard electrophysiological readout of synaptic plasticity and memory-related function in the hippocampus.
