# Myelination

The process in which oligodendrocytes wrap axons in myelin sheaths, allowing fast conduction and trophic support of axons in the central nervous system.

## Connections

- Central nervous system myelination, oligodendrocyte development and oligodendrocyte differentiation were the top gene ontology terms for amyloid-associated differentially acetylated regions in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia from both hippocampus (fold enrichment 3.95) and prefrontal cortex (5.15).
