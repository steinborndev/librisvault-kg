# mt-Keima mitophagy assay

Keima is a pH-sensitive fluorescent protein targeted to the mitochondrial matrix (mt-Keima). Mitochondria inside acidic lysosomes shift its excitation peak from 458 nm to 561 nm, so the 561/458 ratio marks [[Mitophagy]] in living cells, read by flow cytometry (percentage of mitophagic cells) or by confocal imaging (fraction of high-ratio pixels). A transgenic reporter mouse extends it to tissue, and cytosolic Keima distinguishes general macroautophagy from mitophagy.

## Connections

- Used for a 61-compound screen in [[BEAS-2B]] cells, for in vivo hippocampal and cortical readouts after intranasal [[ALT001]], and in 5×FAD mt-Keima crosses, where the authors argue that direct mitophagy activity is a better screening readout than LC3 puncta or Parkin translocation.
