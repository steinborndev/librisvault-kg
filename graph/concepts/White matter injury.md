# White matter injury

White matter injury is loss or damage of myelin, oligodendrocytes and axons in the brain's white matter, seen on diffusion MRI as lower fractional anisotropy and as [[White matter hyperintensity]]. In Alzheimer disease it is present in every brain examined and may precede plaques and tangles in familial disease.
