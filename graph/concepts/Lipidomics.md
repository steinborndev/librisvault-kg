# Lipidomics

Lipidomics is the simultaneous identification and quantification of hundreds of lipid species, usually by liquid chromatography coupled to tandem mass spectrometry, so that whole classes (sphingolipids, phospholipids, neutral lipids) can be compared between groups instead of single metabolites. In untargeted workflows, lipids are assigned by accurate mass and fragment matching and normalised to spiked internal standards.
