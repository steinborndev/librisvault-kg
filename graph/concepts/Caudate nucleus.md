# Caudate nucleus

The caudate nucleus is a deep grey-matter structure of the basal ganglia involved in motor control, learning and memory; it is affected in both Alzheimer and Parkinson disease.

## Connections

- Bulk RNA-seq of postmortem caudate nucleus from 5 controls, 6 [[Alzheimer disease]] and 3 [[Parkinson disease]] cases found higher [[NFKB1]] and [[NFE2L2]] and lower [[HBA1]]. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
