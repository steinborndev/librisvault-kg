# Diagnostic nomogram

A diagnostic nomogram turns a logistic regression on several markers into a points chart, so that the expression of each marker adds points and the total maps to a predicted probability of disease. It is judged by a calibration curve (predicted against observed risk), the area under the ROC curve for discrimination, and decision curve analysis for net clinical benefit. When all three are computed in the same samples used to fit the model, they overstate performance, so validation in separate cohorts is the real test.
