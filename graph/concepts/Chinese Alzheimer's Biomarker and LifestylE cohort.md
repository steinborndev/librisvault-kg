# Chinese Alzheimer's Biomarker and LifestylE cohort

An ongoing cohort begun in 2017 at Qingdao Municipal Hospital (Shandong, China) to find genetic and environmental modifiers of Alzheimer biomarkers in the northern Han Chinese population. Participants aged 40 to 90 years, from cognitively normal to mild cognitive impairment and [[Alzheimer disease]], give blood and cerebrospinal fluid and undergo neuropsychological testing (CM-MMSE, MoCA, ADL, depression and anxiety scales).

## Connections

- 814 members without objective impairment were used to relate blood [[BIN1]] promoter methylation to CSF amyloid and tau. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
- Related cohorts: [[Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative]], [[Chinese Familial Alzheimer's Disease Network]]
