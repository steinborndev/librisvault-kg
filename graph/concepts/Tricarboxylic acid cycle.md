# Tricarboxylic acid cycle

The tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle is the mitochondrial pathway that oxidizes acetyl-CoA from pyruvate and fatty acids, producing the reduced cofactors that drive oxidative phosphorylation. Its arrest is a recognised part of the [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]] of [[Alzheimer disease]], and it follows [[Glycolysis]] in the route from glucose to ATP.
