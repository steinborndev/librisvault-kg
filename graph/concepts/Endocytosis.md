# Endocytosis

Endocytosis is the uptake of membrane and cargo into vesicles that mature into endosomes. At synapses, clathrin-mediated endocytosis internalizes [[APP]], which meets [[BACE1]] in Rab5-positive early endosomes where the β-cut occurs before γ-secretase finishes [[Amyloid-beta formation]]. Adaptors such as [[BIN1]], [[RIN3]] and [[CD2AP]], and the GTPase [[RAB5A]], set how much APP and BACE1 converge there, and endosomal dysfunction is increasingly seen as a core cellular phenotype of [[Alzheimer disease]].
