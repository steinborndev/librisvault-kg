# Czech Brain Aging Study

An ongoing longitudinal, observational, memory-clinic-based cohort recruited at Memory Clinics in Prague and Brno, aimed at detecting early changes of pathological brain ageing. Participants have subjective cognitive complaints and receive neurological, laboratory, neuropsychological, genotyping and MRI work-up, with CSF or amyloid PET biomarkers in a subset; cognitively normal volunteers come from the University of the Third Age or patients' relatives.
