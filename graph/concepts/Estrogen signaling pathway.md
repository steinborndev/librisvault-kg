# Estrogen signaling pathway

Signalling through estrogen receptors, both nuclear (ESR1, ESR2) and membrane (GPER1), that in hippocampus supports synaptic plasticity and dampens neuroinflammation and is linked to MAPK and AKT survival pathways.

## Connections

- The KEGG estrogen signaling pathway was enriched and upregulated in men with Alzheimer disease in both hippocampal CA1 and CA3 but not in women, read as a compensatory response; estrogen-receptor neighbours among susceptibility genes included [[MAPK1]], [[IGF1]], [[AKT1]], [[TP53]] and [[CD44]]. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
- Receptors: [[ESR1]], [[ESR2]], [[GPER1]]; therapy: [[Menopausal hormone therapy]]
- Topic: [[Sex differences in Alzheimer disease]]
- ESR-mediated signaling and extranuclear estrogen signaling were two of five leading Reactome pathways for targets shared by heat-clearing herbs and Alzheimer disease (prediction only). Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
