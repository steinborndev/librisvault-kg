# Optimal sequence kernel association test

SKAT-O is a gene-based test for rare variants that combines a burden test (all variants push the trait the same way) with the sequence kernel association test (variants may act in both directions) and picks the combination that fits the data best. It handles quantitative traits and covariates, which suits biomarker endophenotypes.

## Connections

- Used to test rare variants (minor allele frequency below 0.05) of 12 genes near [[APOE]] against CSF and imaging biomarkers, with and without adjustment for APOE genotype. Study: [[Rare variants near APOE and CSF and neuroimaging biomarkers in ADNI]]
