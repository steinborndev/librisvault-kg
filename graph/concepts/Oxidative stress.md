# Oxidative stress

Oxidative stress is the state in which reactive oxygen species outpace a cell's antioxidant defences, damaging lipids, proteins and DNA; it is thought to be central in the pathogenesis of [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Knocking down [[DICER1]] in cultured neurons induced it, and the antioxidant [[Acetylcysteine]] reversed the Aβ42-induced fall of [[NFE2L2]] and Dicer1. Study: [[Dicer1 loss in APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse brain via amyloid-beta 42 repression of Nrf2]]
- Related: [[Apoptosis]], [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]], [[Lipid peroxidation]]
- Loss of [[NNT]], which regenerates [[NADPH]], created a pro-oxidant shift that sensitised neurons to amyloid-beta toxicity. Study: [[NNT deficiency impairs synapses and cognition in Alzheimer mouse models]]
- Higher [[NFE2L2]] (Nrf2) with lower [[HBA1]] and [[SLC25A6]] in caudate nucleus of [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]] was read as a compensatory but insufficient oxidative stress response. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Misfolded ΔK280 TauRD raised ROS in [[HEK293]] cells (to 109%); [[LM-031]] and [[Licochalcone A]] reversed it (96-98%) and induced the [[NFE2L2]], [[NQO1]], [[GCLC]] defence. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- The oxidant [[4,4'-Dithiodipyridine]] killed mouse cortical neurons sooner when gamma-secretase was inefficient or the endo-lysosomes were disturbed with [[Chloroquine]] or [[Bafilomycin A1]]. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
