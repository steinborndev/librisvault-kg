# Astrocyte activation

Astrocyte activation (astrogliosis) is the change of astrocytes to a reactive state with hypertrophy and raised [[GFAP]], seen after brain injury and in neurodegenerative disease such as [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Fibrillar Aβ1-42 infused into rat hippocampus doubled the count of GFAP-positive cells within 14 days, and Aβ1-42 on cultured astrocytes engaged [[NF-kappaB signaling]] and [[MIR146A]] without cytokine release. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Related: [[Neuroinflammation]]
- Brown seaweed lipid extracts lowered [[GFAP]] expression in [[CCF-STTG1]] astrocytoma cells while synthetic LXR and PPAR agonists raised it, which the authors read as a possible reduction of astrogliosis. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- In APP/PS1 mouse hippocampus, reactive astrocytes that normally engulf amyloid-beta oligomers and dystrophic neurites lost phagocytic capacity as amyloid-beta lowered [[MEGF10]] and [[MERTK]], a proposed vicious cycle. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Chronically activated rat astrocytes, unlike resting ones, expressed [[YY1]] and [[BACE1]], as did reactive astrocytes near plaques in Alzheimer brain, suggesting YY1 drives stimulated BACE1 expression. Study: [[YY1 activates the BACE1 promoter in rat cells and reactive astrocytes]]
- In Alzheimer model mice fed [[cis-9, trans-11-conjugated linoleic acid]], astrocytes expressing the anti-inflammatory cytokines [[IL10]] and [[IL19]] increased in number while total astrocyte number did not change. Study: [[Dietary conjugated linoleic acid lowers amyloid-beta in Alzheimer mouse hippocampus]]
- Hippocampal [[PTK2]] overexpression in 3xTg-AD mice reduced astrocyte density, surface area and process intersections, with lower JAK-STAT signalling ([[STAT3]], [[STAT1]], [[JAK2]]). Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
