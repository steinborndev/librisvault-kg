# Acyl-biotin exchange assay

The acyl-biotin exchange (ABE) assay detects S-palmitoylated proteins. Free cysteine thiols are blocked with N-ethylmaleimide, the thioester-linked palmitate is cleaved with hydroxylamine, the newly freed thiols are labelled with a thiol-reactive biotin and captured on streptavidin beads, and the protein of interest is read by western blot. A sample without hydroxylamine is the negative control.
