# Visual rating of perivascular spaces on MRI

A radiologist counts visible perivascular spaces on T2-weighted MRI in two regions, the basal ganglia and the centrum semiovale, using the slice and hemisphere with the most spaces. Each region gets a degree from 0 (none) through 1 (1-10), 2 (11-20), 3 (21-40) to 4 (more than 40). For analysis the scale is often dichotomised, with degrees 3 and 4 counted as severe; sparse top categories may be merged into the one below. High-resolution isotropic images of about 1 mm are recommended, and very small spaces that look faint are the hardest to classify.
