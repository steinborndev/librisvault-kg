# Neuronal cell cycle re-entry

Post-mitotic neurons that are damaged can attempt to re-enter the cell cycle, which is thought to start DNA repair; when repair fails, as in the oxidatively stressed neurons of [[Alzheimer disease]], re-entry ends in neuronal death. [[ATM]] signalling is one mechanism that couples the two outcomes.

## Connections

- In hippocampus of [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals overexpressing [[PTK2]], raised phosphorylation of [[FYN]], [[PXN]], [[CBL]], [[CDK5]], [[CDK1]], [[CDKN1A]] and [[TP53]] together with lower ATM signalling was read as re-entry followed by neuronal death.
