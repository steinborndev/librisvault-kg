# Blood-brain barrier dysfunction

The blood-brain barrier (BBB) is the semi-permeable endothelial border between cerebral blood vessels and brain tissue. Its breakdown has been tied to amyloid-beta accumulation, tau hyperphosphorylation, neuroinflammation and early cognitive decline, and the [[APOE]] ε4 allele has been linked to barrier dysfunction independent of Alzheimer pathology.
