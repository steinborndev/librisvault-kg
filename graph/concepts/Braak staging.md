# Braak staging

Braak staging grades the spread of neurofibrillary tau pathology through the brain in six stages: transentorhinal and entorhinal cortex and locus coeruleus first (stages I and II), then hippocampal formation and parts of neocortex (III and IV), then wide neocortex (V and VI). It is read from postmortem tissue and is often grouped as low (0 to 2), mid (3 and 4) and high (5 and 6).
