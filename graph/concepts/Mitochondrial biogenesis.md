# Mitochondrial biogenesis

Mitochondrial biogenesis is the growth and division of mitochondria, driven by the transcriptional programme of [[PPARGC1A]], [[NRF1]] and [[TFAM]]; it balances [[Mitophagy]] to set mitochondrial content.

## Connections

- After [[ALT001]], mitochondrial mass first fell and then rose again, with these three regulators induced in cells and in mouse hippocampus, and they were reduced in 5×FAD hippocampus before treatment. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Related: [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
