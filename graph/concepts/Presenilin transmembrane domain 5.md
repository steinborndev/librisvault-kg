# Presenilin transmembrane domain 5

The fifth of the nine transmembrane helices of [[PSEN1]] and [[PSEN2]], flanked by the catalytic aspartates in helices 6 and 7. In PSEN2 it spans about residues 225 to 244 and is held in the membrane by polar and aromatic residues on each side. The helix is thought to pack against helix 4 to form the catalytic site of gamma-secretase, to take part in nicastrin maturation and presenilin enhancer 2 binding, and possibly to shape the access tunnel by which the amyloid precursor protein substrate reaches the active site; loss of it reduces gamma-secretase activity.

Eleven missense changes now lie in the PSEN2 helix: [[PSEN2 p.L225P]], [[PSEN2 p.V226A]], [[PSEN2 p.Q228L]], [[PSEN2 p.Y231C]], [[PSEN2 p.I235F]], [[PSEN2 p.A237V]], [[PSEN2 p.L238F]], [[PSEN2 p.L238P]], [[PSEN2 p.M239T]], [[PSEN2 p.M239V]] and [[PSEN2 p.M239I]]. Those with cell data (I235F, L238F, M239I) raise the amyloid beta 42 to 40 ratio; most of the others have only case reports.
