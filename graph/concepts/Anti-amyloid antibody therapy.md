# Anti-amyloid antibody therapy

Monoclonal antibodies against aggregated amyloid beta (aducanumab, lecanemab, donanemab) clear plaques in the brain and modestly slow cognitive decline in early [[Alzheimer disease]], at the cost of intravenous infusion, high price, limited brain entry and risks such as amyloid-related imaging abnormalities and brain bleeding. They act on the clearance side of the pathway ([[Amyloid-beta clearance]]), in contrast to the production-side target [[BACE1]].

## Connections

- Their benefit is cited as support for the [[Amyloid cascade hypothesis]], and an orally dosed [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitors|APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor]] such as [[FAH65]] is proposed as a partner that lowers production while the antibody clears existing plaque, possibly with a lower antibody dose, or as maintenance afterwards. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Lecanemab and donanemab are the two approved disease-modifying therapies; their modest efficacy, adverse effects (amyloid-related imaging abnormalities) and cost are cited as motivation for repurposing small molecules such as [[Lasmiditan]] that act on non-amyloid pathways. Study: [[Lasmiditan prioritised for Alzheimer disease by regulator docking and dynamics]]
- Cited with the authors' earlier finding that antibody-mediated plaque clearance is TREM2-dependent, as a rationale for combining an anti-Aβ antibody (aducanumab is named) with a microglia-stimulating TREM2 antibody such as [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]], which might allow a lower anti-Aβ dose. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- Active vaccines are the other immunotherapy route: [[CAD106]] against amyloid-beta raised IgG in 41 of 42 participants and slowed plaque formation in a prevention trial that stopped early, with three cases of [[Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities]]; the tau vaccine [[AADvac1]] showed marker and clinical-scale associations in a post hoc phase 2 analysis. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- A 2021 perspective lists the antibody record: [[Bapineuzumab]] (2012), [[Solanezumab]] and [[Crenezumab]] (2019) failed Phase-3 trials, [[Aducanumab]] failed a futility analysis in 2019 and was then given conditional accelerated approval in 2021 on the amyloid-PET surrogate despite a negative advisory vote; some antibodies cleared plaque without slowing cognitive decline. The ongoing crenezumab prevention trial in [[PSEN1 p.E280A]] carriers is the best remaining test. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
