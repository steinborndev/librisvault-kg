# LonDownS cohort

The London Down Syndrome Consortium (LonDownS) is a prospective longitudinal study of adults with [[Down syndrome]] with demographic and health data, blood samples, genome-wide SNP genotyping and dementia status from clinical records.

## Connections

- Source of the 71 adult plasma samples (13 with dementia) in a 14-protein complement profile. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
- Related: [[Alzheimer disease]]
