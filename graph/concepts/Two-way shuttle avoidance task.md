# Two-way shuttle avoidance task

The two-way shuttle avoidance task tests learned active avoidance in a box with two compartments joined by a channel. A light signal is followed after 5 seconds by a mild foot shock unless the mouse crosses to the other side; recorded measures are escape latency, number of shuttles (active avoidance after the light, passive after the shock) and escape failures. It is run after a training day and retested 72 hours later as a memory measure.
