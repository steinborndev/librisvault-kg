# Heat-clearing and detoxifying traditional Chinese medicine

A class of traditional Chinese herbs, listed in the Chinese Pharmacopoeia, used to clear "heat" and "toxin" in the body. In traditional theory the hyperactive heart-liver fire syndrome is typical of Alzheimer disease, which is the rationale for trying these herbs against it. The formula Huanglian-Jie-Du-Tang (Coptis chinensis, Phellodendron bark and Scutellaria baicalensis) is the standard example, and constituents such as geniposide, andrographolide and [[Berberine]] have each been studied in the disease.
