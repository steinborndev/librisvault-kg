# Passive avoidance task

A rodent test of fear-motivated learning and memory. The animal is placed in the lit half of a two-compartment box, receives a mild foot shock on entering the dark half, and on retest the latency to step through (here capped at 5 minutes) measures how well the shock was remembered; a short latency indicates impaired memory.
