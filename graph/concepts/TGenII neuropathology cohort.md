# TGenII neuropathology cohort

TGenII is a collection of brain donors of European ancestry, over 65 at death, whose Alzheimer disease status was verified at autopsy by board-certified neuropathologists (Braak staging of tangles and/or CERAD scoring of neuritic plaques), with donors who had other neurological disease, stroke, cerebrovascular disease or Lewy bodies excluded. Pathology-confirmed cases and controls avoid the misclassification of clinically diagnosed cohorts, which the investigators argue raises power to detect genetic associations; cortical tissue from control donors also supplies array expression data for eQTL work.
