# Stratified LD score regression

A regression of GWAS summary statistics on linkage disequilibrium scores that partitions SNP heritability across genomic annotations, such as cell type-specific regulatory peaks, to test which annotation is enriched for genetic risk.

## Connections

- Applied to two late-onset Alzheimer GWAS and cell type-specific H3K27ac peaks, it found microglia-specific peaks enriched (FDR q 1.6e-4 and 0.011) and neuron- and oligodendrocyte-specific peaks not; a schizophrenia GWAS served as positive control and was enriched only in neuron peaks. Amyloid- and age-associated differentially acetylated peaks showed no enrichment. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
