# Oligodendrocyte-enriched glia

The population of nuclei that stain for neither NeuN nor Pu.1 after [[Fluorescence-activated nuclei sorting]] of postmortem brain, shown to be enriched for oligodendrocytes by H3K27ac signal at oligodendrocyte genes but still a mixture that probably includes astrocytes.

## Connections

- Its H3K27ac peaks were hyperacetylated near OLIG2 and matched oligodendrocyte clusters in single-nucleus RNA-seq (not other clusters) and snATAC-seq; it held about 80% of all amyloid-associated differentially acetylated regions, against 2.4% in microglia, and some late-onset risk-locus peaks (BIN1, PICALM, CLU, ADAM10, NYAP1, CR1) were specific to it. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Genes: [[OLIG2]], [[MYRF]], [[STMN4]], [[BIN1]], [[PICALM]]; process: [[Myelination]]; mark: [[Histone H3K27 acetylation]]
