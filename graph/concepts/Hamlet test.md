# Hamlet test

The Hamlet test is a group-housed, semi-naturalistic behavioural arena: a 1.6 m central agora opens onto five corridors leading to houses for drinking, eating, running, hiding and meeting a stranger mouse. Mice live in it for hours per day, and after water deprivation the latency and number of errors in reaching the drink house measure topographical memory, a spatio-temporal orientation readout.

## Connections

- Water-deprived wild-type and P2X4 knockout mice reached the drink house faster than non-deprived ones, APP/PS1 mice did not, and APP/PS1 crossed to the [[P2rx4 knockout mouse]] did again (12-day training, probes at 72 h and 96 h). Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
- Complementary spatial test: [[Morris water maze]]
