# CpG island shore

A CpG island shore is the stretch of about 2 kb on either side of a CpG island (a cluster of CpG dinucleotides, on average 1000 bp, around the promoters of roughly 70% of human genes). Islands are usually unmethylated; the farther a CpG lies from the island, the likelier it is methylated, so shores are lightly to moderately methylated. Methylation at shores varies with tissue differentiation, reprogramming, ageing and disease, and has been reported to correlate negatively with expression.
