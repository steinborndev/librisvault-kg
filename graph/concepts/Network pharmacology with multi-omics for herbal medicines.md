# Network pharmacology with multi-omics for herbal medicines

A workflow for herbal preparations: identify the compounds that enter serum and brain by liquid chromatography mass spectrometry, map them to disease targets in public databases (TCMSP, TCMID, CTD) and a protein interaction network, check binding by molecular docking, and add proteomics and 16S rRNA gut microbiome profiling to pick pathways for experimental validation.

## Connections

- Applied to the raw and processed root of Rehmannia glutinosa in an amyloid mouse model. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Subject: [[Rehmanniae radix and Rehmanniae radix praeparata]]
- Applied without omics to ginger ([[Zingiber officinale]]): TCMSP compounds (143, bioavailability at least 30%) mapped to 298 targets, intersected with 1088 GeneCards, OMIM and DisGeNET Alzheimer genes (95 shared), then enrichment, a STRING network whose top five nodes by degree were called hub genes, docking and dynamics. Study: [[6-Gingerol from ginger screened for Alzheimer disease by docking and dynamics]]
- Applied across seven herbs of one therapeutic class, without omics or docking: TCMSP ingredients (oral bioavailability at least 30%, drug-likeness at least 0.18) mapped to 711 GeneCards Alzheimer genes, then targets shared by at least 5 herbs (34) fed to a STRING network and Reactome enrichment. Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
