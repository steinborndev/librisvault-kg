# Primary transcript RT-qPCR assay

A primary transcript RT-qPCR assay places primers and probe inside an intron, so it measures unspliced pre-mRNA and not the mature message. It is useful when a gene has intronless pseudogenes whose RNA is co-amplified by exon-based assays, because the pseudogene transcripts contain no intronic sequence. The pre-mRNA level is only a fraction of the spliced mRNA level and splicing efficiency varies between genes, so the method serves as a surrogate that is valid for comparing samples of the same tissue, under the assumption that the splicing ratio stays constant between them.

For [[TOMM40]] the intron 9 version (IVS9) was more sensitive than an exon 6 to intron 6 version and amplified none of the [[TOMM40 pseudogene RNAs]]. Digital PCR was used alongside to estimate absolute fractions of each RNA species in the total pool.
