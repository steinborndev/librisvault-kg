# Methylation quantitative trait locus

A methylation quantitative trait locus (mQTL) is a sequence variant whose genotype correlates with the methylation level of nearby CpG sites in cis. Because of this, DNA methylation has been proposed as an intermediary between genetic risk and disease, and a disease-associated variant that is not an mQTL points to methylation acting independently of it.
