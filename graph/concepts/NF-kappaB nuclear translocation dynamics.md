# NF-kappaB nuclear translocation dynamics

The time course of p65 ([[RELA]]) moving from cytoplasm to nucleus after a stimulus, read by immunofluorescence. Quantifying whole fields with the Manders overlap coefficient of p65 signal over nuclear stain (JACoP plugin in ImageJ, over 100 cells per image) removes operator choice and keeps cell-to-cell variability; it was checked against single-cell nuclear-to-cytoplasmic ratios (correlation 0.80, saturating at 0.81). A short pulse and a prolonged nuclear stay are associated with different transcriptional programmes, the prolonged one with innate immune activation.
