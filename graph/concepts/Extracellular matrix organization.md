# Extracellular matrix organization

The assembly, arrangement and turnover of the protein and polysaccharide meshwork (collagens, fibronectin, proteoglycans, hyaluronan) that surrounds cells. In brain it shapes perineuronal nets, the vessel basement membrane and synaptic plasticity.

## Connections

- Matrix terms (extracellular matrix, external encapsulating structure, collagen-containing matrix) were the one process upregulated in all six post-mortem brain regions studied in Alzheimer disease; the authors tie it to [[S100A4]] and speculate that collagen deposition stiffens tissue, harms the blood-brain barrier and slows amyloid and tau clearance.
