# Ferroptosis

Ferroptosis is an iron-dependent form of regulated cell death driven by accumulation of oxidized membrane lipids; it is blocked by radical-trapping inhibitors such as [[Liproxstatin-1]]. In microglia it links lipid metabolism to loss of function.

## Connections

- Amyloid beta 42 triggered ferroptosis-like changes ([[Lipid peroxidation]], [[TFRC]] induction, loss of viability rescued by [[Liproxstatin-1]]) in APOE3 but not [[APOE p.R136S]] microglia, which reduced their [[Phagocytosis]]. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Genes: [[TFRC]], [[SAT1]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
