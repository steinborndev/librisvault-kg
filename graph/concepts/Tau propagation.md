# Tau propagation

Tau propagation is the spread of pathological tau between connected neurons and across cortical regions as Alzheimer disease advances.

## Connections

- Neuroimaging of presymptomatic people placed early tangles in the entorhinal cortex and found spreading accelerated at the prodromal stage, with the inferior temporal gyrus as the main hub; two modes of amyloid-beta and tau interaction were proposed, remote (across synapses, lateral entorhinal cortex) and local (co-mingling, inferior temporal gyrus). Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- In human synaptosomes, exosome-encapsulated oligomeric tau, but not free tau, seeded a FRET biosensor, and oligomeric amyloid-beta 42 correlated with that activity; see [[Extracellular vesicle]]. PHFs injected into [[5xFAD mouse]] cortex drove endogenous 4R tau oligomerisation in dystrophic neurites near plaques ([[Dystrophic neurite]]).
- Related: [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]], [[Neurofibrillary tangle]]
