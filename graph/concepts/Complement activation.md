# Complement activation

Complement activation is the enzymatic cascade of more than 30 plasma and cell-surface proteins of innate immunity that opsonises pathogens, debris and damaged cells and generates pro-inflammatory fragments; if dysregulated it drives pathology, including neuroinflammation in [[Alzheimer disease]]. It runs through classical, alternative and terminal pathways and ends in the [[Membrane attack complex]].

## Connections

- Plasma components, regulators and activation products were broadly abnormal in [[Down syndrome]], and C3 and factor I were lower with dementia. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
