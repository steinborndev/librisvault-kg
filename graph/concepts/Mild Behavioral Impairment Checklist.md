# Mild Behavioral Impairment Checklist

A 34-item informant-rated scale for neuropsychiatric symptoms in older adults without dementia, validated in subjective cognitive decline and mild cognitive impairment. Each item is scored present or absent and graded mild, moderate or severe across five domains: decreased motivation, affective dysregulation, impulse dyscontrol, social inappropriateness, and abnormal perception and thought content. Symptoms must be of later-life onset and persistent. Total scores run 0 to 102, with cutoffs of 9 or more for subjective cognitive decline and 7 or more for mild cognitive impairment.
