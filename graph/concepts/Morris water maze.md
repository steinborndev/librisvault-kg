# Morris water maze

The Morris water maze is a rodent test of hippocampus-dependent spatial learning and memory. Animals learn over several days to find a hidden platform in an opaque pool using distant cues, scored by escape latency; in a probe trial the platform is removed and platform-crossing numbers or time in the target quadrant measure memory.

## Connections

- Used with a 5-day protocol (16 training trials over 4 days, then a 2-minute probe) with blinded operators: ADSC exosomes shortened escape latency and raised platform crossings in [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] animals, more so after hypoxic pretreatment and circ-Epc1 loading. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Also read out in the [[Intrahippocampal amyloid-beta infusion rat model]] and the [[D-galactose and aluminium chloride mouse model]].
- Probe-trial time and entries in the target quadrant fell after hippocampal [[PTK2]] overexpression in 3xTg-AD mice, not after knockdown or in wild-type mice (5-day acquisition, 60 s probe on day 6). Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Nine-month-old [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]] animals had longer escape latency, fewer platform crossings and less target-quadrant time than wild-type after 5 days of training (probe on day 6), with equal swimming speed. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Aβ1-42-injected mice had longer escape latency and fewer platform crossings and less target-quadrant time; 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]] reversed all three (testing on days 15-21). Complementary memory test: [[Passive avoidance task]]. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- In 10-month-old [[PS2APP mouse]] animals, four weeks of intranasal [[ALT001]] (1 mg/kg) restored time and distance in the target quadrant on the day-7 probe to wild-type levels (n = 6 wild type, 9 per transgenic group). Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Four training days with a hidden platform and a 1-minute probe: [[D-ribose]] lengthened escape latency and shortened target-quadrant time in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals with equal swimming speed; [[Berberine]] reversed this and [[Mdivi-1]] undid it. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- In 8-month-old [[5xFAD mouse]] animals 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]] did not speed four-day acquisition or change the first probe, but sped reversal learning and shifted the second probe from the old to the new platform quadrant; treated wild-type mice were faster on acquisition day 4. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- Five training days (4 trials a day, 60 s cap) and a 30 s probe: AAV-[[UVRAG]] in hippocampal CA1 of 10-month-old [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals improved acquisition and target-quadrant time (6 to 8 mice per group; the legend labels two groups identically). Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Learning curves did not differ between genotypes, but only 12-month APP/PS1 mice showed impaired retention, which was absent in animals also lacking [[P2RX4]]. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
