# Human microglia xenotransplantation model

Human iPSC-derived microglial precursors are injected into the brains of immunodeficient neonatal mice, where they engraft, mature and respond to the host's pathology. Because only the grafted microglia carry the human genotype, a gene such as [[APOE]] can be manipulated in microglia alone while astrocytes and neurons stay mouse.

## Workflow

- Precursors are made with the MIGRATE protocol (embryoid bodies with BMP4, VEGF and SCF, then cytokines in X-VIVO medium; harvested at day 18) and injected into postnatal day 4 pups after endogenous microglia are depleted with the CSF1R inhibitor BLZ945.
- Hosts are Rag2-/- Il2rg-/- mice with human CSF1 knocked in (so human microglia survive and no adaptive immunity rejects them) crossed to [[AppNL-G-F mouse]] amyloid mice.
- Grafted cells are recovered by FACS on human CD45 and CD11b and profiled by RNA-seq, [[ATAC-seq]] or single-cell sequencing. Single-cell work in this model has described eight amyloid-responsive human microglial states, including disease-associated, cytokine-response, antigen-presenting (HLA) and ribosomal states.
