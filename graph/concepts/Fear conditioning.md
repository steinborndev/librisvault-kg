# Fear conditioning

Fear conditioning pairs a tone with a mild foot shock in a chamber; 24 hours later the animal's freezing in the same chamber measures hippocampus-dependent contextual memory, and freezing to the tone in a different chamber measures cued memory.

## Connections

- Used with a 2-minute exploration, two 30 s tone-shock pairings (0.7 mA) and testing at 24 h: hippocampal [[PTK2]] overexpression lowered contextual and cued freezing in [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals, while knockdown did not. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Companion test of spatial memory: [[Morris water maze]]
