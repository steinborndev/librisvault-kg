# Rehmanniae radix and Rehmanniae radix praeparata

Rehmanniae radix (RR) is the dried root of Rehmannia glutinosa used raw in traditional Chinese medicine; Rehmanniae radix praeparata (RRP) is the same root steamed with rice wine. Tradition ascribes a clearing nature to RR and a tonifying nature to RRP, and both are used for dementia.
