# Primary microglia in vitro aging model

Microglia are purified from mixed glial cultures of neonatal mouse cortex by mild trypsinization after 21 days, then kept for 2 or 16 days in vitro (DIV). The 2 DIV cells are amoeboid and activated by the isolation itself; the 16 DIV cells are ramified or rod-like, migrate and phagocytose less, express fewer inflammatory genes and are treated as an aged, desensitized state. It offers aged microglia without waiting years for aged animals, at the cost that age is only time in culture.
