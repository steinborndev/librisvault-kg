# Pharmacogenetics

Pharmacogenetics studies how inherited variants change a person's response to a drug, in effect, adverse reactions or dose needed. A typical design genotypes a cohort of treated patients, measures a response biomarker before and after treatment, and tests genotype by treatment interaction terms in a regression model, usually adjusting for age, sex and body mass.

In an observational cohort the comparison group is often untreated patients, which leaves confounding by indication and prior treatment, and many genotype, drug and outcome combinations call for false discovery rate correction.
