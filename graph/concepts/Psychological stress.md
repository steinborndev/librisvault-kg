# Psychological stress

Psychological stress is the body's response to adverse change, acting through the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis with release of [[CRH]] and [[Cortisol]] (corticosterone in rodents). When chronic it is a proposed risk factor for pain, depression, cognitive decline and dementia.

## Connections

- Reviewed as raising [[APP]], Aβ, tau phosphorylation and plaques in animals, activating [[Mast cell activation]] and weakening the [[Tight junction]] of the blood-brain barrier; early-life stress is linked to late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] dementia. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- Worsens [[Migraine]] and [[Pain]]; lowers [[BDNF]]
- Related: [[Neuroinflammation]], [[Blood-brain barrier dysfunction]]
