# Functional validation of presenilin variants in transfected cells

A candidate [[PSEN1]] or [[PSEN2]] variant is cloned into a plasmid and transfected, next to the wild-type gene, into cells that already express Swedish-mutant [[APP]]. After 48 hours the Aβ40 and Aβ42 peptides in the culture medium are measured by ELISA, and the variant is compared with wild type on Aβ42, Aβ40 and the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio. A rise in Aβ42 and in the ratio is taken as evidence that the variant shifts gamma-secretase cleavage toward the aggregation-prone peptide, as typical disease-causing presenilin changes do.

The readout covers only one mechanism. A variant with no effect on these three numbers is not thereby benign, because a mutant such as APP p.E693G is reported to leave Aβ levels unchanged yet form protofibrils quickly.

## Connections

- Host cells: [[HEK293]] carrying Swedish-mutant APP; peptide measured: [[Amyloid-beta]]
- Used to confirm [[PSEN2 p.N141S]], [[PSEN2 p.M239T]] and [[PSEN2 p.I368F]] and to find no effect for five other variants. Study: [[PSEN2 mutation spectrum and functionally validated variants in a Chinese Alzheimer cohort]]
- Gives functional evidence that feeds [[ACMG variant classification]]; sequence-based counterpart: [[In silico missense variant effect prediction]]
