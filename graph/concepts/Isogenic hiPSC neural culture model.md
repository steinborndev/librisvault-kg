# Isogenic hiPSC neural culture model

A set of human induced pluripotent stem cell (hiPSC) lines that share one genetic background and differ at a single edited locus, differentiated into neurons and astrocytes so that a genotype can be tested without the noise of patient-to-patient variation. In the APOE2 study each parent line was homozygous APOE3 and was edited to APOE2, using a transient reporter for editing enrichment (TREE) that gave clonal homozygous editing near 90%.

## Workflow

- Neural progenitors are made from embryoid bodies (Noggin and dorsomorphin), expanded on laminin, then seeded on laminin-coated microcarriers in low-attachment plates on an orbital shaker and differentiated for at least 30 days in BDNF and GDNF medium.
- Cultures are mixed neurons (TUJ1, MAP2, NEUN) and astrocytes (GFAP). Astrocytes can be separated by magnetic sorting on CD44, giving CD44-negative neurons and CD44-positive astrocytes (92 to 99% pure by flow cytometry), checked by RNA sequencing, APOE secretion and calcium transients.
- Readouts are ELISAs of secreted and cellular proteins (Aβ40, Aβ42, sAPPα, sAPPβ, APP-βCTF, phospho-tau), labelled-Aβ uptake and surface receptor flow cytometry.
