# MethylTarget sequencing

A next-generation sequencing method for multiplexed targeted CpG [[DNA methylation]] analysis: DNA is bisulfite-converted, regions of 100 to 300 nucleotides are amplified with primers flanking the target CpG sites, and the libraries are sequenced on an Illumina platform. Quality filters typically include bisulfite conversion rate, read quality and coverage.

## Connections

- Used with six regions of the [[BIN1]] CpG island in blood DNA from 814 adults; samples were dropped for conversion below 99%, mean coverage below 30-fold or missing rate above 0.01. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
- The earlier bisulfite pyrosequencing approach is described under [[DNA methylation]].
