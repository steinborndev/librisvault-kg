# Arivale Scientific Wellness cohort

A deeply phenotyped cohort of subscribers to the commercial Arivale Scientific Wellness program (July 2015 to May 2019, now closed), held by the Institute for Systems Biology. Participants had a health coach and were profiled with clinical blood tests, whole-genome sequencing, Olink proximity-extension proteomics, Metabolon metabolomics and questionnaires. The cohort is mostly non-Hispanic white, mostly from the US west coast, aged 18 to 89+, and was not selected for disease, which makes it a source of molecular data in people long before any dementia. Data are available to qualified researchers on request to the Institute for Systems Biology.
