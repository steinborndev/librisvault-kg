# Lipid peroxidation

Lipid peroxidation is the oxidative degradation of lipids, especially polyunsaturated membrane lipids, and the execution step of [[Ferroptosis]]. It can be read in live cells with the BODIPY 581/591 C11 dye, whose emission shifts from red to green on oxidation.
