# Purinergic signaling

Purinergic signaling is cell communication through extracellular ATP, ADP and adenosine acting on P2X channels and P2Y and adenosine receptors. Microglia carry a large set of these receptors whose pattern depends on activation state: [[P2RY12]] is high in homeostatic microglia and falls in reactive ones, while [[P2RX4]] is absent from homeostatic cells and induced on activation.

## Connections

- P2X4 is switched on in neuropathic pain, status epilepticus and plaque-associated microglia of the [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]], where it promotes ApoE degradation; in [[Multiple sclerosis]] its expression is reported as beneficial through myelin phagocytosis. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
