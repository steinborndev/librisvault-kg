# PI3K-Akt-GSK3B axis

A survival and plasticity pathway in which phosphoinositide 3-kinase activates [[AKT1]], and Akt phosphorylates [[GSK3B]] at Ser9 to switch it off. Loss of Akt signalling leaves GSK-3β active, a state linked in the "GSK3 hypothesis" to Aβ production, tau phosphorylation, microglial inflammation, synapse loss and memory deficits in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- In [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain, phosphorylated Akt (Ser473) and phosphorylated GSK-3β (Ser9) were lowered, and [[Magnolol]] (both doses) and [[Donepezil]] raised both ratios; the pathway link is inferred from western blots, not from an inhibitor experiment. Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Parallel inflammatory arm in the same study: [[NF-kappaB signaling]]
- Phospho-[[PIK3R1]] (Tyr607), phospho-[[AKT1]] (Ser473) and Ser9 phospho-[[GSK3B]] were all low in 5xFAD hippocampus and rose after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]], with total proteins unchanged (n = 4); the link to amyloid is argued from the literature. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
