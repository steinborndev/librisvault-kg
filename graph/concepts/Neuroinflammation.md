# Neuroinflammation

Neuroinflammation is the innate immune response of the brain, driven largely by microglia and astrocytes; in Alzheimer disease amyloid-beta engages [[TLR4]] and the [[NLRP3]] inflammasome and raises [[IL1B]].

## Connections

- Modulated in this study by ajugol and isoacteoside through the [[TLR4]], [[RELA]], [[NLRP3]] and [[CASP1]] axis. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- TNFA signaling via NFKB, interferon gamma response and allograft rejection pathways in blood and brain ran in opposite directions in APOE ε3/ε4 carriers and non-carriers, and their leading-edge genes tracked brain vascular injury proteins. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
- APOE4 human microglia raised cytokine genes and showed an interferon-related STAT2 motif in open chromatin, while APOE2 microglia raised immune-response and TNF-family genes; whether these responses protect or harm is left open. See [[CCL3]], [[STAT2]], [[TNFRSF25]]. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
- Activated Iba1-positive microglia, GFAP-positive reactive astrocytes and brain [[TNF]], [[IL1B]] and [[IL6]] mRNA were all lower in [[FAD4T mouse]] animals after BACE1 silencing with [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]; the authors attribute this to the lower amyloid burden. See [[Disease-associated microglia]]. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- In rat primary astrocytes Aβ1-42 alone raised [[MIR146A]] through [[NF-kappaB signaling]] without inducing IL6, IL1B, TNF or CXCL1, an anti-inflammatory feedback that blocking miR-146a-5p removed; see [[Astrocyte activation]]. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Brain IL-6, TNF-α and IL-1β fell, and hippocampal microglia shifted from CD11b-positive M1 toward CD206-positive M2, after circ-Epc1-containing stem cell exosomes in Alzheimer mice; see [[Microglial cell activation]] and [[Adipose-derived stem cell exosome therapy]]. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Mast cells close to nerve endings, meninges and vessels release [[Histamine]], tryptase, [[NGF]] and [[TNF]] and feed back with glia, so that even few cells could sustain neuroinflammation; stress and [[CRH]] are proposed triggers. See [[Mast cell activation]] and [[Psychological stress]]. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- In aging [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] cortex, [[TSPO]] binding and [[IL1B]] protein rose before serotonin transporter ([[SLC6A4]]) density and uptake fell; TSPO and transporter binding were inversely correlated in parietal cortex, and age alone raised TSPO, IL-1β, IL-6 and TNF in wild-type mice. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
- Amyloid-beta 1-42 raised [[TNF]], [[IL1B]], [[IL6]], [[HMGB1]], [[IL18]], [[TLR2]] and [[TLR4]] in young cultured mouse microglia but only TNF and IL1B, from a 10-fold lower baseline, in aged ones; see [[Cellular senescence]]. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Brain RNA-seq in APP/PS1 mice showed induced inflammatory genes ([[CST7]], [[CCL3]], [[C3]], [[CLEC7A]]); [[C3]] and [[CCL3]] rose further with Sirt3 deletion. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- Inhibition or deficiency of [[SCD]] suppresses neuroinflammation in models of [[Multiple sclerosis]], [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]]. Study: [[SCD1 as a therapeutic target in neurological disorders review]]
- Activated microglia limit their own inflammatory response by secreting [[IL19]], an interleukin-10 family cytokine that rose in an Alzheimer mouse model as disease progressed. Study: [[Interleukin-19 as an autocrine brake on activated microglia]]
- A diet rich in [[cis-9, trans-11-conjugated linoleic acid]] was read as suppressing inflammation in an Alzheimer mouse model, via more [[IL10]]- and [[IL19]]-expressing astrocytes. Study: [[Dietary conjugated linoleic acid lowers amyloid-beta in Alzheimer mouse hippocampus]]
- A shared inflammatory signature (TNF via NF-κB, higher [[NFKB1]] and [[PLCG2]]) was seen in caudate nucleus of both [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]]. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Iba-1-positive microglia and brain [[TNF]] and [[IL1B]] rose after intracerebroventricular Aβ1-42 and fell with 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]]; see [[AIF1]]. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- Transplanting old APP/PS1 microbiota into young wild-type mice raised microglial Iba1 and iNOS in cortex and hippocampus without plaques, read as early neuroinflammation driven by gut-derived Aβ. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- In [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain, microglia and astrocytes were more numerous and TNF-α, IL-1β and IL-6 were raised with IL-10 lowered; [[Magnolol]] reversed these, and the authors place NF-κB p65 phosphorylation in the mechanism. Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Aβ-driven NF-κB activation in human iPSC microglia-like cells depended on the human-specific [[CHRFAM7A]] gene. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
- Elevated TNF-α was proposed as the trigger of neuronal [[Necroptosis]] in Alzheimer brain, a later, secondary death route after Aβ-driven inflammation. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Predicted shared mechanism of seven heat-clearing herbs against Alzheimer disease, via PTGS1, IL-6, CASP3, VEGFA and IL-4/IL-13 signaling; untested. Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
