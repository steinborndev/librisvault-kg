# Chromosome conformation capture assay

The 3C assay tests whether two chosen genomic regions, an anchor and a target, lie physically close in the nucleus. Cells or tissue are fixed with formaldehyde, chromatin is cut with a restriction enzyme, the cut ends are ligated under dilute conditions so that neighbouring fragments join, and the cross-links are reversed. Primers spanning the junction then detect a chimeric anchor-target fragment by PCR, confirmed by Sanger sequencing. It is a targeted one-versus-one counterpart of genome-wide [[Hi-C chromatin interaction analysis]].

## Connections
