# Retrograde transport from endosome to Golgi

The route by which cargo receptors leave endosomes and return to the trans-Golgi network, carried out by the retromer complex, or are recycled to the cell surface. [[SORL1]] acts as the cargo-binding component with retromer: binding [[APP]] speeds its exit from the early endosome, which limits APP cleavage and amyloid beta secretion, as covered in [[Amyloid-beta formation]]. SORL1 reaches the endosome as a monomer, dimerises through its 3Fn and VPS10p domains and uses its cytoplasmic tail to contact the VPS26 subunit of retromer.

- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]; variants that misfold SORL1 and block its exit from the endoplasmic reticulum are discussed in [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
