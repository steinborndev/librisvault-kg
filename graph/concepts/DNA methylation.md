# DNA methylation

The addition of methyl groups to cytosines, mostly at CpG sites, an epigenetic mark that influences chromatin state and gene expression. It is measured here by bisulfite conversion followed by pyrosequencing.

## Connections

- Methylation of the differentially methylated region in the [[APOE]] exon 4 CpG island did not differ between Alzheimer disease and control frontal lobe; it correlated positively with chromatin contact strength in controls and negatively in ε3/ε3 carriers, but [[Causal mediation analysis]] gave little evidence that it mediates disease or genotype effects on the APOE-[[APOC1]] contact. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
- Related process: [[Chromatin looping]]
- Gene-level methylation was integrated with GWAS signals in network modules; [[GNAS]] was the most significantly hypermethylated gene in Alzheimer disease. Study: [[Network-based integration of Alzheimer GWAS and DNA methylation finds BIN1 and GNAS module]]
- Targeted bisulfite sequencing ([[MethylTarget sequencing]]) of the [[BIN1]] promoter in blood found lower methylation in subjective cognitive decline and with amyloid positivity, confined to one CpG-island region, unrelated to age, sex or APOE ε4 status. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
- ROSMAP prefrontal cortex methylation (761 participants, Illumina 450K) was tested against amyloid load and PHF-tau tangle density for genes in lipid-covariant early-onset Alzheimer regions; a site in MS4A6A associated with both (amyloid P = 0.02, tau P = 0.005). Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
- The PINK1 promoter was more methylated, with higher [[DNMT1]] and lower PINK1 protein, in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] hippocampus and [[Neuro2a]] cells exposed to [[D-ribose]]; [[Berberine]] and [[Azacitidine]] reversed this (measured by [[Bisulfite sequencing PCR]]). Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Targeted clone sequencing of [[CpG island shore]] regions in Japanese blood found lower methylation in Alzheimer disease at [[CR1]], [[CLU]], [[PICALM]] and [[TREM2]], but not at [[APOE]], [[BIN1]] or [[ABCA7]]; see also [[Methylation quantitative trait locus]] and [[Blood-brain DNA methylation concordance]]. Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
