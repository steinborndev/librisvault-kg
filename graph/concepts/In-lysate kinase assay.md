# In-lysate kinase assay

A screen for substrates of one kinase. Tissue lysate proteins are chemically cleaved at cysteine residues, which destroys endogenous kinase, phosphatase and protease activity; the lysate is then incubated with ATP with or without purified active kinase, biotin-labelled, and hybridised to a phosphosite-specific antibody array ([[Kinexus KAM-1325 antibody microarray]]). Sites with higher signal after adding the kinase are candidate direct or kinase-dependent targets.

## Connections

- Run with recombinant human [[PTK2]] on pooled hippocampal lysate from five [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals, it listed 182 tyrosine-phosphorylated proteins, among them [[ROCK1]], [[ROCK2]], [[ABL1]], [[MST1R]], [[IGF1R]], [[ERBB2]], [[ERBB3]], [[NTRK2]], [[JAK2]], [[STAT1]], [[CDK5]] and [[GRIN2A]].
