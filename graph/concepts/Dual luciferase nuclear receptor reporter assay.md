# Dual luciferase nuclear receptor reporter assay

A cell-based reporter assay for nuclear receptor activation: cells are transfected with a receptor cDNA, [[RXRA]] and a firefly luciferase gene under a receptor response element (LXRE or PPRE), plus Renilla luciferase for normalisation, and the firefly-to-Renilla ratio after treatment, relative to the solvent control, gives fold activation.

## Connections

- Used with murine LXRα, LXRβ, PPARα and PPARγ cDNAs in [[HEK293]], [[CCF-STTG1]], [[SH-SY5Y]] and [[CHME3]] cells, with [[T0901317]], [[Pirinixic acid]] and [[Pioglitazone]] as positive controls; groups were compared with a Kruskal-Wallis test. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Receptors: [[NR1H3]], [[NR1H2]], [[PPARA]], [[PPARG]]
