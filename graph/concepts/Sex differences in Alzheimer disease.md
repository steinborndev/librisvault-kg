# Sex differences in Alzheimer disease

Women make up about two-thirds of Alzheimer disease cases and show faster cognitive decline, including in mild cognitive impairment, and a stronger link between hippocampal loss and disease. Proposed contributors are gonadal hormones (estrogen loss at menopause, early menopause and late start of hormone therapy raising tau vulnerability), immune differences and sex-specific genetic effects.

## Connections

- Sex-split postmortem hippocampus expression: male cases upregulate the [[Estrogen signaling pathway]] and downregulate the [[GABA signaling pathway]], female cases downregulate the [[Dopamine receptor signaling pathway]] and change [[Calcium signalling]] in a subfield-dependent way; [[SLC1A7]] runs in opposite directions. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
- Healthy men showed higher [[BACE1]] expression than healthy women in hippocampal subfields. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
- Blood proteins linked to a risk variant at [[PICALM rs3851179]] moved in opposite directions in men and women. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Treatment angle: [[Menopausal hormone therapy]]; receptors [[ESR1]], [[ESR2]], [[GPER1]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Anxiety and depression were more frequent and severe in women than men with Alzheimer disease (moderate to severe anxiety about twice as common), though response to treatment was similar; men had more cardiovascular risk factors but these were unrelated to mood. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
- The largest oligodendrocyte acetylation set (1,962 hypoacetylated peaks) was found only in women's hippocampus, but men had less amyloid and an interaction test found little sex specificity. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- In 5xFAD-M mice, males had more severe CA1 spine loss than females, and both [[EDR peptide]] and [[KED peptide]] restored mushroom spines in males but not females; the sex difference was not tested as an interaction. Study: [[EDR and KED tripeptides restore dendritic spines in 5xFAD mice]]
