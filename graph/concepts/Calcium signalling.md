# Calcium signalling

Calcium signalling is the use of changes in cytosolic calcium as a messenger. In macrophages and microglia, receptor-linked phospholipase C generates inositol trisphosphate that opens store channels and floods the cytosol with calcium, which in turn feeds phagocytosis, cytokine release and endocytosis.
