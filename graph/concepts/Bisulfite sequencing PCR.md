# Bisulfite sequencing PCR

Bisulfite sequencing PCR (BSP) converts unmethylated cytosines to uracil with bisulfite, amplifies a chosen promoter or CpG-island region with primers designed for converted DNA (for example with MethPrimer), and reads the sequence of individual clones to give the methylation state of each CpG. It is a low-throughput, region-specific counterpart to [[MethylTarget sequencing]] for [[DNA methylation]].

## Connections

- Used on mouse hippocampus to show that the PINK1 promoter was more methylated with [[D-ribose]] and less so with [[Berberine]] or [[Azacitidine]]. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Related measure of methylation: [[MethylTarget sequencing]]
- Used on blood DNA with 32 cloned colonies per amplicon (at least 20 sequences analysed with QUMA), about 450 bp long, with pyrosequencing as a shorter-read check. Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
