# Cell migration

Cell migration is the directed movement of a cell from one place to another. Microglia migrate towards amyloid plaques and sites of neuronal damage, which is a precondition for clearing them.

## Connections

- A co-expression module for proliferation and migration was higher in APOE2 than in APOE3 or APOE4 human microglia in amyloid mice, and the authors argue migration is reduced in APOE4 and raised in APOE2 microglia, with lower [[CHCHD2]] in APOE4. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
- Related: [[Phagocytosis]], [[Neuroinflammation]], [[APOE]]
- In a Boyden chamber, mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture migrated toward amyloid-beta 1-42 (1.7-fold) and ATP (2.0-fold); those kept 16 days did not. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
