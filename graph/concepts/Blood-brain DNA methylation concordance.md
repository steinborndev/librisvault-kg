# Blood-brain DNA methylation concordance

The degree to which the methylation of a CpG site in blood predicts its methylation in brain tissue of the same person. It decides whether blood [[DNA methylation]] can serve as a surrogate for brain changes in neurodegeneration. For most CpG sites, between-person variation in whole blood is a weak predictor of that in brain; public tools such as BECon (Blood-Brain Epigenetic Concordance) and the Blood Brain DNA methylation Comparison Tool report it per CpG from matched samples on the Illumina 450K array.

## Connections

- At five probes covering the hypomethylated shores of [[CR1]], [[CLU]] and [[PICALM]], blood and brain methylation were uncorrelated, apart from one PICALM CpG in the entorhinal cortex; brain variation between people was also smaller than blood variation.
