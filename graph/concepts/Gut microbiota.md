# Gut microbiota

The bacteria, fungi and viruses living in the gastrointestinal tract. Its composition changes with age and in Alzheimer patients and transgenic mouse models, which makes it a candidate modifier of [[Alzheimer disease]] through the [[Gut-brain axis]].

## Connections

- In 12-month-old [[APP-PS1 mouse]] faeces, Bacteroidales, Prevotellaceae and Prevotella rose and beneficial Akkermansia, Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus fell; alpha-diversity was unchanged. Transferred by [[Fecal microbiota transplantation]] into young wild-type mice it raised colonic [[BACE1]] and [[Amyloid-beta]] and primed microglia. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Gut microbiota was also profiled in nine-month-old APP/PS1 males after a herbal extract. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Related: [[Increased intestinal permeability]]
- Faecal 16S sequencing of 8-month-old 5xFAD mice found less Prevotellaceae and more Eggerthellaceae, Streptococcaceae and Escherichia-Shigella than wild type; [[D-Pinitol]] for 18 weeks lowered Streptococcaceae and Escherichia-Shigella, and shifted several other families differently in the two genotypes. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
