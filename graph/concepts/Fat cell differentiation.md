# Fat cell differentiation

Fat cell differentiation is the process by which a precursor cell (preadipocyte) becomes a mature adipocyte, with [[PPARG]] as a central driver.

## Connections

- In mice, SCD2 ([[SCD]]) is not required for preadipocyte differentiation or for PPARG expression in vivo, although it is required in vitro. Study: [[SCD2 in murine development, metabolism and disease review]]
