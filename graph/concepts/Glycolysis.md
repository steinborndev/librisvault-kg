# Glycolysis

Glycolysis breaks glucose down to pyruvate, with [[Lactic acid]] as the end product when pyruvate is not oxidized in mitochondria. Hexokinase, which traps glucose as glucose-6-phosphate, is its first enzyme. When glycolysis is high relative to oxygen use (the Warburg effect, or aerobic glycolysis), ATP production is shifted away from mitochondria. In [[Alzheimer disease]] brain, glucose hypometabolism and mitochondrial impairment are early features, and glycolytic activity not matched by oxidative phosphorylation is seen in regions prone to amyloid beta accumulation.
