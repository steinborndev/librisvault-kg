# Translational initiation

The assembly of a ribosome on an mRNA at a start codon. Which AUG is used depends on its Kozak context and on 5′ untranslated region structure, so a variant in the 5′UTR can move the start site and extend or shorten the protein's N-terminus.

## Connections

- Example: [[BCHE rs1126680]] was linked to an upstream in-frame start in [[BCHE]] that adds 41 amino acids to the signal peptide, seen by serum mass spectrometry. Study: [[BCHE noncoding variants and APOE ε4 in Polish late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- The splice-based route to extended N-termini is covered in [[Alternative splicing]]
