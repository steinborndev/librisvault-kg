# Protein palmitoylation

Protein palmitoylation is the reversible attachment of the fatty acid palmitate to cysteine residues, added by DHHC-family palmitoyl acyltransferases such as [[ZDHHC21]] and removed by thioesterases. It governs membrane association, trafficking and lipid-raft targeting of its substrates.

## Connections

- Hippocampal palmitoylation of [[APP]] (+47.6%) and [[FYN]] (+25.2%) was raised in mice carrying [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]]; the authors argue palmitoylated APP is a better [[BACE1]] substrate in lipid rafts and palmitoylated FYN drives NMDA receptor phosphorylation. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Measured with the [[Acyl-biotin exchange assay]]; blocked in cells by [[2-Bromopalmitate]] and [[Cerulenin]]
