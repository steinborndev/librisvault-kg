# Interleukin-17 signaling pathway

Signalling by the pro-inflammatory cytokine interleukin-17, which promotes immune-cell infiltration of the brain and has been reported to inhibit adult hippocampal neurogenesis.

## Connections

- The one signaling pathway upregulated in both sexes in both hippocampal subfields of Alzheimer disease, suggested by the authors as a therapeutic target common to men and women. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
