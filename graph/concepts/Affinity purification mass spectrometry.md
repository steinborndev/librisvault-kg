# Affinity purification mass spectrometry

Affinity purification mass spectrometry captures a native protein complex with an antibody on beads and identifies the co-purified partners by tandem mass spectrometry; comparing wild-type with knockout material removes antibody background. Detergent choice in membrane preparations sets which interactions survive.

## Connections

- Applied to membrane-enriched [[Bone marrow-derived macrophages]] with an anti-P2X4 antibody (LTQ-Orbitrap, searched against a mouse database); [[APOE]] was the only partner with significant coverage in two independent experiments and was confirmed by reciprocal co-immunoprecipitation. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
