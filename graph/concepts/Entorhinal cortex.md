# Entorhinal cortex

The entorhinal cortex sits in the medial temporal lobe next to the hippocampus and relays information between hippocampus, amygdala and neocortex, which makes it central to learning and memory. It is one of the first cortical regions to lose neurons and to collect amyloid and tangles in [[Alzheimer disease]] (see [[Braak staging]]). Monkeys such as the cynomolgus macaque resemble humans in its structure and position.
