# FDG PET

Positron emission tomography with the glucose analogue fluorodeoxyglucose (18F-FDG) maps regional glucose uptake in the living brain, which is used as a measure of regional neuronal metabolism. Uptake is usually expressed as a standardized uptake value ratio (SUVR), the regional uptake divided by that of a reference region such as the pons.

In [[Alzheimer disease]] uptake falls early and in a typical pattern, in posterior cingulate and temporo-parietal cortex, and regional values have been used to predict conversion from [[Mild cognitive impairment]] to dementia. Because FDG PET gives one value per region per person, correlating regions across participants yields a metabolic network rather than a time series, which needs a different reading than functional MRI networks.
