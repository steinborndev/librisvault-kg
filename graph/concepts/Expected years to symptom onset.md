# Expected years to symptom onset

Expected years to symptom onset (EYO) is the difference between a participant's age at a visit and the expected age at symptom onset for their family, usually the mean onset age of affected relatives or of the same variant. It puts carriers of different variants and ages on one disease-time axis in dominantly inherited [[Familial Alzheimer disease]], so biomarker and cognitive trajectories can be compared before and after onset. Models often treat it as a restricted cubic spline to allow non-linear change.
