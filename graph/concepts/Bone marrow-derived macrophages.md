# Bone marrow-derived macrophages

Bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMDM) are primary macrophages differentiated in culture from mouse femur and tibia marrow, here in L929-cell conditioned medium. They are an accessible myeloid model with abundant lysosomes for biochemistry that is hard to do in sorted microglia.

## Connections

- Wild-type and P2X4-null BMDM were used to purify native [[P2RX4]] complexes, to show that [[APOE]] accumulates without P2X4 (mRNA unchanged), and to test protease inhibitors and [[CTSB]] activity; macrophages from cathepsin B-deficient mice served as a control. Results were echoed in primary microglia. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
