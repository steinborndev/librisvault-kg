# Acute-phase response

The rapid systemic or local change in protein production that follows infection, injury or inflammation, driven largely by [[IL6]] and [[IL1B]].

## Connections

- Acute-phase response genes were higher in microglia of 5xFAD;CD33-/- mice and lower in those of 5xFAD;TREM2-/- mice, and the CD33-knockout rise needed TREM2. Study: [[TREM2 acts downstream of CD33 in 5xFAD mouse microglia]]
- Related: [[Neuroinflammation]], [[CXCL8]]
