# Label-free quantitative mass spectrometry proteomics

Untargeted discovery proteomics in which proteins are digested to peptides, separated by liquid chromatography and identified by tandem mass spectrometry, and relative abundance is read from peptide signal intensity without isotope labels. Data-independent acquisition (DIA) fragments all peptides in fixed mass windows, and parallel accumulation serial fragmentation (PASEF) on a timsTOF instrument adds ion mobility. It contrasts with targeted panels such as [[Multiple reaction monitoring mass spectrometry]].

## Connections

- Used on mouse CSF and sorted microglia, human iPSC-derived microglia lysates and media, and human CSF, with sample preparation by in-solution digestion, filter-aided preparation or single-pot solid-phase-enhanced preparation (SP3), and searches by MaxQuant, Spectronaut or DIA-NN at 1% false discovery rate; missing values were not imputed and a protein needed signal in at least three replicates. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related model system: [[iPSC-derived microglia knockout models]]

- Applied to CSF from three cohorts (n = 110, 112 and 78) covering Alzheimer disease, Lewy body and frontotemporal dementia, amyloid-positive and negative mild cognitive impairment, and controls. Study: [[CSF proteomics of Alzheimer, Lewy body and frontotemporal dementia]]
