# Fecal microbiota transplantation

Transfer of faecal microbiota from a donor to a recipient, in mouse work by oral gavage of a sonicated faecal supernatant, to test whether a microbiome alone carries a phenotype. Self-gavaged littermates are the usual control.

## Connections

- Supernatant from 12-month-old [[APP-PS1 mouse]] donors, given three times a week to just-weaned wild-type mice, shifted their microbiota within a month, raised colonic [[BACE1]] and [[Amyloid-beta]] 42, and by two months gave weaker Y-maze and novel object recognition with microglial activation but no plaques.
