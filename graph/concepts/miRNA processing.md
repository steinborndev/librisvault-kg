# miRNA processing

miRNA processing is the multi-step maturation of a microRNA: a primary transcript (pri-miRNA) is cut in the nucleus by Drosha into a precursor (pre-miRNA), which is exported to the cytoplasm and cut by [[DICER1]] into the mature ~22-nucleotide microRNA. Altered processing can change mature levels without a matching change in the primary transcript.

## Connections

- Mature [[MIR650]] was higher in Alzheimer cortex while its primary and precursor forms were lower, taken as altered primary-to-mature processing. Study: [[MicroRNA-650 targets CDK5 and lessens amyloid pathology in APP-PSEN1 mice]]
- Short and long amyloid oligomer exposure change the processing enzyme Dicer1 in opposite ways. Study: [[Amyloid oligomers rapidly raise neuronal Dicer1 by reducing phosphorylation]]
