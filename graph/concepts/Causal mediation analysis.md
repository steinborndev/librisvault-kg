# Causal mediation analysis

A statistical decomposition of how much of an exposure's total effect on an outcome passes through an intermediate variable (the mediator). It reports an average causal mediation effect (ACME, the indirect path), an average direct effect (ADE) and the total effect, each with confidence intervals, and assumes no unmeasured confounding of the exposure-mediator-outcome links.

## Connections

- Used with [[DNA methylation]] of the [[APOE]] exon 4 differentially methylated region as mediator, disease status and genotype as exposures, and chromatin contact strength or [[APOC1]] mRNA as outcomes: the indirect effect was minimal, so methylation did not mediate the genotype and disease effects.
