# Cholesterol efflux

Cholesterol efflux is the export of cholesterol from cells to extracellular acceptors; in the brain astrocytes use [[ABCA1]] and [[ABCG1]] to load [[APOE]] particles that supply neurons.

## Connections

- All brown seaweed lipid extracts raised the efflux genes ABCG1, ABCA1 and APOE in [[CCF-STTG1]] cells; the authors argue this could support neuronal regeneration, Aβ clearance and lower neuroinflammation. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Regulators: [[NR1H3]], [[NR1H2]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Brain export through [[CYP46A1]], [[ABCA1]] and [[ABCG1]] is reported impaired in Alzheimer disease. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
