# Whole-genome sequencing

Whole-genome sequencing reads essentially the whole genome of a person, coding and non-coding, and so captures rare variants in regions that arrays and exome capture miss. Rare variants are individually too infrequent to test one by one, so they are usually pooled by gene and tested as a group.
