# Synapse organization

The assembly, arrangement and maintenance of synapses, carried out by scaffolds, adhesion molecules and receptors such as [[SHANK2]], [[HOMER1]], [[NRXN1]] and [[GRIN2A]].

## Connections

- Genes with reduced CTCF binding in Alzheimer frontal cortex were enriched in this process, as were genes that lose H3K27 acetylation. Study: [[Diminished CTCF binding at synaptic genes in Alzheimer frontal cortex]]
- Related: [[Synapse loss]], [[Postsynaptic density]], [[Alzheimer disease]]

- Synaptic processes were enriched among CSF proteins altered in frontotemporal dementia. Study: [[CSF proteomics of Alzheimer, Lewy body and frontotemporal dementia]]
- Top enriched term among the 244 genes that differ between two Alzheimer brain subtypes defined by [[CDK5R1]] level. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
