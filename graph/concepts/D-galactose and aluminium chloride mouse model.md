# D-galactose and aluminium chloride mouse model

A non-transgenic model in which ICR mice receive D-galactose (60 mg/kg/day) and aluminium chloride (100 mg/kg/day) for 8 weeks to induce accelerated aging, spatial memory deficits and amyloid staining in the hippocampus.

## Connections

- Used to compare ajugol, isoacteoside and the two root extracts with donepezil, read by the Morris water maze, open-field test, thioflavin-S staining and western blots. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
