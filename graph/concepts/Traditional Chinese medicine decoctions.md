# Traditional Chinese medicine decoctions

Multi-herb water extracts taken as prescriptions in traditional Chinese medicine. Five were compared in a mouse brain expression study for Alzheimer disease: Liu-Wei-Di-Huang (LW), Ba-Wei-Di-Huang (BW), Danggui-Shaoyao-San (DSS), Huang-Lian-Jie-Du (HL) and Tiao-Xin-Fang (TXF). They differ in constituents but were treated as sharing a beneficial effect on the disease.
