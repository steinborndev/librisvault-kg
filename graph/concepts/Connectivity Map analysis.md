# Connectivity Map analysis

Up- and down-regulated gene lists of a disease are compared with a reference library of expression profiles after drug treatment, gene overexpression or knockdown. A connectivity score from -100 to 100 expresses how closely a perturbation resembles (positive) or opposes (negative) the query; strongly negative scores flag compounds or knockdowns that might reverse the disease signature.

## Connections

- Region signatures of Alzheimer disease gave FGFR inhibitors ([[Orantinib]]) in four cortical regions, 17 compounds shared by four regions, and a bromodomain-inhibitor class whose targets [[BRD2]] and [[BRD4]], when knocked down, reversed the signature.
