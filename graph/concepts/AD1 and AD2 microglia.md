# AD1 and AD2 microglia

Two disease-linked microglial profiles defined by single-nucleus sequencing of human cortex. AD1 microglia are a phagocytic/activated state, comparable to [[Disease-associated microglia]] of amyloid mouse models, whose abundance follows [[Amyloid-beta]] load and which localises to plaques. AD2 microglia stay close to the homeostatic profile but express neuron-related genes such as [[GRID2]], rise with phospho-tau load and are more common where tau pathology is overt. The two profiles share few genes and both arise from homeostatic microglia without evidence of conversion between them.
