# Menopausal hormone therapy

Estrogen-based treatment of menopausal symptoms, which acts through ESR1 and ESR2 (and GPER1) and has been proposed to delay Alzheimer disease when started early; early menopause and late initiation have instead been linked to greater tau vulnerability when amyloid is high.
