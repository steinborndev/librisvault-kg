# Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative

ADNI is a longitudinal cohort that collects MRI and PET imaging, CSF and blood biomarkers, clinical assessments and genetic data from cognitively normal older adults, people with mild cognitive impairment and people with [[Alzheimer disease]]. It is widely used for biomarker-based genetic association work.

## Connections

- 817 ADNI blood DNA samples underwent [[Whole-genome sequencing]] for a rare-variant analysis around [[APOE]] against CSF and imaging endophenotypes. Study: [[Rare variants near APOE and CSF and neuroimaging biomarkers in ADNI]]
- Related imaging: [[Amyloid PET]], [[FDG PET]]
- Its biofluid protocol was followed for CSF collection in the Dominantly Inherited Alzheimer Network. Study: [[PSEN1 transmembrane versus cytoplasmic variants and progression in DIAN]]
- CSF Aβ1-42 and Illumina genotypes were used to test the [[BACE2]] locus in people with Alzheimer disease, mild cognitive impairment and controls, with signals only in APOE ε4 non-carriers; see [[BACE2 rs2837994]]. Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
- 385 ADNI participants (150 Alzheimer, 235 MCI that progressed), with some SORL1 and GAB2 SNPs imputed to the 1000 Genomes panel, served as the replication sample for Taiwanese cognitive-decline trajectories; only the APOE effect on the midpoint age reproduced. See [[Bayesian hierarchical model of cognitive decline trajectories]]. Study: [[Genetic effects on cognitive decline trajectories in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
