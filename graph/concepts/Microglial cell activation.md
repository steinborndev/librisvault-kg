# Microglial cell activation

Microglial cell activation is the change of resting microglia into reactive states. A simplified scheme splits it into a pro-inflammatory M1 state, which releases cytokines such as [[IL6]], [[TNF]] and [[IL1B]] and can injure neurons, and an anti-inflammatory M2 state, with neuroprotective effects; the balance shifts with disease stage, and the scheme is a simplification of a wider range of states (see [[Disease-associated microglia]]).

## Connections

- In [[BV2]] cells and in Alzheimer mouse hippocampus, circ-Epc1 lowered CD11b-positive ([[ITGAM]]) M1 cells and raised CD206-positive ([[MRC1]]) M2 cells, partly through [[MIR770]] and [[TREM2]]. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Related: [[Neuroinflammation]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Amyloid-beta 1-42 gave mainly M1 markers ([[NOS2]], MHC class II, [[IL1B]]) in young cultured mouse microglia and a mix of M1 and M2 markers ([[ARG1]], [[TGFB1]], [[CD86]]) in aged ones, which the authors read as separate subsets rather than a clean M1/M2 switch. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Activated microglia make [[IL19]], which acts on their own receptor ([[IL20RA]] and [[IL20RB]]) to suppress [[IL6]] and [[TNF]] production, a proposed self-limiting loop. Study: [[Interleukin-19 as an autocrine brake on activated microglia]]
- Microglia numbers rose in the hippocampus of Alzheimer model mice fed [[cis-9, trans-11-conjugated linoleic acid]], together with lower [[Amyloid-beta]]. Study: [[Dietary conjugated linoleic acid lowers amyloid-beta in Alzheimer mouse hippocampus]]
- Iba-1 ([[AIF1]]) immunostaining, used as the activation marker, was raised in hippocampus and cortex of Aβ1-42-injected mice and lowered by 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]]. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- Gut-injected oligomeric human Aβ42 ended up mostly inside cortical and hippocampal microglia and activated them; vagotomy reduced hippocampal activation in 5-month-old but not 10-month-old mice. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
