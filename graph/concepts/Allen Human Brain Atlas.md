# Allen Human Brain Atlas

A public map of gene expression across the adult human brain, built from microarray measurements in several thousand tissue samples from six donors. A processed version gives a median expression profile across donors for each region of a standard cortical atlas, with a consistency score per gene that says how representative the profile is of the six donors. Genes can then be tested for spatial correlation with imaging maps, such as regional metabolism.

The atlas holds expression from donors, not from the people imaged, and carries no APOE genotype, so correlations with imaging from other cohorts are between group-level spatial patterns.
