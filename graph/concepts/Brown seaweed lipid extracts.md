# Brown seaweed lipid extracts

Lipid-soluble fractions of brown seaweeds, made by chloroform-methanol extraction under UVC light, that contain LXR-activating (oxy)phytosterols such as [[Saringosterol]] and PPAR-activating fatty acids.

## Connections

- Extracts of [[Sargassum fusiforme]] prevented disease progression in an Alzheimer mouse model without the hepatic steatosis and hypertriglyceridemia of synthetic LXR agonists; six European species were screened as substitutes, [[Himanthalia elongata]] being the best LXR activator. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Targets: [[NR1H3]], [[NR1H2]], [[PPARA]], [[PPARG]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
