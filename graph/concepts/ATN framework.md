# ATN framework

The 2018 National Institute on Aging and Alzheimer's Association research framework that classifies people by biomarkers of [[Amyloid-beta]] deposition (A), tau pathology (T) and neurodegeneration or neuronal injury (N), independent of symptoms. In CSF, A is low Aβ42, T is raised phosphorylated tau and N is raised total tau.

## Connections

- Participants without objective impairment were grouped into stage 0 (A-T-N-), stage 1 (A+T-N-) and stage 2 (A+ with T+ or N+), using cohort-tertile cut-offs; blood [[BIN1]] promoter methylation fell stepwise across the stages. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
- Markers: [[Decreased amyloid beta 42 peptide CSF concentration]], [[Increased CSF phosphorylated tau concentration]], [[Increased CSF total tau concentration]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
