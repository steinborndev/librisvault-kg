# CSF soluble TREM2 target engagement assay

A sandwich electrochemiluminescence immunoassay (Meso Scale Discovery) that measures how much of the soluble TREM2 in a fluid is bound by a therapeutic antibody. Two readings are taken: the bound fraction, detected with a labelled antibody against the therapeutic antibody itself, and the total, measured after adding excess therapeutic antibody to saturate what is left; target engagement is bound divided by total, as a percentage. The same format works for plasma, cerebrospinal fluid and brain lysate, and the CSF version is proposed as clinically translatable. A related format with denaturation of the samples (to release bound antibody) quantifies soluble and membrane-bound TREM2 in cell culture.
