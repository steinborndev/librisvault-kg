# BCCAS mouse model

In the bilateral common carotid artery stenosis (BCCAS) model, microcoils are placed around both common carotid arteries of a C57BL/6J mouse, narrowing them by about 70% and cutting cerebral blood flow by about 60% to 70% on the first day, after which flow partly recovers. The result is a mainly subcortical, watershed pattern of infarcts in striatum and hippocampus, with astrocyte and microglia activation around the lesions, so the model is used to study vascular dementia and [[Small vessel ischemic disease]]. Tissue is usually taken at 2 days (acute) and 7 days (subacute); MRI at 24 hours and 1 week gives lesion volume and flow.

## Connections

- Used to measure transcripts of amyloid-degradation genes ([[A2M]], [[PLAT]], [[CTSD]], [[CTSB]], [[KLK6]]) and risk genes ([[APOE]], [[CD33]], [[MS4A6A]]) in prefrontal cortex, hippocampus and striatum, and to stain amyloid beta oligomers in hippocampus.
