# Hi-C chromatin interaction analysis

Hi-C is a chromosome conformation capture assay that records which genomic regions lie close together in the nucleus. Reads are binned into contact matrices (here 10 kb), and tools such as Fit-Hi-C and FastHiC call significant interaction hotspots with a false discovery rate. It is used to link non-coding risk variants to the genes they may regulate at a distance.

## Connections

- Public adult and fetal brain Hi-C showed hotspots over the NECTIN2, APOE and APOC1 risk haplotype regions, with the APOC1 region contacting the NECTIN2-TOMM40 region; see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].

- Complements [[Expression quantitative trait locus analysis]]; a related chromatin assay is [[ATAC-seq]].
