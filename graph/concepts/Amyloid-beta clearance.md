# Amyloid-beta clearance

The removal of extracellular amyloid-beta (Aβ) from the brain, by uptake and degradation in glia (mainly astrocytes and microglia), receptor-mediated transport and other routes. Together with Aβ production ([[Amyloid-beta formation]]), it sets the amount of Aβ available to aggregate in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- In isogenic hiPSC cultures, astrocytes took up far more Aβ42 than neurons and their removal raised secreted Aβ, but APOE2 astrocytes did not take up more Aβ than APOE3 ones except in the [[APP p.V717I]] line, so the lower Aβ with APOE2 was attributed more to production than clearance. Receptors tested: [[LRP1]] (unchanged) and [[LDLR]] (lower in APOE2 astrocytes in three of four lines). Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]
- Ligand carrier: [[APOE]]
- Young cultured mouse microglia took up more Aβ (monomers and dimers) and aged ones left most deposits outside the cell, with high [[CD33]] and low [[TREM2]] and [[MFGE8]]; [[MMP2]] and [[MMP9]] release rose with Aβ and is a possible extracellular route. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- In [[5xFAD mouse]] animals, deleting Cd33 lowered amyloid-beta pathology only when Trem2 was present, while Trem2 deletion worsened it whatever the Cd33 status. Study: [[TREM2 acts downstream of CD33 in 5xFAD mouse microglia]]
- A review lists three routes through which astrocytic [[AQP4]] aids Aβ removal from the brain: lymphatic clearance, transcytotic delivery and glial degradation. Study: [[Aquaporin-4 and amyloid-beta clearance in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Degrading enzymes [[IDE]] and [[MME]] rose with [[Nicotinamide riboside]] in mouse cortex, while Sirt3 loss and Western diet lowered IDE. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- Raised [[IDE]] protein with fewer Aβ40 and Aβ42 plaques in 5xFAD hippocampus after [[D-Pinitol]], the proposed degradation route. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
