# 14-3-3 proteins

A family of seven conserved mammalian adaptor and chaperone proteins that bind phosphorylated motifs on many targets. In neurons they help traffic membrane proteins, including the NMDA receptor subunit [[GRIN2C]], from the endoplasmic reticulum to the cell surface after phosphorylation by PKB (AKT). They are also a cerebrospinal fluid marker of neuronal damage in prion disease ([[Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease]]), Alzheimer disease, stroke and brain cancers.
