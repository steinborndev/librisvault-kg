# Drug repurposing

Finding new indications for compounds already developed for another disease. Its appeal is that toxicity data often exist from earlier trials, which shortens development time and cost. Genomic data supply the disease side: genes found by association or expression studies define pathways, and compounds are chosen for their effect on those pathways.
