# Protein quantitative trait locus analysis

A protein quantitative trait locus (pQTL) analysis tests whether the genotype at a variant tracks the abundance of a protein, in the encoded gene's own region (cis) or elsewhere in the genome (trans). It measures the protein level directly, which can differ from what a transcript-level [[Expression quantitative trait locus analysis]] shows, and a trans effect points to cross-talk between loci.
