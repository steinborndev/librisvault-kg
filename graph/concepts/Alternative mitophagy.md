# Alternative mitophagy

Alternative mitophagy is a form of [[Mitophagy]] that needs [[ULK1]] activation and [[RAB9A]]-associated autophagosomes derived from the Golgi, but not [[MAP1LC3B]] lipidation, [[ATG7]] or [[PINK1]]. It is blocked by [[Brefeldin A]], which stops Golgi vesicle transport, whereas canonical mitophagy is not. It was first linked to cardiac protection under stress.

## Connections

- [[ALT001]] activates it in human cell lines and mouse hippocampus; ULK1 or Rab9 knockdown, brefeldin A and, in vivo, hippocampal Rab9 shRNA remove the effect and the downstream rescue of long-term potentiation in 5×FAD mice. The authors propose it as a therapeutic target for [[Alzheimer disease]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
