# Amyloid PET

Amyloid PET is positron emission tomography with a radiotracer that binds deposited [[Amyloid-beta]], giving a map of plaque burden across the brain in a living person. The tracers in use are carbon-11 [[Pittsburgh compound B]] and the fluorine-18 compounds [[Florbetaben]], [[Florbetapir]] and [[Flutemetamol]]. The scan is read as amyloid-positive or amyloid-negative, or quantified as a standardized uptake value ratio against a reference region, as for [[FDG PET]].

Its role is to establish the amyloid component of [[Alzheimer disease]] without autopsy, to predict progression from [[Mild cognitive impairment]], and to select and monitor participants in trials of amyloid-directed therapy.
