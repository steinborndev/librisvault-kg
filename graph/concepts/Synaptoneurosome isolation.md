# Synaptoneurosome isolation

A biochemical fractionation of brain tissue into a synaptically enriched pellet. Tissue is homogenised, filtered through nylon and then a 5 μm membrane, and spun at low speed; the pellet holds resealed pre- and post-synaptic fragments. Enrichment is shown by western blot for synaptic proteins ([[SYP]], [[DLG4]]) and absence of a nuclear marker (histone H3), and protein quality in post-mortem tissue by the ratio of full-length to degraded NMDAR2B.

## Connections

- Used on fresh-frozen temporal cortex to show that clusterin rises in synapses of Alzheimer cases, most in APOE4 carriers, although the crude homogenate showed no genotype effect. Study: [[Clusterin and amyloid-beta accumulate in synapses of APOE4 carriers in Alzheimer disease brain]]
