# Reelin signaling pathway

[[RELN]] binds the lipoprotein receptors [[LRP8]] and VLDLR, which engages the adaptor [[DAB1]] and downstream kinases including [[FYN]] and [[CDK5]] with its activator [[CDK5R1]]. The pathway guides neuronal migration in development and tunes synaptic plasticity and memory in the adult brain.
