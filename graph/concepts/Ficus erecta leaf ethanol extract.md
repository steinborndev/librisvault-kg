# Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract

EEFE is a freeze-dried 80 °C aqueous-ethanol extract of the leaves of Ficus erecta Thunb., a fig-family shrub of Korean, Japanese and Chinese coasts used as food and as a folk remedy for inflammatory conditions. The leaf extract (yield 18.71%) inhibited Aβ1-42 aggregation in vitro with an IC50 of 43.43 μg/ml, while the branch extract did not (above 100 μg/ml).

Its HPLC-quantified markers are [[Rutin]] (5.49 mg/g), [[Chlorogenic acid]] (1.80 mg/g) and [[Kaempferol-3-O-rutinoside]] (1.38 mg/g); most of the extract remains uncharacterised.

## Connections

- Given orally at 150 mg/kg/day for 20 days, it lowered Aβ load, restored memory, neuron counts and phospho-[[CREB1]] and [[BDNF]], and reduced Iba-1, [[TNF]] and [[IL1B]] in the [[Intracerebroventricular amyloid-beta injection mouse model]].
