# iPSC-derived microglia knockout models

Human induced pluripotent stem cells are edited with CRISPR/Cas9 and differentiated through hematopoietic progenitors into microglia-like cells (hiMGL) with M-CSF, TGF-beta and IL-34. A monoculture gives lysates and conditioned medium free of other brain cell types, so secreted proteins can be assigned to microglia.

## Connections

- A knockout of [[GRN]] gave a hyperactivated state and a knockout of [[TREM2]] a state locked as homeostatic; GRN knockout cells raised cathepsins, [[APOE]] and LGALS3, lowered [[P2RY12]], and shared 33% of upregulated lysate proteins with Grn knockout mouse microglia. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related models: [[iPSC neuron-microglia co-culture and neuroimmune organoid models]], [[Isogenic hiPSC neural culture model]]; measurement: [[Label-free quantitative mass spectrometry proteomics]]
- A knock-in counterpart: human iPSC lines made isogenic for the fusion gene [[CHRFAM7A]] with TALENs were differentiated through yolk-sac embryoid bodies with CSF1 and IL-34 into microglia-like cells to study Aβ uptake and NF-κB responses. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
