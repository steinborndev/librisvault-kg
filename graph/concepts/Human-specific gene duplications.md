# Human-specific gene duplications

Genes created by duplication or fusion since the human-chimpanzee split, often as truncated copies of an ancestral gene, that give humans functions absent from model animals. Examples named in the study below are SRGAP2C (more dendritic spines), ARHGAP11B (more cortical neurons) and [[CHRFAM7A]]; a common pattern is that the truncated copy acts as a negative regulator of the ancestral pathway.
