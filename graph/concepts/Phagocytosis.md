# Phagocytosis

Phagocytosis is the engulfment of particles by a cell. Microglia use it to clear apoptotic neurons, myelin debris, oxidized lipids, degenerating synapses and protein aggregates, and several Alzheimer risk genes (APOE, CLU, ABCA7, GRN, TREM2, CD33) are linked to it.

## Connections

- Measured with pHrodo-labelled synaptosomes, which fluoresce red once taken into acidic compartments. It fell in APOE3 microglia exposed to amyloid beta 42 or PSEN1 mutant neurons, recovered with [[Liproxstatin-1]] or an [[APOE mimetic peptide]], and was preserved in [[APOE p.R136S]] microglia. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Genes raised in the protected microglia: [[ANXA3]], [[CD93]], [[SLC11A1]]
- Related: [[Ferroptosis]], [[TREM2]], [[MAPT]], [[Endocytosis]]
- Human APOE2 microglia took up more pHrodo E. coli particles than APOE3, APOE4 or knockout microglia, and a larger share took up myelin than APOE4 microglia; knockout cells held very large amounts of intracellular myelin, suggesting failed digestion. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
- [[BV2]] microglia took up the [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]] carrier independently of the nicotinic-receptor ligand (free [[RVG29 peptide]] did not block it; [[Chlorpromazine]] and [[Genistein]] did), and after BACE1 silencing took up more labelled amyloid beta, though not significantly more than with the non-targeted carrier. See [[Disease-associated microglia]]. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Amyloid-beta 1-42 halved latex bead uptake in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture, down to the low level of those kept 16 days, with lower [[MFGE8]], higher [[TREM2]] only in the young cells and higher [[CD33]] mostly in the aged cells. See [[Primary microglia in vitro aging model]]. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Phagocytosis-related genes were higher in microglia of 5xFAD;CD33-/- mice and lower in 5xFAD;TREM2-/- mice, and the CD33 effect needed [[TREM2]]. Study: [[TREM2 acts downstream of CD33 in 5xFAD mouse microglia]]
- Overnight pretreatment with [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]] raised the share of primary mouse microglia that had taken up fluorescent myelin (after 2 h) and Aβ(1-42), but not E. coli particles, and this needed TREM2 (no binding to Trem2 knockout cells). Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- Uptake of pHrodo E. coli and zymosan particles was lower in mouse macrophages, primary microglia and iPSC-derived microglia carrying [[PLCG2 p.P522R]], and extra [[Phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate]] raised it, more so in the variant cells; at low particle doses the deficit disappeared. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
- Fluorescent bead uptake confirmed function of iPSC microglia-like cells, and Aβ1-42 uptake was lower with [[CHRFAM7A]]; the antagonist [[Methyllycaconitine]] raised uptake in non-carriers. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
