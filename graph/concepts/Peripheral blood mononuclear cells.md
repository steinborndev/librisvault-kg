# Peripheral blood mononuclear cells

Peripheral blood mononuclear cells are the round-nucleus cells of blood, mainly lymphocytes and [[Monocyte]]s, separated from whole blood on a density gradient such as Ficoll-Paque. They are an accessible stand-in for immune-cell state, used for gene expression readouts of cholinergic and inflammatory markers in neurodegenerative disease.

## Connections

- Used to measure nicotinic receptor subunit, acetylcholinesterase and cytokine mRNA in dementia with Lewy bodies, Alzheimer disease and controls. Study: [[PBMC nicotinic receptor and cytokine expression in DLB and Alzheimer disease]]
- Genes read out: [[CHRNA3]], [[CHRNA4]], [[CHRNA7]], [[CHRNB2]], [[CHRNB4]], [[ACHE]], [[IL1B]], [[IL6]], [[TNF]]
- Related process: [[Neuroinflammation]]
