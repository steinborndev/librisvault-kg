# NF-kappaB signaling

NF-κB signaling is the pathway through which Toll-like receptors, interleukin-1 and TNF receptors activate NF-κB transcription factors (such as [[RELA]]) via adaptors including [[IRAK1]] and [[TRAF6]], switching on inflammatory cytokine genes and also genes of negative-feedback regulators.

## Connections

- In rat primary astrocytes Aβ1-42 raised [[MIR146A]] through this pathway (blocked by the inhibitor BMS-345541), yet no cytokine was induced; the authors propose a negative feedback loop in which miR-146a-5p lowers IRAK1 and holds TRAF6 steady. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Related: [[Neuroinflammation]], [[NFKBIA]]
- TNF-alpha signalling via NF-κB was the top or second-ranked gene set in caudate nucleus RNA-seq of both [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]], with [[NFKB1]] and [[NFE2L2]] higher in both. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Reduced phosphorylated p65 accompanied the anti-inflammatory effect of [[Magnolol]] and [[Donepezil]] in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain; the authors cite magnolol blocking NF-κB in macrophage and lupus nephritis models. Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- The alpha7 nicotinic receptor normally restrains this pathway in microglia; the human fusion gene [[CHRFAM7A]] releases the restraint and lengthens nuclear p65 residence. See [[Cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway]]. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
- TNF-α with Smac mimetic and Z-VAD-FMK lowered nuclear [[RELA]] in [[SH-SY5Y]] cells and its binding to the Uvrag promoter, which cut [[UVRAG]] transcription and tied this pathway to [[Autophagy]] and [[Necroptosis]]. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
