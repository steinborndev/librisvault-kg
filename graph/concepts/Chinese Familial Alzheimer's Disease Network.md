# Chinese Familial Alzheimer's Disease Network

The Chinese Familial Alzheimer's Disease Network (CFAN) is a registry of Chinese families with Alzheimer disease or mild cognitive impairment, set up to study the genetics of [[Familial Alzheimer disease]] in Chinese people. It had enrolled 1330 patients in 404 pedigrees.

## Connections

- In its pedigrees 13.12% carried PSEN or APP missense variants, 3.71% synonymous or untranslated-region variants and 83.17% neither; the family that yielded [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]] was recruited from it. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Genes: [[APP]], [[PSEN1]], [[PSEN2]]
