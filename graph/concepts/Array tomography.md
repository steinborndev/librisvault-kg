# Array tomography

A high-resolution fluorescence imaging method in which fixed tissue is embedded in resin, cut into ribbons of serial ultrathin sections (here 70 nm) and stained. Antibodies can be eluted from the ribbon and a second set applied, so several markers are imaged in the same synapse, and aligned stacks give three-dimensional counts of synaptic puncta and their co-localisation with other signals.

## Connections

- Used on superior temporal cortex of Alzheimer cases and controls to count synaptophysin puncta near and far from plaques and the share of synapses with oligomeric [[Amyloid-beta]], [[CLU]] or both; tissue is scarce, so group sizes were small. Study: [[Clusterin and amyloid-beta accumulate in synapses of APOE4 carriers in Alzheimer disease brain]]
- Measures [[Synapse loss]] at plaque-defined distances; companion method: [[Synaptoneurosome isolation]]
