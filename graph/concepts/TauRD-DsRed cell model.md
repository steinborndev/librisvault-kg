# TauRD-DsRed cell model

Human cell lines carrying a doxycycline-inducible (Tet-On) Tau repeat domain fused in frame to DsRed: the pro-aggregant [[MAPT p.K280del]] version misfolds, which also misfolds DsRed and lowers its fluorescence, so rescue of fluorescence reports less Tau misfolding; thioflavin S staining gives an independent aggregation read-out.

## Connections

- Built in [[HEK293]] (aggregation, ROS, caspase 3) and [[SH-SY5Y]] (differentiated with retinoic acid for neurite outgrowth and signalling) cells; used to screen [[Licochalcone A]] and five derivatives, with [[Congo red]] for comparison. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Read-outs: [[Tau aggregation]], [[Oxidative stress]], [[Apoptosis]]
