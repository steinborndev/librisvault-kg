# CIS-dPCR chromatin interaction strength assay

CIS-dPCR is a digital PCR readout for a single chromatin contact. After a [[Chromosome conformation capture assay]] library is made, TaqMan primers and a probe spanning the anchor-target junction count the chimeric fragments in thousands of partitions, giving an absolute number rather than a band on a gel. The count is normalised to input DNA with the beta-globin gene HBB as internal reference and to losses during processing with a fixed amount of spiked-in GFP DNA, so contact strength can be compared across cell types, genotypes and disease stages.

## Connections

- Developed and applied to the contact between the [[APOE]] exon 4 CpG island and the [[APOC1]] promoter; contact strength was higher in Alzheimer frontal lobe than in controls and highest in ε3/ε4 carriers. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
- Complements genome-wide [[Hi-C chromatin interaction analysis]], which maps contacts but is not quantitative for one pair of sites.
