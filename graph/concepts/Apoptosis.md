# Apoptosis

Apoptosis is regulated cell death executed by caspases; mitochondrial release of [[CYCS]] under control of the [[BCL2]] family leads to activation of [[CASP3]].

## Connections

- Ajugol was the compound linked to shifts in this pathway in an Alzheimer mouse model and in [[BV2]] cells. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- APP overexpression raised the apoptotic fraction of [[BE2-M17]] cells by annexin V/propidium iodide flow cytometry, and [[Osthol]] conjugated to [[Zinc oxide]] nanoparticles lowered it. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
- TUNEL-positive hippocampal neurons of [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] animals fell after ADSC exosomes, more after hypoxia-pretreated ones and most after circ-Epc1-containing ones. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Knocking down [[DICER1]] in cultured neurons induced apoptosis together with [[Oxidative stress]]. Study: [[Dicer1 loss in APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse brain via amyloid-beta 42 repression of Nrf2]]
- Regulation of apoptosis was significantly disturbed by all five traditional Chinese medicine decoctions in brain subnetworks of [[SAMP8 mouse]] animals. Study: [[Common nodes of five Chinese medicine decoctions in SAMP8 mouse brain subnetworks]]
- [[LM-031]] lowered caspase 3 activity and [[BAX]] and raised [[BCL2]] in ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed cells, through [[NFE2L2]] and [[CREB1]]. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- TUNEL-positive NeuN-labelled neurons were more numerous in CA1 of 9-month-old [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]] animals. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- In neuronal cultures TNF-α with Smac mimetic activated [[CASP8]] and [[CASP3]] (nuclear fragmentation), whereas adding [[Z-VAD-FMK]] switched the death to [[Necroptosis]]. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
