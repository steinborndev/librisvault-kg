# Protein phosphorylation

Protein phosphorylation is the addition of a phosphate group to a protein residue by a kinase, reversed by phosphatases; it switches enzyme activity, interactions and stability.

## Connections

- Short exposure of neurons to amyloid oligomers lowered phosphorylation of [[DICER1]] and [[TARBP2]]; the kinases [[MAPK8]] and [[MAPK1]] and the phosphatase [[PPP3CA]] (calcineurin) were implicated through inhibitor experiments.
