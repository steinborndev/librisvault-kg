# miRNA binding

miRNA binding is the base-pairing of a microRNA to a target RNA. Messenger RNAs are repressed when a microRNA binds their 3' untranslated region, whereas an RNA with binding sites of its own, such as a circular RNA, can act as a sponge that sequesters the microRNA and so releases its mRNA targets. Binding is tested by dual-luciferase reporter assays with wild-type and mutated sites.
