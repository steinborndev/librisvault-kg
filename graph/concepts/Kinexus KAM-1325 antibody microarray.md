# Kinexus KAM-1325 antibody microarray

A slide carrying about 1350 antibodies, each printed in quadruplicate, against total and phosphorylated signalling proteins. Lysate proteins are chemically cleaved, biotinylated and captured; signal is reported as percent change from control (% CFC), where 100% means a 2-fold increase. Enrichment of changed proteins is then tested with DAVID against the proteins on the array.

## Connections

- Used on hippocampal lysates of control and [[PTK2]]-overexpressing [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals after fear conditioning and water maze testing, and as the readout of the [[In-lysate kinase assay]]. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
