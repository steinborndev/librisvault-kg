# Open field test

The open field test places a rodent in a square arena for a few minutes and records total distance, speed and the time spent in the centre versus the periphery. Less time and distance in the centre is read as anxiety-like behaviour, and total distance as general locomotion.

## Connections

- 5-minute test in a 50 x 50 x 40 cm arena divided into 16 squares: [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals given [[D-ribose]] spent more time at the edge and less in the centre, and neither [[Berberine]] nor [[Mdivi-1]] changed this ([[Anxiety]]).
