# Targeted gene panel sequencing

Targeted gene panel sequencing captures and reads a fixed list of genes at high depth, far cheaper per gene than whole-exome sequencing and suited to small clinical cohorts. Its blind spots are genes outside the panel, copy number changes and repeat expansions unless added by design.

## Connections

- Used on 50 neurodegenerative-disease genes with the Ion Torrent PGM system in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients, with Sanger confirmation; copy number variants and the C9orf72 repeat expansion were not screened. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- Broader alternative: [[Whole-exome sequencing]]
- Targeted exome sequencing of eleven dementia genes (exons plus 25 flanking nucleotides; Agilent SureSelect with Illumina MiSeq, or AmpliSeq with IonTorrent) in 102 Swedish patients; the authors note it cannot detect structural variants, repeats such as the C9ORF72 expansion, or genes outside the panel. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
