# Disease-associated microglia

Disease-associated microglia (DAM) are microglial states that appear around amyloid pathology and differ from homeostatic microglia. In a two-stage scheme stage 1 (DAM-1) is a phagocytosis-capable state, whereas stage 2 (DAM-2) is phagocytosis-dysfunctional and pro-inflammatory and is linked to [[Alzheimer disease]] pathology.

## Connections

- A cited study found that deleting [[BACE1]] in microglia keeps the DAM-1 state and improves Aβ clearance; this is the rationale for silencing BACE1 in microglia as well as neurons with [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]. In treated [[FAD4T mouse]] animals Iba1-positive activated microglia, reactive astrocytes and [[TNF]], [[IL1B]], [[IL6]] mRNA fell. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Related: [[Phagocytosis]], [[Neuroinflammation]], [[TREM2]]
- GRN knockout hyperactivates microglia, with raised Apoe, Clec7a, Lgals3, cathepsins and Lyz2 and lowered homeostatic markers ([[P2RY12]]), the reverse of TREM2 knockout; [[GPNMB]] is cited as part of the disease-associated microglia signature in amyloid and tau mouse models. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- The TREM2 antibody [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]] raised TREM2 and lowered the homeostatic marker [[P2RY12]] in microglia clustered around plaques of [[AppNL-G-F mouse]] animals, which the authors read as promoting the homeostatic-to-DAM transition. They also note that loss of TREM2 locks microglia in a homeostatic state whereas loss of [[GRN]] arrests them in a disease-associated state, and both are harmful. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- In single-nucleus RNA sequencing of human cortex, the amyloid-linked AD1 microglia state carried nearly all the top DAM genes from the mouse literature (and the ARM gene set), with [[TREM2]] expressed before [[APOE]] along the trajectory, while a second, tau-linked state (AD2) had no mouse counterpart. See [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
- The mouse DAM signature (Apoe, Trem2, Lpl, Cst7 and others) differs from the human Alzheimer microglia response, whose top pathways include chemotaxis, IL-6 and NF-κB; the authors tie this to [[CHRFAM7A]], present in over 99% of people and absent in mice. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
- [[P2RX4]] protein was found almost only in plaque-associated microglia of 12-month APP/PS1 mice and Alzheimer cortex, where it co-localised with [[APOE]] and CD68; the authors infer it belongs to the DAM population, noting that the gene is rarely flagged in transcriptomic studies (a fourfold mRNA rise appears in laser-captured plaque-associated microglia). Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
