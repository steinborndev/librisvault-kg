# Streptozocin-induced hyperglycemia model

Rodents given [[Streptozocin]] by intraperitoneal injection develop [[Hyperglycemia]], used here to accelerate Alzheimer-like pathology in transgenic mice because impaired brain glucose metabolism tracks with symptoms.

## Connections

- In 6-month-old male homozygous [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals, four doses of 100 mg/kg (days 2, 3, 9, 10) raised glucose from 113 mg/dl to 220-314 mg/dl by days 15-29 and worsened Y-maze, water-maze, NeuN, Aβ and Tau measures; the test drug [[LM-031]] was given from day 15. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
