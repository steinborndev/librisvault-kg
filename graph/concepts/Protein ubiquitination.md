# Protein ubiquitination

Protein ubiquitination is the covalent attachment of ubiquitin to a target protein by an E1-E2-E3 enzyme cascade; depending on the chain type it can mark the protein for degradation by the proteasome (see [[Ubiquitin-proteasome system]]) or change its activity or location.
