# HK1

HK1 encodes hexokinase 1, the first enzyme of glycolysis, which phosphorylates glucose.

## Connections

- Inhibition by [[DNM1L]] (Drp1) lowers oligodendrocyte glycolysis and activates [[NLRP3]] in Alzheimer mouse models. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
