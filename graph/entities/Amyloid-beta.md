# Amyloid-beta

Amyloid-beta (Aβ) is the peptide of 38 to 43 residues cut from [[APP]] that forms the plaques of [[Alzheimer disease]]; its soluble oligomers are considered the toxic species at synapses.

## Connections

- Oligomeric Aβ (antibody 1C22) was found in a larger share of synapses near plaques than far from them, and in more synapses of APOE4 than APOE3 carriers; synapses holding both Aβ and [[CLU]] were most frequent in Alzheimer APOE4 cases near plaques. Study: [[Clusterin and amyloid-beta accumulate in synapses of APOE4 carriers in Alzheimer disease brain]]
- Reviewed as a driver of early synaptic dysfunction through endosomes and the vesicle cycle. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Pathway pages: [[Amyloid-beta formation]], [[Amyloid-beta clearance]], [[Amyloid cascade hypothesis]]
- A pre-aggregated mix of Aβ1-42 oligomers and fibrils (1000 nM, 24 h) was not toxic to mouse primary microglia but, in cells kept 2 days in culture, enlarged them, drove migration toward it, and cut bead phagocytosis, while microglia kept 16 days barely responded and left most Aβ outside the cell. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- In [[AppNL-G-F mouse]] animals treated with the TREM2 antibody [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]], soluble Aβ (diethylamine fraction) and soluble Aβ42 fell, while insoluble (formic acid) Aβ did not change significantly. Primary microglia also took up more Aβ(1-42) with the antibody. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- Its production and fibrillation can be inhibited by BRI2 ([[ITM2B]]), according to a review that places BRI2 in the amyloid cascade. Study: [[BRI2 in familial British and Danish dementia and Alzheimer disease review]]
- Its brain clearance is reviewed as depending partly on [[AQP4]] through lymphatic clearance, transcytotic delivery and glial degradation. Study: [[Aquaporin-4 and amyloid-beta clearance in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Deposited Aβ is the target of PET tracers ([[Pittsburgh compound B]], [[Florbetaben]], [[Florbetapir]], [[Flutemetamol]]); a review calls Aβ burden on imaging an excellent predictive biomarker. See [[Amyloid PET]]. Study: [[Amyloid PET imaging in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Amyloid-beta load in tissue correlated strongly with the abundance of AD1 microglia (phagocytic/activated, localised to plaques) in cortex with amyloid-beta pathology only, but not in tissue that also had tau; 'cellular response to amyloid-beta' was enriched in the later AD1 subclusters. See [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
- Reviewed as the main driver of Alzheimer pathogenesis, with soluble oligomers (AβOs) the most neurotoxic species and Aβ42 more aggregation-prone than Aβ40; circulating AβO levels correlated with lower brain volume, and oligomers bind postsynaptic [[TMEM97]], PrPc and PSD-95. They reduce astrocytic phagocytosis through [[MEGF10]] and [[MERTK]], and Aβ42 injected into P301L tau mice raised tangles fivefold. See [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]] and [[Dystrophic neurite]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- The Aβ42 level and Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio in medium of cells expressing Swedish APP rose with three PSEN2 variants (up to 5.3-fold and 5-fold for [[PSEN2 p.I368F]]) while Aβ40 stayed unchanged. Study: [[PSEN2 mutation spectrum and functionally validated variants in a Chinese Alzheimer cohort]]; method: [[Functional validation of presenilin variants in transfected cells]]
- Chronic Aβ42 exposure lowered [[NFE2L2]] and then [[DICER1]] in cultured neurons, reversed by [[Acetylcysteine]]; Dicer1 overexpression reversed Aβ42-induced neurite deficits. Study: [[Dicer1 loss in APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse brain via amyloid-beta 42 repression of Nrf2]]
- Short exposure of neurons to Aβ42 oligomers raised [[DICER1]] protein, not its mRNA, by lowering Dicer1 and [[TARBP2]] phosphorylation, in contrast to the transcriptional fall seen after longer exposure. Study: [[Amyloid oligomers rapidly raise neuronal Dicer1 by reducing phosphorylation]]
- Hippocampal Aβ levels fell in Alzheimer model mice fed [[cis-9, trans-11-conjugated linoleic acid]], with more microglia and more [[IL10]]- and [[IL19]]-expressing astrocytes; abstract-only. Study: [[Dietary conjugated linoleic acid lowers amyloid-beta in Alzheimer mouse hippocampus]]
- Neurons lacking [[NNT]] were more sensitive to Aβ toxicity, and NNT was proposed to aid clearance of toxic Aβ accumulation. Study: [[NNT deficiency impairs synapses and cognition in Alzheimer mouse models]]
- Amyloid-beta was among the detergent-insoluble proteins found in demented but not control human amygdala. Study: [[Detergent-insoluble proteins in amygdala of cognitively impaired elderly]]
- Soluble 1-42 oligomers triggered calcium release (at 40 µM, not at 0.5 µM) and PIP2 loss that were stronger in macrophages and microglia with the protective [[PLCG2 p.P522R]] variant, and these cells took up the oligomers faster by endocytosis, which the authors suggest could reduce toxic effects of soluble amyloid-beta. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
- Hippocampal [[PTK2]] overexpression raised plaque number in 3xTg-AD mice with worse memory, while knockdown changed neither. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Reviewed as toxic to mature oligodendrocytes and inhibiting myelin sheet formation, in a loop with cholesterol dysmetabolism and white matter injury. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Aβ immunoreactivity (area 136-185% of control) rose in streptozocin-treated [[3xTg-AD mouse]] hippocampus and cortex and [[LM-031]] lowered it. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Soluble Aβ40 rose 53.0%, Aβ42 129.7% and the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio 56.7% in hippocampus of 6-month-old [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]] animals, with intraneuronal staining but no plaques. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Pre-aggregated Aβ1-42 given intracerebroventricularly drove memory loss, neuron loss and cytokine rise in mice; Aβ-positive cells and Aβ on western blot were lower after 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]], and the extract inhibited Aβ1-42 aggregation in vitro (IC50 43.43 μg/ml). See [[Intracerebroventricular amyloid-beta injection mouse model]]. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- In wild-type mouse cortical neurons sorted for low gamma-secretase activity, Aβ38 fell and Aβ42 rose, raising the Aβ42/38 ratio (three experiments, ELISA of lysates). Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Amyloid burden by PET and CSF Aβ42/40 did not distinguish transmembrane from cytoplasmic PSEN1 carriers, leaving less-measured forms (Aβ43, Aβ37/38, membrane-retained Aβ45-49, oligomers) as candidate explanations. Study: [[PSEN1 transmembrane versus cytoplasmic variants and progression in DIAN]]
- Aβ1-42 at 10 μM for 24 h injured [[PC12]] cells as a cheap Alzheimer cell model, in which [[6-Gingerol]] modestly improved viability. Study: [[6-Gingerol from ginger screened for Alzheimer disease by docking and dynamics]]
- Secreted tandem Aβ42 in fly neurons was neurotoxic (about 50% smaller eye, 50% shorter life, the largest climbing loss of 60 to 70%) and was partly rescued by the DYRK1A inhibitor [[PST-001]]. See [[Drosophila models of Alzheimer disease and Down syndrome]]. Study: [[PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome]]
- Aβ and the number of beta-amyloid-positive plaques in the dentate gyrus fell in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] hippocampus overexpressing [[MIR650]] (n = 3 per group). Study: [[MicroRNA-650 targets CDK5 and lessens amyloid pathology in APP-PSEN1 mice]]
- Aβ was found throughout mouse gut and in human gut, with a higher Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio in gut than brain (P < 0.0001, different donors); colonic Aβ42 rose with age and after microbiota transplantation, and gut-injected labelled Aβ42 reached brain within 1.5 to 2 hours, mainly by blood in 10-month-old mice and partly by the vagus nerve in 5-month-old mice ([[Gut-brain axis]]). Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Brain Aβ40 and Aβ42 (guanidine extract ELISA) were far above wild type in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] animals and fell with [[Magnolol]] (Aβ42 less so at 20 mg/kg) and [[Donepezil]] (Aβ42 only). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Secreted Aβ1-40 and Aβ1-42 fell when [[BACE2]] was overexpressed in [[BE2-M17]] cells, and CSF Aβ1-42 tracked BACE2 SNPs in ADNI participants who do not carry [[APOE]] ε4. Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
- Hippocampal and cortical amyloid-beta rose in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals given [[D-ribose]] and fell with [[Berberine]]. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Aβ1-42 uptake by iPSC microglia-like cells was partly alpha7-dependent and reduced by [[CHRFAM7A]], which nonetheless raised NF-κB activation and IL1B, IL6 and TNF transcription in response to it. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
- Oligomeric Aβ42 (5 µM, 24 h) raised sphingomyelin, phosphatidylinositol and triglyceride totals in [[U251]] astroglioma cells, partly echoing plasma lipid changes in Alzheimer patients. Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
- Soluble Aβ1-42 in cortex was lower in 12-month APP/PS1 mice lacking [[P2RX4]] (0.94 vs 1.22 ng/mg) while insoluble Aβ and plaque number and size were unchanged, alongside higher microglial [[APOE]] and rescued memory; the authors link the soluble species to the synaptic and cognitive deficits. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
