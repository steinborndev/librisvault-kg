# RIN3

RIN3 encodes a guanine nucleotide exchange factor for Rab5 that recruits [[BIN1]] and [[CD2AP]] to Rab5-positive early endosomes, promoting [[BACE1]] processing of [[APP]] there. It is an Alzheimer disease risk locus.

## Connections

- In [[Tg2576 mouse]] mice RIN3 was slightly raised in hippocampus and lowered in retina; the tau antibody [[12A12 monoclonal antibody]] normalized it in the retina only. Study: [[Tau antibody 12A12 and amyloid-beta in Tg2576 mouse hippocampus and retina]]
- Acts through [[RAB5A]]; see [[Endocytosis]].
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
