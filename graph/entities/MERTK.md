# MERTK

MERTK encodes MER tyrosine kinase, a phagocytic receptor of glial cells.

## Connections

- Down-regulated together with [[MEGF10]] on reactive astrocytes by amyloid-beta in APP/PS1 mouse hippocampus, reducing clearance of amyloid-beta oligomers and [[Dystrophic neurite]]. See [[Astrocyte activation]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
