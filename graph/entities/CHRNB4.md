# CHRNB4

CHRNB4 encodes the beta4 subunit of the neuronal nicotinic acetylcholine receptor, which assembles with alpha subunits such as [[CHRNA3]].

## Connections

- mRNA in blood mononuclear cells was lower than in controls in both diseases and lower in Alzheimer disease than in Lewy body dementia (median fold change about 0.0 against 0.4); it correlated positively with CHRNA4 in Alzheimer disease.
