# NRF1

NRF1 encodes a transcription factor that activates nuclear genes for respiratory chain subunits and for [[TFAM]], linking [[PPARGC1A]] to [[Mitochondrial biogenesis]].

## Connections

- Induced alongside PPARGC1A and TFAM by [[ALT001]] in human cells and mouse hippocampus, and restored in 5×FAD hippocampus. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Related: [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]]
