# PSEN1 p.E184G

A missense change from glutamate to glycine at residue 184 of [[PSEN1]], previously reported in two French families with early-onset Alzheimer disease. A different change at the same residue, p.E184D, is also linked to early onset.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PSEN1]]; disease: [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]], including its frontal variant
- Found in a 41-year-old Thai woman (patient #7) with dementia in several relatives over four generations; absent from KRGDB, ExAC and 1000 Genomes. She was also homozygous for [[PRNP p.M129V]]. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
