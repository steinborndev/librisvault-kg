# MMP9

MMP9 encodes gelatinase B, a secreted zinc protease that breaks down extracellular matrix and can also cut amyloid-beta; in Alzheimer disease it is regarded as a mainly harmful gelatinase that can loosen the blood-brain barrier.

## Connections

- Gelatin zymography showed MMP9 activity in the medium of mouse microglia rose 1.7-fold after amyloid-beta 1-42 at 2 days in culture and 1.5-fold at 16 days; the authors cite raised MMP9 activity in serum and brain of patients with mild cognitive impairment and Alzheimer disease.
