# bEnd.3

bEnd.3 is a mouse brain endothelial cell line that forms a monolayer in culture and is used to build in vitro models of the blood-brain barrier.

## Connections

- Grown on Transwell inserts to a resistance of about 200 Ω·cm² as the upper layer of a barrier model, with [[Neuro2a]] or [[BV2]] cells below; [[RVG29 peptide]]-decorated nanoparticles crossed it better than undecorated ones. Uptake there was cut by [[Amiloride]] and [[Chlorpromazine]]. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
