# Licochalcone A

Licochalcone A is a chalcone obtained from the root of Glycyrrhiza inflata (licorice) that boosts the [[NFE2L2]] antioxidant defence in several cell types.

## Connections

- At 1 μM it raised [[HSPB1]] and soluble TauRD, lowered Tau misfolding, ROS and caspase 3 activity, and restored neurite length in ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed cells, but was more cytotoxic than its derivative [[LM-031]] (IC50 7.0 μM in HEK293 against 55.9 μM). Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Parent compound of: [[LM-031]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
