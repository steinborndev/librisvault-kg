# CD33 rs3865444

rs3865444 is a promoter variant of [[CD33]] and a late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] GWAS SNP, in almost complete linkage disequilibrium with the exon 2 variant [[CD33 p.A14V]] (rs12459419).

## Connections

- Gene: [[CD33]]
- Allele-specific effects were confined to exon 2 in monocytes (p = 4.52E-12 and 2.05E-6 at two exon 2 SNPs) and in the temporal cortex, posterior cingulate and caudate; only two of seven CD33 transcripts overlap exon 2 and showed the effect, confirming rs3865444 as a splicing QTL of exon 2. The near-complete linkage disequilibrium prevents separating the promoter variant from the exonic one. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- No association with the midpoint age or rate of cognitive decline in 299 Han Chinese Alzheimer and MCI patients. Study: [[Genetic effects on cognitive decline trajectories in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
