# P2RY12

P2RY12 encodes the P2Y12 purinergic receptor, a marker of homeostatic microglia that is lost as microglia become disease-associated.

## Connections

- Homeostatic marker that fell at protein and mRNA level in Grn knockout mouse microglia and in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia, and was slightly higher in TREM2 knockout iPSC-derived microglia, which are locked in a homeostatic state. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related: [[GRN]], [[TREM2]], [[Disease-associated microglia]], [[Microglial cell activation]]
- The number of P2RY12-positive cortical cells fell in [[AppNL-G-F mouse]] animals given the TREM2 antibody [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]] relative to isotype control, around plaques, while total microglia (IBA1) numbers were unchanged; read as a shift away from homeostatic microglia. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- Homeostatic marker of the microglia subclusters lost in Alzheimer cortex; its immunoreactivity was inversely correlated with [[ITGAX]] in microglia from 29 donors, and it was also kept in the tau-linked AD2 microglia. See [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
- Cited as the most expressed purinergic receptor of homeostatic microglia, falling in reactive states, in contrast to [[P2RX4]], which is induced. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
