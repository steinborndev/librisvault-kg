# SUMO3

SUMO3 encodes small ubiquitin-like modifier 3, a small protein that is attached to other proteins (SUMOylation) to change their activity, location or stability.

## Connections

- One of five genes in the [[Ts65Dn mouse]] hippocampus whose association with sponging of [[MIR155]] and [[MIR802]] was experimentally validated. Study: [[miR-155 and miR-802 targets in the hippocampus of Ts65Dn Down syndrome mice]]
