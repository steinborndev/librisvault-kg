# Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease

Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease is a fatal prion disease with rapidly progressive dementia, caused by misfolding of the prion protein encoded by [[PRNP]].

## Connections

- Variants: [[PRNP p.M129V]], [[PRNP p.E219K]]. Both were found in Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- A cohort of 27 cases and 20 controls (frontal cortex and cerebellum, immune gene panel) was pooled with four Alzheimer microarray sets because both are conformational disorders; early-onset Alzheimer with high CSF total tau or 14-3-3 resembles it. Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
