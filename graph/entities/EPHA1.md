# EPHA1

EPHA1, on chromosome 7q34.1, encodes a member of the ephrin family of receptor tyrosine kinases involved in cell morphology, motility and synaptic plasticity, and is a genome-wide association locus for late-onset Alzheimer disease.

## Connections

- [[EPHA1 rs11763230]] is the intronic variant tested in the late-onset subgroup.
- [[Alzheimer disease]] is the disease whose risk the locus is tied to.
- Study: [[SNP association study of Alzheimer disease in southern Chinese population]]
- [[EPHA1 p.V160A]] and [[EPHA1 p.M900V]] were both associated with lower risk of [[Small vessel ischemic disease]] in 96 early-onset patients against 368 elderly controls. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Putative risk gene since 2011, but how it contributes to synapse dysfunction in late-onset disease is unknown; related Eph receptors [[EPHA4]] and [[EPHB2]] regulate spines and NMDA receptor function. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
