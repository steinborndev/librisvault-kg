# NOTCH3 p.R75L

A rare missense change from arginine to leucine at residue 75 of [[NOTCH3]], found as a second rare variant in a Korean woman who also carried [[PSEN2 p.V226A]]. It does not add or remove a cysteine, which is why it is read as possibly benign, unlike the cysteine-altering changes of [[CADASIL]]; a different change at the same residue, Arg75Pro, has been linked to small vessel dysfunction and dementia.
