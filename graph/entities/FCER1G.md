# FCER1G

FCER1G encodes the common gamma subunit that signals for several immune Fc receptors, and it is expressed mainly in microglia and monocytes among the cell types of the brain and blood.

## Connections

- An allele-specific association with the 5' UTR variant [[FCER1G rs11421]] (itself in moderate linkage disequilibrium, r = 0.59, with the GWAS SNP [[ADAMTS4 rs4575098]]) was seen in the brain regions, in microglia and in classical monocytes with the same direction of effect; because microglia are the main FCER1G-expressing cell, the tissue-level signal is attributed to microglia.
