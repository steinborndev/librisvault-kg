# GHRL

GHRL encodes the precursor of ghrelin, a gut-derived appetite hormone also discussed as pro-cognitive.

## Connections

- Plasma ghrelin rose in 5xFAD mice, not significantly in wild type, after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]]; the authors cite earlier reports of raised ghrelin with the compound in rats and humans. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- Related: [[LEP]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
