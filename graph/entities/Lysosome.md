# Lysosome

The lysosome is the acidic, hydrolase-filled organelle that degrades material delivered by endocytosis and autophagy. In neurons, endo-lysosomal compartments are also where much gamma-secretase activity and Aβ generation take place.

## Connections

- Enlarged endo-lysosomes (LysoPrime Green puncta) marked wild-type mouse neurons with low gamma-secretase activity and neurons treated with [[DAPT]]; raising lysosomal pH or blocking acidification with [[Chloroquine]] or [[Bafilomycin A1]] made neurons die faster under oxidant stress. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Related: [[Endosome]], [[Gamma-secretase complex]], [[Autophagy]], [[Endocytosis]]
- [[P2RX4]] sits mostly in lysosomes and co-localised there with [[APOE]] and [[CTSB]] in macrophages and plaque-associated microglia; its presence promoted cathepsin B-dependent ApoE degradation without changing lysosomal pH (data not shown by the authors). Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
