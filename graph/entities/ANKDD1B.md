# ANKDD1B

ANKDD1B encodes the ankyrin repeat and death domain containing 1B protein and sits at chromosome 5q13.3, where a region shares genetic signal between [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] and blood cholesterol.

## Connections

- Top-ranked gene in the 5q13.3 covariance region of early-onset Alzheimer disease with total, [[LDL cholesterol]] and [[Non-HDL cholesterol]], with an eQTL in cortex and hippocampus that colocalizes with the Alzheimer signal. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
- Reported in the literature to associate with LDL cholesterol and [[Type 2 diabetes mellitus]], to be one of 28 genes in a severe-hypercholesterolaemia polygenic score, and to link [[Migraine]] and major depression.
