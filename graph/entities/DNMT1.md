# DNMT1

DNMT1 encodes DNA methyltransferase 1, the enzyme that copies CpG [[DNA methylation]] patterns onto the new strand after replication and so maintains gene silencing.

## Connections

- DNMT1 protein rose with [[D-ribose]] in [[Neuro2a]] cells and [[APP-PS1 mouse]] hippocampus while PINK1 fell, and fell again with [[Berberine]] or [[Azacitidine]] as PINK1 returned. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Target gene in this setting: [[PINK1]]; process: [[Mitophagy]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
