# SIRT2 rs10410544

rs10410544 is a C/T single-nucleotide polymorphism in [[SIRT2]]. The T allele has been reported in earlier work as raising the risk of [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]] 1.709-fold in people without the [[APOE]] ε4 allele.

## Connections

- Gene: [[SIRT2]]
- In 1086 patients the genotypes were C/C 34.72%, C/T 50.92% and T/T 14.36%; C/T carriers were the best and C/C the worst responders to a multifactorial treatment, with only minor effects on age at onset, lipids, liver, blood counts and baseline cognition. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Interacts with [[APOE]] genotype and [[CYP2D6]] metabolizer class; see [[Pharmacogenetics]].
