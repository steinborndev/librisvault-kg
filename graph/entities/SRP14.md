# SRP14

SRP14 encodes the 14 kDa subunit of the signal recognition particle, which targets secretory proteins to the rough endoplasmic reticulum membrane; it has been identified among tau-associated endoplasmic reticulum proteins in [[Alzheimer disease]] brain.

## Connections

- Plasma SRP14 was higher in carriers of the ε2-defining SNP [[APOE rs7412]]. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
