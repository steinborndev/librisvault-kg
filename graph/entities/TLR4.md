# TLR4

TLR4 encodes toll-like receptor 4, a pattern-recognition receptor on microglia and other immune cells that signals through NF-kappa-B to induce inflammatory cytokines.

## Connections

- TLR4 protein fell in mouse hippocampus and in amyloid-beta-challenged [[BV2]] cells after ajugol or isoacteoside, and isoacteoside docked to it with a computed binding energy of -9.668 kcal/mol. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Pathway partners: [[RELA]], [[NFKBIA]], [[NLRP3]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- mRNA rose 2.0-fold with amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture but not in those kept 16 days, alongside [[TLR2]] and [[HMGB1]]. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- LPS and amyloid-beta oligomers both signal through TLR4, and both drove larger calcium responses in macrophages and microglia carrying the protective [[PLCG2 p.P522R]] variant; the authors argue this is consistent with TLR4 signalling via PLCγ2 and PI(4,5)P2. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
- Upregulated in the cerebellar signature of Alzheimer disease, alongside heightened immune and inflammatory terms. Study: [[Predictive gene expression signatures of Alzheimer disease in six brain regions]]
- Small-intestine Tlr4 mRNA was higher in 5xFAD than wild-type mice and fell in both genotypes after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]], alongside lower plasma [[Lipopolysaccharide]]. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- LPS acting on TLR4 was used in iPSC microglia-like cells as a stimulus independent of alpha7; CHRFAM7A carriers still gave a stronger NF-κB-dependent cytokine response, suggesting the proinflammatory switch is broader than the Aβ response. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
