# CST3 p.A25T

A missense change from alanine to threonine at residue 25 of the [[CST3]] signal peptide, reported in ClinVar as pathogenic and linked to reduced cystatin C secretion.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CST3]]
- Homozygous carriers were 4 of 96 patients with [[Small vessel ischemic disease]] versus 6 of 368 elderly controls. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Earlier work ties the homozygous state to [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]] and macular degeneration
