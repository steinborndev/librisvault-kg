# CD28

CD28 encodes a T-cell surface receptor that delivers the main co-stimulatory signal for T-cell activation.

## Connections

- Plasma CD28 was modestly higher in carriers of the ε4-defining SNP [[APOE rs429358]]; the authors found no earlier report and suggest it may follow APOE isoform effects on inflammation. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
