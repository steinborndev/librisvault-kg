# SORL1 p.D1108N

A missense change from aspartate to asparagine at residue 1108, a calcium-cage residue in the first CR domain of [[SORL1]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- Observed in three unrelated cases (age at onset 73, 69 and 54); calcium-cage variants as a group were found in 13 cases and no controls. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
