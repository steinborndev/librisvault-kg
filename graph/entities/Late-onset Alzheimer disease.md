# Late-onset Alzheimer disease

Late-onset Alzheimer disease (LOAD) is [[Alzheimer disease]] with onset at 65 years or older, about 95% of all cases. It is a complex disorder with an estimated heritability of 58% to 79% from twin studies, and the APOE-TOMM40 region of chromosome 19 is its main common risk locus.

## Connections

- Parent disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; contrast with [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Risk genes: [[APOE]], [[TOMM40]], [[NECTIN2]], [[APOC1]], [[CLU]], [[CR1]], [[CD33]], [[SORL1]], [[TCF4]]
- X-linked risk: [[PCDH11X]] and [[PCDH11X rs5984894]], found in a two-stage genome-wide study (odds ratio 1.75 for female homozygotes). Study: [[GWAS identifies PCDH11X variant rs5984894 for late-onset Alzheimer disease]]; the association did not replicate in 889 cases and 850 controls, see [[PCDH11X polymorphisms fail to replicate in late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Risk variants: [[APOE rs429358]], [[APOE rs769449]], [[TOMM40 rs2075650]], [[NECTIN2 rs6857]]
- Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- Coding variants at GWAS loci tested in vessel disease: [[EPHA1 p.V160A]], [[CD33 p.A14V]], [[ABCA7 p.G1527A]], [[CR1 p.Q1022H]]; genes [[MS4A6A]], [[ABCA7]], [[CST3]]. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- The 39-variant common-variant risk score was associated with onset after 85 years (OR 1.29 per standard deviation). Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
- Truncating [[SORL1]] variants carried an 8.6-fold risk and high-priority missense variants a 4.2-fold risk of late-onset disease. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- The late-onset group showed an elevated modelled probability even at a low polygenic score, with ε4 carriers above all other genotypes. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- A cohort of 189 late-onset patients on [[Statin]] therapy showed variant-dependent lipid responses ([[ACE Alu insertion-deletion]], [[APOE]], [[CETP rs5882]], [[CETP rs708272]], [[LDLR rs11669576]]). Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- The T allele of [[SIRT2 rs10410544]] is cited as a 1.709-fold risk in APOE ε4-negative people. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Risk haplotypes at the [[NECTIN2]] and [[APOC1]] regions near [[APOE]] raised late-onset risk independently of ε4 and ε2 in Chinese and mainly European-ancestry cohorts. Study: [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]]
- Allele-specific expression analysis of 33 GWAS SNPs found that 24 loci affect a local gene's allelic expression in at least one brain region, with many effects confined to one region, exon or cell type (microglia, monocytes, oligodendrocytes). Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- Modelled in rats by non-genetic intrahippocampal infusion of fibrillar Aβ1-42, which lowered serum miR-146a-5p, -29a-3p, -29c-3p, -125b-5p and -191-5p; see [[Intrahippocampal amyloid-beta infusion rat model]]. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Two rare variants, [[NOTCH3 p.A284T]] (mean onset 80.5 years) and [[TREM2 p.Q33X]], were proposed as possible fully penetrant causes of late-onset disease in European-ancestry exomes. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Reviewed for Aβ-independent synaptic dysfunction through trafficking risk genes ([[APOE]], [[ABCA7]], [[BIN1]], [[CD2AP]], [[PICALM]], [[EPHA1]], [[SORL1]]); heritability is quoted as 58 to 79% and a GWAS meta-analysis lists 37 putative risk factors. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- The APOE region on chromosome 19 holds no common variant that stays significant after adjustment for APOE genotype, but rare variants of [[CBLC]], [[BCAM]] and [[RELB]] associated with CSF amyloid-beta 1-42 after it. Study: [[Rare variants near APOE and CSF and neuroimaging biomarkers in ADNI]]
- Autosomal dominant late-onset family: a Northern Italian pedigree without APP, PSEN1 or PSEN2 mutations carried [[GRIN2C p.A1072V]] in all 6 affected members tested and none of 9 unaffected (LOD 2.07), pointing to NMDA receptor signalling via [[GRIN2C]]. Study: [[Exome sequencing finds GRIN2C p.A1072V in an Italian late-onset Alzheimer family]]
- Polish cohort of 55 patients and 18 controls, all [[APOE]] ε4 carriers: the [[BCHE]] K variant ([[BCHE rs1803274]]) with the noncoding variants [[BCHE rs1126680]] and [[BCHE rs55781031]] was present in 10 patients (18%) and no control; the K variant alone did not differ. Study: [[BCHE noncoding variants and APOE ε4 in Polish late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- A subpopulation of wild-type mouse neurons showed familial-type gamma-secretase, endo-lysosome and vulnerability phenotypes, which the authors offer as a possible shared pathway between sporadic and familial disease; not tested in aged or human neurons. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Onset begins around 65 years with incidence rising 10-fold over 20 years, two to three decades after typical familial onset; it is polygenic and environmental, partitionable into subtypes, and the author questions the assumption that it follows the familial mechanism more slowly. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]; see [[Heterogeneity of late-onset Alzheimer disease]]. A Phase-2 trial of [[Levetiracetam]] targets its hyperexcitability.
- Late-onset GWAS SNPs were enriched in microglia-specific H3K27ac peaks, and a subset (BIN1, PICALM, CLU, ADAM10, NYAP1, CR1) lay in oligodendrocyte-enriched peaks. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- The [[BACE2]] locus showed nominal, APOE ε4 non-carrier-specific association in a neuropathology-confirmed cohort ([[TGenII neuropathology cohort]]). Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
- Mouse perturbations of risk genes captured different human late-onset modules: Apoe loss matched immune and microglial modules, Clu loss neuronal-system modules; see [[Apoe knockout mouse]], [[Clu knockout mouse]] and [[AMP-AD consensus clusters]]. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Blood methylation at the [[CpG island shore]] of [[CR1]], [[CLU]] and [[PICALM]] was lower in Japanese patients, and CLU methylation plus APOE ε4 allele number classified patients at AUC 0.80 to 0.85, below the 80% sensitivity and specificity benchmark. Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
- Rare loss-of-function variant [[ADAM17 p.R215I]] segregated with disease in one family and occurred in five of about 5,450 further samples, all cases with onset at 79 to 90 years. Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
