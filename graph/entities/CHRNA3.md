# CHRNA3

CHRNA3 encodes the alpha3 subunit of the neuronal nicotinic acetylcholine receptor, a ligand-gated ion channel; it assembles with beta subunits such as [[CHRNB4]].

## Connections

- mRNA in blood mononuclear cells was lower in dementia with Lewy bodies than in controls, higher in Alzheimer disease than in Lewy body dementia (median fold change 4.6 against 0.7), and positively correlated with CHRNA4 in both diseases, with CHRNB2 in Lewy body dementia, and negatively with CHRNA7 in Alzheimer disease.
