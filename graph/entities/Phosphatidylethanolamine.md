# Phosphatidylethanolamine

Phosphatidylethanolamines (PEs) are glycerophospholipids with an ethanolamine head group, the second most abundant membrane phospholipid, including ether-linked plasmalogen forms.

## Connections

- Most plasma PE species, including several plasmalogens with 20 to 22-carbon polyunsaturated chains, were lower in Alzheimer cases than controls; more than 30% of PE species associated differently with the [[CLU]] and [[CR1]] risk SNPs in cases and controls. The lyso-PE to PE ratio rose in [[U251]] cells given oligomeric [[Amyloid-beta]]. Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
