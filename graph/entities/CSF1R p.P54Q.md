# CSF1R p.P54Q

A missense change from proline to glutamine at residue 54 of [[CSF1R]], in exon 2, outside the tyrosine kinase domain. PolyPhen-2 and SIFT classed it as benign and tolerated, so it is not read as a cause of [[Hereditary diffuse leukoencephalopathy with spheroids]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[CSF1R]]; disease: [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Absent from KCDC, ExAC and 1000 Genomes; PolyPhen HumDiv 0.145, HumVar 0.215, SIFT 0.53. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
