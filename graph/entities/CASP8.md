# CASP8

CASP8 encodes caspase-8, an initiator caspase of death-receptor [[Apoptosis]] that cleaves [[RIPK1]] and thereby restrains [[Necroptosis]].

## Connections

- TNF-α with Smac mimetic strongly activated caspase-8 and caspase-3 in [[SH-SY5Y]] cells (less in primary neurons), and [[Z-VAD-FMK]] blocked it and shifted the death mode to phospho-MLKL-positive necroptosis. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Downstream executioner: [[CASP3]]
