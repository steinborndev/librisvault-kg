# TFRC

TFRC encodes transferrin receptor 1, which imports iron into cells and is a marker and driver of [[Ferroptosis]].

## Connections

- Higher in APOE3 than [[APOE p.R136S]] microglia beside PSEN1 mutant neurons, and induced by amyloid beta 42 in APOE3 microglia only. Forced doxycycline-inducible expression lowered [[Phagocytosis]] in microglia of both genotypes. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Related: [[SAT1]], [[Liproxstatin-1]]
