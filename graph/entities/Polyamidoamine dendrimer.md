# Polyamidoamine dendrimer

Polyamidoamine (PAMAM) is a branched cationic polymer with many surface amino groups; it binds negatively charged nucleic acids by electrostatic attraction and can buffer endosomal acidity, which makes it a common siRNA carrier.

## Connections

- PEGylated and coupled to the [[RVG29 peptide]] (PPR), it held siRNA at a 5:1 mass ratio, formed particles of about 100 nm by light scattering, and escaped lysosomes in three cell lines; the non-targeted PEGylated form (PP) served as control. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Part of: [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]
