# Rutin

Rutin is a flavonol glycoside (quercetin bound to the disaccharide rutinose, molecular weight about 610) found in many food plants, with reported antioxidant, anti-amyloidogenic and anti-inflammatory activity.

## Connections

- The most abundant of three quantified constituents of [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]] (5.49 mg/g); the authors cite rutin or its metabolites as able to cross the blood-brain barrier and propose it contributes to the extract's effect. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Co-constituents: [[Chlorogenic acid]], [[Kaempferol-3-O-rutinoside]]
