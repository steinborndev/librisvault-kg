# NOTCH3 p.P2074Q

A missense change from proline to glutamine at residue 2074 of [[NOTCH3]] (exon 33), near the end of the coding sequence.

## Connections

- Gene: [[NOTCH3]]
- Seen in 4 Alzheimer cases and no controls in exomes, but frequent in Europeans (0.024) and Africans (0.091) and in 286 whole-genome carriers across cases, mild cognitive impairment and controls, so probably benign.
