# 2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol

2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol (DAPG) is a small acetylated derivative of phloroglucinol produced by Pseudomonas bacteria, where it protects plants against fungi and other microbes. In cell and mouse models of [[Alzheimer disease]] it lowers secreted amyloid beta and raises the α-secretase product sAPPα.

## Connections

- Lowered secreted Aβ1-40 and Aβ1-42 in [[HEK293]] cells expressing Swedish-mutant [[APP]] and in transgenic mouse primary astrocytes, and Triton-soluble Aβ1-40 after injection into the mouse hippocampus; it did not work after intraperitoneal dosing, and is predicted not to cross the blood-brain barrier.
- Raised sAPPα in a manner blocked by the [[ADAM10]] inhibitor [[GI254023X]] but not by the [[ADAM17]] inhibitor TAPI; its Aβ-lowering effect was reversed by [[Brefeldin A]] and, in astrocytes, [[Golgicide A]].
