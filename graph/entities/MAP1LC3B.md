# MAP1LC3B

MAP1LC3B encodes LC3B; its lipidated form LC3-II sits on autophagosome membranes and is the standard readout of [[Autophagy]].

## Connections

- LC3-II tended to fall after ajugol or isoacteoside (not significantly in the model mice), alongside a rise in [[SQSTM1]]. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Autophagy partners: [[SQSTM1]], [[MTOR]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- LC3-positive puncta were low in mouse microglia kept 16 days in culture and rose with amyloid-beta 1-42 to near the 2-day level, which the authors read as autophagosome formation with impaired turnover. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- LC3B-II and GFP-LC3 puncta did not rise with [[ALT001]] (they did with [[Carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenyl hydrazone]]), taken as LC3-independent mitophagy; see [[Alternative mitophagy]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- LC3B protein and LC3A/B-[[TOMM20]] co-localization fell with [[D-ribose]] and rose with [[Berberine]] in [[Neuro2a]] cells and APP/PS1 mouse hippocampus. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- LC3-II rose dose-dependently with TNF-α and further with TSZ in neuronal cultures, and a GFP-mCherry-LC3 reporter showed fewer red-only autolysosomes and more yellow autophagosomes, read as blocked flux. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
