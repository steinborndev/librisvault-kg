# Kaempferol-3-O-rutinoside

Kaempferol-3-O-rutinoside, also called nicotiflorin (molecular weight about 594), is a kaempferol glycoside reported to protect against oxidative stress and inflammation and to ease memory dysfunction in animal studies.

## Connections

- Quantified at 1.38 mg/g in [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]]; the authors cite evidence that it crosses the blood-brain barrier. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Co-constituents: [[Rutin]], [[Chlorogenic acid]]
