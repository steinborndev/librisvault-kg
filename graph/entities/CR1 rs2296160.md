# CR1 rs2296160

rs2296160 is a missense variant of [[CR1]] in near-complete linkage disequilibrium with the GWAS SNP [[CR1 rs2093760]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[CR1]]
- The only CR1 exonic variant strongly tied to allelic expression in the temporal cortex (p = 8.95E-16), so it likely mediates the rs2093760 signal. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
