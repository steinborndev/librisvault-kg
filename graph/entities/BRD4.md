# BRD4

BRD4 encodes a BET-family bromodomain protein that binds acetylated histones and recruits transcription machinery; it is the target of bromodomain inhibitors.

## Connections

- Knockdown of BRD4 in Connectivity Map data reversed the Alzheimer expression signature in temporal cortex, superior temporal gyrus and dorsolateral prefrontal cortex; the authors propose bromodomain inhibition as a repurposing route but note limited brain penetration.
