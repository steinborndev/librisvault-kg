# CR1 rs2093760

rs2093760 is a late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] GWAS SNP at the [[CR1]] locus, in near-complete linkage disequilibrium (r = 0.995) with the CR1 missense variant [[CR1 rs2296160]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[CR1]]
- It was a strong allele-specific QTL of CR1 across several exons in the temporal cortex only (CR1 is barely detectable in the other regions), and a genotype-level eQTL of CR1 in oligodendrocytes but not in microglia, suggesting oligodendrocytes mediate the tissue signal; the missense variant could be the true driver. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
