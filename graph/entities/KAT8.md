# KAT8

KAT8 encodes a lysine acetyltransferase of the MYST family that acetylates histone H4 at lysine 16; it lies at a genome-wide significant late-onset Alzheimer disease locus.

## Connections

- One of only two Alzheimer sentinel SNPs whose nearest H3K27ac peak was significantly enriched in neurons rather than microglia or oligodendrocyte-enriched glia (log2 fold change 0.77 in neurons, q = 4.7e-13). Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Disease context: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
