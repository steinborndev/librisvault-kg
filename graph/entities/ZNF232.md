# ZNF232

ZNF232 encodes a zinc finger protein and lies near a genome-wide association signal for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- The association of the GWAS SNP rs113260531 with ZNF232 became significant only after the [[Hierarchical Poisson mixed model]] added homozygous subjects, because few double-heterozygous subjects were available. Related genes at the same SNP: [[RABEP1]], [[CHRNE]]. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
