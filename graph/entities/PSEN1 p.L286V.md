# PSEN1 p.L286V

A missense change of PSEN1 in [[PSEN1]] (leucine 286 to valine), seen in familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. A known pathogenic mutation found in one Taiwanese patient with onset at 43 and a positive family history.
