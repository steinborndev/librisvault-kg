# ICAM1

ICAM1 encodes intercellular adhesion molecule 1, an endothelial adhesion protein induced by inflammation that mediates leukocyte binding.

## Connections

- Measured in dorsolateral prefrontal cortex lysate as one of three vascular injury proteins (with [[VCAM1]] and serum amyloid A). Brain expression of [[TRIP10]], [[FOSL1]], [[VASP]] and [[C4B]] was associated with its level. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Cerebrospinal fluid level was lower in carriers of the [[NECTIN2]] alpha risk haplotype (FDR 0.054, ADNI); see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].
