# APP p.V717I

A familial missense change from valine to isoleucine at residue 717 of [[APP]], within the transmembrane region next to the γ-secretase cleavage site; it is a common cause of [[Familial Alzheimer disease]] and raises Aβ42 production.

## Connections

- Gene: [[APP]]; pathway: [[Amyloid-beta formation]]
- In an isogenic hiPSC line carrying it, converting APOE3 to APOE2 lowered secreted Aβ but not the Aβ42/40 ratio, and shifted APP processing only modestly; APOE2 astrocytes with this mutation were the only ones to take up more Aβ. The authors attribute the weaker effect to a strong mutation-specific push toward β-secretase cleavage. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]
- Five early-onset cases carrying it had abundant dense-core plaques; dystrophic neurites around some cores stained for APP and [[TMCC2]], others for APP alone, and TMCC2-APP co-staining of dystrophies was rarer than in late-onset disease. Study: [[TMCC2 in dense-core plaques and Down syndrome pathology in Alzheimer brain]]
