# PSEN2 p.L238F

A missense change from leucine to phenylalanine at residue 238 of [[PSEN2]], in the fifth transmembrane helix. It has been reported in sporadic, early-onset and late-onset patients, including a Dominantly Inherited Alzheimer Network family with onset at 49 and Alzheimer's Disease Sequencing Project cases, with conflicting conclusions on pathogenicity; in a neuroblastoma cell model it doubled the amyloid beta 42 to 40 ratio.
