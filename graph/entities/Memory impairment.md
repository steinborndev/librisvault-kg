# Memory impairment

Memory impairment is a reduced ability to learn new information or recall it; it is the leading clinical sign of [[Alzheimer disease]] and is tested in rodents with spatial tasks such as the Morris water maze.

## Connections

- Rats given fibrillar Aβ1-42 in the hippocampus swam longer paths and took longer to reach the platform on days 4 and 5 of training, 14 days after surgery. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
