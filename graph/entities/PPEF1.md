# PPEF1

PPEF1 encodes a calcium-regulated serine/threonine protein phosphatase with EF-hand domains, expressed in sensory neurons and brain.

## Connections

- Downregulated in five of six post-mortem brain regions in Alzheimer disease and a co-expression hub gene. Study: [[Predictive gene expression signatures of Alzheimer disease in six brain regions]]
