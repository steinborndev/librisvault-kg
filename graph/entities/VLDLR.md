# VLDLR

VLDLR encodes the very low density lipoprotein receptor, an ApoE receptor of the LDL receptor family that is present at synapses and binds Reelin.

## Connections

- Together with [[LRP8]], VLDLR is activated presynaptically by [[RELN]], raising calcium and vesicle fusion with up-regulation of a SNARE complex of VAMP7 and SNAP-25. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Ligand: [[APOE]]; relatives: [[LDLR]], [[LRP1]]
