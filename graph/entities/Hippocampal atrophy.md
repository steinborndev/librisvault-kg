# Hippocampal atrophy

Hippocampal atrophy is loss of hippocampal volume on brain imaging, an early structural sign of memory decline in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Smaller hippocampal volume was associated with the [[NECTIN2]] alpha risk haplotype, independently of APOE ε4; see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Mild cognitive impairment]]
- CT of a woman with [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]] and early dementia showed severe hippocampal atrophy, medial temporal atrophy score 3 on both sides. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- In PSEN1 variant carriers, hippocampal volume was lower in transmembrane than cytoplasmic carriers from about 10.9 years before expected symptom onset and fully mediated their MMSE difference; the gap persisted after adjusting for amyloid PET and CSF phospho-tau 181. Study: [[PSEN1 transmembrane versus cytoplasmic variants and progression in DIAN]]
