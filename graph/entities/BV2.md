# BV2

BV2 is an immortalised mouse microglial cell line, widely used as a cultured stand-in for brain microglia.

## Connections

- Main model for the [[TREM2]] promoter work: reporter assays, nuclear extract for pull-down and gel shift, [[YY1]] knock-down and [[Lipopolysaccharide]] treatment. Study: [[TREM2 regulation by YY1 in microglia and Alzheimer disease models]]
- Compared with [[Neuro2a]] and [[HEK293]] in the same study.
- Challenged with amyloid-beta 1-42 (10 μM, 24 h) as an in vitro inflammation model to test [[Ajugol]] and [[Isoacteoside]] on cytokines ([[IL1B]], [[IL4]], [[IFNG]], [[CSF3]]) and on inflammation, apoptosis and autophagy proteins. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Microglial cell in a [[bEnd.3]] Transwell model: took up targeted and non-targeted siRNA carriers equally, independent of the [[RVG29 peptide]], silenced [[BACE1]], and took up more FITC-labelled amyloid beta after silencing. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Transfected with circ-Epc1 (see [[EPC1]]), a [[MIR770]] mimic and TREM2 siRNA and challenged with [[Lipopolysaccharide]] to show that circ-Epc1 raises [[TREM2]], lowers M1 and raises M2 markers ([[ITGAM]], [[MRC1]]) and cytokines ([[IL6]], [[TNF]], [[IL1B]]) through miR-770-3p. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- With a SIRT3 shRNA version, treated with nicotinamide riboside: SIRT3 rose 78% and IDE rose in both lines, more in the SIRT3-silenced cells (64%). Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
