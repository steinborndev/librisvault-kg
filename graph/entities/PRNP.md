# PRNP

PRNP encodes the prion protein, whose misfolding underlies [[Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease]] and other prion diseases.

## Connections

- Variants [[PRNP p.M129V]] and [[PRNP p.E219K]] occurred in Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients; mutant and normal prion protein structures were modelled with RaptorX. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- PrPc, the prion protein, is named with [[TMEM97]] and PSD-95 as a candidate postsynaptic receptor for amyloid-beta oligomers. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Prion protein induction in APP/PS1 and APP/PS1/Sirt3-knockout brain was comparable; the authors note that prion protein regulates production of neurotoxic Aβ. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- Among 71 ADAM17 substrates, PRNP mRNA had the strongest inverse correlation with ADAM17 mRNA in human prefrontal cortex (r = -0.77), just ahead of [[APP]] (-0.74). Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
