# Bexarotene

Bexarotene is a synthetic retinoid X receptor agonist.

## Connections

- Used at 1 µM as an RXR control: it raised cholesterol efflux genes and lowered [[DHCR24]] and [[HMGCR]] in [[CCF-STTG1]] cells like the seaweed extracts, and raised the tested fatty acid synthesis genes. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Target: [[RXRA]]
- Clearance of amyloid-beta by the RXR agonist is said to need ABCA1 lipidation of APOE. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
