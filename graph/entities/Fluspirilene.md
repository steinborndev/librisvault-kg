# Fluspirilene

Fluspirilene is a long-acting injectable diphenylbutylpiperidine antipsychotic used in schizophrenia.

## Connections

- Formed a stable simulated complex with [[ETV6]] (RMSD 3.26 Å, 68.39 hydrogen bonds, logBB 0.42) but is a likely P-glycoprotein substrate, so it was ranked below [[Lasmiditan]] for [[Alzheimer disease]]; it is among the six docked drugs already studied in Alzheimer disease.
