# SHARPIN p.S17F

A missense variant in [[SHARPIN]] (serine 17 to phenylalanine) that raises the risk of [[Alzheimer disease]]; it is not in linkage disequilibrium with the second SHARPIN missense variant, [[SHARPIN p.P294S]].
