# CFH

CFH encodes factor H, the main plasma regulator of the alternative pathway C3 convertase.

## Connections

- Plasma factor H was higher in adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (p < 0.001), as was the factor H-related protein [[CFHR4]]; an earlier report found higher factor H in post-mortem Down syndrome brain, liver and spleen. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
