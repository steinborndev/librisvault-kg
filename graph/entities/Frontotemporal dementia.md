# Frontotemporal dementia

Frontotemporal dementia is a group of progressive neurodegenerative disorders of the frontal and temporal lobes; heterozygous loss-of-function mutations in [[GRN]] are one genetic cause and, per the source below, account for 5 to 20% of cases.

## Connections

- In CSF of 11 symptomatic GRN mutation carriers, GRN was lower, [[NEFL]] higher and six microglial activity proteins ([[FABP3]], [[MDH1]], [[GDI1]], [[CAPG]], [[CD44]], [[GPNMB]]) higher than in 12 non-carriers; the panel separated the groups with an AUC of 0.87. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Of seven frontotemporal dementia genes on a 50-gene panel, only [[MAPT p.P513A]] was found in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]

- Five CSF proteins were differentially expressed and validated across three cohorts, with synaptic and immune processes altered and no rise in glycolytic enzymes, unlike Alzheimer disease. Study: [[CSF proteomics of Alzheimer, Lewy body and frontotemporal dementia]]
- Dominant MAPT mutations cause FTD with parkinsonism and tau pathology in neurons and glia (tauopathy); this made tau genetics seem unrelated to Alzheimer disease, although the triple transgenic mouse carries such an FTD mutation. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- Blood methylation at the CLU and APOE CpG island shores did not differ from controls in 27 patients. Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
- Three FUS deletions and the PSEN2 p.A252T change were found in a gene screen of Swedish dementia patients, two of them in behavioral variant FTD and semantic variant primary progressive aphasia; no likely pathogenic variant in TARDBP, CHMP2B or VCP. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
