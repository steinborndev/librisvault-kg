# GRIN1

GRIN1 encodes the obligatory GluN1 subunit of the NMDA-type glutamate receptor, a calcium-permeable ion channel at excitatory synapses.

## Connections

- GluN1 was substantially lost from synaptic fractions in Alzheimer brain, together with GluR2. Study: [[Shank proteins and glutamate receptors at the Alzheimer postsynaptic density]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; location: [[Postsynaptic density]]; related: [[Excitotoxicity]], [[SHANK3]]
- Cotransfected as HA-GluN1 with GluN2C so that functional NMDA receptors formed in rat neurons carrying [[GRIN2C p.A1072V]]. Study: [[Exome sequencing finds GRIN2C p.A1072V in an Italian late-onset Alzheimer family]]
- GluN1 was reduced in the hippocampal synaptosome fraction of 12-month APP/PS1 mice and not in mice also lacking [[P2RX4]] (supplementary blot), offered as support for restored synaptic integrity when soluble Aβ falls. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
