# MS4A7

MS4A7 encodes a member of the membrane-spanning 4-domains family on chromosome 11, expressed in myeloid cells.

## Connections

- A microglia-specific allele-specific association was detected for MS4A7 but not in the tissue-level analysis, probably because of its low whole-tissue expression. Related family gene: [[MS4A6A]]. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
