# HDL cholesterol

HDL cholesterol (HDL-C) is the cholesterol carried in high-density lipoprotein particles, the fraction that rises as LDL-C falls.

- Shares the [[APOE]], [[LILRA5]] and [[LRRC25]] loci with early-onset Alzheimer disease in a direct GWAS comparison, but showed no significant local genetic covariance with it. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
