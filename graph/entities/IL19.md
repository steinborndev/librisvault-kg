# IL19

IL19 encodes interleukin-19, a cytokine of the interleukin-10 family that signals through a receptor made of [[IL20RA]] and [[IL20RB]].

## Connections

- Strongly upregulated in activated mouse microglia, which are the only nervous-system cells reported to express its receptor; IL-19 deficiency raised [[IL6]] and [[TNF]] output and added IL-19 suppressed it, making it an autocrine anti-inflammatory brake. Rose in affected areas of an Alzheimer mouse model with disease progression. Study: [[Interleukin-19 as an autocrine brake on activated microglia]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Related: [[Microglial cell activation]], [[Neuroinflammation]]
- The number of IL-19-expressing astrocytes rose in the hippocampus of Alzheimer model mice fed [[cis-9, trans-11-conjugated linoleic acid]], alongside more [[IL10]]-expressing astrocytes. Study: [[Dietary conjugated linoleic acid lowers amyloid-beta in Alzheimer mouse hippocampus]]
