# TNF

TNF encodes tumor necrosis factor, a cytokine released mainly by monocytes and macrophages that signals through TNFRSF1A and TNFRSF1B.

## Connections

- Higher in blood mononuclear cells after exercise and one of seven genes (with [[APP]], [[CCR1]], [[ATF3]], [[KLF4]], [[HES4]] and [[MAFB]]) kept as the ERADMT panel; co-expression regulon analysis made [[ATF3]] its positive regulator. A TNF signalling route linked CD14+ and CD16+ monocytes to other blood cells. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- TNF-family receptors [[TNFRSF25]] and [[TNFRSF21]] were higher in APOE2 than in APOE4 xenografted human microglia, contrasting with a mouse report of more TNF-alpha in APOE4 microglia. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
- Brain mRNA fell in [[FAD4T mouse]] animals treated with [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Not induced in rat astrocytes by Aβ1-42 alone, and not induced even when [[MIR146A]] was blocked (p = 0.803). Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- TNF-α protein by ELISA fell in Alzheimer mouse brain after ADSC exosomes (most with circ-Epc1-containing exosomes) and in LPS-challenged [[BV2]] cells overexpressing circ-Epc1; [[MIR770]] mimic or TREM2 knockdown partly reversed the cell effect. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Mast cells are said to supply about 25% of rat brain TNF-α; mast cell TNF-α sensitizes meningeal nociceptors and down-regulates [[OCLN]], [[CLDN5]], [[TJP1]] and VE-cadherin at the blood-brain barrier. See [[Mast cell activation]]. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- Cortical TNF rose with age in transgenic and wild-type mice alike, with no genotype effect and no link to serotonin transporter loss. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
- mRNA rose with amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture and, less, in those kept 16 days, whose baseline was about 10-fold lower. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Higher in activated mouse microglia lacking [[IL19]] and lowered by added IL-19, which acts as an autocrine brake on microglia. Study: [[Interleukin-19 as an autocrine brake on activated microglia]]
- mRNA in blood mononuclear cells was higher than in controls in both dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer disease, and lower in Lewy body dementia than in Alzheimer disease (p < 0.0001); no correlation with nicotinic receptor subunit expression. Study: [[PBMC nicotinic receptor and cytokine expression in DLB and Alzheimer disease]]
- TNF-related inflammation ranked first or second among enriched gene sets in caudate nucleus RNA-seq of [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]]; the authors cite TNF-alpha as driver of chronic inflammation in both. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Brain TNF-α measured by ELISA rose in Aβ1-42-injected mice and was lowered by 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]]. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- TNF-α was raised in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and lowered by [[Magnolol]] at 20 and 40 mg/kg (p < 0.001) and by [[Donepezil]] (ELISA, n = 6 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Plasma TNF-α fell only in wild-type mice given [[D-Pinitol]], not in 5xFAD mice. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- TNF-α transcript rose after Aβ1-42 in [[CHRFAM7A]] carrier iPSC microglia-like cells and fell in non-carriers, while TNF-α and TNF-β protein fell unexpectedly after LPS in the isogenic carrier. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
- Injected into the lateral ventricle (5 μg) it raised phospho-[[MLKL]] about 3-fold in wild-type mouse CA1 and killed about 13.75% of neurons, and in cultured neurons it drove [[Necroptosis]] through [[TNFRSF1A]] and [[RIPK1]], with strong effect only when caspases and cIAPs were also blocked. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
