# GLP1R

GLP1R encodes the receptor for glucagon-like peptide 1, a G protein-coupled receptor through which incretin drugs act; it also signals in brain neurons.

## Connections

- Of ten neurotransmitter receptor genes (dopamine, GLP-1, opioid, serotonin) tested in the caudate nucleus, only GLP1R changed significantly in [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]] against controls, with lower RNA read as reduced signalling that may weaken mitochondrial function through [[SLC25A6]]. The GLP1R gene lies within 50 Mb of a telomere in rat, chimpanzee and human, and its transcript is longer in chimpanzee than in the other three species. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Cited background: reduced GLP1R RNA in Alzheimer caudate nucleus, and a phase II trial in which the GLP-1 receptor agonist lixisenatide improved motor function in early Parkinson disease.
- Cell-signalling partners in the same study: [[SLC9A9]], [[NFKB1]].
