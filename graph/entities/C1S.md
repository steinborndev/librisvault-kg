# C1S

C1S encodes complement C1s, the serine protease subunit of the first component of the classical complement pathway.

## Connections

- It was one of five proteins consistently selected for separating progressing from stable mild cognitive impairment and added to the 21-protein set, but it fell outside the 13-protein universal panel after backward elimination.
