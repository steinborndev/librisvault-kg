# EDR peptide

EDR (Glu-Asp-Arg, marketed as Pinealon) is a synthetic tripeptide proposed as a neuroprotective bioregulator; it was derived from the cortex peptide complex Cortexin and is reported to bind double-stranded DNA in the minor groove.

## Connections

- Given daily at 400 μg/kg for 2 months to [[5xFAD mouse]] animals, it raised CA1 [[Dendritic spine]] density by 11% (13% in males, 12% in females) and lowered thin spines, but did not restore the mushroom fraction in the pooled or female groups; docking placed it in the minor groove with an ICM score of -44.7, stronger than [[KED peptide]]. Study: [[EDR and KED tripeptides restore dendritic spines in 5xFAD mice]]
- Docking-predicted promoter targets: [[CASP3]], [[NES]], [[GAP43]], [[APOE]], [[SOD2]], [[PPARA]], [[PPARG]], [[GPX1]]
