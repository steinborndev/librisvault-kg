# NFE2L2

NFE2L2 encodes Nrf2, a transcription factor that binds antioxidant response elements (ARE) in gene promoters and switches on the cell's defence against [[Oxidative stress]].

## Connections

- Chronic [[Amyloid-beta]] 42 lowered Nrf2 in cultured neurons, reversed by [[Acetylcysteine]]; Nrf2 overexpression raised [[DICER1]] and chromatin immunoprecipitation showed Nrf2 binding the ARE motifs of the Dicer1 promoter. Study: [[Dicer1 loss in APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse brain via amyloid-beta 42 repression of Nrf2]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Transcript of NFE2L2 (Nrf2) was higher in the caudate nucleus of both [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]] postmortem brain; the authors read it as a compensatory antioxidant response to TNF-driven oxidative stress that is not enough to protect neurons. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Nrf2 knockout mice lose myelin diffusely by 10 months of age, taken to show myelin survival depends on antioxidant status. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- [[LM-031]] raised NRF2 in ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed [[SH-SY5Y]] cells (75% to 109%) with its targets [[NQO1]] and [[GCLC]]; it still rescued neurite length and lowered caspase 3 activity when NFE2L2 was partly knocked down by shRNA. In streptozocin-treated [[3xTg-AD mouse]] hippocampus NRF2 fell to 53% and LM-031 returned it to 92%. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
