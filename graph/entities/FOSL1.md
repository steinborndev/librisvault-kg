# FOSL1

FOSL1 encodes FRA-1, a subunit of the AP-1 transcription factor.

## Connections

- A leading-edge gene of the TNFA signaling via NFKB pathway whose brain expression, with [[TRIP10]], was associated with ICAM-1 levels. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
- Differentially expressed in Alzheimer blood CD14+ and CD16+ monocytes and a signature regulator, with [[MAFB]] and [[HES4]], of the M4 transcription factor module; not kept in the final seven-gene exercise panel. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
