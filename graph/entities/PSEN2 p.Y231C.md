# PSEN2 p.Y231C

A missense change from tyrosine to cysteine at residue 231 of [[PSEN2]], in the fifth transmembrane helix. It was reported in a 52-year-old Italian patient with [[Frontotemporal dementia]] and a family history of dementia, without segregation analysis.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PSEN2]]; region: [[Presenilin transmembrane domain 5]]
