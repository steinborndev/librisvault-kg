# TgCRND8 mouse

An aggressive transgenic amyloid mouse model expressing human [[APP]] with the Swedish (KM670/671NL) and Indiana (V717F) familial Alzheimer mutations at about five times the endogenous murine level, on a mixed C57BL/6J and C3H/HeJ background. Aβ40 and Aβ42 appear at one month, and by three months plaques, astrocyte and microglial activation, neuritic dystrophy and memory deficits are present.

## Connections
