# AIFM1

AIFM1 encodes apoptosis-inducing factor, a mitochondrial flavoprotein that supports respiratory-chain assembly and, when released into the nucleus, triggers caspase-independent cell death.

## Connections

- Its protein was found in the detergent-insoluble fraction of demented amygdala and in inclusion body-like structures of cognitively impaired subjects. Study: [[Detergent-insoluble proteins in amygdala of cognitively impaired elderly]]
- Disease and phenotype: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Cognitive impairment]]
