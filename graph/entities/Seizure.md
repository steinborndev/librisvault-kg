# Seizure

A seizure is a transient episode of abnormal, excessive neuronal activity. Seizures are more frequent in familial than in sporadic [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Disease: [[Familial Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: 2 of 9 index patients with [[PSEN1 p.M146I]] had seizures (22.2%), against 24% cited for PSEN1 carriers in general; a woman with [[PSEN1 p.G206D]] developed seizures after memory loss at 33. See [[Familial Alzheimer disease mutations in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
