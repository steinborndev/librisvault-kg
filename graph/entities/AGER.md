# AGER

AGER encodes RAGE, a multiligand cell-surface receptor that carries amyloid-beta from blood into brain across the blood-brain barrier.

## Connections

- Proposed to be raised by cholesterol and by age in cerebral endothelium, increasing amyloid-beta influx while [[LRP1]] falls. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Related process: [[Amyloid-beta clearance]]; chemical: [[Amyloid-beta]]
