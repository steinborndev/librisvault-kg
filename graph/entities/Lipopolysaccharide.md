# Lipopolysaccharide

Lipopolysaccharide (LPS) is the outer-membrane endotoxin of Gram-negative bacteria and a standard stimulus used to provoke inflammatory activation of microglia and macrophages in culture.

## Connections

- At 2 μg/ml overnight it lowered both [[YY1]] and [[TREM2]] protein in [[BV2]] cells, which the authors use as a model of neuroinflammation. Study: [[TREM2 regulation by YY1 in microglia and Alzheimer disease models]]
- At 1 μg/mL for one day it served as the inflammatory challenge for [[BV2]] cells in which circ-Epc1, a [[MIR770]] mimic and TREM2 siRNA were tested on M1/M2 microglial markers and cytokines. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- At 50 ng/ml it raised calcium and diacylglycerol and lowered PIP2 more in macrophages and microglia carrying [[PLCG2 p.P522R]] than in controls. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
- Reviewed as injuring white matter, raising amyloid-beta influx and lowering efflux at the blood-brain barrier, and disturbing cholesterol handling through cholesterol 24-hydroxylase. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Plasma LPS was higher in untreated 5xFAD than wild-type mice (p < 0.001) and fell in both genotypes after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]], read as less gut leakage; no direct permeability test was run. See [[Increased intestinal permeability]]. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- At 1 μg/mL it was used as a pathogen-associated stimulus in iPSC microglia-like cells, giving a stronger, longer NF-κB response with [[CHRFAM7A]] than without it. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
