# Extracellular vesicle

Extracellular vesicles (EVs) are membrane-bound particles released by cells that carry proteins, RNAs including microRNAs, and lipids between cells. Brain-derived EVs can cross the blood-brain barrier, so their cargo can be sampled in blood; amyloid beta and phosphorylated tau species have been found in EVs from [[Alzheimer disease]] patients.
