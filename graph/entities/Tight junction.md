# Tight junction

Tight junctions are the protein seals between neighbouring endothelial cells that close the paracellular route across the blood-brain barrier. Their main components are claudins (claudin-5 predominating), occludin, zonula occludens proteins and junctional adhesion molecules, tied inside the cell to the actin filaments.
