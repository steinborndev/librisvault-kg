# Nasu-Hakola disease

Nasu-Hakola disease is a rare autosomal-recessive disorder of early-onset dementia with multifocal bone cysts, caused by loss of function of [[TREM2]] or of its adaptor TYROBP.

## Connections

- Gene: [[TREM2]]; the stop-gain [[TREM2 p.Q33X]] causes it in homozygous form, whereas four heterozygous carriers in an exome study had late-onset Alzheimer disease without bone cysts. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Related: [[Alzheimer disease]]
