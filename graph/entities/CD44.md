# CD44

CD44 encodes a cell-surface adhesion receptor for hyaluronan and other ligands, expressed on microglia, astrocytes and many other cells.

## Connections

- Up in CSF of symptomatic GRN mutation carriers and in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia medium, and the only panel protein significantly up in Grn knockout mouse CSF. It correlated with [[CHI3L1]] in the human CSF but did not differ between diagnostic groups in an Alzheimer cohort. The authors note that soluble [[GPNMB]] is a ligand of CD44 and link the pair to lipid handling. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related panel members: [[FABP3]], [[MDH1]], [[GDI1]], [[CAPG]]
- Upregulated in four of six post-mortem brain regions of Alzheimer disease (not DLPFC or cerebellum), read as immune-cell expression. Study: [[Predictive gene expression signatures of Alzheimer disease in six brain regions]]
- Among the 16 top-200 genes upregulated in hippocampal CA1 and CA3 of both sexes with Alzheimer disease, and a neighbour of [[ESR1]] in the Alzheimer susceptibility-gene interaction network. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
