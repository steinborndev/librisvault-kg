# Chlorpromazine

Chlorpromazine is a phenothiazine antipsychotic that, at the micromolar doses used in cell work, also blocks clathrin-mediated endocytosis.

## Connections

- At 30 μmol/L it cut uptake of [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]] by 77.1% in [[bEnd.3]] cells and also reduced it in [[Neuro2a]] and [[BV2]] cells, pointing to clathrin-dependent entry. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
