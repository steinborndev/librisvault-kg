# TARBP2

TARBP2 encodes TRBP, a double-stranded RNA-binding protein that partners with [[DICER1]] in processing precursor microRNAs and loading them onto the silencing complex.

## Connections

- TRBP phosphorylation fell in neurons after short [[Amyloid-beta]] oligomer exposure, together with Dicer1 phosphorylation; JNK and ERK inhibitors changed phospho-TRBP differently. Study: [[Amyloid oligomers rapidly raise neuronal Dicer1 by reducing phosphorylation]]
