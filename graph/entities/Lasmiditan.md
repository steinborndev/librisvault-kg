# Lasmiditan

Lasmiditan is a 5-HT1F receptor agonist approved for the acute treatment of [[Migraine]]; it enters the brain and, unlike triptans, does not constrict blood vessels.

## Connections

- Ranked the top repurposing candidate for [[Alzheimer disease]] after docking against the transcription factor [[ETV6]] (most stable complex of the seven simulated) and a favourable predicted brain-entry profile; no experimental test yet.
