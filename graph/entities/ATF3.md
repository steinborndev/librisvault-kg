# ATF3

ATF3 encodes a stress-inducible basic leucine zipper transcription factor of the ATF/CREB family.

## Connections

- Differentially expressed after exercise and in the M4 module of Alzheimer blood monocytes; its regulon made it the positive regulator of [[TNF]], and in blood expression from 204 patients and 530 controls it correlated negatively with [[APP]]. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
