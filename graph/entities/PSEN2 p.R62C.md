# PSEN2 p.R62C

A missense change (arginine to cysteine at residue 62) in [[PSEN2]], located in the intracellular region next to the N-terminus, outside the presenilin domain; a previously published variant, classed here as of uncertain significance. In transfected [[HEK293]] cells it did not change Aβ42, Aβ40 or the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio relative to wild-type PSEN2, which the authors say does not by itself show that it is harmless.
