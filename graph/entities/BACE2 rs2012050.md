# BACE2 rs2012050

rs2012050 is a common [[BACE2]] SNP associated with BACE2 expression in control cortex (uncorrected p = 0.0425) and with CSF amyloid-beta 1-42 in ADNI mild cognitive impairment; in [[APOE]] ε4 non-carriers the T/T genotype went with lower BACE2 expression and higher CSF Aβ1-42.
