# NOTCH3

NOTCH3 encodes a single-pass transmembrane receptor of the [[Notch signaling]] pathway, activated by delta- and Jagged-type ligands such as [[JAG1]]; its extracellular domain is a string of EGF-like repeats. Coding mutations in it cause [[CADASIL]], an inherited small vessel disease with early strokes and dementia.
