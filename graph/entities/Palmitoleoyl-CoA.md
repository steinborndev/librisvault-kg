# Palmitoleoyl-CoA

Palmitoleoyl-CoA is the coenzyme A thioester of palmitoleic acid (16:1n7), a monounsaturated fatty acid used as an energy source and as a signalling molecule.

## Connections

- A major product of the mouse stearoyl-CoA desaturase-2 ([[SCD]]). Study: [[SCD2 in murine development, metabolism and disease review]]
- Companion product: [[Oleoyl-CoA]]
