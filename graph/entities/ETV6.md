# ETV6

ETV6 encodes an ETS-family transcription factor that acts in blood-cell development and monocyte differentiation and is used as a marker of microglia.

## Connections

- A myeloid-specific differentially active regulator in Brodmann area 10 of Alzheimer prefrontal cortex and validated in an independent multiome set; its predicted pocket scored above 0.7 for druggability and it formed the most stable simulated complexes with several drugs, best with [[Lasmiditan]].
