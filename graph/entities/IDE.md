# IDE

IDE encodes insulin-degrading enzyme, a 110 kDa zinc metallo-endopeptidase that cuts insulin, glucagon, amylin and amyloid-beta, so it ties insulin clearance to [[Amyloid-beta clearance]].

## Connections

- In mouse brain IDE expression fell about 50% with Sirt3 deletion, brain IDE fell with Western diet in Sirt3-knockout and APP/PS1 mice, and it rose after [[Nicotinamide riboside]] (protein 65%, activity 84%) and in [[BV2]] cells, which the authors link to [[SIRT3]] activity. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- Related amyloid-degrading and amyloid-producing enzymes: [[MME]], [[BACE1]]; context: [[Insulin resistance]], [[Metabolic syndrome]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- IDE protein was lowered in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain; [[Magnolol]] at 40 mg/kg (not 20 mg/kg) and [[Donepezil]] raised it (n = 3 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Cited as the Aβ-degrading enzyme whose efficiency is only exceeded by [[BACE2]], which also degrades Aβ. Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
- IDE protein was higher in the hippocampus of 5xFAD mice after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]] (p < 0.001), proposed as the route to fewer Aβ plaques. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
