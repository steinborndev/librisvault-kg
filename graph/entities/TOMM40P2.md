# TOMM40P2

TOMM40P2 is one of the intronless, processed pseudogenes of [[TOMM40]] scattered across the human genome. Its RNA is transcribed in human cell lines; it is a close relative of the unnamed pseudogene P1b, so the two were assayed together as P1b/P2.
