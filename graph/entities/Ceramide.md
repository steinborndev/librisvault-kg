# Ceramide

Ceramides (Cer) are sphingolipids built from a sphingoid base and a fatty acid; they are structural membrane lipids, precursors of [[Sphingomyelin]] and [[Lactosylceramide]], and signalling molecules.

## Connections

- Almost all of 43 plasma ceramide species differed between Alzheimer cases and controls, in opposite directions by structure (higher for dihydroxy species with 18-carbon chains of 0 or 2 double bonds and for 19-carbon species; lower for most monohydroxy and trihydroxy species), while the class sum did not differ.
