# APP p.G676R

The rodent-to-human substitution at residue 676 (APP770 numbering) of [[APP]], glycine to arginine, in the amyloid beta sequence. Modelling places the added positive charge next to glutamate 326 of [[BACE1]], an extra contact that makes the humanized β-CTF a better β-secretase substrate.

## Connections

- Gene: [[APP]]; enzyme: [[BACE1]]
- Introduced with [[APP p.F681Y]] and [[APP p.R684H]] in the [[Humanized App mouse]] and [[Humanized App Psen1 M139T rat]]
- Study: [[Humanized APP knock-in mouse and rat with PSEN1 M139T for Alzheimer disease]]
