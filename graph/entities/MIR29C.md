# MIR29C

MIR29C encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-29c, a small non-coding RNA that represses target messenger RNAs after transcription.

## Connections

- Its interactions with [[BACE1]] and a DNMT3 DNA methyltransferase were confirmed in cerebrospinal fluid or blood samples, and RNA deep sequencing supported it as a circulating marker; one of six miRNAs found in both the vesicle and disease-database lists, though not every study saw a fluid difference.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see also [[MicroRNA-target interaction databases]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- Serum miR-29c-3p was reduced at every time point after intrahippocampal Aβ1-42 infusion in rats (most at day 7) and in 15-month-old APPswe-PS1dE9 mice; sister gene [[MIR29A]]. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
