# GRIN2C

GRIN2C encodes GluN2C, a 1,233-residue subunit of the heterotetrameric NMDA-type glutamate receptor that assembles with [[GRIN1]]. GluN2C-containing receptors have distinct biophysical properties and are much less abundant in brain than those with [[GRIN2A]] or [[GRIN2B]].

## Connections

- A rare missense variant, [[GRIN2C p.A1072V]], segregated with late-onset Alzheimer disease in an Italian family, raised NMDA currents and surface expression in rat hippocampal neurons and reduced colocalization with [[14-3-3 proteins]].
