# ST18

ST18 encodes a zinc-finger transcription factor of the myelin transcription factor family, expressed in oligodendrocytes and neurons.

## Connections

- A neuronal regulator with lower activity in Alzheimer prefrontal cortex, ranked among the top transcription factors by an independent enrichment check (ChEA3) and in the validation cohort; one of five regulators docked and simulated.
