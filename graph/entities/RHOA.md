# RHOA

RHOA encodes RhoA, a small GTPase that signals through ROCK kinases to regulate the cytoskeleton.

## Connections

- Phosphorylation of RhoA at Ser188, triggered by soluble [[TREM2]] binding [[TAGLN2]] on cultured mouse neurons, inactivated the RhoA-ROCK axis and so [[GSK3B]]; in tau P301S mice TREM2 overexpression or soluble TREM2 lowered tau phosphorylation and cognitive and behavioural deficits. See [[TREM2 ectodomain shedding]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
