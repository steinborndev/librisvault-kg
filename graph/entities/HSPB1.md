# HSPB1

HSPB1 encodes a small heat shock protein that acts as a molecular chaperone, helping unfolded proteins refold and limiting their aggregation.

## Connections

- [[LM-031]] and [[Licochalcone A]] (1 μM) raised HSPB1 protein in [[MAPT p.K280del]] TauRD-DsRed [[SH-SY5Y]] cells (88% to 112-113%) and the soluble TauRD level, with no change in the TauRD RNA, so the chaperone is proposed as the route by which the compounds reduce [[Tau aggregation]]. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- The authors cite earlier work in which HSPB1 overexpression eased symptoms in APP/PS1 mice and protected against Aβ and Tau toxicity.
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]; related chaperones: [[HSPA1A]], [[HSPA2]]
