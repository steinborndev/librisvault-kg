# HMGCR

HMGCR encodes 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl-CoA reductase, the rate-limiting enzyme of intracellular cholesterol synthesis and the binding target of [[Statin]] drugs.

## Connections

- Variant: [[HMGCR rs3846662]], monomorphic (all AA) in a Brazilian Alzheimer disease cohort and therefore not testable. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Drugs that inhibit it: [[Atorvastatin]], [[Simvastatin]]
- Related genes: [[LDLR]], [[NR1H2]], [[ACE]]
- Expression fell in [[CCF-STTG1]] astrocytoma cells treated with brown seaweed lipid extracts, [[T0901317]] or [[Bexarotene]], alongside other cholesterol synthesis genes such as [[SREBF2]]. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
