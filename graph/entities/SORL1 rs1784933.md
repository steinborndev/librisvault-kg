# SORL1 rs1784933

rs1784933 is a [[SORL1]] SNP that an earlier study in Taiwan associated with risk of [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Mild cognitive impairment]] in Han Chinese people.

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- No association with the midpoint age or the rate of cognitive decline in 299 Han Chinese patients; the SNP was imputed in the ADNI replication sample. Study: [[Genetic effects on cognitive decline trajectories in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
