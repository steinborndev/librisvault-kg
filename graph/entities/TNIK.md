# TNIK

TNIK encodes TRAF2 and NCK interacting kinase, a serine/threonine kinase enriched at synapses that acts in Wnt signalling and cytoskeletal regulation.

## Connections

- Its protein was among the detergent-insoluble species recovered from demented amygdala and was seen in inclusion body-like structures; rare variants in the gene showed a nominal association with Alzheimer-type dementia risk.
