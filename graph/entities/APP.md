# APP

APP encodes the amyloid precursor protein, whose cleavage by beta- and gamma-secretases yields the amyloid beta peptides that form plaques in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Amyloid beta 1-42 monomer and oligomer were two of the protein markers on the nanoparticle kit; the oligomer level was high in all four patient plasma samples that were read as early or severe disease, and it was strongly stained in the brains of the APP/PS/ApoE knockdown mouse model.
- Processing: cleavage by [[ADAM10]] (α-secretase) gives sAPPα and precludes Aβ; see [[Amyloid-beta formation]]. In cells expressing Swedish-mutant APP ([[HEK293]] 293sw) and in APPsw/PS1dE9 mouse astrocytes and hippocampus, [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]] raised sAPPα and lowered secreted Aβ.
- Endosomal handling: in [[Tg2576 mouse]] mice, which carry Swedish-mutant human APP, synaptic APP and [[BACE1]] were raised in hippocampus and retina and were lowered by the tau antibody [[12A12 monoclonal antibody]], alongside shifts in [[BIN1]], [[RIN3]] and [[RAB5A]]; see [[Endocytosis]].
- Studies: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]], [[DAPG lowers beta-amyloid secretion in Alzheimer disease models]], [[Tau antibody 12A12 and amyloid-beta in Tg2576 mouse hippocampus and retina]]
- No pathogenic APP mutation was found in 96 early-onset [[Small vessel ischemic disease]] patients; amyloid beta oligomers appeared in neurons and reactive astrocytes of hypoperfused mouse hippocampus within 7 days (see [[BCCAS mouse model]]). Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- The common intronic variant [[APP rs2154481]] lowers the risk of sporadic [[Alzheimer disease]], which the authors take as support for APP production or processing as a causal pathway beyond familial disease. Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
- Target of [[MIR193B]]: the interaction was validated in an Alzheimer mouse model, in patient samples and in vesicles, and is one of seven body-fluid miRNA-target pairs highlighted as biomarker candidates. Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- Binding of APP by the [[SORL1]]-retromer complex speeds its exit from the early endosome and limits amyloid beta formation; the study on rare SORL1 variants excluded carriers of pathogenic APP variants. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Named, with [[PSEN1]] and [[PSEN2]], among very rare highly penetrant mutations whose effect a polygenic score masks; they are added to a disease probability as an intrinsic probability (supplementary curves). Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- CSF amyloid-beta 40 was higher in people with more enlarged perivascular spaces in both the basal ganglia and the centrum semiovale, regardless of amyloid status, which the authors take as a sign of altered CSF clearance; amyloid positivity was defined as a CSF Aβ42/40 ratio below 0.071. Study: [[Perivascular spaces and CSF biomarkers in cognitively unimpaired Alzheimer continuum]]
- Two rare variants in one Korean patient with early-onset disease: [[APP p.V551M]] in the E2 ectodomain (outside the secretase cleavage sites, predicted to alter dimerisation) and [[PSEN2 p.H169N]]. Study: [[Double APP and PSEN2 mutations in a Korean patient with early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Cleavage into the APP intracellular domain was reduced by δ-opioid receptor ([[OPRD1]]) activation and increased by μ-opioid receptor ([[OPRM1]]) activation, tracking [[BACE1]] changes; Aβ42 fell with DOR agonist and rose with [[DAMGO]] in APPswe [[SH-SY5Y]] cells. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
- Higher in CD14+ monocytes of Alzheimer blood and higher in blood mononuclear cells after exercise; kept with [[TNF]], [[CCR1]], [[ATF3]], [[KLF4]], [[HES4]] and [[MAFB]] as the ERADMT panel, with negative correlation to [[ATF3]] and [[HES4]] in a 204-patient blood cohort. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
- Lentiviral APP overexpression in human neuroblastoma [[BE2-M17]] cells lowered viability and raised [[Apoptosis]]; [[Osthol]] conjugated to [[Zinc oxide]] nanoparticles reversed both and restored synaptic proteins. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
- Reviewed as regulated by [[SIRT1]], which favours non-amyloidogenic processing. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- One Taiwanese woman with onset at 50 carried [[APP p.D678H]] in exon 16, the commonest APP mutation in Taiwan by the authors' count. Study: [[Familial Alzheimer disease mutations in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
- Rodent APP differs from human at three residues of the Aβ sequence ([[APP p.G676R]], [[APP p.F681Y]], [[APP p.R684H]]); replacing them in the endogenous mouse and rat genes made β-CTF rise 2.5 to 4.2-fold and Aβ40 about 2.3 to 2.8-fold, so human APP is the better BACE1 substrate. See [[Humanized App mouse]] and [[Humanized App Psen1 M139T rat]]. Study: [[Humanized APP knock-in mouse and rat with PSEN1 M139T for Alzheimer disease]]
- Aβ1-42 in brain and in plasma exosomes of [[FAD4T mouse]] animals fell after BACE1 silencing with [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]], and microglia took up more labelled Aβ in culture. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Stress raised APP and Aβ in animals (even after one hour of restraint); Aβ crosses the blood-brain barrier via LRP-1 and RAGE, and RAGE-linked Aβ toxicity damages the [[Tight junction]]. See [[Psychological stress]]. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- In isogenic hiPSC neural cultures with an APP duplication or [[APP p.V717I]], the APOE2 isoform lowered secreted Aβ without changing total APP, with a lower sAPPβ/sAPPα ratio and βCTF level, indicating a shift away from amyloidogenic cleavage. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]
- One of three excitatory-neuron differentially expressed genes (with [[TKT]] and [[GABRA1]]) that are targets of drugs in Alzheimer trials. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Aβ produced from endocytosed APP accumulates in endosomes and multivesicular bodies and impairs the [[Synaptic vesicle cycle]] and glutamate receptor trafficking; APP at the synapse may serve as a receptor for extracellular Aβ, since APP knockout neurons resist exogenous Aβ toxicity. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Soluble Aβ40 at 100 nM, not Aβ42, lowered the Vmax of serotonin uptake by about 20% in wild-type mouse brain membranes, and the Aβ40 share of soluble Aβ rose in 20-month-old [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] cortex when uptake fell; see [[SLC6A4]]. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
- Isoform switch in Alzheimer temporal cortex without a change in total expression: two isoforms lacking exon 7 (the Kunitz protease inhibitor domain) fell and two rose; the authors infer more KPI-containing APP in neurons, and note that lower [[RBFOX1]] has been tied to exon 7 inclusion. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
- Selective inhibition of its BACE1 cleavage by [[FAH65]] lowered sAPPβ and Aβ1-42 in [[CHO]] cells and brain βCTF in mice, and raised sAPPα (more substrate for α-secretase); the protective variant [[APP p.A673T]] sits at the BACE1 cleavage site. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Appeared as a neuronal regulator-level hub: lower inferred activity in Alzheimer prefrontal neurons, the central gene of the gene-disease network, and one of five regulators docked and simulated against approved drugs; [[Vorapaxar]] bound it most strongly (-8.489 kcal/mol) but formed few hydrogen bonds. Study: [[Lasmiditan prioritised for Alzheimer disease by regulator docking and dynamics]]
- Rare variant [[APP p.V604M]] (exon 14, outside the amyloid beta region) was found in a Thai early-onset patient with progressive non-fluent aphasia; the authors suggest a mechanism other than faster fibril formation. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- Reviewed with [[ITM2B]] (BRI2) as a potential regulator of APP processing that can inhibit Aβ production and fibrillation; AβPP mutations underlie familial Alzheimer disease. Study: [[BRI2 in familial British and Danish dementia and Alzheimer disease review]]
- Reviewed as a synaptic transmembrane protein that regulates iron export, plasticity and synapse formation; sequential cleavage by [[BACE1]] and gamma-secretase gives Aβ40 and Aβ42, sAPPβ and the intracellular domain, while [[ADAM10]] cleavage inside the Aβ sequence gives sAPPα and C83 and spares Aβ. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Binding partner [[TMCC2]]: in non-demented human temporal cortex the two stained almost identically; in Alzheimer brain APP-positive dystrophic neurites around plaques co-stained for TMCC2 in late-onset cases but often not in early-onset cases with APP Val717 mutations ([[APP p.V717I]], [[APP p.V717G]]) or Down syndrome. Study: [[TMCC2 in dense-core plaques and Down syndrome pathology in Alzheimer brain]]
- 3xTg-AD mice carrying APP Swedish had more 6E10-positive plaques after hippocampal [[PTK2]] overexpression; CDK5 activation downstream of FAK is proposed to raise APP expression. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Cholesterol binds APP and its cholesterol-binding site shapes processing to less toxic amyloid-beta; APP is prominent in Alzheimer white matter. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Palmitoylation of APP was 47.6% higher in hippocampus of mice carrying [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]]; the authors propose that palmitoylated APP sits in lipid rafts and is a better [[BACE1]] substrate, raising Aβ before secretase cleavage. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- The human APP C99 fragment is the substrate in the FRET sensors that read gamma-secretase activity per neuron; in a low-activity subpopulation of wild-type mouse neurons cleavage left C99/C-terminal fragments and favoured Aβ42 over Aβ38, and the authors suggest C99 or the YENPTY motif links inefficient cleavage to endo-lysosomal swelling. See [[FRET biosensors of gamma-secretase activity]]. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Expression per cell rose in most cell types of aged cynomolgus monkey entorhinal cortex, while the fraction of APP-positive cells did not change; more neurons than other cells expressed it. Study: [[Single-nucleus atlas of aging monkey entorhinal cortex and Alzheimer disease]]
- Mapped to chromosome 21 in 1987 (three copies cause [[Down syndrome]], with early amyloid plaques); the first family with APP mutations was reported in 1991, followed by missense alleles, whole-gene duplications and regulatory changes that raise expression, pointing to a gain of function. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- The promoter peak was hypoacetylated in female hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load (log2 fold change -0.38, FDR 0.045), the nearest peak to the transcription start was oligodendrocyte-enriched, and two peaks lost acetylation with age in microglia. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- The Aβ part of the gene product, expressed as a secreted tandem Aβ42 in fly neurons, shortened life by 50%, shrank the eye by about 50% and lowered climbing, sleep and memory; the [[DYRK1A]] inhibitor [[PST-001]] gave partial rescue of eye, life and climbing and restored memory but not sleep. The paper notes DYRK1A can phosphorylate APP. Study: [[PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome]]
- Its mRNA was 2 to 6 times higher in mouse stomach, intestine and colon than in brain at 3, 6 and 12 months; colonic APP protein trended up with age (P = 0.19 in wild type) and rose significantly in 12-month-old [[APP-PS1 mouse]] colon (P < 0.01), and gut-derived Aβ ([[Gut-brain axis]]) is proposed to feed brain pathology. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Triplication of APP on chromosome 21 is the likely cause of accelerated amyloid deposition and Alzheimer pathology by age 40 in [[Down syndrome]], the background for a complement biomarker study in that group. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
- [[TgCRND8 mouse]] animals carry human APP with the Swedish and Indiana mutations; brain Aβ40, Aβ42 and phosphorylated APP (Thr668) were raised and fell with [[Magnolol]] or [[Donepezil]]. Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Cleaved by [[BACE2]] at a θ-secretase site (Phe) within the Aβ domain, which prevents Aβ formation, in addition to weak β-site and α-like cleavage. Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
- The Swedish/Indiana double mutant (APP Swe/Ind) expressed in [[SH-SY5Y]] cells caused mitochondrial ROS rise, ATP loss and damaged mitochondria that [[ALT001]] reversed; see [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- The Uppsala deletion [[APP p.690_695del]] (six residues of amyloid-beta) was found in two siblings and a cousin with aggressive early-onset disease in a Swedish memory-clinic screen. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
- APP mRNA and protein fell in SH-SY5Y cells overexpressing wild-type [[ADAM17]] but not the maturation-defective [[ADAM17 p.R215I]], and APP mRNA correlated inversely with ADAM17 mRNA in human cortex; a 6-day TAPI-0 treatment restored APP expression. Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
