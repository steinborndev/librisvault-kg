# FABP3

FABP3 encodes fatty acid binding protein 3, a small cytosolic protein that carries long-chain fatty acids inside cells and is released into cerebrospinal fluid.

## Connections

- It was the strongest member of the six-protein microglial activity panel: up by more than 50% in CSF of symptomatic GRN mutation carriers, up in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia lysate and medium, and alone separating carriers from controls with an AUC of 0.93. In an Alzheimer cohort it was higher in [[Mild cognitive impairment]] and [[Alzheimer disease]], higher in amyloid-positive than amyloid-negative mild cognitive impairment, and reached an AUC of 0.92 for amyloid-positive Alzheimer disease versus amyloid-negative controls. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
