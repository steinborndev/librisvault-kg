# SERPINA7

SERPINA7 encodes thyroxine-binding globulin, the main plasma carrier of thyroid hormone.

## Connections

- It was one of five proteins added to the universal panel because L1-regularization repeatedly selected it for separating progressing from stable mild cognitive impairment, rather than for separating Alzheimer disease from controls.
