# Resveratrol

A polyphenol of red wine, grapes and berries and the best known activator of [[SIRT1]].

## Connections

- Reviewed as neuroprotective in Alzheimer models: it favours non-amyloidogenic [[APP]] processing, induces autophagy dependent on SIRT1, PARP1 and tyrosyl-tRNA synthetase, reverses Aβ-induced memory and synaptic deficits in rats, and lowers tau phosphorylation. A phase II placebo-controlled trial in mild-to-moderate [[Alzheimer disease]] found it crosses the blood-brain barrier and modulates the central immune response.
