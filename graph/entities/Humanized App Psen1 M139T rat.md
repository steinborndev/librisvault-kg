# Humanized App Psen1 M139T rat

A Long Evans rat with the human amyloid beta sequence in endogenous App and, in the double line, [[PSEN1 p.M139T]] in endogenous Psen1. Humanization raises β-CTF about 4.2-fold and Aβ40 about 2.8-fold; the Psen1 mutation adds a shift toward Aβ42 and away from Aβ43, but no plaques or tangles formed by 2 years.
