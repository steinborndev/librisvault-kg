# CLEC7A

CLEC7A encodes Dectin-1, a C-type lectin receptor on myeloid cells; its induction is used as a phagocytic marker of activated microglia.

## Connections

- Induced in APP/PS1 brain by amyloid pathology without further change on Sirt3 deletion; the authors read it as a protective microglial response. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
