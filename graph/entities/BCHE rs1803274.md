# BCHE rs1803274

The K variant of [[BCHE]] (c.1699G>A, p.Ala539Thr in the mature-protein numbering, p.A567T counting the signal peptide) is the most frequent coding variant of butyrylcholinesterase, in the tetramerization domain; it has long been tested for joint risk with [[APOE]] ε4 in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[BCHE]]
- Occurs in cis with [[BCHE rs1126680]] and [[BCHE rs55781031]]; alone it was carried by about one third of both patients and controls, and only the triple combination with ε4 was specific to patients. Study: [[BCHE noncoding variants and APOE ε4 in Polish late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
