# TMCC2

TMCC2 encodes an endomembrane protein (709 amino acids) that binds apolipoprotein E and forms a conserved complex with [[APP]]; its Drosophila orthologue, dementin, causes Alzheimer-like neurodegeneration when mutated. It sits on chromosome 1q32.1, so it is not triplicated in [[Down syndrome]].

## Connections

- In human temporal cortex TMCC2 staining overlapped almost completely with APP in non-demented brain, filled the core of [[Dense-core amyloid plaque]] in [[Alzheimer disease]], and co-stained with APP in the [[Dystrophic neurite]] around the core, more often in APOE3 than APOE4 homozygotes and rarely in early-onset disease. Down syndrome brain also showed thread-like TMCC2-positive, amyloid-dye-positive lesions. Western blots resolved three TMCC2 bands whose ratio differed between cerebellum and temporal gyrus and with [[APOE]] genotype.
