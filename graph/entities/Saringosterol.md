# Saringosterol

Saringosterol is an oxidised phytosterol made from [[Fucosterol]] in brown seaweed; it activates liver X receptors, crosses the blood-brain barrier and, as the 24(S) form, prevented cognitive decline in Alzheimer mouse models.

## Connections

- All six European seaweeds contained it; [[Himanthalia elongata]] extract matched [[Sargassum fusiforme]] in concentration, and extract doses were normalised to it. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Targets: [[NR1H3]], [[NR1H2]]
