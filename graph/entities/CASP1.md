# CASP1

CASP1 encodes caspase-1, the protease activated by the [[NLRP3]] inflammasome that matures [[IL1B]].

## Connections

- Caspase-1 was lowered by ajugol and isoacteoside in the hippocampus of model mice. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Inflammasome partners: [[NLRP3]], [[IL1B]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
