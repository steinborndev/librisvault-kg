# SORL1 rs3737529

rs3737529 is a [[SORL1]] SNP reported as one of the most significant SORL1 signals in Asian populations; its T allele is the one studied for protection against cognitive decline.

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- Each T allele delayed the midpoint age of cognitive decline by about 2.6 years and the SNP was linked to the rate of decline in 299 Han Chinese Alzheimer and MCI patients; with APOE it formed the best-fitting model and the authors judge the effect independent of APOE genotype. It did not replicate in ADNI, where the SNP was imputed. Study: [[Genetic effects on cognitive decline trajectories in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
- Other SORL1 SNPs tested in the same cohort: [[SORL1 rs1784933]], [[SORL1 rs2298813]]
