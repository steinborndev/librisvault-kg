# TOMM40 rs2075650

rs2075650 is a single nucleotide polymorphism in [[TOMM40]] on chromosome 19, next to [[APOE]]; its G allele is the risk allele for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[TOMM40]]
- Neighbouring variants at the locus: [[APOE rs429358]], [[APOE rs769449]], [[NECTIN2 rs6857]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- TOMM40 RNA level in postmortem frontal lobe did not differ between carriers and non-carriers of the G allele, so this risk SNP does not appear to act through brain TOMM40 expression; see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
- Lies in the TOMM40 intron 2 to 3 region that loops to the APOE promoter in glia-like cell lines and carries an enhancer mark; the authors suggest the allele may alter contact strength and APOE expression, untested. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
