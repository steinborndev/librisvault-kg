# MIR613

MIR613 encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-613, a small non-coding RNA that represses target messenger RNAs after transcription.

## Connections

- Its partner [[BDNF]] was detected with it in serum and cerebrospinal fluid of Alzheimer patients and in Alzheimer mouse models, making the pair one of seven body-fluid interactions highlighted as strong biomarker candidates.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see also [[MicroRNA-target interaction databases]]
