# LRRK2 p.N551K

A missense change from asparagine to lysine at residue 551 of [[LRRK2]], linked to [[Parkinson disease]] in Chinese patients and not thought to change Alzheimer disease risk.

## Connections

- Gene: [[LRRK2]]; disease: [[Parkinson disease]]
- Found in a 51-year-old Thai patient (#4) with early-onset Alzheimer disease. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
