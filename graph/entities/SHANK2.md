# SHANK2

SHANK2 encodes Shank2, a multidomain scaffold of the postsynaptic density that couples glutamate receptors to the actin cytoskeleton.

## Connections

- Shank2 protein was increased in Alzheimer brain, opposite to Shank3. Study: [[Shank proteins and glutamate receptors at the Alzheimer postsynaptic density]]
