# HeLa

HeLa is a human cervical carcinoma cell line used as a general non-neuronal reference line.

## Connections

- Stained positive for [[IQCK]] in nucleus and cytoplasm, showing the protein is not neuron-specific. Study: [[IQCK protein is raised in Alzheimer hippocampus and found in amyloid plaques]]
- Compared in the same experiment with [[SH-SY5Y]] (positive), [[HMC3]] and [[CHO]] (negative).
- ATG7 knockout HeLa cells expressing Parkin and PINK1-knockdown cells showed that [[ALT001]] mitophagy bypasses the canonical pathway, while organelle markers in HeLa-Parkin cells showed it spares ER, Golgi and peroxisomes. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
