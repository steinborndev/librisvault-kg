# Type 2 diabetes mellitus

Type 2 diabetes mellitus is a chronic disorder of insulin resistance and raised blood glucose, and a recognised risk factor for Alzheimer disease.

## Connections

- Reviewed as injuring later-myelinating white matter, interacting with [[APOE]] ε4, and lowering LRP1 while raising RAGE in a rodent model. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Related: [[LRP1]], [[AGER]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Streptozocin-induced [[Hyperglycemia]] was used to accelerate Alzheimer phenotypes in [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals, citing human data that hyperglycemia drives progression in mild cognitive impairment. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- ANKDD1B, prioritised at an early-onset Alzheimer disease and cholesterol covariance region, has been associated with type 2 diabetes in major GWAS. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]; gene: [[ANKDD1B]]
