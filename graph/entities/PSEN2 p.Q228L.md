# PSEN2 p.Q228L

A missense change from glutamine to leucine at residue 228 of [[PSEN2]], in the fifth transmembrane helix. It was reported in a 60-year-old Polish patient with cognitive impairment, personality change and insomnia whose mother had late-onset Alzheimer disease; it was absent from 100 Polish controls and 100 Polish sporadic cases, is rare in gnomAD, and segregation was not tested.
