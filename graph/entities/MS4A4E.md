# MS4A4E

MS4A4E is a member of the membrane-spanning 4-domains family in the chromosome 11q12 cluster that also holds [[MS4A6A]]; it is a recognised Alzheimer risk gene.

## Connections

- The gene prioritised in the early-onset Alzheimer disease GWAS at the chromosome 11q12.2 locus ([[MS4A4E rs11824734]]) that is also associated with total cholesterol; the nearest gene to the SNP is MS4A6E. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
- Same family and locus as [[MS4A6A]], the top-scoring gene in the covariance region there.
- Disease: [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
