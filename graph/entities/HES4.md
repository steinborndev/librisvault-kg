# HES4

HES4 encodes a basic helix-loop-helix transcription factor of the Hes family, downstream of NOTCH signalling.

## Connections

- Differentially expressed in Alzheimer blood monocytes, with [[MAFB]] and [[FOSL1]] defining the M4 transcription factor module; in blood expression from 204 patients and 530 controls it correlated negatively with [[APP]]. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
