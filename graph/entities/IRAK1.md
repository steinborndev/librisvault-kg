# IRAK1

IRAK1 encodes interleukin-1 receptor-associated kinase 1, an adaptor kinase downstream of Toll-like and interleukin-1 receptors that passes the signal to [[TRAF6]] and the NF-κB pathway; its mRNA carries a binding site for [[MIR146A]].

## Connections
