# CD33

CD33 encodes a sialic-acid-binding immunoglobulin-like lectin expressed on myeloid cells and is a genome-wide-association risk gene for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Linked to [[APOE]] in the protein interaction network of genes tested
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- [[CD33 p.A14V]] was associated with lower risk of [[Small vessel ischemic disease]], and mouse Cd33 rose up to 7-fold in hypoperfused brain; the exon 2-lacking isoform tied to this variant is thought to relieve CD33 inhibition of [[TREM2]]. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- CD33 expression correlated negatively with cortical glucose metabolism (r = -0.700, z = -2.208), third most extreme of 20,736 genes tested, and was high in parahippocampal and entorhinal cortex; the correlation strengthened as risk rose. The authors cite earlier reports of a 5-fold rise of CD33 mRNA in patient brain and a risk allele raising monocyte CD33 surface expression 7-fold, and note no causal link can be drawn from the correlation. Study: [[Brain metabolism and Alzheimer risk gene expression in normal elderly]]
- Allele-specific effects of the GWAS SNP [[CD33 rs3865444]] were restricted to exon 2 in monocytes and in temporal, posterior cingulate and caudate cortex, supporting an exon 2 splicing effect in microglia; only two of seven transcripts overlapping exon 2 were affected. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- One of five GWAS-list genes that are also targets of drugs in phase 1 to 3 Alzheimer trials, with [[TREM2]], [[CHRNA2]], [[PRSS8]] and [[ACE]]. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- The CD33 risk allele led to greater [[TREM2]] expression in monocytes from 226 people, and CD33 suppression decreased TREM2. Study: [[CD33 modulates TREM2 in a monocyte protein QTL analysis of Alzheimer loci]]
- CD33 mRNA rose 1.7-fold after amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture and 2.8-fold in those kept 16 days, matching their poor phagocytosis; the authors recall that CD33-reactive microglia track insoluble Aβ42 and plaque burden, and that alleles inhibiting CD33 lower Alzheimer risk while alleles inhibiting [[TREM2]] raise it. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Cd33 knockout in [[5xFAD mouse]] animals lowered amyloid-beta pathology and improved cognition, and raised microglial genes for [[Phagocytosis]], IL-6 and IL-8 signalling and the [[Acute-phase response]]; all of this was lost when Trem2 was also knocked out, so CD33 acts upstream of [[TREM2]]. Study: [[TREM2 acts downstream of CD33 in 5xFAD mouse microglia]]
- [[CD33 rs3865444]] showed no association with the age or rate of cognitive decline in Han Chinese Alzheimer and MCI patients. Study: [[Genetic effects on cognitive decline trajectories in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
