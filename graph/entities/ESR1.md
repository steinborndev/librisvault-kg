# ESR1

ESR1 encodes estrogen receptor alpha, a ligand-activated nuclear receptor and one of the two main targets of menopausal hormone therapy.

## Connections

- In postmortem hippocampus, ESR1 was slightly higher (logFC +0.13, nominal p = 0.047) in men with Alzheimer disease than in women with it in the CA1 subfield, not in CA3; the authors read it as sex-specific modulation of estrogen receptor expression. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
- In a protein interaction network of Alzheimer susceptibility genes ESR1 linked to 19 genes including [[TP53]], [[MAPK1]], [[IGF1]], [[AKT1]], [[CD44]], [[EGFR]] and [[ESR2]]; it also touches [[GPER1]]. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
- Pathway: [[Estrogen signaling pathway]]; treatment: [[Menopausal hormone therapy]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; topic: [[Sex differences in Alzheimer disease]]
- Degree 22 in the interaction network of 34 targets shared by at least 5 heat-clearing herbs and Alzheimer disease; ESR-mediated and extranuclear estrogen signaling were among the five top Reactome pathways of those targets.
