# Ts65Dn mouse

Ts65Dn is a segmentally trisomic mouse line that carries an extra copy of a region of mouse chromosome 16 syntenic to part of human chromosome 21. It is a standard model of [[Down syndrome]].

## Connections

- Two chromosome 21 microRNAs, [[MIR155]] and [[MIR802]], are triplicated and overexpressed in its brain; lentiviral sponges injected into the hippocampus lowered them and rescued [[MECP2]], Ship1 ([[INPP5D]]) and [[FOXM1]]. Study: [[miR-155 and miR-802 targets in the hippocampus of Ts65Dn Down syndrome mice]]
- Method: [[Lentiviral miRNA sponge]]
- Disease modelled: [[Down syndrome]]
