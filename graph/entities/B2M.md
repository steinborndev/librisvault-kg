# B2M

B2M encodes beta-2-microglobulin, the light chain of the MHC class I molecule, which accumulates during inflammation.

## Connections

- Upregulated in microglia of aged cynomolgus monkey entorhinal cortex, read by the authors as raised inflammation. Study: [[Single-nucleus atlas of aging monkey entorhinal cortex and Alzheimer disease]]
- Related: [[Neuroinflammation]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
