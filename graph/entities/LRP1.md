# LRP1

LRP1 encodes a large endocytic receptor that binds [[APOE]]-bearing lipoproteins and is proposed to take up amyloid-beta into astrocytes.

## Connections

- Surface LRP1 on hiPSC-derived astrocytes did not differ between APOE2 and APOE3 isogenic pairs, and its level correlated modestly and positively with amyloid-beta uptake only by mutation type or disease status, not by APOE genotype. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]
- Related receptor: [[LDLR]]; process: [[Amyloid-beta clearance]]
- Binds ApoE-Aβ complexes for endocytosis at synapses; postsynaptically its interaction with PSD-95 is released on NMDA activation and it regulates NMDA receptor endocytosis. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Cholesterol is proposed to down-regulate LRP1 at the blood-brain barrier, lowering amyloid-beta and degraded myelin basic protein efflux and impairing oligodendrocyte precursor survival and remyelination; counterpart receptor [[AGER]]. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
