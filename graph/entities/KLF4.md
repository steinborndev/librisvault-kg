# KLF4

KLF4 encodes a zinc-finger transcription factor that controls cell differentiation, including monocyte fate.

## Connections

- Differentially expressed after exercise and in the M4 module of Alzheimer blood monocytes; its regulon made it the positive regulator of [[CCR1]]. The authors write the symbol as KFL4 in places and cite a recent report tying it to dementia onset. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
