# 24S-Hydroxycholesterol

24S-Hydroxycholesterol is the oxysterol made by [[CYP46A1]] that carries cholesterol out of the brain across the blood-brain barrier.

## Connections

- CSF levels are higher in Alzheimer disease than controls, and raising it (with CYP46A1) improved cognitive deficits in THY-Tau22 tau mice. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Related: [[27-Hydroxycholesterol]], [[Cholesterol efflux]]
