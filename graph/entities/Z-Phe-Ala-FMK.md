# Z-Phe-Ala-FMK

Z-Phe-Ala-FMK is a fluoromethyl-ketone inhibitor used here as a cathepsin B inhibitor.

## Connections

- At 20 µM overnight it raised ApoE in the supernatant of wild-type but not P2X4-null macrophages, whereas specific cathepsin L and S inhibitors had no effect (data not shown), pointing to [[CTSB]] as the protease acting downstream of [[P2RX4]]. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
