# Isoacteoside

Isoacteoside is a phenylethanoid glycoside present in the processed root of Rehmannia glutinosa ([[Rehmanniae radix and Rehmanniae radix praeparata]]); the aqueous extract contained about 0.03 mg/g.

## Connections

- Found in mouse brain after the processed root; imaging mass spectrometry located it in hippocampus. It raised [[SQSTM1]] and lowered [[MTOR]] phosphorylation and [[MAP1LC3B]] in model mice and [[BV2]] cells, and docked strongly to [[TLR4]], [[NFKBIA]] and [[NLRP3]]. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Counterpart compound: [[Ajugol]]
- Process: [[Autophagy]]; disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
