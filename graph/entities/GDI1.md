# GDI1

GDI1 encodes GDP dissociation inhibitor 1, which keeps Rab GTPases in an inactive cytosolic pool and recycles them between membranes.

## Connections

- Up in CSF of symptomatic GRN mutation carriers and in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia medium. In an Alzheimer cohort it was higher in [[Mild cognitive impairment]] and [[Alzheimer disease]], separated amyloid-positive from amyloid-negative mild cognitive impairment, and reached an AUC of 0.90 for amyloid-positive Alzheimer disease versus amyloid-negative controls. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
