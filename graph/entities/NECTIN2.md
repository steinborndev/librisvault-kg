# NECTIN2

NECTIN2 (formerly PVRL2) encodes nectin cell adhesion molecule 2, a plasma membrane adhesion glycoprotein and herpesvirus entry receptor that lies within the chromosome 19 locus around [[APOE]] and [[TOMM40]].

## Connections

- Variant tested: [[NECTIN2 rs6857]]
- Neighbouring genes at the same locus: [[APOE]], [[TOMM40]], [[APOC1]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- Two minor haplotypes (alpha, beta) of the NECTIN2 (PVRL2) region raised risk independently of APOE ε4 and ε2, and lowered blood NECTIN2 transcript; the alpha haplotype was linked to worse memory, smaller hippocampi ([[Hippocampal atrophy]]) and lower plasma Aβ1-42 and cerebrospinal fluid [[ICAM1]]. Variants: [[NECTIN2 rs6859]], [[NECTIN2 rs11668861]], [[NECTIN2 rs519113]], [[NECTIN2 rs60389450]]; predicted regulator [[MIR595]].
