# LAMC3

LAMC3 encodes the laminin gamma-3 chain of basement-membrane laminins, which several experimental studies connect to Alzheimer disease; the gene has been associated with age at onset.

## Connections

- Three LAMC3 variants occurred in the same 7 Alzheimer cases and no controls. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
