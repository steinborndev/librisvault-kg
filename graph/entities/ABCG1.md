# ABCG1

ABCG1 encodes a membrane transporter that exports cholesterol to lipidated acceptor particles and is induced by liver X receptors.

## Connections

- Expression rose in [[CCF-STTG1]] astrocytoma cells after treatment with each of the seaweed lipid extracts. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Process: [[Cholesterol efflux]]
- Partner genes: [[ABCA1]], [[APOE]]
- Cholesterol transport to CSF through ABCG1 is reported 33% lower in Alzheimer disease. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
