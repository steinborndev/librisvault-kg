# ADAM9

ADAM9 encodes a membrane metalloprotease that, with [[ADAM10]] and [[ADAM17]], shows alpha-secretase activity on [[APP]].

## Connections

- Its mRNA correlated only very weakly and negatively with APP mRNA in human prefrontal and temporal cortex, unlike the strong inverse correlation of ADAM17. Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
