# PICALM

PICALM encodes a clathrin assembly protein that drives endocytosis and is a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- In microglia, the GWAS SNP rs867611 was among the top genotype-level cis-eQTL associations for PICALM (p = 9E-4), preserved after adjusting for diagnosis. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- Contributed to the 'amyloid' cluster of Alzheimer risk variants (lower CSF amyloid-beta 42 with higher phosphorylated tau), together with [[BIN1]], [[ABCA7]] and [[CLU]]. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Encodes the clathrin adaptor CALM (paralog of AP180): sorts VAMP2 and recaptures synaptophysin and synaptotagmin during vesicle recycling, and modulates GluA2 endocytosis postsynaptically. See [[Synaptic vesicle cycle]]. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- The protective variant [[PICALM rs3851179]] interacted with sex on 30 correlated plasma proteins, five long-chain fatty acids and [[Docosahexaenoic acid]] in healthy adults, and lowered Alzheimer risk more strongly in men in a consortium case-control meta-analysis. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Sentinel SNPs [[PICALM rs10792832]] and [[PICALM rs3851179]] overlap microglia and oligodendrocyte-enriched peaks; promoter hypoacetylated in hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load (-0.40) and transcript lower in high-amyloid hippocampal Olig2-positive nuclei (g 1.235, q = 0.027). Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Its CpG island shore was less methylated in Alzheimer blood, independent of [[PICALM rs3851179]] genotype, age and Mini-Mental State Examination score; in matched blood and brain data only one CpG correlated (entorhinal cortex). Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
- More than 30% of plasma [[Cholesteryl ester]] species associated differently with the PICALM risk SNP in Alzheimer cases than in controls, together with [[SLC24A4]] and [[SORL1]] (nominal, not surviving correction). Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
