# P2RX4

P2RX4 encodes the P2X4 receptor, an ATP-gated, calcium-permeable cation channel that sits mostly in lysosomes and is switched on de novo in a subset of reactive microglia. Mouse work is on the human orthologue's page.

## Connections

- In 12-month [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] cortex P2X4 was found almost only in microglia clustered around plaques, and in post-mortem Alzheimer cortex it co-localised with Iba1 and plaques; it bound [[APOE]] in macrophage lysosomes and promoted [[CTSB]]-dependent ApoE loss, independent of ATP binding (K69A mutant). Its deletion lowered soluble [[Amyloid-beta]] and rescued memory. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
