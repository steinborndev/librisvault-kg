# Chrysin

Chrysin is a flavone found in plants and honey with anti-inflammatory and antioxidant activity.

## Connections

- Favoured candidate: it lowers NF-κB p65 and C/EBP activity in reactive glia and raises NRF2 antioxidant signalling; absorption is poor, and lipid nanoparticles improved memory in an amyloid rat model. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see [[Neuroinflammation]]; method: [[Drug repurposing]]
