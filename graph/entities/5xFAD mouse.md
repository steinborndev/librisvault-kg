# 5xFAD mouse

A transgenic mouse line carrying five familial Alzheimer mutations (APP K670N/M671L, I716V, V717I and PSEN1 M146L, L286V) under a neuron-specific promoter, which deposits [[Amyloid-beta]] early and quickly.

## Connections

- Crossed with Cd33 and Trem2 knockouts: CD33 loss lowered amyloid pathology and improved cognition only when [[TREM2]] was present, and TREM2 loss worsened pathology regardless of [[CD33]]. Study: [[TREM2 acts downstream of CD33 in 5xFAD mouse microglia]]
- Related models: [[APOE4-TR-5xFAD mouse]], [[APP-PS1 mouse]], [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]]
- Human Alzheimer tau filaments injected into 5xFAD cortex caused a working-memory deficit and oligomerisation of endogenous 4R tau in dystrophic neurites near plaques; cortical CDK5 activity was higher than in wild type. See [[Tau propagation]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Hippocampal mitophagy in 4-month-old mt-Keima crosses was about 27% below wild type with lower phospho-[[ULK1]] and biogenesis factors; 1 mg/kg intranasal [[ALT001]] restored them, and four weeks of dosing raised depressed hippocampal long-term potentiation from 108% to 148% (wild type 150%), an effect lost with AAV Rab9 shRNA (114%). See [[Mitophagy]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Homozygous 5xFAD mice (B6/SJL background, Thy-1 promoter, Tg6799 line) given [[D-Pinitol]] at 200 mg/kg/day for 18 weeks from 3.5 months had fewer hippocampal Aβ40 and Aβ42 plaques, more [[IDE]], less AT8 tau phosphorylation, faster reversal learning in the [[Morris water maze]] (not faster acquisition), restored insulin and glucagon, and lower plasma [[Lipopolysaccharide]]. Untreated, they already showed lower sucrose preference ([[Anhedonia]]), a shifted elevated plus maze result, a different faecal microbiota and no excess AT8 tau at 8 months. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- Crossed with Thy1-EGFP "M" mice (5xFAD-M) to image CA1 spines: five-month-old animals had lower spine density, fewer mushroom and more thin spines than M mice, males more affected than females. Daily intraperitoneal [[EDR peptide]] or [[KED peptide]] (400 μg/kg, 2 months) restored spine density or the mushroom fraction in different groups, with only non-significant LTP trends (genotype difference p = 0.057). Study: [[EDR and KED tripeptides restore dendritic spines in 5xFAD mice]]
- Ten-month-old animals had raised phospho-[[MLKL]] in hippocampal CA1, stronger than in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals, which the authors tie to heavier pathology. See [[Necroptosis]]. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
