# ZCL-278

ZCL-278 is a small-molecule inhibitor of the Golgi-associated small GTPase Cdc42, used to disrupt intra-Golgi trafficking.

## Connections

- In 293sw cells it lowered basal secreted Aβ and blocked the sAPPα rise caused by [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]] much as the [[ADAM10]] inhibitor [[GI254023X]] did, yet left the compound's lowering of secreted Aβ intact, which supports partly independent handling of sAPPα and Aβ.
- Study: [[DAPG lowers beta-amyloid secretion in Alzheimer disease models]]
