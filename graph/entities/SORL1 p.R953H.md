# SORL1 p.R953H

A missense change from arginine to histidine at residue 953 in the 5th blade of the YWTD β-propeller of [[SORL1]]; a different substitution at the same residue (p.R953C) is known as the Seattle variant.

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- Carried by five cases (age at onset 46-78) and one control; carrying any YWTD-domain variant gave a 4.6-fold risk of [[Alzheimer disease]]. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
