# Verubecestat

Verubecestat (MK-8931) is a small-molecule [[BACE1]] inhibitor that lowered amyloid beta in clinical trials in [[Alzheimer disease]] without improving cognition; the authors of the study below use it as a non-selective clinical comparator.

## Connections

- Inhibited BACE1 cleavage of [[APP]] (IC50 about 0.002 μM) but also of [[NRG1]] (EC50 below 0.01 μM), a substrate selectivity ratio under 2.5; in rats it reached a brain Cmax of 157 ng/g, similar to [[FAH65]], and lowered sAPPβ. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Class contrast: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitors]]; related inhibitor [[Lanabecestat]]
- Listed with atabecestat as a BACE inhibitor that failed over serious toxicity concerns ([[Atabecestat]]). Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
