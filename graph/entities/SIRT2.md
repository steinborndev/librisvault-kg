# SIRT2

SIRT2 encodes sirtuin 2, an NAD+-dependent deacetylase (chromosome 19q13.2, the same band as [[APOE]]) that removes acetyl groups from alpha-tubulin and regulates mitotic structures. Its role in [[Alzheimer disease]] is disputed: it is raised in neurodegenerative brain and its inhibition lowers amyloid production in models, but it also protects some cells from oxidative death.

## Connections

- Variant: [[SIRT2 rs10410544]], whose genotype was paired with APOE and CYP2D6 status in a treatment-response study of 1086 patients. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Reported in the review to deacetylate alpha-synuclein; loss or inhibition (AK1, AGK-2, AK-7) restores microtubule stability, improves autophagy and lowers Aβ production in models. See [[Sirtuin modulators]] and [[Autophagy]].
