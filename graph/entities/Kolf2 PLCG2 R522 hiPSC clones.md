# Kolf2 PLCG2 R522 hiPSC clones

Isogenic human iPSC clones derived from the male Kolf2 line (HipSci HPSI0114i-kolf_2), CRISPR-edited by homology-directed repair to be homozygous for the protective arginine at residue 522 of [[PLCG2]] and screened through a new Eag1 restriction site. Three P522/P522 and three R522/R522 clones were differentiated into microglia-like cells for assays.

## Connections

- Carries [[PLCG2 p.P522R]]; disease context [[Alzheimer disease]].
- The iPSC-derived microglia showed larger calcium responses, less [[Phagocytosis]] and more [[Endocytosis]] of amyloid oligomers with R522. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
