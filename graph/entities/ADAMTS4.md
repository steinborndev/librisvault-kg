# ADAMTS4

ADAMTS4 encodes a secreted metalloprotease that cleaves extracellular matrix proteoglycans, and lies at a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- The GWAS SNP [[ADAMTS4 rs4575098]] lies in the UTR of ADAMTS4. It was an allele-specific QTL for ADAMTS4 (not seen in earlier gene-level brain eQTL results), for B4GALT3, and through the linked FCER1G variant for [[FCER1G]]; in microglia it was also a genotype-level eQTL of ADAMTS4 (p = 7.4E-4). Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- Oligodendrocyte ADAMTS4 generates N-truncated amyloid-beta peptides, suggesting oligodendrocytes can release amyloidogenic peptides. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Promoter hypoacetylated in hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia (-0.46) and hyperacetylated in prefrontal (0.48) with high amyloid load; transcript was significantly lower in high-amyloid hippocampal Olig2-positive nuclei (Hedges g 1.346, q = 0.027). Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
