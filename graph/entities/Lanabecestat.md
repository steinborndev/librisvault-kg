# Lanabecestat

Lanabecestat (AZD3293) is a clinically tested small-molecule [[BACE1]] inhibitor that did not slow cognitive decline in [[Alzheimer disease]] trials.

## Connections

- In a side-by-side assay it inhibited BACE1 cleavage of [[APP]] (IC50 about 0.004 μM) and of [[NRG1]] (EC50 below 0.01 μM) alike, i.e. no substrate selectivity, unlike [[FAH65]]. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Related inhibitor: [[Verubecestat]]; class contrast: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitors]]
- Listed as the BACE inhibitor that failed for lack of efficacy; related failures [[Atabecestat]] and [[Verubecestat]]. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
