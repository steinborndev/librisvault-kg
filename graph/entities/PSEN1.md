# PSEN1

PSEN1 encodes the catalytic subunit of gamma-secretase, which cleaves [[APP]] to release amyloid beta, and its mutations cause autosomal dominant [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Its one rare coding variant (p.E318G) in a small vessel ischemic disease cohort was benign and outside the pathogenic transmembrane helix surface. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Pathway: [[Amyloid-beta formation]]; partner genes [[PSEN2]], [[APP]]
- The Paisa founder mutation [[PSEN1 p.E280A]], engineered into iPSC neurons, made APOE3 microglia lose phagocytic capacity and raised phosphorylated Tau in organoids; a homozygous [[APOE p.R136S]] carrier with this mutation had delayed disease onset. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Named with [[PSEN2]] and [[APP]] as the causal-mutation genes whose carriers, with APOE ε4 carriers, are the only people currently treated as high genetic risk in prevention trials; polygenic scores could widen that selection. Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
- Carriers of known pathogenic PSEN1 variants were excluded from a SORL1 sequencing study; the onset age of high-risk SORL1 variant carriers overlaps that of some PSEN1 variants (typical PSEN1 onset about 45 years). Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Treated as a very rare highly penetrant gene whose mutations are modelled as an intrinsic disease probability rather than inside a polygenic score. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- The most frequently mutated gene in a Taiwanese familial series (16 of 17 mutation carriers among 77 patients); [[PSEN1 p.M146I]] alone accounted for 9 of the 16, and [[PSEN1 p.H131R]] was upgraded to likely pathogenic on amyloid PET evidence. Study: [[Familial Alzheimer disease mutations in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
- The familial mutation [[PSEN1 p.M139T]], knocked into rat Psen1 on a humanized App background, raised the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio (0.10 to 0.27) and cut Aβ43 2.5-fold without changing total Aβ, and no plaques appeared by 2 years. Study: [[Humanized APP knock-in mouse and rat with PSEN1 M139T for Alzheimer disease]]
- [[PSEN1 p.A75V]] (7 cases) and [[PSEN1 p.R35Q]] (4 cases) occurred only in cases in a case-only exome screen, matching earlier reports. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- The familial mutation [[PSEN1 p.A246E]] in an isogenic hiPSC line was the one pair where APOE2 lowered the Aβ42/40 ratio and phosphorylated tau as well as total Aβ. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]
- The human PSEN1 exon 9 deletion in [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] animals went with later loss of serotonin transporter binding and uptake in cortex. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
- Differentially expressed or usage-changed in at least one temporal lobe brain RNAseq dataset of Alzheimer cases. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
- [[PSEN1 p.E184G]], previously seen in two French families, was found in a 41-year-old Thai woman with a four-generation family history; residue 184 may be important since p.E184D also causes early onset. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- Named with [[APP]] and [[PSEN2]] as the genes of familial Alzheimer disease, in contrast to [[ITM2B]] mutations that cause the British and Danish dementias. Study: [[BRI2 in familial British and Danish dementia and Alzheimer disease review]]
- As catalytic core of the [[Gamma-secretase complex]]; familial PSEN mutations (over 300, about 90% of familial Alzheimer mutations) impair carboxypeptidase-like trimming and favour long Aβ. Wild-type mouse neurons with low gamma-secretase activity reproduced this shift without a mutation. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Dominant mutations reported in 1995; the gene encodes a gamma-secretase subunit, and the triple transgenic mouse carries a PSEN1 mutation. The Colombian [[PSEN1 p.E280A]] kindred is the setting of the [[Crenezumab]] prevention trial. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- Carriers in the Dominantly Inherited Alzheimer Network were split by whether the variant changes a transmembrane or cytoplasmic region; transmembrane carriers (N = 135) had faster MMSE decline and hippocampal atrophy than cytoplasmic carriers (N = 65) with similar amyloid burden, and a split at codon 200 did not separate them. See [[PSEN1 transmembrane and cytoplasmic domain grouping]]. Study: [[PSEN1 transmembrane versus cytoplasmic variants and progression in DIAN]]
- Promoter H3K27ac was lower in female hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load (log2 fold change -0.41, FDR 0.046); the nearest peak was enriched in that population and in microglia. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Predicted as a [[MIR650]] target; miR-650 lowered a PSEN1 3' untranslated region reporter, and deleting the site removed the effect (not tested in mice). Study: [[MicroRNA-650 targets CDK5 and lessens amyloid pathology in APP-PSEN1 mice]]
- Colonic PS1 protein was unchanged with age in wild-type mice and lower in 12-month-old [[APP-PS1 mouse]] colon (P < 0.05); mRNA did not clearly differ between gut and brain. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- PS-1 protein was raised in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and lowered by [[Magnolol]] at both doses and by [[Donepezil]] (n = 3 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Two known pathogenic mutations, [[PSEN1 p.P264L]] (two siblings, onset 50 and 42) and [[PSEN1 p.M146V]] (onset 34, no family history), were found among 102 Swedish memory-clinic patients. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
