# PSEN2 p.H220Y

A missense change (histidine to tyrosine at residue 220) in [[PSEN2]], located in a transmembrane region; a variant of uncertain significance. In transfected [[HEK293]] cells it did not change Aβ42, Aβ40 or the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio relative to wild-type PSEN2, which the authors say does not by itself show that it is harmless.
