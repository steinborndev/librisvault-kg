# PC12

PC12 is a rat pheochromocytoma cell line that acquires neuron-like properties when differentiated.

## Connections

- Treated with 20 μM amyloid beta 1-42 oligomer to model Alzheimer injury, with DOR and MOR ligands and DOR siRNA, to measure [[BACE1]] activity and expression and Aβ42. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
- Companion human line: [[SH-SY5Y]] (APPswe).
- Used as one of three rat cell systems in which [[YY1]] raised [[BACE1]] promoter activity. Study: [[YY1 activates the BACE1 promoter in rat cells and reactive astrocytes]]
- Injured with amyloid-beta 1-42 (10 μM, 24 h) as an Alzheimer cell model; [[6-Gingerol]] gave a qualitative improvement in CCK-8 viability, with no concentrations or percentages in the text. Study: [[6-Gingerol from ginger screened for Alzheimer disease by docking and dynamics]]
- One of three neuronal culture systems in which TNF-α alone cut viability by 21.5% and TNF-α with Smac mimetic and Z-VAD-FMK produced about 53% necrosis, a model of [[Necroptosis]]. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
