# APOA4

APOA4 encodes apolipoprotein A-IV, a plasma apolipoprotein involved in lipid transport and cholesterol efflux.

## Connections

- It was among the proteins most consistently reported as an Alzheimer plasma marker (more than five earlier cohorts), was stably selected for the Alzheimer versus control classifier, and is part of the 13-protein universal panel; it belongs to the lipoprotein remodeling and cholesterol efflux cluster of the 43 significantly different proteins.
