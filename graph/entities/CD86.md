# CD86

CD86 encodes a co-stimulatory ligand on antigen-presenting cells that activates T cells through CD28; on microglia it is used as an activation marker and is higher in aged mouse hippocampus.

## Connections

- CD86-positive, CD11b-negative cells rose 4-fold (to about 25%) after amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 16 days in culture, 24-fold above the young cells; the authors suggest an M2b-like subset able to co-stimulate infiltrating T cells, without testing it.
