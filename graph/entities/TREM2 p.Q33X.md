# TREM2 p.Q33X

A stop-gain change at glutamine 33 of [[TREM2]] that, in homozygous form, causes [[Nasu-Hakola disease]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[TREM2]]; disease [[Nasu-Hakola disease]]
- Heterozygous in 4 of 5,617 (0.071%) Alzheimer cases and no controls; all met criteria for probable Alzheimer disease and none had bone cysts or unusual behaviour. The authors suggest an intermediate rather than dominant inheritance and call it, with [[NOTCH3 p.A284T]], possibly fully penetrant in people surviving to late age. It is rarer than the well-documented TREM2 R47H risk variant. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
