# cis-9, trans-11-conjugated linoleic acid

cis-9, trans-11-conjugated linoleic acid (c-9, t-11-CLA, rumenic acid) is the predominant dietary isomer of conjugated linoleic acid, accounting for up to about 90% of total CLA, and is thought to carry the health benefits attributed to CLA.

## Connections

- Fed to an Alzheimer mouse model, it lowered hippocampal [[Amyloid-beta]], raised the number of astrocytes expressing [[IL10]] and [[IL19]], and was found, free and as [[Lysophosphatidylcholine]], at higher levels in brain.
