# MLKL

MLKL encodes the pseudokinase that executes [[Necroptosis]]: once phosphorylated by [[RIPK3]] it disrupts the plasma membrane, and phospho-MLKL is the standard marker of the process.

## Connections

- Total and phospho-MLKL were higher in Braak VI Alzheimer cortex than in controls and phospho-MLKL was strongly raised in CA1 of 10-month-old [[APP-PS1 mouse]] and [[5xFAD mouse]] animals; TNF-α injection, and its loss after TNFR1, p62 or UVRAG manipulation, moved it up or down. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Pathway partners: [[RIPK1]], [[RIPK3]], [[SQSTM1]], [[UVRAG]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
