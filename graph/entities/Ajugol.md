# Ajugol

Ajugol is an iridoid glycoside found in the raw root of Rehmannia glutinosa ([[Rehmanniae radix and Rehmanniae radix praeparata]]), where an aqueous extract contained about 0.27 mg/g.

## Connections

- Detected in serum and brain of mice given either root preparation; in mice and [[BV2]] cells it lowered markers of the [[TLR4]], [[RELA]] and [[NLRP3]] inflammation axis and shifted apoptosis proteins ([[BCL2]], [[BAX]], [[CYCS]], [[CASP3]]), but it was not seen in hippocampal tissue by imaging mass spectrometry. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Counterpart compound: [[Isoacteoside]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
