# RELB

RELB encodes RelB, a subunit of the NF-κB family of transcription factors that acts mainly in the non-canonical NF-κB pathway; it lies in the chromosome 19 region around [[APOE]].

## Connections

- Rare variants in the gene stayed associated with CSF amyloid-beta 1-42 after adjustment for APOE genotype, and were linked to cortical amyloid burden in bilateral frontal and parietal lobes on voxel-wise [[Amyloid PET]] analysis. Study: [[Rare variants near APOE and CSF and neuroimaging biomarkers in ADNI]]
- Phenotype: [[Decreased amyloid beta 42 peptide CSF concentration]]
