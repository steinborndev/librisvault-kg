# LRRK2 p.R1628P

A missense change from arginine to proline at residue 1628 of [[LRRK2]], earlier associated with [[Parkinson disease]] risk in Chinese patients.

## Connections

- Gene: [[LRRK2]]; disease: [[Parkinson disease]]
- Heterozygous in Thai patient #8 with early-onset Alzheimer disease; its role in Alzheimer disease is untested. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
