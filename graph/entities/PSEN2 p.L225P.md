# PSEN2 p.L225P

A missense change from leucine to proline at residue 225 of [[PSEN2]], at the border between the fifth transmembrane helix and its loop. It was found in a United Kingdom screen of early-onset Alzheimer cohorts and is rare in gnomAD; age at onset and phenotype were not given. Proline is a helix breaker, but modelling showed no visible kink here because the residue sits near the loop.
