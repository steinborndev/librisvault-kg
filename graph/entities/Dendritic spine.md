# Dendritic spine

Dendritic spines are the small protrusions of a dendrite that carry most excitatory postsynaptic sites. By shape they are classed as thin ("learning" spines), mushroom (large, stable "memory" spines with the most postsynaptic density) and stubby (neckless, mostly immature).

## Connections

- Loss of mushroom spines with a relative rise in thin spines is described in hippocampal Alzheimer pathology; in 5-month-old [[5xFAD mouse]] CA1 neurons, mushroom spines fell and thin spines rose relative to controls, and [[EDR peptide]] and [[KED peptide]] shifted the balance back by different routes.
