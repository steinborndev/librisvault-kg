# BIN1 rs17014923

A common single nucleotide variant in the [[BIN1]] promoter region (minor allele frequency 0.10 in a northern Han Chinese cohort).

## Connections

- Strongly associated with [[BIN1]] promoter methylation level (p < 0.0001) but not with preclinical Alzheimer status or CSF amyloid and tau markers; adjusting for it did not change the methylation findings. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
