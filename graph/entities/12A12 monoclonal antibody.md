# 12A12 monoclonal antibody

12A12 is a monoclonal antibody against residues 26-36 of the N-terminus of [[MAPT]] (tau). It is cleavage-specific: in vivo it neutralizes the toxic N-terminal 20-22 kDa tau fragment (NH2htau) without touching full-length tau.

## Connections

- Given by tail-vein injection to [[Tg2576 mouse]] mice, it lowered synaptic [[APP]] and [[BACE1]], restored [[BIN1]] and (in retina) [[RIN3]] and [[RAB5A]], and normalized lactate and the glycolytic index. Study: [[Tau antibody 12A12 and amyloid-beta in Tg2576 mouse hippocampus and retina]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
