# Arteriolosclerosis

Arteriolosclerosis is thickening and stiffening of the walls of small arteries and arterioles; in the brain it is a common small-vessel pathology of older age.

## Connections

- Brain arteriolosclerosis was among the neuropathologies associated with parkinsonism or dexterity in older adults, but not with the Alzheimer [[Polygenic risk score]].
