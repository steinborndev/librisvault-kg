# GSK3B

GSK3B encodes glycogen synthase kinase-3 beta, a kinase downstream of PI3K/AKT that phosphorylates tau and is held in check by Wnt signalling.

## Connections

- Total and phosphorylated GSK-3β were lower after [[Osthol]]-[[Zinc oxide]] treatment of APP-overexpressing [[BE2-M17]] cells; the authors link this to less tau phosphorylation. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
- Pathway: [[Wnt signaling pathway]], [[CTNNB1]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]

- Reviewed as a tau kinase held down by the soluble TREM2-[[TAGLN2]]-[[RHOA]] route in neurons. See [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
