# CSF1R

CSF1R encodes the receptor tyrosine kinase for colony stimulating factor 1, central to microglial survival; kinase-domain mutations cause [[Hereditary diffuse leukoencephalopathy with spheroids]].

## Connections

- Two variants outside the kinase domain, [[CSF1R p.P54Q]] and [[CSF1R p.L536V]], were found in Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients; the authors call this the first report of CSF1R variants in such patients. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- Named by the authors as an upstream receptor of [[PLCG2]], whose protective variant [[PLCG2 p.P522R]] alters microglial calcium signalling and PIP2 levels; CSF1R inhibitors are in Alzheimer trials. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
- Raised in Apoe knockout mouse brain, one of 22 genes common to the Apoe-driven ivory module and human post-mortem microglial modules. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
