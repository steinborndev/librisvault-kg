# CLU rs4236673

rs4236673 is a late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] GWAS SNP at the [[CLU]] locus, in almost complete linkage disequilibrium with the CLU variant [[CLU rs7982]] and in mild linkage disequilibrium with the splicing variant rs9331888.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CLU]]
- Allele-specific effects on CLU appeared only in the temporal cortex (p = 1E-50 at rs7982) and not in the prefrontal, posterior cingulate or caudate regions, and the exon-dependent effects at different SNPs reflect opposite effects on different transcripts.
