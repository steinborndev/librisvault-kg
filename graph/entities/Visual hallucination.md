# Visual hallucination

A visual hallucination is a visual perception, such as seeing people or animals, without an external stimulus. In dementia it is a core feature of [[Dementia with Lewy bodies]] and occurs in a minority of people with [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- A Korean woman with [[PSEN2 p.V226A]] saw people and animals at night and spoke to them; hallucinations are rare in [[PSEN2]] disease, and carriers of N141I, S130L, V191E and A415S have been described with them. Study: [[Novel PSEN2 p.V226A in a Korean patient with atypical Alzheimer disease]]
- Co-occurs with [[Memory impairment]] in the presenting picture of the same patient.
