# MIR191

MIR191 encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-191, a small non-coding RNA that represses target messenger RNAs after transcription.

## Connections

- Serum miR-191-5p was lower in rats given 2.5 µg/µL fibrillar Aβ1-42 and in 15-month-old APPswe-PS1dE9 mice; the authors note it is frequently low in sera of patients and propose that, like the miR-29 family, its fall might raise [[BACE1]].
