# MBP

MBP encodes myelin basic protein, a major structural component of the axonal myelin sheath that has been reported to inhibit amyloid beta fibril formation and to be deficient in people with [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Protein level was lower in the brains of FAD4T mice than in wild type and returned to wild-type level after treatment with [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]; the authors read this as less need for MBP to neutralise Aβ1-42 once [[BACE1]] was silenced. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Model: [[FAD4T mouse]]
- Intact MBP is proposed to bind and degrade amyloid-beta so plaques form only where myelin is degraded; degraded MBP is said to seed aggregation and compete with amyloid-beta for LRP1-mediated removal. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
