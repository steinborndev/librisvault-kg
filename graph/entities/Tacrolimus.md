# Tacrolimus

Tacrolimus (FK506) is a macrolide immunosuppressant that binds FKBP12 and thereby inhibits calcineurin ([[PPP3CA]]), the calcium-dependent protein phosphatase 2B.

## Connections

- Added to neurons with amyloid oligomers it raised [[DICER1]] protein further, taken as evidence that calcineurin dephosphorylation helps determine Dicer1 stability. Study: [[Amyloid oligomers rapidly raise neuronal Dicer1 by reducing phosphorylation]]
