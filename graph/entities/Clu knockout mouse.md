# Clu knockout mouse

A mouse with homozygous deletion of Clu, the mouse orthologue of [[CLU]] (clusterin), on a [[C57BL-6 mouse]] background.

## Connections

- At 6 months, whole-brain RNA-seq of females showed the largest transcriptional change of the late-onset models (1,759 differentially expressed genes) and five driven co-expression modules enriched in synaptic function, endocytosis and RNA transport. These overlapped neuronal-system, organelle-biogenesis and cell-cycle modules of human post-mortem brain, and the brain-wide pattern correlated negatively with human immune modules. [[TYROBP]] was lowered and [[SYT7]] raised. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Shared the steelblue module with the [[Cd2ap heterozygous mouse]]; see [[Mouse-human module comparison]]
- Related models: [[Apoe knockout mouse]], [[APP-PS1 mouse]]
