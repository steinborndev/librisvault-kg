# C1QA

C1QA encodes the A chain of C1q, the recognition subunit that triggers the classical complement pathway and binds aggregated amyloid-beta. The page also stands for C1q as measured in plasma, which is assembled from the C1QA, C1QB and C1QC chains.

## Connections

- Plasma C1q was higher in adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (mean 191 versus 110 μg/ml) and, not significantly, higher with dementia; aggregated Aβ activates complement through C1q, and C1q marks amyloid plaques in Alzheimer and Down syndrome brain. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
- Related: [[SERPING1]], [[C1R]], [[C1S]], [[Complement activation]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Raised in Apoe knockout mouse brain together with [[C1QB]] and [[C1QC]], within a set of 22 genes common to the Apoe-driven ivory module and human post-mortem microglial modules. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
