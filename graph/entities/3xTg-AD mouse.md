# 3xTg-AD mouse

A triple-transgenic mouse model of Alzheimer disease that develops both amyloid and tau pathology with age.

## Connections

- Hippocampal microarray and qPCR work at 12, 36 and 52 weeks found 644 up- and 624 downregulated genes at both 12 and 52 weeks, with immune-response genes raised and GABA receptor genes ([[GABRG3]], [[GABBR1]], [[GABRR2]]) lowered. Study: [[GABA-related gene changes in 3xTg-AD mouse hippocampus from early to end stage]]
- Disease modelled: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Related models: [[5xFAD mouse]], [[APP-PS1 mouse]], [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]], [[Tg2576 mouse]]
- Used for hippocampal lentiviral [[PTK2]] knockdown and overexpression at 8 months: overexpression, not knockdown, impaired fear conditioning and water maze memory and raised 6E10-positive plaque number. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Six-month-old male homozygous animals made hyperglycemic with [[Streptozocin]] showed worse Y-maze and water-maze performance, lower NeuN, and higher Aβ and Tau staining; [[LM-031]] (40 mg/kg i.p.) improved these and restored hippocampal NRF2 and phospho-CREB. See [[Streptozocin-induced hyperglycemia model]]. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Called the breakthrough that produced plaques and tangles in mice, but judged to lack face and construct validity because one of its three mutations (in MAPT) causes frontotemporal dementia, not Alzheimer disease. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]; see [[Mouse model validity for Alzheimer disease]].
