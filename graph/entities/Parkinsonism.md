# Parkinsonism

Parkinsonism is the group of motor signs of bradykinesia, rigidity, tremor and parkinsonian gait. Mild forms are common in older adults who do not have [[Parkinson disease]] and are scored on a modified Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale.

## Connections

- Associated with the Alzheimer [[Polygenic risk score]], especially gait and rigidity.
- Candidate mediators: [[MIR132]], [[IGFBP5]], [[Neurofibrillary tangle]] density.
- Part of [[Motor impairment]] in older adults.
- Study: [[Alzheimer polygenic risk score and motor impairment in older adults]]
