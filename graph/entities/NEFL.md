# NEFL

NEFL encodes the neurofilament light chain, a structural protein of myelinated axons that leaks into cerebrospinal fluid and blood when axons are damaged, so it serves as a general marker of neurodegeneration.

## Connections

- In amyloid-positive, cognitively unimpaired adults higher CSF NfL was associated with enlarged perivascular spaces in the basal ganglia, but it did not stay associated in the centrum semiovale. Study: [[Perivascular spaces and CSF biomarkers in cognitively unimpaired Alzheimer continuum]]
- Related: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Enlarged perivascular spaces]]
- CSF NEFL was significantly higher in 11 symptomatic GRN mutation carriers than in 12 non-carriers, which the authors read as marked neurodegeneration. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Plasma neurofilament light, a marker of axonal damage, was lower in patients with a stronger antibody response to the tau vaccine [[AADvac1]] (p = 0.0139). Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
