# C57BL-6 mouse

C57BL/6 is the common inbred laboratory mouse strain, used as the wild-type background and control for transgenic Alzheimer models such as the [[APP-PS1 mouse]].

## Connections

- Wild-type animals served as ageing controls for colonic Aβ42 and microbiome work, and as faecal-transplant recipients and gut-injection hosts. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Related: [[Fecal microbiota transplantation]]
- Control and background strain for the Apoe, Clu, Bin1, Cd2ap and APOE ε4 risk-gene mice profiled at 6 months. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
