# GRM5

GRM5 encodes metabotropic glutamate receptor 5, a G-protein-coupled receptor that links glutamate to phosphoinositide hydrolysis at synapses.

## Connections

- Long-term depression induced by Aβ dimers from human brain required mGluR5, and selectively blocking Aβ-induced PtdIns(4,5)P2 hydrolysis through presynaptic mGluR5 restored glutamate release and synaptic transmission in APP/PS1 mice.
