# NSF

NSF encodes an ATPase that disassembles SNARE complexes after membrane fusion, which recycles the fusion machinery for neurotransmitter release.

## Connections

- Lower in Alzheimer cortex in three expression datasets, densest in excitatory and inhibitory neurons, and higher early in excitatory neuron pseudotime; genetically predicted higher expression went with lower Alzheimer risk (odds ratio 0.897).
