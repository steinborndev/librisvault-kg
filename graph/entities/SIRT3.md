# SIRT3

SIRT3 encodes the main mitochondrial sirtuin (chromosome 11p15.5), a NAD+-dependent deacetylase that protects mitochondrial integrity, lowers reactive oxygen species and supports fatty acid oxidation.

## Connections

- Reviewed as lower in Alzheimer cortex and in APP/PS1 mice; it opposes p53-driven mitochondrial damage and acts through FOXO3a to raise antioxidant enzymes in microglia. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Activated by [[Honokiol]] and raised in the brain by [[Nicotinamide riboside]]; see [[Sirtuin modulators]].
- Related sirtuins: [[SIRT1]], [[SIRT2]], [[SIRT6]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- In mouse brain, Sirt3 deletion and Western diet lowered [[IDE]] (about 50% in knockout cortex), and activating SIRT3 with [[Nicotinamide riboside]] raised IDE, [[MME]] and its own level while lowering [[BACE1]]; the double mutant [[APP-PS1-Sirt3 knockout mouse]] models [[Metabolic syndrome]] with amyloid pathology. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
