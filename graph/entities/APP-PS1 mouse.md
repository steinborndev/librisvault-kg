# APP-PS1 mouse

APP-PS1 is a double-transgenic amyloid mouse model carrying human [[APP]] and presenilin-1 mutations, which develops amyloid plaques with age.

## Connections

- Nine-month-old males were used to profile root-extract compounds, the brain proteome and the gut microbiota. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Related models: [[APP-PS45 mouse]], [[Tg2576 mouse]]
- Cortical RNA-seq at 8 months found induction of [[CST7]] (about 50-fold), [[CCL3]], [[ITGAX]], [[CLEC7A]], [[TYROBP]], [[CD83]] and [[C3]], and Western diet halved brain [[SIRT3]] and [[IDE]]; crossed with Sirt3 knockout as the [[APP-PS1-Sirt3 knockout mouse]]. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- One-year-old APP Swedish plus PSEN1 Δexon 9 mice given AAV-GFP-miR-650 in the right dentate gyrus and AAV-GFP in the left had lower hippocampal [[CDK5]], fewer amyloid plaques, lower Aβ and more MAP2-positive neurons after one month (n = 3 to 5). Study: [[MicroRNA-650 targets CDK5 and lessens amyloid pathology in APP-PSEN1 mice]]
- Colonic Aβ42 rose with age in these mice; their 12-month faecal microbiota (more Bacteroidales and Prevotella, leakier gut) raised colonic BACE1 and Aβ42 when transplanted into young wild-type mice ([[Fecal microbiota transplantation]]). Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Given [[D-ribose]] to worsen Alzheimer-like change: higher amyloid-beta and phospho-Tau, neuron damage, poorer water-maze and shuttle-box performance and more anxiety-like behaviour; [[Berberine]] reversed all but the anxiety, and the rescue was blocked by [[Mdivi-1]]. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Used as the early-onset comparison for risk-gene mice: its brain transcriptome separated from all late-onset models on the first principal component, with 250 differentially expressed genes, lipid and metabolism module signals and some shared neuroimmune change. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- At 10 months, phospho-[[MLKL]] and [[SQSTM1]] were raised in hippocampal CA1; TNFR1 shRNA, anti-TNFR1 antibody and p62 shRNA lowered phospho-MLKL by 55.5%, 45.5% and 47%, and AAV-[[UVRAG]] lowered p62 by 34.5% and phospho-MLKL by 36.5% and improved water-maze performance (3 weeks after injection). See [[Necroptosis]]. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
