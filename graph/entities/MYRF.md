# MYRF

MYRF encodes a transcription factor that directly activates the genes of central nervous system [[Myelination]] in oligodendrocytes.

## Connections

- Its promoter was hypoacetylated at H3K27 (log2 fold change -0.48, FDR 0.03) in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia from the hippocampus of women with high [[Amyloid-beta]] load, and transcript in sorted Olig2-positive hippocampal nuclei fell with amyloid load (Hedges g 1.567, q = 0.018). The authors cite a prior link to late-onset disease. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]; cell population: [[Oligodendrocyte-enriched glia]]
