# RELN

RELN encodes reelin, a large signalling glycoprotein that guides neuronal migration in brain development and regulates synaptic plasticity, neurotransmission and memory in the adult brain.

## Connections

- Bridge between the [[APOE]], [[CACNA1C]] and CTNNA2 subnetworks of the protein interaction network
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- Reelin activation of presynaptic [[LRP8]] and [[VLDLR]] raises calcium and vesicle fusion; ApoE4 blocks the postsynaptic Reelin-dependent NMDA receptor activation. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Isoform switch (differential transcript usage) in Alzheimer temporal lobe, assigned to GABAergic neurons only. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
- Lower in the C2 than the C1 Alzheimer brain subtype defined by [[CDK5R1]] level, and positively correlated with monocyte, M2 macrophage and activated dendritic cell fractions; part of the [[Reelin signaling pathway]] gene set. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
