# ATP6V1G2

ATP6V1G2 encodes the neuron-enriched G2 subunit of the V1 domain of the vacuolar proton-pumping ATPase (V-ATPase), which acidifies intracellular vesicles.

## Connections

- Lower in Alzheimer cortex in three expression datasets, and genetically predicted higher expression went with lower Alzheimer risk (odds ratio 0.932); barely detected in single-cell data. Study: [[Four synaptic vesicle cycle genes protective in Alzheimer disease by MR]]
