# SOAT1

SOAT1 encodes ACAT1, an enzyme that esterifies cholesterol for storage and transport.

## Connections

- ACAT inhibition or Acat1 ablation lowered brain amyloid-beta and plaques in a mouse model, with higher [[24S-Hydroxycholesterol]]. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
