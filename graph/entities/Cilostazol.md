# Cilostazol

A phosphodiesterase-3 inhibitor used as an antiplatelet and vasodilator.

## Connections

- In Swedish-mutant APP neuroblastoma (N2aSwe) cells it raised autophagy markers, lowered intracellular Aβ1-42, and the effect depended on [[SIRT1]], LKB1 and AMPK signalling with mTOR suppression. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
