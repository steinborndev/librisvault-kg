# Quercetin

Quercetin is a plant flavonol found in many herbs, fruit and vegetables, with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity.

## Connections

- The highest-degree ingredient in six of seven heat-clearing herb networks (degree 58 in Coptis chinensis, 32-33 in Lonicera, Houttuynia, Centella, Gardenia and Phellodendron); with [[Chlorogenic acid]] it was proposed as a shared anti-Alzheimer ingredient, by database prediction only.
