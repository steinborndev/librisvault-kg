# PSEN1 p.H131R

A missense change of PSEN1 in [[PSEN1]] (histidine 131 to arginine), seen in familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. First reported as a variant of unknown significance in a Japanese woman with onset at 45 and no family history. A Taiwanese woman with onset at 49, a sister affected at 52 and a father who died at 65 had extensive cortical and striatal amyloid deposition on Pittsburgh compound B PET, which the authors take as clinical evidence moving the variant to likely pathogenic. Cell work cited from earlier studies showed a raised amyloid beta 42/40 ratio, about twofold in mouse neuroblastoma cells.
