# MIR155

MIR155 encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-155, a small non-coding RNA that represses gene expression after transcription.

## Connections

- miR-155 gave a fluorescence signal in HT22 cells and was among the three microRNAs with the largest change after amyloid beta oligomer treatment, but it showed no clear expression pattern across the four patient plasma samples.
- Marker for [[Alzheimer disease]] on the nanoparticle kit; see [[Molecular beacon nanoparticle sensor]].
- Study: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]]
- Amyloid-beta 1-42 lowered miR-155 (0.7-fold) in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture; it was already about half as high in those kept 16 days and did not respond, which the authors read as dormancy. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Triplicated and overexpressed in the brain of the [[Ts65Dn mouse]] together with [[MIR802]]; a lentiviral sponge against both lowered them and rescued [[MECP2]], Ship1 ([[INPP5D]]) and [[FOXM1]]. Study: [[miR-155 and miR-802 targets in the hippocampus of Ts65Dn Down syndrome mice]]
