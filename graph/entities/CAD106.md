# CAD106

CAD106 is an active immunotherapy vaccine that makes the immune system produce antibodies against amyloid-beta.

## Connections

- In the CAD106 cohort of Generation Study 1 (NCT02565511), which stopped early, it raised serum amyloid-beta IgG in 41 of 42 participants, reduced the formation rate of amyloid plaques (P < 0.001) and three cases of [[Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities]] were reported; the review concludes larger trials in genetically prone people are warranted.
