# Multiple sclerosis

Multiple sclerosis is a chronic inflammatory disease of the central nervous system in which immune attack damages myelin and nerve fibres, leaving lesions in brain and spinal cord.

## Connections

- Reviewed as a disease in which inhibition or deficiency of [[SCD]] suppresses [[Neuroinflammation]] and protects neurons, in part by enhancing remyelination. Study: [[SCD1 as a therapeutic target in neurological disorders review]]
- Mouse stearoyl-CoA desaturase-2 ([[SCD]]), the main SCD isoform in brain, is reviewed as relevant to this disease. Study: [[SCD2 in murine development, metabolism and disease review]]
- P2X4 expression in reactive microglia is cited as beneficial here, increasing myelin phagocytosis and remyelination, unlike its deleterious roles elsewhere; see [[P2RX4]]. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
