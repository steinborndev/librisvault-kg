# APOE mimetic peptide

A synthetic peptide spanning residues 133-149 of [[APOE]], the receptor-binding region, that blocks the interaction of APOE with its cellular receptors.

## Connections

- In APOE3 microglia it lowered amyloid-beta-induced [[Lipid droplet]] number and [[Lipid peroxidation]] to [[APOE p.R136S]] levels, prevented the loss of [[Phagocytosis]], and reduced phosphorylated [[MAPT]] in neurons challenged with Alzheimer synaptosomes. Its site of action (neuron, microglia or their interface) is untested. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
