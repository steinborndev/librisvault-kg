# CRK

CRK encodes an SH2 and SH3 domain adaptor protein that passes tyrosine kinase signals to downstream effectors, including in the [[Reelin signaling pathway]] and in immune cells such as natural killer cells.

## Connections

- Lower in the C2 than the C1 subtype of Alzheimer brain samples; its expression correlated positively with memory B cells, gamma delta T cells and monocytes and negatively with CD8 T cells and activated NK cells. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
