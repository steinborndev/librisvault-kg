# HLA-DQA1

HLA-DQA1 encodes the alpha chain of the HLA-DQ class II antigen-presenting molecule and lies in an Alzheimer GWAS region.

## Connections

- In ROSMAP it was nominally less expressed in Alzheimer cases than controls in both blood and brain, with the same direction in each tissue, and it was more highly expressed in blood dendritic cells and monocytes than other genes in its group.
