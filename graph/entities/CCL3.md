# CCL3

CCL3 encodes a pro-inflammatory chemokine that attracts monocytes and other immune cells.

## Connections

- Higher in APOE4 than in APOE2 or APOE3 xenografted human microglia, part of a general rise in cytokine genes that the authors link to a shift towards the cytokine-response microglial state. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
- Paralogues measured with it: [[CCL3L1]], [[CCL4L2]]; isoform context: [[APOE]]
- Induced about tenfold in APP/PS1 mouse brain, and more in the APP/PS1/Sirt3-knockout comorbid brain according to the discussion. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
