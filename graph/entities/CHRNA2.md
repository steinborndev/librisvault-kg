# CHRNA2

CHRNA2 encodes the alpha-2 subunit of a neuronal nicotinic acetylcholine receptor.

## Connections

- One of five late-onset Alzheimer GWAS-list genes that are also targets of drugs in Alzheimer clinical trials (with [[TREM2]], [[CD33]], [[PRSS8]] and [[ACE]]). Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
