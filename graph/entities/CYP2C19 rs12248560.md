# CYP2C19 rs12248560

A promoter variant (-806C>T) defining the CYP2C19 *17 allele, which raises enzyme expression and gives the ultra-rapid metabolizer phenotype.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CYP2C19]]
- Ultra-rapid metabolizers (*1A/*17 and *17/*17) were 1.7% of 1006 Alzheimer disease patients and showed minimal improvement in depression after treatment; the authors also cite earlier work linking this variant to depression severity.
