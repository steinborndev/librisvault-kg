# CR1

CR1 encodes complement receptor 1, a regulator of complement activation, and is among the stronger genome-wide-association risk genes for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] outside the APOE region.

## Connections

- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Related risk genes: [[CLU]], [[CD33]]
- Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- [[CR1 p.Q1022H]] showed only a nominal protective association with [[Small vessel ischemic disease]]. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- The GWAS SNP [[CR1 rs2093760]] was an allele-specific QTL across several CR1 exons only in the temporal cortex and a genotype-level eQTL in oligodendrocytes but not microglia; the missense variant [[CR1 rs2296160]] in near-complete linkage may be the driver. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- A novel genome-wide significant locus for lower CSF amyloid-beta 42 (Z = -6.01, p = 1.8e-9) but not for tau; it colocalized with the Alzheimer GWAS signal (posterior probability 0.97) and with a CR1 eQTL in six brain regions, supporting a role in amyloid clearance. Variant: [[CR1 rs6656401]]. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Its sentinel SNP overlaps an H3K27ac peak significantly enriched in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Soluble CR1 was lower in the plasma of adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (p < 0.01), yet carriers of the minor allele of [[CR1 rs6656401]] had higher sCR1 (14.6 versus 11.8 ng/ml, p < 0.05); [[CR1 rs6691117]] had no effect. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
- Its CpG island shore was less methylated in blood of Japanese Alzheimer patients than controls (mean difference under 10%); methylation did not track CR1 expression, [[CR1 rs3818361]] genotype, Mini-Mental State Examination score or age. Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
- More than 30% of plasma [[Phosphatidylethanolamine]] species associated differently with the CR1 risk SNP in Alzheimer cases than in controls (nominal, not surviving correction). Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
