# PSEN2

PSEN2 encodes a second gamma-secretase catalytic subunit, homologous to [[PSEN1]], and rare mutations in it cause autosomal dominant [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Two rare variants (p.L2F, p.R62H) in a small vessel ischemic disease cohort lay outside the pathogenic region; none of the known PSEN2 mutations cause cerebral amyloid angiopathy. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Pathway: [[Amyloid-beta formation]]; partner genes [[PSEN1]], [[APP]]
- Named with [[PSEN1]] and [[APP]] as the causal-mutation genes whose carriers are treated as high genetic risk for trial selection. Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
- Typical onset in PSEN2 mutation carriers (about 55 years) overlaps that of [[SORL1]] truncating and high-priority missense variant carriers (median 62 to 64 years). Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Treated as a very rare highly penetrant gene whose mutations are modelled as an intrinsic disease probability rather than inside a polygenic score. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- [[PSEN2 p.H169N]], a transmembrane domain III change seen only in East Asian patients, was found alongside [[APP p.V551M]] in a Korean woman with early-onset disease; the authors suggest a founder mutation. Study: [[Double APP and PSEN2 mutations in a Korean patient with early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- No PSEN2 mutation was found in any of 77 Taiwanese familial or early-onset patients tested for PSEN1, PSEN2 and APP. Study: [[Familial Alzheimer disease mutations in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
- Differentially expressed or usage-changed in at least one temporal lobe brain RNAseq dataset of Alzheimer cases. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
- Named with [[APP]] and [[PSEN1]] as the genes of familial Alzheimer disease, in contrast to [[ITM2B]] mutations that cause the British and Danish dementias. Study: [[BRI2 in familial British and Danish dementia and Alzheimer disease review]]
- Nine rare variants in 702 Chinese patients with Alzheimer disease; [[PSEN2 p.N141S]], [[PSEN2 p.M239T]] and [[PSEN2 p.I368F]] raised Aβ42 and the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio in transfected cells (0.4% of the cohort, all familial early-onset), five others did not. Most of the 87 known PSEN2 variants lack functional evidence. Study: [[PSEN2 mutation spectrum and functionally validated variants in a Chinese Alzheimer cohort]]
- Novel [[PSEN2 p.V226A]] in the fifth transmembrane helix of a Korean woman with hallucinations and early-onset disease; ten other changes in the same helix are modelled and collected under [[Presenilin transmembrane domain 5]]. Study: [[Novel PSEN2 p.V226A in a Korean patient with atypical Alzheimer disease]]
- Catalytic partner of PSEN1 in the [[Gamma-secretase complex]]; its mutations are named among the causes of familial Alzheimer disease, whose neuronal phenotypes a low-activity wild-type subpopulation mimicked. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Reported with PSEN1 in 1995 as a second presenilin gene with dominant early-onset mutations; it encodes a gamma-secretase subunit. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- Promoter hypoacetylation in female hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia (-0.42, FDR 0.049), but four distal peaks were hyperacetylated in prefrontal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia (log2 fold change 0.47 to 0.60) while the transcript was nonsignificantly lower; the nearest peak was microglia-enriched. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Gut and brain mRNA did not clearly differ in mice, and colonic PS2 protein did not change between 3 and 12 months in wild-type or APP/PS1 mice. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- [[PSEN2 p.I144L]] (new, in an Alzheimer patient with family history) and [[PSEN2 p.A252T]] (earlier seen in African controls, in a behavioral variant FTD patient) were found among 102 Swedish memory-clinic patients; pathogenicity unclear. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
