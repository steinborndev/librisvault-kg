# PSEN2 p.A252T

A missense change of [[PSEN2]] (alanine 252 to threonine) at a residue conserved between PSEN1 and PSEN2, previously seen in two African controls (Mandenka and Yoruba), so its pathogenicity is unclear. Found in a patient with behavioral variant [[Frontotemporal dementia]] (male, onset 69 years) who also carried [[FUS p.229_231del]]. Population frequency 0.00023, SIFT 0.42, PolyPhen-2 0.172.
