# LDLR

LDLR encodes the low-density lipoprotein receptor, which takes up LDL particles and apolipoprotein E-bound lipoproteins into cells; statins raise its expression.

## Connections

- Variants: [[LDLR rs11669576]], [[LDLR rs5930]]
- G-allele carriers of rs11669576 had lower total and LDL cholesterol on simvastatin and lower total cholesterol and triglycerides on atorvastatin. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Ligand: [[APOE]]; drug class that upregulates it: [[Statin]]
- Surface LDLR was lower on APOE2 than APOE3 isogenic hiPSC-derived astrocytes in three of four lines, but receptor level tracked mutation type and not APOE genotype and did not explain amyloid-beta uptake; see [[Amyloid-beta clearance]]. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]; sibling receptor: [[LRP1]]
- Internalises ApoE-Aβ complexes at synapses; long-term spatial memory may require LDLR binding to ApoE4. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Plasma LDLR was higher in carriers of the APOE ε2 SNP [[APOE rs7412]], most in ε2 homozygotes, which may compensate for the poor binding of APOE2 to LDLR and explain their lower LDL cholesterol. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
