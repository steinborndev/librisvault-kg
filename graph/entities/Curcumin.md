# Curcumin

The yellow polyphenol of turmeric (Curcuma longa) with antioxidant and anti-apoptotic activity.

## Connections

- In cultured cortical neurons it blocked Aβ25-35 toxicity, steadied mitochondrial membrane potential and raised [[SIRT1]] with lower Bax, an effect removed by SIRT1 siRNA; it also countered acrolein toxicity. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Related: [[Sirtuin modulators]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
