# Phosphatidylcholine

Phosphatidylcholines (PCs) are the most abundant membrane glycerophospholipids, with a choline head group; they are also the fatty acid donor for cholesterol esterification in plasma and the parent of [[Lysophosphatidylcholine]].

## Connections

- Short-chain PC species were mostly higher in plasma of Alzheimer cases, a few polyunsaturated species lower, with no difference in the class sum; PC species were among those most often showing a nominal polygenic-score by diagnosis interaction.
