# CADASIL

CADASIL (cerebral autosomal dominant arteriopathy with subcortical infarcts and leukoencephalopathy) is an inherited small vessel disease caused by [[NOTCH3]] mutations, with severe headaches in young adulthood followed by strokes and dementia later in life. Most of the more than 25 reported causal mutations lie in exons 3 and 4 and gain or remove a cysteine residue.

## Connections

- Gene: [[NOTCH3]]; example variant [[NOTCH3 p.R332C]]
- Carriers of [[NOTCH3 p.A284T]] had Alzheimer rather than CADASIL features: onset at about 80 years, memory-led presentation, no clinical strokes and, in the one autopsy, no arteriosclerosis, lacunes or microinfarcts. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Related: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Vascular dementia]], [[Cerebral amyloid angiopathy]]
