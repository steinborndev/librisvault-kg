# KLK6

KLK6 encodes kallikrein-related peptidase 6 (neurosin), a serine protease that degrades amyloid beta and alpha-synuclein.

## Connections

- Mouse Klk6 rose in striatum and hippocampus 7 days after carotid stenosis; the authors cite earlier work showing about two-fold higher KLK6 in frontal cortex of vascular dementia patients. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Disease: [[Vascular dementia]]; related clearance genes: [[A2M]], [[PLAT]], [[CTSD]], [[CTSB]]
