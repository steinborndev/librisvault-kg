# NFIL3

NFIL3 encodes a basic leucine zipper transcription factor that represses or activates immune-cell genes.

## Connections

- One of four M4-module transcription factors (with [[ATF3]], [[BCL3]] and [[KLF4]]) that overlapped the exercise-responsive gene list in blood monocytes, but not kept in the final seven-gene panel. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
