# Himanthalia elongata

Himanthalia elongata is a brown seaweed of Atlantic European shores, eaten as sea spaghetti.

## Connections

- Its lipid extract had the highest saringosterol content of the European species, activated liver X receptors most strongly (comparable to [[Sargassum fusiforme]]) and regulated cholesterol efflux and lipid synthesis genes in [[CCF-STTG1]] cells alike. Total arsenic was about a fifth of that in S. fusiforme, so the authors propose it as a European alternative. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Compound: [[Saringosterol]]; also see [[Arsenic]]
