# TMEM97

TMEM97 encodes the sigma-2 receptor, a transmembrane protein reviewed as a postsynaptic binding site for amyloid-beta oligomers.

## Connections

- In temporal cortex of Alzheimer patients, amyloid-beta oligomers had candidate postsynaptic receptors in TMEM97, PrPc ([[PRNP]]) and PSD-95 ([[DLG4]]); in APP/PS1 mice and neurons derived from human pluripotent stem cells, oligomer binding to TMEM97 changed the transcription of specific genes, leading to neurodegeneration and neuroinflammation. The review's statement that TMEM97 is essential for postsynaptic oligomer binding and toxicity is its own reading of those findings.
