# Carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenyl hydrazone

CCCP is a protonophore that dissipates the mitochondrial membrane potential and is the standard experimental trigger of PINK1-Parkin mitophagy; its toxicity to mitochondria rules out therapeutic use.

## Connections

- Positive control (10 μM) in the mt-Keima screen: it lowered membrane potential, caused cell death, depended on [[ATG7]] and [[PINK1]], and was blocked by [[Acetylcysteine]], whereas [[ALT001]] was not. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
