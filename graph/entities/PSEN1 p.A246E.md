# PSEN1 p.A246E

A familial missense change from alanine to glutamate at residue 246 of [[PSEN1]], the γ-secretase catalytic subunit, that causes [[Familial Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[PSEN1]]; pathway: [[Amyloid-beta formation]]
- In an isogenic hiPSC line carrying it, converting APOE3 to APOE2 lowered secreted Aβ, the Aβ42/40 ratio, the sAPPβ/sAPPα ratio and the phospho-tau to total tau ratio; it was the only pair in which the ratio and phospho-tau fell. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]
