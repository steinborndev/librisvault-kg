# APOE p.R269G

A missense change of [[APOE]] (arginine 269 to glycine) previously reported as a likely cause of autosomal dominant hypercholesterolemia. Found in an early-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] patient with a family history (male, onset 56 years, APOE ε3/ε4) whose presentation differed from the earlier reported cases. Population frequency 0.00036, SIFT 0, PolyPhen-2 0.533.
