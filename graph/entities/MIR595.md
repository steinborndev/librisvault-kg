# MIR595

MIR595 encodes microRNA miR-595, a small non-coding RNA that represses messenger RNAs by binding their untranslated regions.

## Connections

- Predicted by sequence alignment to bind the untranslated region of [[NECTIN2]] at the major G allele of [[NECTIN2 rs6859]] but not the minor A allele; see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
