# Membrane attack complex

The membrane attack complex, also called the terminal complement complex (TCC), is the pore-forming assembly of C5b with C6 to C9 that ends the terminal complement pathway; clusterin inhibits its formation.

## Connections

- Plasma TCC, a marker of terminal pathway activation, was higher in adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (p < 0.001), very high in a subset, and lower in women. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
