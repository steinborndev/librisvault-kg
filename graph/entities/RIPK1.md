# RIPK1

RIPK1 encodes a kinase at the branch point of [[TNF]] receptor signalling, where it steers cells between NF-κB survival signalling, apoptosis and [[Necroptosis]].

## Connections

- Phospho-RIPK1 (Ser166) and urea-insoluble RIPK1 rose in neuronal cultures under TNF-α with Smac mimetic and Z-VAD-FMK; [[Necrostatin-1]] or RIPK1 shRNA blocked the downstream cascade. [[SQSTM1]] bound RIPK1 through its ZZ domain and promoted its activation. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Cascade partners: [[RIPK3]], [[MLKL]], [[CASP8]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
