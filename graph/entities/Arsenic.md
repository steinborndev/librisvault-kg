# Arsenic

Arsenic is a toxic metalloid that accumulates in brown seaweeds and limits how much of them can be eaten safely.

## Connections

- Total arsenic was highest in [[Sargassum fusiforme]] (67.26 mg/kg dry weight) and lowest in Ascophyllum nodosum (7.64), with [[Himanthalia elongata]] at 13.58; the toxic inorganic fraction was not measured. Cadmium was found only in S. fusiforme (1.03 mg/kg). Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
