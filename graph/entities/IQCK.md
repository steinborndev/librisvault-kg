# IQCK

IQCK encodes IQ motif containing protein K, a poorly characterized protein with a predicted calmodulin-binding IQ motif (about 34 kDa). It is a late-onset Alzheimer risk gene from genome-wide analyses, and has also been linked to obesity and obsessive-compulsive disorder.

## Connections

- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; reported to share a risk-allele direction with [[Obesity]] and to be associated at gene level with [[Obsessive-compulsive disorder]].
- In mouse brain the protein was highest in the brainstem and lowest in the hippocampus, was present in neurons, astrocytes and oligodendrocytes but not microglia, and was about 2-fold higher in Alzheimer than control hippocampus, with staining in amyloid plaques. Study: [[IQCK protein is raised in Alzheimer hippocampus and found in amyloid plaques]]
- Named as a novel locus in a GWAS meta-analysis of non-Hispanic whites. Study: [[SNP association study of Alzheimer disease in southern Chinese population]]
