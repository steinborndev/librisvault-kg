# NOTCH3 p.A198E

A missense change from alanine to glutamate at residue 198 of [[NOTCH3]] (exon 4), in the JAG1-binding region.

## Connections

- Gene: [[NOTCH3]]
- Inherited with [[NOTCH3 p.G248A]] by an affected pair of half first cousins in Utah (frequency 0.0002) and absent from the whole-genome samples; it is unclear which of the two matters. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
