# APP p.F681Y

The rodent-to-human substitution at residue 681 (APP770 numbering) of [[APP]], phenylalanine to tyrosine. Its extra hydroxyl group is modelled as a hydrogen-bond donor to asparagine 294 of [[BACE1]], strengthening binding of the humanized β-CTF.

## Connections

- Gene: [[APP]]; enzyme: [[BACE1]]
- Introduced with [[APP p.G676R]] and [[APP p.R684H]] in the [[Humanized App mouse]] and [[Humanized App Psen1 M139T rat]]
- Study: [[Humanized APP knock-in mouse and rat with PSEN1 M139T for Alzheimer disease]]
