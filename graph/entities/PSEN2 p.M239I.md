# PSEN2 p.M239I

A missense change from methionine to isoleucine at residue 239 of [[PSEN2]], in the fifth transmembrane helix, and a common Alzheimer-associated change in Brazilian, European and Chinese patients, with onset mostly in the 40s to 60s and sometimes seizures or motor impairment; functional studies showed a raised amyloid beta 42 to 40 ratio and it may alter calcium handling.
