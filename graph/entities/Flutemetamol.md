# Flutemetamol

Flutemetamol is a fluorine-18-labelled amyloid-binding radiotracer, a derivative of Pittsburgh compound B, used in amyloid PET.

## Connections

- Named among the PET tracers that bind amyloid-beta deposits in the brain and so allow plaque burden to be visualised and quantified in living subjects. Study: [[Amyloid PET imaging in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Method: [[Amyloid PET]]
- Target: [[Amyloid-beta]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Used for visually read amyloid PET (GM-EDGE method; 90-minute late phase) to establish amyloid positivity in 41 of 62 memory-clinic patients with amnestic mild cognitive impairment. Study: [[Mild behavioral impairment with APOE and BDNF in early Alzheimer disease]]
