# Pioglitazone

Pioglitazone is a thiazolidinedione antidiabetic drug and agonist of [[PPARG]].

## Connections

- PPARγ positive control (10 µM) in the reporter and expression assays; it raised [[GFAP]] expression in [[CCF-STTG1]] cells where the seaweed extracts lowered it. Benefits of pioglitazone and rosiglitazone on cognition in patients with Alzheimer disease, especially with diabetes, are cited in the introduction. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Together with cholesterol it restored differentiation of LRP1-deficient oligodendrocyte precursor cells. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
