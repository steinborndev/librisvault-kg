# MYO1F

MYO1F encodes an unconventional class I myosin found in macrophages, neutrophils, dendritic cells and natural killer cells, where it supports motility and cytokine secretion.

## Connections

- Higher genetically predicted expression went with higher Alzheimer risk (estimate 1.083), with expression largely restricted to macrophages; the authors tie it to pro-inflammatory macrophage polarisation and [[IL1B]] release.
