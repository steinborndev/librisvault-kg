# NR1H3

NR1H3 encodes liver X receptor alpha (LXRα), an oxysterol-sensing nuclear receptor that heterodimerises with [[RXRA]] and drives genes of cholesterol efflux and lipogenesis.

## Connections

- A reporter assay with the murine receptor in four human cell lines showed that lipid extracts of all seven brown seaweeds tested activated LXRα, most strongly the extract of [[Himanthalia elongata]], comparable to [[Sargassum fusiforme]].
