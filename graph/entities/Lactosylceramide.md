# Lactosylceramide

Lactosylceramide is a glycosphingolipid of the sphingolipid family, a ceramide carrying a lactose head group.

## Connections

- Two lactosylceramide species were lower in carriers of the risk allele of [[ABCA7 p.G1527A]], visible from the youngest adult age group; the authors read this as support for the lipid function of [[ABCA7]].
