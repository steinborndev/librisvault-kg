# TYROBP

TYROBP encodes DAP12, a small transmembrane adaptor with an immunoreceptor tyrosine-based activation motif. It pairs with [[TREM2]] on myeloid cells and, once the receptor is engaged, recruits [[SYK]].

## Connections

- Co-expressed with mouse TREM2 in HEK293 Flp-In cells (as a DAP12-P2A-TREM2 construct) to reconstitute signalling for the antibody [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]]; the introduction recalls that full-length TREM2 with DAP12 forms the complex that activates phospho-SYK. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- Partner gene: [[TREM2]]
- Modestly induced in APP/PS1 brain, unchanged by Sirt3 deletion; the authors call it the TREM2 adaptor and consider its induction neuroprotective. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- One of 22 genes common to the Apoe-driven ivory module and human post-mortem microglial modules; lowered in Clu knockout mouse brain. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
