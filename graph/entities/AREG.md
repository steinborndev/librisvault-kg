# AREG

AREG encodes amphiregulin, an EGF-family growth factor that acts as a ligand of the EGF receptor.

## Connections

- One of four ligand-receptor genes whose blood monocyte transcript level changed with exercise (with [[TNF]], [[CCR1]] and [[APP]]); unlike the other three it was not carried into the final seven-gene panel. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
