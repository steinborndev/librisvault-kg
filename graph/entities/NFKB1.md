# NFKB1

NFKB1 encodes the p105 precursor and the p50 subunit of the NF-κB transcription factor family, a regulator of inflammation, cell survival and immune responses.

## Connections

- Transcript was higher in the caudate nucleus of both [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]] postmortem brain than in controls (bulk RNA-seq, 5 controls, 6 Alzheimer, 3 Parkinson cases); the authors propose it as a shared biomarker. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Part of the TNF-alpha signaling via NF-κB gene set that ranked first or second in both diseases; see [[NF-kappaB signaling]] and [[TNF]].
- Rose together with [[NFE2L2]] and [[SQSTM1]] in the same tissue.
