# CYP2C19 rs4244285

The loss-of-function variant defining the CYP2C19 *2 allele (c.681G>A, called 19154G>A in the study); carriers of one copy are intermediate and carriers of two copies are poor metabolizers.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CYP2C19]]
- Genotyped in 1006 Alzheimer disease patients; *1A/*2A intermediate metabolizers were 25.8% and *2A/*2A poor metabolizers 1.4%. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
