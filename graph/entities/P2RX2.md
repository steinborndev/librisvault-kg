# P2RX2

P2RX2 encodes the P2X2 subunit of the ATP-gated P2X cation channel family, a paralog of [[P2RX4]].

## Connections

- Mouse P2X2 co-expressed with ApoE in COS-7 cells did not change ApoE levels (1.18 vs 1.34 unnormalised), whereas P2X4 did, which the authors read as P2X4-specific regulation. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
