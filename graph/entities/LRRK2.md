# LRRK2

LRRK2 encodes a large kinase whose mutations are the commonest genetic cause of dominantly inherited [[Parkinson disease]].

## Connections

- Seven missense variants were found in Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients, including [[LRRK2 p.R1628P]], [[LRRK2 p.N551K]] and [[LRRK2 p.R1398Q]]; the authors suggest exonic variants may add to Alzheimer susceptibility. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
