# RELA

RELA encodes p65, the transcriptionally active subunit of the NF-kappa-B complex; phosphorylation of p65 marks activation of the inflammatory signalling that follows [[TLR4]] engagement.

## Connections

- The phosphorylated-to-total p65 ratio fell with ajugol and isoacteoside in a mouse Alzheimer model and in [[BV2]] cells, most clearly with ajugol. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Upstream and downstream: [[TLR4]], [[NFKBIA]], [[NLRP3]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- The p-NF-κB p65/p65 ratio was raised in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and lowered by [[Magnolol]] at 20 and 40 mg/kg and by [[Donepezil]] (p < 0.001, n = 3 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- p65 moved into the nucleus within 45 minutes of LPS in iPSC microglia-like cells without [[CHRFAM7A]], and stayed from 2 h to 6 h with it; phospho-p65 stayed raised 4 h after Aβ1-42 only in carriers. See [[NF-kappaB nuclear translocation dynamics]]. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
- ChIP-qPCR showed RelA bound a predicted NF-κB site in the human UVRAG promoter and that TNF-α with Smac mimetic and Z-VAD-FMK reduced the binding and nuclear RelA. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
