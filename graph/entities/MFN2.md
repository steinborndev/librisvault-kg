# MFN2

MFN2 encodes mitofusin 2, an outer mitochondrial membrane GTPase that mediates mitochondrial fusion and is degraded during [[Mitophagy]], so its level serves as a marker of mitochondrial turnover.

## Connections

- MFN2 protein fell in mouse hippocampus after intranasal [[ALT001]], consistent with mitochondrial clearance, in wild-type and 5×FAD animals. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
