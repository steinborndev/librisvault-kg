# OPRM1

OPRM1 encodes the μ-opioid receptor (MOR), the main target of morphine-type opioids, activated by the peptide agonist [[DAMGO]].

## Connections

- In rat [[PC12]] and Swedish-mutant [[SH-SY5Y]] cells, MOR activation by DAMGO raised [[BACE1]] mRNA, protein and activity, sped [[APP]] cleavage and raised Aβ42, the opposite of [[OPRD1]]. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
- The authors link DADLE's pro-amyloid effects in earlier work to its side action at MOR. Context: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Amyloid-beta formation]].
