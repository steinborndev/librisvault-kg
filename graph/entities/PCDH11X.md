# PCDH11X

PCDH11X is the X-linked protocadherin 11 gene, at Xq21.3, encoding a cell-adhesion protein of the cadherin superfamily. A common variant in it, [[PCDH11X rs5984894]], was reported as associated with susceptibility to [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]], but an independent study did not replicate the association.
