# Chlorogenic acid

Chlorogenic acid is a caffeic acid ester of quinic acid (molecular weight about 354) widespread in coffee and plants, with antioxidant activity and reported protection against oxidative neurotoxicity.

## Connections

- Quantified at 1.80 mg/g in [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]]; the authors cite evidence that it crosses the blood-brain barrier. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Co-constituents: [[Rutin]], [[Kaempferol-3-O-rutinoside]]
- Used as the inclusion criterion for seven heat-clearing herbs (alongside Alzheimer relevance), and proposed with [[Quercetin]] as a shared anti-inflammatory active ingredient, though its own targets were not shown in the results.
