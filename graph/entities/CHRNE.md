# CHRNE

CHRNE encodes the epsilon subunit of the nicotinic cholinergic (acetylcholine) receptor.

## Connections

- The 3' UTR variant [[CHRNE rs72835061]] is associated with higher risk of [[Alzheimer disease]], and its risk allele raises CHRNE expression, which the authors read as a possible link between cholinergic function and disease risk. Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
- A new allele-specific association between the GWAS SNP rs113260531 and CHRNE was found in the prefrontal cortex and caudate nucleus; the same SNP also tracks [[RABEP1]] and [[ZNF232]]. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
