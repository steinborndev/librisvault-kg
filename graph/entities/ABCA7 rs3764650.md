# ABCA7 rs3764650

rs3764650 is an [[ABCA7]] SNP associated with [[Alzheimer disease]] risk; its minor G allele has been linked to more neuritic plaques, lower cerebrospinal fluid amyloid-beta, cortical and hippocampal atrophy, and a later onset with shorter disease duration.

## Connections

- Gene: [[ABCA7]]
- Each G allele moved the midpoint age of cognitive decline earlier by about 1.75 years in 299 Han Chinese Alzheimer and MCI patients, with no effect on the rate and no replication in ADNI. The same group had earlier found the GG genotype protective against disease risk in Taiwan, whereas it raises risk in Caucasians and African Americans. Study: [[Genetic effects on cognitive decline trajectories in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
