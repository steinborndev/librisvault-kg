# CCL2

CCL2 encodes the chemokine MCP-1, which draws monocytes and microglia to sites of inflammation and is an NF-κB target gene.

## Connections

- In iPSC microglia-like cells the secretion of MCP-1 rose after [[Lipopolysaccharide]] in the line without [[CHRFAM7A]], and together with [[IL6]] and [[CXCL8]] it was named a driver of the NF-κB-mediated difference between carrier and non-carrier lines at baseline and after LPS.
