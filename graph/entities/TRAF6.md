# TRAF6

TRAF6 encodes an E3 ubiquitin ligase and adaptor that lies downstream of the [[IRAK1]]/[[IRAK2]] complex and drives NF-κB activation; its mRNA carries a binding site for [[MIR146A]].

## Connections

- Unchanged in rat primary astrocytes treated with fibrillar Aβ1-42, which fits the absence of cytokine induction; an anti-miR-146a-5p oligonucleotide raised TRAF6 mRNA (p = 0.0035) together with IL6, IL1B and CXCL1, and the authors suggest TRAF6 level sets a threshold for cytokine production. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Process: [[NF-kappaB signaling]]
