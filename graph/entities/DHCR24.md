# DHCR24

DHCR24 encodes 24-dehydrocholesterol reductase, which converts desmosterol to cholesterol.

## Connections

- Expression fell in [[CCF-STTG1]] cells treated with the seaweed lipid extracts, and also with [[T0901317]] and [[Bexarotene]]. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Related synthesis genes: [[DHCR7]], [[HMGCR]], [[SREBF2]]
- Knockdown activates Ras/MEK/ERK signalling and tau hyperphosphorylation in the cholesterol-synthesis literature reviewed. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
