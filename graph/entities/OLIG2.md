# OLIG2

OLIG2 encodes a basic helix-loop-helix transcription factor expressed in the oligodendrocyte lineage and used as an oligodendrocyte marker.

## Connections

- The H3K27ac peak nearest the OLIG2 promoter was hyperacetylated in sorted NeuN-negative, Pu.1-negative nuclei relative to neurons and microglia (log2 fold change 1.23, FDR 3.8e-52), one of the signs that this population is enriched for oligodendrocytes; Olig2 antibody was later used to sort oligodendrocyte nuclei for RT-qPCR.
