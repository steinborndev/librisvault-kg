# Bin1 heterozygous mouse

A mouse with one deleted copy of Bin1, the mouse orthologue of [[BIN1]], kept on a [[C57BL-6 mouse]] background by crossing heterozygotes to wild type because homozygous loss is lethal.

## Connections

- At 6 months, whole-brain RNA-seq of females showed only 16 differentially expressed genes (down genes enriched for [[Endocytosis]] and Fc gamma receptor phagocytosis), a small drop in Bin1 itself (log2 fold change -0.3) and no module of its own, yet its profile correlated with several human immune and neuronal modules. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Grouped with the [[Cd2ap heterozygous mouse]] in principal component analysis, nearest to controls; see [[Mouse-human module comparison]]
