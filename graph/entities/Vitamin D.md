# Vitamin D

Vitamin D is a fat-soluble secosteroid whose active form signals through the nuclear receptor [[VDR]]; deficiency has been linked to higher risk of [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Proposed as an upstream influence on APOE2 microglia, which show a VDR motif in open chromatin and raised [[IL10RA]]: vitamin D acting via VDR would raise anti-inflammatory IL-10 and suppress pro-inflammatory cytokines. The authors note APOE4 carriers may have higher vitamin D levels, so supplementation could help non-carriers more. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
