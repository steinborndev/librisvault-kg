# CDK5

CDK5 encodes a neuronal cyclin-dependent kinase that, activated as CDK5/p25, drives cytoskeletal damage and cell death and enhances tau phosphorylation by [[GSK3B]].

## Connections

- Named as a target of [[Alsterpaullone]], which inhibits it less strongly than GSK3B. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- In mice fed a high-salt diet, lower nitric oxide from cerebral endothelial cells let calpain-2 cleave p35, which activated CDK5 and raised tau phosphorylation and aggregation in hippocampus and neocortex; a CDK5 peptide inhibitor protected cognition. Cortical CDK5 activity was also higher in 5xFAD than wild-type mice after tau filament injection. See [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Tyrosine phosphorylation was 58% higher when active [[PTK2]] was added to 3xTg-AD hippocampal lysate, and Y15 phosphorylation rose 49% in hippocampus of FAK-overexpressing mice; CDK5 is proposed to raise APP expression and tau phosphorylation downstream of FYN, CBL and paxillin. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Lower in the C2 than the C1 Alzheimer brain subtype defined by its activator [[CDK5R1]] (subtype AUC 0.766); see [[Reelin signaling pathway]]. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
- A validated target of [[MIR650]]: the miR-650 duplex lowered a CDK5 3' untranslated region reporter (abolished by deleting the site), and AAV-miR-650 in the dentate gyrus of 1-year-old [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals lowered hippocampal CDK5 protein and transcript, alongside fewer plaques and more MAP2-positive neurons. Study: [[MicroRNA-650 targets CDK5 and lessens amyloid pathology in APP-PSEN1 mice]]
- Hippocampal total CDK5 did not differ between wild-type and 5xFAD mice or with [[D-Pinitol]], but the p35 to CDK5 ratio fell with 18 weeks of treatment while p25 stayed raised in 5xFAD mice; the inference that the compound lowers CDK5 activity rests on these blot ratios alone. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
