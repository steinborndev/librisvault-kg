# PDPK1

PDPK1 encodes PDK1, the PI3K-dependent master kinase that activates [[AKT1]] and [[RPS6KB1]].

## Connections

- Expression was 57% higher in FAK-overexpressing 3xTg-AD hippocampus. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
