# Clemastine

Clemastine is an antihistamine with pro-myelinating activity in oligodendrocyte precursor cells.

## Connections

- In APP/PS1 mice it increased myelin renewal and improved memory tasks without changing plaque number. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
