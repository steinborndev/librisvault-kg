# SLC2A1

SLC2A1 encodes glucose transporter-1 (GLUT-1), which carries glucose across the blood-brain barrier and is highly expressed in brain endothelium.

## Connections

- Higher in rat frontal cortex after restraint stress, alongside lower [[CLDN5]], [[OCLN]] and [[GFAP]] signal. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
