# CHRNA4

CHRNA4 encodes the alpha4 subunit of the neuronal nicotinic acetylcholine receptor; with [[CHRNB2]] it forms the high-affinity alpha4/beta2 receptor, which is expressed in brain and immune cells and linked to cognition.

## Connections

- mRNA in blood mononuclear cells was lower than in controls in both dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer disease, and lower again in Alzheimer disease than in Lewy body dementia (median fold change about 0.0 against 0.3); it correlated positively with CHRNA3 in both diseases and with CHRNB4 in Alzheimer disease.
