# CDK5R1

CDK5R1 encodes p35, the neuronal activator of [[CDK5]]; it acts in the [[Reelin signaling pathway]] and in neuronal differentiation and migration.

## Connections

- Lower in Alzheimer brain than in controls in three public expression datasets (entorhinal cortex, hippocampus and superior frontal gyrus, not postcentral gyrus), and its level was used to define two Alzheimer subtypes with different immune cell profiles. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
- In mice on a high-salt diet, calpain-2 cleaves p35 and activates CDK5, raising tau phosphorylation. See [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- p35 protein was higher in 5xFAD than wild-type hippocampus and the p35 to CDK5 ratio fell after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]]; an earlier rat study cited by the authors found the compound lowered CDK5R1 and tau phosphorylation. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
