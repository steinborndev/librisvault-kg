# Mitochondrial dysfunction

Mitochondrial dysfunction is a reduced capacity of mitochondria to make energy and maintain their own genome, shown for example by fewer mitochondrial DNA copies per cell and a lower mitochondrial membrane potential. It is an early and consistent feature of [[Alzheimer disease]] brain.

## Connections

- In cultured cell lines under [[Hydrogen peroxide]] stress, falling mitochondrial DNA copy number and membrane potential coincided with a rise in [[TOMM40]] RNA; see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
- In [[Tg2576 mouse]] hippocampus, lower glucose-driven oxygen consumption with higher lactate pointed to glycolysis compensating for impaired oxidative phosphorylation, and [[12A12 monoclonal antibody]] partly reversed it; see [[Glycolysis]] and [[Tau antibody 12A12 and amyloid-beta in Tg2576 mouse hippocampus and retina]].
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Gene: [[TOMM40]]
- Sirt3 deletion, which impairs mitochondrial function, is used to model [[Metabolic syndrome]] in the brain and is shown to lower [[IDE]]. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- Knocking down [[DICER1]] in cultured neurons reduced mitochondrial membrane potential. Study: [[Dicer1 loss in APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse brain via amyloid-beta 42 repression of Nrf2]]
- The upregulated Alzheimer brain gene set (including [[BNIP3]] and [[TOMM70]]) was associated with mitochondrial dysfunction and inflammation. Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
- Down-regulated [[Tricarboxylic acid cycle]] enzyme genes in Alzheimer brain and blood extend the picture to the cycle itself. Study: [[TCA cycle enzyme genes in Alzheimer brain and blood transcriptomes]]
- Loss of [[NNT]] in an Alzheimer mouse model depleted [[NADPH]]-dependent antioxidants and, with amyloid-beta, collapsed membrane potential and energy stores. Study: [[NNT deficiency impairs synapses and cognition in Alzheimer mouse models]]
- Principal component analysis of caudate nucleus RNA-seq from [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]] cases pointed to mitochondrial genes (MT-ATP6P1, [[MT-CYB]]), with altered [[SLC25A6]] and [[GLP1R]]. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- APP Swedish/Indiana expression in [[SH-SY5Y]] cells and [[PS2APP mouse]] hippocampus raised mitochondrial ROS and lowered ATP and membrane potential; [[ALT001]] reversed both through [[Mitophagy]], and the cell effect needed Rab9. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- [[D-ribose]] lowered membrane potential (JC-1), raised reactive oxygen species and cytochrome c release and increased apoptosis in [[Neuro2a]] cells; [[Berberine]] reversed this unless mitophagy was blocked with [[Mdivi-1]] or PINK1 siRNA. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
