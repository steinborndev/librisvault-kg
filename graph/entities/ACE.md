# ACE

ACE encodes angiotensin-converting enzyme, which raises blood pressure by producing angiotensin II. An Alu insertion-deletion polymorphism in intron 16 changes serum enzyme levels, with higher levels in the presence of the deletion allele.

## Connections

- Variant: [[ACE Alu insertion-deletion]]
- In Alzheimer disease outpatients the insertion allele went with lower total and LDL cholesterol regardless of statin use, and the authors suggest lower enzyme levels reduce angiotensin II drive on HMG-CoA reductase. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Related genes in the same lipid analysis: [[HMGCR]], [[CETP]], [[LDLR]]
- One of five late-onset Alzheimer GWAS-list genes that are also targets of drugs in phase 1 to 3 Alzheimer trials, with [[TREM2]], [[CD33]], [[CHRNA2]] and [[PRSS8]]. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
