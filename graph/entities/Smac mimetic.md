# Smac mimetic

Smac mimetics are small molecules that imitate the mitochondrial protein Smac/DIABLO and inhibit cIAP1/2, which removes ubiquitin-dependent survival signalling downstream of [[TNFRSF1A]] and favours caspase-8 activation or, with caspases blocked, [[Necroptosis]].

## Connections

- A commercial Smac mimetic (MCE HY-12600) was combined with TNF-α and [[Z-VAD-FMK]] (TSZ) to induce neuronal necroptosis; alone it markedly increased TNF-α-induced death and, by inhibiting cIAP1/2, lowered nuclear [[RELA]] and so [[UVRAG]] transcription. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
