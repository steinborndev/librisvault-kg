# BDNF p.V66M

The common valine-to-methionine substitution at codon 66 of [[BDNF]] (rs6265, G196A). The Met allele is described as a risk polymorphism that worsens clinical progression of Alzheimer disease and raises the risk of late-life depression.

## Connections

- Met carriage (20 of 52 genotyped patients) was unrelated to informant-rated [[Mild behavioral impairment]] severity or depressive symptoms in amnestic [[Mild cognitive impairment]] due to Alzheimer disease, but after adjusting for [[APOE]] ε4 and the APOE-by-BDNF interaction it was associated with higher self-reported [[Anxiety]] (B = 10.73, p = 0.012, uncorrected).
