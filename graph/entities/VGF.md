# VGF

VGF encodes a neuropeptide precursor induced by neurotrophins and expressed in neurons. Its protein abundance in prefrontal cortex was linked to the Alzheimer polygenic risk score, to dexterity and to cognitive decline.

## Connections

- Candidate mediator of [[Motor impairment]] in people with high genetic risk of [[Alzheimer disease]]; a direct upstream node of the dexterity score in a Bayesian network.
- Reported as dysregulated in brains of both Alzheimer disease and [[Parkinson disease]].
- Study: [[Alzheimer polygenic risk score and motor impairment in older adults]]
- Down in both temporal and frontal lobe of Alzheimer brains, the only strongly shared gene of the two lobe-level gene lists. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
