# MAPK3

MAPK3 encodes ERK1, a mitogen-activated protein kinase of the ERK pathway; it is the paralogue of [[MAPK1]] (ERK2).

## Connections

- Phosphorylation at Y204 (+64%), T207 (+11%) and S265 (+26%) rose in FAK-overexpressing 3xTg-AD hippocampus, a change read as feeding both impaired insulin signalling and [[CDK1]]-linked cell cycle re-entry. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; mouse model: [[3xTg-AD mouse]]
- Phospho-ERK1/2 (T202/Y204) was restored by [[LM-031]] (90% to 117%) in ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed [[SH-SY5Y]] cells with total ERK unchanged (see also [[MAPK1]]). Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
