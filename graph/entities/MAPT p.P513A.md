# MAPT p.P513A

A rare missense change from proline to alanine at residue 513 of [[MAPT]] (c.1537C>G), first reported in a Chinese family with [[Progressive non-fluent aphasia]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[MAPT]]; related disease: [[Frontotemporal dementia]]
- Found in a Thai man (patient #2) diagnosed with Alzheimer disease and logopenic aphasia, who also carried [[TREM2 p.L211P]]; PolyPhen-2 HumDiv 0.823, and an ExAC South Asian allele frequency of 0.0001217. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
