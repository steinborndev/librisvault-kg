# HMC3

HMC3 is an immortalized human microglial cell line, derived from primary human microglia.

## Connections

- Negative for [[IQCK]] by immunocytochemistry with two different antibodies, matching the lack of IQCK in Iba1-positive mouse brain microglia. Study: [[IQCK protein is raised in Alzheimer hippocampus and found in amyloid plaques]]
- Compared with [[SH-SY5Y]], [[HeLa]] and [[CHO]] in that experiment.
- Microglia-like line showing both the APOE promoter to TOMM40 intron 2 to 3 contact and the APOE exon 4 island to APOC1 promoter contact. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
