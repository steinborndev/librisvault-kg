# Cholesteryl ester

Cholesteryl esters (ChEs) are [[Cholesterol]] esterified to a fatty acid, the form in which cholesterol is stored and carried in the core of lipoprotein particles; in plasma they are formed mainly by lecithin-cholesterol acyltransferase.

## Connections

- A ChE-based model separated Alzheimer cases from controls at more than 80% AUC, and more than 30% of plasma ChE species associated differently with the [[PICALM]], [[SLC24A4]] and [[SORL1]] risk SNPs in cases and controls.
