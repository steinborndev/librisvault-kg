# PSEN2 p.M239V

A missense change from methionine to valine at residue 239 of [[PSEN2]], in the fifth transmembrane helix, considered pathogenic in several studies. Structure prediction suggests abnormal helix motion from the smaller, less hydrophobic valine.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PSEN2]]; region: [[Presenilin transmembrane domain 5]]
