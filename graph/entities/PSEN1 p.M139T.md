# PSEN1 p.M139T

A missense change of [[PSEN1]] (methionine 139 to threonine) that causes early-onset [[Familial Alzheimer disease]], with a mean onset between 39 and 51 years. Knocked into the endogenous rat Psen1 gene, it raised the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio and lowered Aβ43 without changing total Aβ and without plaques at 2 years.
