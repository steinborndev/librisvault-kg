# SLC1A7

SLC1A7 encodes a glutamate transporter of the EAAT family (EAAT5).

## Connections

- Up in men and down in women with Alzheimer disease in both hippocampal subfields, and the top opposite-direction gene between the sexes in both subfields; it also ranks among the top opposite genes of the male-versus-female contrasts in CA1.
