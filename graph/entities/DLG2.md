# DLG2

DLG2 encodes PSD-93 (chapsyn-110), a membrane-associated guanylate kinase scaffold of the postsynaptic density that clusters glutamate receptors and signalling proteins.

## Connections

- PSD93 protein was lowered in the brains of [[TgCRND8 mouse]] animals and restored by [[Magnolol]] 40 mg/kg and by [[Donepezil]] (western blot, n = 3 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
