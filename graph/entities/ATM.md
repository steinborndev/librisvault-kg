# ATM

ATM encodes the kinase that senses DNA double-strand breaks and couples cell cycle re-entry to DNA repair or neuronal death; its levels and activity are reduced in Alzheimer brain.

## Connections

- ATM signalling pathway was downregulated in FAK-overexpressing stimulated 3xTg-AD hippocampus, alongside more neuronal death signalling. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; mouse model: [[3xTg-AD mouse]]
