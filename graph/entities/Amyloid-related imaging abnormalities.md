# Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities

Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA) are MRI changes, such as vasogenic oedema or microbleeds, seen in people given anti-amyloid immunotherapy.

## Connections

- Three cases were recorded in the CAD106 cohort of Generation Study 1 ([[CAD106]]); the review treats them as a manageable adverse event of active amyloid immunisation. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Related: [[Anti-amyloid antibody therapy]]
- The form ARIA-E (edema) can progress to brain hemorrhage and was the safety worry about high-dose [[Aducanumab]]; antibody-amyloid complexes activating microglia may explain it. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
