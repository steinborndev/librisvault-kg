# NOTCH3 p.G248A

A missense change from glycine to alanine at residue 248 of [[NOTCH3]] (exon 5), in the JAG1-binding region.

## Connections

- Gene: [[NOTCH3]]
- Found, with [[NOTCH3 p.A198E]], in both affected half first cousins of a Utah high-risk pedigree (frequency 0.0002), probably inherited together; not seen in the whole-genome samples, and no features of [[CADASIL]] in the family. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
