# Familial Danish dementia

An inherited dementia caused by mutations in [[ITM2B]], with amyloid angiopathy and neurofibrillary tangles similar to those of [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Reviewed together with [[Familial British dementia]] as sharing pathophysiological pathways to dementia with Alzheimer disease; both originate from BRI2 (ITM2B) mutations. Study: [[BRI2 in familial British and Danish dementia and Alzheimer disease review]]
- Pathology: [[Cerebral amyloid angiopathy]], [[Neurofibrillary tangle]]
