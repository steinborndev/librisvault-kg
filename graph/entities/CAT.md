# CAT

CAT encodes catalase, the peroxisomal enzyme that breaks down hydrogen peroxide and so limits [[Oxidative stress]].

## Connections

- One of five hub genes (with [[ALB]], [[ACTB]], [[GAPDH]] and [[CASP3]]) by degree in the protein interaction network of 95 targets shared between ginger compounds and Alzheimer disease genes; it was not docked or simulated.
