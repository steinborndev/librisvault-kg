# Glutamate

Glutamate is the main excitatory neurotransmitter of the brain; in excess it drives calcium overload and cell death ([[Excitotoxicity]]).

## Connections

- At 100 μM it produced a calcium rise and early propidium iodide uptake in cultured mouse cortical neurons, both larger in neurons with low gamma-secretase activity or pre-treated with [[DAPT]]; no rise occurred without extracellular calcium. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
