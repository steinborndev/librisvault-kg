# ANGPT2

ANGPT2 encodes angiopoietin 2, a ligand of the TIE2 receptor that destabilizes blood vessels and regulates vessel remodelling.

## Connections

- Upregulated in five of six post-mortem brain regions in Alzheimer disease (all except parahippocampal gyrus) and a co-expression hub gene; discussed as a marker of vascular instability and blood-brain barrier breakdown. Study: [[Predictive gene expression signatures of Alzheimer disease in six brain regions]]
