# Osthol

Osthol is a natural coumarin (C15H16O3) extracted from Cnidium monnieri, described as anti-inflammatory, anti-oxidant, anti-apoptotic and anti-tumour.

## Connections

- Conjugated to [[Zinc oxide]] nanoparticles, it restored viability and synaptic proteins in APP-overexpressing [[BE2-M17]] cells, raised [[MIR132]] and lowered [[GSK3B]] and [[CTNNB1]] markers. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
