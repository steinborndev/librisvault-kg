# Increased intestinal permeability

A loosened gut epithelial barrier, measured in mice as more orally gavaged FITC-dextran appearing in serum, and linked to inflammation and to the composition of the [[Gut microbiota]].

## Connections

- Higher in older mice than young ones and highest in 12-month-old [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals, together with more Bacteroides, Prevotella and Ruminococcus and less Akkermansia, Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Context: [[Gut-brain axis]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Inferred in 8-month-old 5xFAD mice from higher plasma [[Lipopolysaccharide]] and intestinal [[TLR4]] and [[CLDN3]] mRNA, and lower [[OCLN]]; [[D-Pinitol]] moved these toward wild-type values. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
