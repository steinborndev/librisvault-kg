# TOMM20

TOMM20 encodes a receptor subunit of the translocase of the outer mitochondrial membrane and is widely used as a marker of the mitochondrial outer membrane.

## Connections

- Co-localization of LC3A/B with TOMM20 in [[Neuro2a]] cells, a readout of [[Mitophagy]], was reduced by [[D-ribose]] and restored by [[Berberine]]. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Related outer-membrane genes: [[TOMM40]], [[TOMM70]]
