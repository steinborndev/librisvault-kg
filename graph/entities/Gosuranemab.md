# Gosuranemab

Gosuranemab (BIIB092) is a monoclonal antibody against extracellular N-terminal fragments of tau (eTau), tested in [[Mild cognitive impairment]] and mild [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- A Phase-2 trial failed on all clinical endpoints although the antibody lowered eTau in cerebrospinal fluid; the author suggests eTau may be the wrong target and reminds that a marker that correlates with progression is not necessarily causal. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- Target protein: [[MAPT]]; related lesion [[Neurofibrillary tangle]]
