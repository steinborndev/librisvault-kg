# MARK1

MARK1 encodes a microtubule affinity regulating kinase that acts upstream of DIXDC1 in controlling cytoskeletal dynamics and dendritic morphology.

## Connections

- Levels were 48% lower in FAK-overexpressing 3xTg-AD hippocampus, proposed to reduce DIXDC1 phosphorylation and limit basal dendrite remodelling. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; mouse model: [[3xTg-AD mouse]]
