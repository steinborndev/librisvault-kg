# Zinc oxide

Zinc oxide (ZnO) is an inorganic zinc compound used here as a nanoparticle carrier; zinc supplementation has also been proposed to delay dementia.

## Connections

- Laser-ablated zinc oxide nanoparticles (mean 35 nm) were conjugated with [[Osthol]] (about 149 nm) and tested in an [[Alzheimer disease]] cell model. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
