# Amiloride

Amiloride is a potassium-sparing diuretic that blocks sodium channels and, at high doses in cell work, inhibits macropinocytosis.

## Connections

- At 100 μmol/L it cut uptake of [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]] by 67.5% in [[bEnd.3]] cells, indicating macropinocytosis as a main entry route there. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
