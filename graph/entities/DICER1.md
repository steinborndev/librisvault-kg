# DICER1

DICER1 encodes Dicer, the ribonuclease III enzyme that cuts precursor microRNAs and double-stranded RNA into the short duplexes that load onto the silencing complex; it is needed for neuronal survival and neurite growth.

## Connections

- Protein and mRNA were lower in the hippocampus of [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] animals. Chronic [[Amyloid-beta]] 42 exposure lowered it through weaker [[NFE2L2]] binding to antioxidant response elements in its promoter, and re-expressing it rescued neurite deficits in cultured neurons and spatial learning in the mice. Study: [[Dicer1 loss in APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse brain via amyloid-beta 42 repression of Nrf2]]
