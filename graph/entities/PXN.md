# PXN

PXN encodes paxillin, a focal adhesion scaffold phosphorylated by [[PTK2]]; high phosphorylation of both is seen in dystrophic neurites around plaque cores in Alzheimer brain.

## Connections

- Y31 (+48%) and Y118 (+107%) phosphorylation rose in FAK-overexpressing 3xTg-AD hippocampus. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; mouse model: [[3xTg-AD mouse]]
