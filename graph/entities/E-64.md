# E-64

E-64 is an irreversible, broad-spectrum inhibitor of cysteine proteases, including the lysosomal cathepsins and calpains, used as a research tool.

## Connections

- Overnight 10 µM E-64 raised ApoE in wild-type macrophages but not in P2X4-null ones, the first sign that a cysteine protease degrades ApoE downstream of [[P2RX4]]; narrowed to [[CTSB]] with [[Z-Phe-Ala-FMK]]. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
