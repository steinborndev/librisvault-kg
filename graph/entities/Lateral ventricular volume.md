# Lateral ventricular volume

The volume of the brain's lateral ventricles on MRI, which rises with brain atrophy and is used as a marker of neurodegeneration.

## Connections

- Genetic overlap with CSF phosphorylated tau at [[GMNC]] and [[C16orf95]]; the alleles that raise tau go with smaller ventricles. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Phenotype: [[Increased CSF phosphorylated tau concentration]]
- Lateral ventricle volume, used as a central atrophy measure, diverged between transmembrane and cytoplasmic PSEN1 carriers at 10.9 years before expected onset. Study: [[PSEN1 transmembrane versus cytoplasmic variants and progression in DIAN]]
