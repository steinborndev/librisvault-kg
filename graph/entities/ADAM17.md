# ADAM17

ADAM17 (TACE) encodes a membrane metalloprotease that sheds many cell-surface proteins and is a candidate regulated α-secretase for [[APP]], alongside [[ADAM10]].

## Connections

- The ADAM17 inhibitor TAPI raised basal secreted Aβ in 293sw cells but did not change the sAPPα rise caused by [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]], which argues that the compound acts through [[ADAM10]] rather than ADAM17.
- Study: [[DAPG lowers beta-amyloid secretion in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Reported to cleave [[TREM2]] to release soluble TREM2; context in [[TREM2 regulation by YY1 in microglia and Alzheimer disease models]]
- Not up-regulated in hypoperfused mouse brain, unlike amyloid-degradation genes such as [[A2M]] and [[PLAT]]. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Its cutting of a TREM2 stalk peptide in a test tube was blocked by [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]] and by its Fab, though in cells only the bivalent antibody reduced shedding. See [[TREM2 ectodomain shedding]]. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- Carries the rare variant [[ADAM17 p.R215I]] in a family with late-onset Alzheimer disease and five further patients; the change blocks pro-domain cleavage so no mature enzyme forms. Wild-type ADAM17, unlike the variant, lowered APP mRNA, protein and secreted Aβ in SH-SY5Y cells, and its brain mRNA correlated inversely with APP (r² 0.55 in 284 prefrontal cortices). Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
