# ZDHHC21 p.T209S

A heterozygous missense change (c.999A>T, exon 6) in [[ZDHHC21]] that replaces threonine 209 with serine at a highly conserved residue. It is absent from gnomAD, DisGeNET, the Alzheimer and frontotemporal dementia mutation database and HGMD.

## Connections

- Present in all affected and none of the unaffected tested members of a Han Chinese family with [[Familial Alzheimer disease]] whose exome showed no [[APP]], [[PSEN1]] or [[PSEN2]] mutation; knock-in mice homozygous for it show hyperpalmitoylation of [[FYN]] and [[APP]], memory loss and synaptic damage.
