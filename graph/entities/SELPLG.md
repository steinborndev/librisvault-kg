# SELPLG

SELPLG encodes P-selectin glycoprotein ligand 1, a leukocyte adhesion protein that [[BACE1]] also cuts.

## Connections

- Used with [[NRG1]] as a second non-APP BACE1 substrate in a cell assay: [[FAH65]] and FAH17 did not inhibit its cleavage (EC50 above 10 μM) whereas BACE-IV did (about 0.05 μM). Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
