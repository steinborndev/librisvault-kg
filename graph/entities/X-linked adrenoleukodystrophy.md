# X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy

X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy is an inherited peroxisomal disorder in which very long-chain fatty acids accumulate, damaging the cerebral white matter, spinal cord and adrenal cortex.

## Connections

- Reviewed as the one disease where metabolic rerouting of fatty acids through [[SCD]] improves pathology, so SCD1 inhibition may be harmful here although it is protective in other neurological diseases. Study: [[SCD1 as a therapeutic target in neurological disorders review]]
