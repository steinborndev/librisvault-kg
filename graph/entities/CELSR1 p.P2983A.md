# CELSR1 p.P2983A

A missense change from proline to alanine at residue 2983 of [[CELSR1]], also reported in craniorachischisis.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CELSR1]]
- One of two CELSR1 missense variants (with rs75983687) in 10 Alzheimer cases, 8 of whom also carried two [[GTSE1]] variants on a rare 12-SNP, 77.6 kb haplotype; the source writes the change as p.2983A in the results and also lists P2983A under ABCD4. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
