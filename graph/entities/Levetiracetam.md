# Levetiracetam

Levetiracetam is a widely used antiepileptic drug.

## Connections

- A Phase-2 randomised, placebo-controlled trial is testing whether it improves memory in mild-to-moderate [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]], following positive results in [[Mild cognitive impairment]]; the rationale is the hyperexcitability of the late-onset brain, seen as seizures or subclinical epileptiform activity on EEG or magnetoencephalography.
