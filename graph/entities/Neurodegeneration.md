# Neurodegeneration

Neurodegeneration is the progressive loss of neurons and their function, the common feature of [[Alzheimer disease]] and other dementias.

## Connections

- In the fly eye, human tau, secreted [[Amyloid-beta]] 42 or the [[DYRK1A]] orthologue minibrain destroyed photoreceptors and shrank the eye (by about 60%, 50% and 25%); [[PST-001]] enlarged the damaged eyes by roughly 20 to 30% and fully restored the minibrain eye. Study: [[PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome]]
- Models: [[Drosophila models of Alzheimer disease and Down syndrome]]; related disease: [[Down syndrome]]
