# Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors

SSRIs are antidepressants that block the serotonin transporter [[SLC6A4]] and so raise extracellular [[Serotonin]]; their effects on cognition and dementia risk in [[Alzheimer disease]] are inconsistent across studies.

## Connections

- Used as the motivating problem: reports range from raised dementia risk to delayed conversion from mild cognitive impairment to Alzheimer disease, and transgenic mice given SSRIs may gain cognition but die more often. The authors propose that falling transporter density and Aβ40-dependent uptake inhibition make SSRI effect depend on amyloid stage, and that anti-amyloid therapy may serve late-stage serotonergic symptoms better.
