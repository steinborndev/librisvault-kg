# S100A4

S100A4 encodes a calcium-binding S100 protein that regulates cell motility and extracellular matrix turnover.

## Connections

- The only gene upregulated in all six regions in post-mortem Alzheimer brain signatures (parahippocampal gyrus, superior temporal gyrus, temporal cortex, inferior frontal gyrus, dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, cerebellum), and a co-expression hub gene; the authors link it to matrix remodelling.
