# CXCL1

CXCL1 encodes a chemokine that attracts neutrophils and is produced by astrocytes and other cells in inflammation.

## Connections

- Not induced in rat primary astrocytes by fibrillar Aβ1-42 alone (mRNA and ELISA), strongly induced by LPS, and induced when miR-146a-5p was blocked before Aβ1-42 (p = 0.01). Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Related cytokines: [[IL6]], [[IL1B]], [[TNF]]
- Plasma KC/GRO (the mouse CXCL1 chemokine) fell only in wild-type mice given [[D-Pinitol]], not in 5xFAD mice. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- GRO-α responded to LPS in both iPSC microglia-like lines and was higher at baseline with [[CHRFAM7A]]. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
