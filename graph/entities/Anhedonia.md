# Anhedonia

Anhedonia is the reduced ability to feel pleasure; in mice it is scored as a fall in preference for sucrose over water, with 65% preference or less used as the cut-off in the paper below.

## Connections

- Untreated 5xFAD mice drank less sucrose than wild type at baseline, and 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]] raised their intake to near the 65% threshold (p < 0.01); the authors note anhedonia is common in Alzheimer patients and linked to cortical amyloid. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
