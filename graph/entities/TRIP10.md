# TRIP10

TRIP10 encodes a Cdc42-interacting adaptor protein involved in actin and membrane remodelling.

## Connections

- A leading-edge gene of the TNFA signaling via NFKB pathway whose brain expression was one of only two associated with ICAM-1 levels; the authors note it appears in a published multi-omic Alzheimer network. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
