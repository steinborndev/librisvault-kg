# MIR29A

MIR29A encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-29a, a small non-coding RNA that represses target messenger RNAs after transcription.

## Connections

- Lower in serum of rats after intrahippocampal Aβ1-42 infusion, most at day 7 and partly recovering by days 14 and 21, and lower in 15-month-old APPswe-PS1dE9 mice; the authors link the miR-29a/b family to repression of [[BACE1]].
