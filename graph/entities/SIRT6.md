# SIRT6

SIRT6 encodes a nuclear NAD+-dependent histone H3 lysine 9 deacetylase (chromosome 19p13.3) that maintains telomeres, supports DNA repair and restrains NF-κB signalling.

## Connections

- Reviewed as deficient in Alzheimer brain: Aβ42 lowers it, and overexpression or p53 upregulation protects hippocampal neurons from Aβ42-induced DNA damage; it also affects tau stability and phosphorylation through GSK3.
