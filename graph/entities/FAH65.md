# FAH65

FAH65 is an orally dosed fluoro aminohydantoin small molecule derived from [[Phenytoin]] that inhibits [[BACE1]] cleavage of [[APP]] with little effect on other BACE1 substrates; its active S-configured (-) enantiomer, FAH65E(-), is the preclinical candidate. It is the lead of the [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitors]].

## Connections

- Potency about 0.01-0.03 μM for the racemate and 0.005 μM for FAH65E(-) in the cell-free assay; substrate selectivity (NRG1/APP IC50 ratio above 200) and a 47 μM IC50 on [[CTSD]]. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Lowered sAPPβ and Aβ1-42 in [[CHO]] cells, raised sAPPα and improved novel object and novel location memory in [[B254 mouse]] animals, and reached a brain Cmax of 188 nM as FAH65E(-) in [[APOE4-TR-5xFAD mouse]] animals. Same study.
- Compared with [[Verubecestat]] in rats: similar brain entry, larger fall in cerebrospinal fluid Aβ1-40.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
