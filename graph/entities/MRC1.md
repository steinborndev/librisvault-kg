# MRC1

MRC1 encodes the macrophage mannose receptor (CD206), a surface marker widely used for the anti-inflammatory M2 state of macrophages and microglia.

## Connections

- CD206-positive microglia rose with circ-Epc1 overexpression in LPS-challenged [[BV2]] cells and in the hippocampus of Alzheimer mice given circ-Epc1-containing exosomes, and the rise was partly reversed by [[MIR770]] mimic or [[TREM2]] knockdown. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Paired with [[ITGAM]] (CD11b, the M1 marker in that study); see [[Microglial cell activation]].
