# Folic acid

The synthetic form of vitamin B9.

## Connections

- The review reports 6 to 10% of Alzheimer patients are folate deficient, that folic acid is cardio- and neuroprotective in early-stage disease in transgenic mice, and that it restores [[SIRT1]] expression in 3×Tg mice through AMPK. It was part of the supplementation in the treatment regimen studied, and folate levels differed by [[SIRT2 rs10410544]] genotype (C/C against C/T).
