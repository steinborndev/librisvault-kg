# VASP

VASP encodes an actin-associated protein that regulates cytoskeleton dynamics and endothelial barrier function.

## Connections

- A member of the 17-gene subnetwork of the light green co-expression network; its brain expression was associated with ICAM-1 (P = 3.7 × 10^-4) and SAA (P = 1.0 × 10^-4) after multiple-testing correction, and the authors note a published link to a microglial network in Alzheimer disease.
