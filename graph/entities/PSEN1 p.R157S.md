# PSEN1 p.R157S

A missense change of PSEN1 in [[PSEN1]] (arginine 157 to serine), seen in familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. Found in one Taiwanese man with amnestic onset at 51 and no family data; the same change was reported earlier in a Chinese man with onset at 60. It is rare in East Asian populations (frequency 0.0009244 in ExAC East Asian and 0.00033 in Taiwan Biobank) and predicted deleterious by PolyPhen-2, SIFT and CADD, but with little family evidence it is classed as a variant of unknown significance.
