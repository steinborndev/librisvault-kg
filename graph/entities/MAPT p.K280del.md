# MAPT p.K280del

A deletion of lysine 280 within the repeat domain of [[MAPT]] (Tau441 numbering) that makes the repeat domain highly prone to misfolding and aggregation; it has been reported in patients with Alzheimer disease and other tauopathies.

## Connections

- The pro-aggregant ΔK280 repeat domain (residues 244-372) fused to DsRed drives the human cell models in which [[LM-031]] was screened. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
