# INSR

INSR encodes the insulin receptor, a receptor tyrosine kinase that phosphorylates [[IRS1]] and starts PI3K signalling for neuronal growth, plasticity and memory.

## Connections

- INSR expression was 47% lower in hippocampus of FAK-overexpressing 3xTg-AD mice than in controls. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; mouse model: [[3xTg-AD mouse]]
