# 6-Gingerol

6-Gingerol is the main pungent phenolic compound of fresh ginger ([[Zingiber officinale]]), known for anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties.

## Connections

- Picked from 143 ginger compounds as the lead for [[Alzheimer disease]] by network pharmacology, a PC12 viability test against amyloid-beta 1-42, docking and 100 ns dynamics; it is linked to 16 key targets, binds [[MMP2]], [[ACHE]], [[PTGS2]] and [[CASP3]] in simulation, and gave a modest viability improvement in [[PC12]] cells. Study: [[6-Gingerol from ginger screened for Alzheimer disease by docking and dynamics]]
- Named in the source's introduction among food-derived antioxidants, beside [[Curcumin]]
- Methods: [[Molecular docking]], [[Molecular dynamics simulation]]
