# LLOMe

LLOMe is a lysosomotropic dipeptide ester that is processed in lysosomes into a membranolytic product, damaging the lysosomal membrane.

## Connections

- At 1 to 10 mM for 5 or 24 h it shortened the time to propidium iodide uptake after oxidant challenge in several experiments of cultured mouse cortical neurons but made no difference in others, unlike the consistent effect of [[Chloroquine]] and [[Bafilomycin A1]]. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
