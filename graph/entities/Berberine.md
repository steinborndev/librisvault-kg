# Berberine

Berberine is a plant isoquinoline alkaloid used in traditional medicine.

## Connections

- Showed mitophagy-inducing activity in a 61-compound screen and, with [[Palmatine]], was the starting material for the derivatives that led to [[ALT001]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Pathway: [[Mitophagy]]
- Reversed [[D-ribose]]-induced loss of membrane potential, cytochrome c release, apoptosis, amyloid-beta and phospho-Tau rise, neuron damage and memory deficits in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals and [[Neuro2a]] cells, raising PINK1, Parkin and LC3B and clearing p62; it also lowered PINK1 promoter methylation and [[DNMT1]] like [[Azacitidine]]. The rescue was lost with [[Mdivi-1]] or PINK1 siRNA and did not extend to anxiety-like behaviour. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Named as a heat-clearing herb constituent (Coptis chinensis, Phellodendron) with reported effects in Alzheimer disease; see [[Heat-clearing and detoxifying traditional Chinese medicine]]. Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
