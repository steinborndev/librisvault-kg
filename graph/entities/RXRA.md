# RXRA

RXRA encodes retinoid X receptor alpha, the obligate heterodimer partner of liver X receptors and PPARs on their response elements.

## Connections

- The reporter assay co-expressed it with each receptor. The RXR agonist [[Bexarotene]] also raised cholesterol efflux genes and lowered [[DHCR24]] and [[HMGCR]], so the authors cannot exclude a contribution of RXR homodimers to the extract effects.
