# ADAM10

ADAM10 encodes a membrane metalloprotease that acts as the main constitutive α-secretase: it cuts [[APP]] inside the amyloid beta sequence, releasing soluble APPα (sAPPα) and the C83 fragment and so preventing [[Amyloid-beta formation]].

## Connections

- In 293sw cells and in mouse primary astrocytes the ADAM10 inhibitor [[GI254023X]] blocked the rise in sAPPα caused by [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]], and raised basal secreted Aβ. DAPG changed neither ADAM10 level, maturation nor total α-secretase activity, so its effect is proposed to work through ADAM10 localisation or binding partners.
- Inhibition did not prevent the DAPG-driven fall in secreted Aβ, though intracellular Aβ was higher with the inhibitor present, so ADAM10 accounts for only part of that drop.
- Study: [[DAPG lowers beta-amyloid secretion in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Reported to cleave [[TREM2]] to release soluble TREM2; context in [[TREM2 regulation by YY1 in microglia and Alzheimer disease models]]
- None of 14 amyloid-production genes including ADAM10 and [[ADAM17]] was up-regulated in the hypoperfused mouse brain, and ADAM10 carried no coding variant in 96 [[Small vessel ischemic disease]] patients. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Reviewed as regulated by [[SIRT1]], which favours non-amyloidogenic processing. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- A GWAS SNP (rs442495) was a strong allele-specific QTL of ADAM10 in the temporal cortex, where ADAM10 is expressed, and not in the other regions. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- Differentially expressed or usage-changed in at least one temporal lobe brain RNAseq dataset of Alzheimer cases. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
- Named as the putative α-secretase whose competing cleavage of APP raises sAPPα when BACE1 is inhibited by [[FAH65]]. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Co-cleaves the TREM2 stalk after His157 with [[ADAM17]]; an antibody against the stalk, [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]], was proposed to block this selectively because broad ADAM inhibition would also remove non-amyloidogenic APP processing and other physiological substrates. See [[TREM2 ectodomain shedding]]. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- Named as the primary α-secretase of the neuroprotective non-amyloidogenic route, releasing sAPPα and leaving C83 in the membrane. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Promoter hypoacetylated in female hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load (-0.44, FDR 0.037), and its sentinel SNP overlapped an oligodendrocyte-enriched peak. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Its mRNA did not clearly differ between mouse gut segments and brain at 3, 6 and 12 months. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Two rare ADAM10 missense variants in late-onset familial families were also found to impair pro-domain cleavage, as reported for [[ADAM17 p.R215I]]. In human brain ADAM10 mRNA correlated weakly and positively with APP mRNA, against the strong inverse correlation of ADAM17. Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
