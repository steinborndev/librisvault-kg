# CSF3

CSF3 encodes granulocyte colony-stimulating factor, a haematopoietic cytokine that also acts as a neurotrophic factor in the brain.

- G-CSF rose in amyloid-beta-challenged [[BV2]] cells after treatment, and the authors read isoacteoside as the compound that boosts neurotrophic factors. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Cytokine partners: [[IL4]], [[IL1B]], [[IFNG]]
