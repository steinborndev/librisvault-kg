# BCL3

BCL3 encodes a nuclear IκB-family regulator that modulates NF-κB transcription factor activity.

## Connections

- One of four M4-module transcription factors (with [[ATF3]], [[KLF4]] and [[NFIL3]]) that overlapped the exercise-responsive gene list in blood monocytes, but not kept in the final seven-gene panel. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
