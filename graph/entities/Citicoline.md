# Citicoline

A choline donor and phospholipid precursor used as a neuroprotectant in several countries.

## Connections

- Given at 500 mg a day in the one-year multifactorial regimen in which SIRT2, APOE and CYP2D6 genotypes were compared; the review also notes it raises [[SIRT1]] expression. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Related: [[Sirtuin modulators]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Also given at 500 mg a day in a multifactorial regimen assessed for anxiety and depression response by APOE and cytochrome P450 genotype. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
