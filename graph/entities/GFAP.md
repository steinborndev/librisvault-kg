# GFAP

GFAP encodes glial fibrillary acidic protein, the intermediate filament of astrocytes whose raised expression marks reactive astrocytes.

## Connections

- Staining for GFAP showed 2.1-fold more positive cells in hippocampal CA1/CA2 and 1.7-fold more in CA3 plus dentate gyrus 14 days after fibrillar Aβ1-42 infusion in rats. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Process: [[Astrocyte activation]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Brown seaweed lipid extracts lowered GFAP expression in [[CCF-STTG1]] astrocytoma cells, whereas [[T0901317]], [[Pirinixic acid]] and [[Pioglitazone]] raised it. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Used as the astrocyte marker in co-staining that showed [[IQCK]] in astrocytes of mouse cortex. Study: [[IQCK protein is raised in Alzheimer hippocampus and found in amyloid plaques]]
- Astrocytic GFAP immunofluorescence fell in rat frontal cortex after restraint stress, while [[SLC2A1]] rose. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- CSF GFAP was used as an astrogliosis reference in correlation analysis with six microglial activity proteins; it was not significantly altered in GRN mutation carriers. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Not changed in brain of [[B254 mouse]] animals treated for 10 days with [[FAH65]], alongside [[CXCL10]] as a microglial marker. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Staining showed fewer, smaller GFAP-positive astrocytes in hippocampus of 3xTg-AD mice overexpressing [[PTK2]], while Iba1 microglia were unchanged. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- GFAP-positive astrocyte density was raised in hippocampus and cortex of [[TgCRND8 mouse]] animals and reduced by [[Magnolol]] and [[Donepezil]] (n = 4 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
