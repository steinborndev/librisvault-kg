# HLA-DQB1

HLA-DQB1 encodes the beta chain of the HLA-DQ class II antigen-presenting molecule.

## Connections

- The GWAS SNP rs6931277 was associated with allelic expression of HLA-DQB1 in classical monocytes and in microglia, consistent with an earlier monocyte eQTL study (DICE); the same SNP is a splicing QTL of several class II HLA genes in nearly all GTEx tissues, so it may act on different genes in different cell types. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- Related class II genes at the same SNP: [[HLA-DQA2]], [[HLA-DOB]]
