# Prostaglandin A1

Prostaglandin A1 (PGA1) is a neuroprotective lipid made from membrane phospholipids by PLA2 and COX-2 that raises neurotrophic factor expression and activates [[Protein phosphatase 2A]].

## Connections

- It formed a covalent Michael adduct with Cys377 of the PP2A A subunit. Given into the cerebral ventricles of tau P301S mice it lowered tau phosphorylation at several serine and threonine residues in a dose-dependent way, and 2.5 mg/kg/day for 6 months averted cognitive decline; it also dephosphorylated tau in Neuro2a cells.
