# AADvac1

AADvac1 is an active immunotherapy vaccine that elicits antibodies against pathological forms of tau.

## Connections

- In a post hoc analysis of the 24-month phase 2 trial ADAMANT (EudraCT 2015-000630-30, NCT02579252; 196 patients, 119 analysed), plasma [[NEFL]] was lower (p = 0.0139), and a greater antibody response was associated with reduced brain atrophy (p < 0.05) and with CDR-SB (p = 0.0376) and activities of daily living (p = 0.0201); the text does not state the direction of the CDR-SB and activities-of-daily-living associations. Machine-learning models predicted amyloid-positive and tau-positive status from baseline MRI with positive predictive values of 97.7% and 96.2%.
