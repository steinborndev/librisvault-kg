# MTCH2

MTCH2 encodes mitochondrial carrier 2, a protein of the outer mitochondrial membrane.

## Connections

- Allelic imbalance of MTCH2 was linked to rs3740688 in excitatory neurons, but the SNP was not a genotype-level eQTL, so the signal likely reflects linkage disequilibrium with the exonic proxy SNP in double-heterozygous subjects.
