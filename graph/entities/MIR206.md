# MIR206

MIR206 encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-206, a small non-coding RNA that represses target messenger RNAs after transcription.

## Connections

- Raised miR-206 in Alzheimer blood samples was linked to [[IGF1]] and to inflammation; it is proposed as a circulating marker and as a possible pharmacological target.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see also [[MicroRNA-target interaction databases]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
