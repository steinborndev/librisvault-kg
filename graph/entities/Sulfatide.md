# Sulfatide

Sulfatide is a sulfated galactosylceramide that is a major myelin lipid.

- Reported depleted by up to 93% in grey matter and 58% in white matter in mild Alzheimer dementia. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
