# DAPT

DAPT is a gamma-secretase inhibitor that blocks cleavage of [[APP]] and so lowers amyloid beta production (see [[Amyloid-beta formation]]).

## Connections

- Conditioned medium from PSEN1 mutant neurons treated with DAPT no longer induced [[Lipid droplet]] formation, [[Lipid peroxidation]] or phagocytosis loss in APOE3 microglia, indicating that neuronal amyloid beta drives the microglial phenotype. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Related gene: [[PSEN1]]
- At 1 μM for 16 h in mouse cortical neurons it enlarged endo-lysosomes and raised glutamate-evoked calcium and oxidant-induced membrane damage without changing baseline calcium, reproducing the phenotypes of the low-activity subpopulation. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
