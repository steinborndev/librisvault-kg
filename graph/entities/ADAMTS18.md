# ADAMTS18

ADAMTS18 encodes a secreted metalloprotease of the ADAMTS family, related to the Alzheimer risk gene [[ADAMTS4]].

## Connections

- A promoter-proximal peak was hyperacetylated in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia from prefrontal cortex with amyloid load, and ADAMTS18 transcript was significantly higher in oligodendrocytes from Alzheimer prefrontal cortex in an earlier single-nucleus dataset (log2 fold change 0.113, q = 0.008 for no pathology versus pathology; 0.48, q = 1.2e-9 for early pathology). Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Cell population: [[Oligodendrocyte-enriched glia]]
