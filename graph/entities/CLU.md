# CLU

CLU encodes clusterin (apolipoprotein J), a secreted chaperone-like glycoprotein and the second most abundant apolipoprotein in the brain, and is a late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] risk gene.

## Connections

- Clusterin was a protein marker on the nanoparticle kit and was measured in patient plasma.
- Study: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]]
- Variant [[CLU rs1532278]] was not associated alone but took part in a protective three-locus genotype combination with [[TCF4 rs17594526]] and [[APOE rs429358]]; CLU was also a hub in the protein interaction network. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- CLU expression correlated negatively with cortical glucose metabolism in all risk groups; the authors count it among four immune-related risk genes (with CD33, MEF2C and TREM2). Study: [[Brain metabolism and Alzheimer risk gene expression in normal elderly]]
- Plasma clusterin was stably selected by L1-regularized regression for the Alzheimer disease versus control classifier but did not itself reach significance and was not kept in the 13-protein universal panel; it was among 15 proteins whose direction differed between two Moscow cohorts, none significant in one of them. Study: [[Plasma protein panel for Alzheimer disease risk by MRM mass spectrometry]]
- Region-dependent effects: [[CLU rs4236673]] and [[CLU rs7982]] affected CLU allelic expression only in the temporal cortex, consistent with an earlier intron retention report; the opposite exon-level signs reflect different transcripts. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- Contributed to the 'amyloid' cluster of Alzheimer risk variants (lower CSF amyloid-beta 42 with higher phosphorylated tau), together with [[BIN1]], [[PICALM]] and [[ABCA7]]. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Clusterin accumulates in synapses of post-mortem brains of APOE4 Alzheimer carriers. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- In superior temporal cortex, clusterin was higher in Alzheimer cases than controls in both homogenate and synaptoneurosomes, but only the synaptic fraction was higher in APOE4 than APOE3 Alzheimer cases; by [[Array tomography]], the share of synapses with clusterin, and with clusterin plus oligomeric [[Amyloid-beta]], was highest near plaques in APOE4 carriers. The authors leave open whether this is protective or harmful. Study: [[Clusterin and amyloid-beta accumulate in synapses of APOE4 carriers in Alzheimer disease brain]]; method: [[Synaptoneurosome isolation]]
- Differentially expressed or usage-changed in at least one temporal lobe brain RNAseq dataset of Alzheimer cases. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
- Its sentinel SNP overlaps an H3K27ac peak enriched in neurons and, in a second reading, in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia; the promoter was hyperacetylated in prefrontal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load (0.49) and transcript was higher in Alzheimer oligodendrocytes (log2 fold change 0.18, q = 4.2e-3). Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Plasma clusterin was higher in adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (292 versus 172 μg/ml, p < 0.001) but did not differ with dementia; [[CLU rs11136000]] did not alter it. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
- Clusterin loss in mice ([[Clu knockout mouse]]) changed 1,759 brain genes at 6 months and drove synaptic, endocytosis and RNA-transport modules that matched human neuronal-system modules, with correlations opposite in sign to those of the Apoe knockout; the authors read this as consistent with a protective role for CLU. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- The CLU shore was less methylated in Alzheimer blood (confirmed by pyrosequencing in two sample sets) and in dementia with Lewy bodies, but not in vascular or frontotemporal dementia; with APOE ε4 allele number it separated patients from controls at an AUC of 0.80 to 0.85 (ε4 alone 0.73 to 0.78). Variant: [[CLU rs11136000]]. Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
- 43.4% of plasma [[Sphingomyelin]] species and more than 30% of [[Phosphatidylethanolamine]] species associated differently with the CLU risk SNP in Alzheimer cases than in controls (nominal, not surviving correction). Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
