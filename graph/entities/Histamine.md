# Histamine

Histamine is a biogenic amine stored in mast cell granules and released on activation; it signals through H1 to H4 receptors, including on nociceptive nerve endings.

## Connections

- The review states that about half of rat brain histamine comes from mast cells, that neuronal CGRP triggers its release, and that anti-histamine drugs reduce pain in people. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
