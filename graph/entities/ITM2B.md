# ITM2B

ITM2B encodes the integral membrane protein BRI2. Mutations in it cause [[Familial British dementia]] and [[Familial Danish dementia]], and the protein is a candidate regulator of [[APP]] processing.

## Connections

- Reviewed as a potential regulator of APP processing that can inhibit both the production and the fibrillation of [[Amyloid-beta]], and as the mutated gene in familial British and Danish dementias, whose amyloid angiopathy and tangles resemble [[Alzheimer disease]]. Study: [[BRI2 in familial British and Danish dementia and Alzheimer disease review]]
- Related genes: [[APP]], [[PSEN1]], [[PSEN2]]
- Concept: [[Amyloid cascade hypothesis]]
