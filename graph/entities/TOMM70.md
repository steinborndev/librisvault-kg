# TOMM70

TOMM70 encodes a receptor subunit of the translocase of the outer mitochondrial membrane that helps import precursor proteins into mitochondria.

## Connections

- Reported upregulated in Alzheimer brain across several microarray datasets; one of the five signature genes; docking predicted high-affinity binding by [[Antrocin]]. Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Full text: localised to mitochondria; best docking affinity of the five for [[Antrocin]] (-6.80 kcal/mol, one hydrogen bond at SER271). The study cites a clinical report of two patients with de novo C-terminal changes (T607I, I554F) who had white matter abnormalities, hypotonia, dystonia and cognitive deficits.
