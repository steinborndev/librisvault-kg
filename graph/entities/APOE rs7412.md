# APOE rs7412

One of the two [[APOE]] SNPs, with [[APOE rs429358]], whose combination defines the ε2, ε3 and ε4 isoforms; the T allele marks ε2.

## Connections

- Gene: [[APOE]]
- Genotyped with rs429358 to call APOE haplotypes; the number of ε4 alleles was used in the analysis. ε4 carriers had a larger HDL cholesterol rise on atorvastatin. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- The variant defining ε2: the T allele raised CSF amyloid-beta 42 (Z = 11.97, p = 5.1e-33) and lowered phosphorylated tau (Z = -6.59, p = 4.5e-11), replicated in an independent set. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Its T allele defines the ε2 isoform that, introduced by genome editing into APOE3 hiPSC lines, lowered secreted amyloid-beta in familial Alzheimer disease neural cultures. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]
- Genotyped with rs429358 by TaqMan to assign ε-genotypes in postmortem brain used for chromatin contact analysis. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
- The ε2-defining SNP was associated in healthy adults with lower LDL-cholesterol measures (also in the 16 ε2 homozygotes), higher monoglyceride and [[Diacylglycerol]] levels, and six higher plasma proteins: [[LDLR]], [[HMOX1]], [[SLAMF8]], [[RNF31]], [[CNTNAP2]] and [[SRP14]]. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Genotyped with [[APOE rs429358]] to call APOE genotypes in a cohort assessed for anxiety and depression response to treatment. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
- Top SNP for LDL, non-HDL and total cholesterol at the APOE early-onset Alzheimer disease locus (P about 1e-305 for each). Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
