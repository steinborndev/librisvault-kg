# IGFBP5

IGFBP5 encodes insulin-like growth factor binding protein 5, a secreted protein that binds and modulates insulin-like growth factors. In prefrontal cortex, its protein abundance was linked both to the Alzheimer polygenic risk score and to parkinsonism in older adults.

## Connections

- Candidate mediator between Alzheimer genetic risk and [[Parkinsonism]], and a direct upstream node of the parkinsonism score in a Bayesian network.
- Related disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Alzheimer polygenic risk score and motor impairment in older adults]]
