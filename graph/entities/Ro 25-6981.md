# Ro 25-6981

Ro 25-6981 is a potent, selective inhibitor of NMDA receptors containing the [[GRIN2B]] (NMDAR2B, GluN2B) subunit, used as a pharmacological probe.

## Connections

- At 1 µM it normalised the enlarged spontaneous calcium signals of neurons from [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]] and barely affected wild-type neurons, placing the calcium excess on GRIN2B overactivity. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
