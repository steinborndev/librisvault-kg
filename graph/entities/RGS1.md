# RGS1

RGS1 encodes a GTPase-activating protein that dampens G protein signalling in immune cells, including chemokine receptor signalling.

## Connections

- The top upregulated gene by fold change in hippocampal CA1 and CA3 of both women and men with Alzheimer disease, read by the authors as a shared immune response. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
