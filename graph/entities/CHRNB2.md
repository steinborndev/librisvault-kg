# CHRNB2

CHRNB2 encodes the beta2 subunit of the neuronal nicotinic acetylcholine receptor and partners [[CHRNA4]] in the alpha4/beta2 receptor.

## Connections

- mRNA in blood mononuclear cells was lower than in controls in both diseases and lowest in dementia with Lewy bodies (median fold change about 0.0 against 0.7 in Alzheimer disease); it correlated positively with CHRNA3 in Lewy body dementia.
