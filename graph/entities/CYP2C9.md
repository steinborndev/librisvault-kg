# CYP2C9

CYP2C9 encodes a cytochrome P450 enzyme that metabolises many drugs, including some SSRIs such as fluoxetine. The *2 and *3 alleles reduce activity and define intermediate and poor metabolizers.

## Connections

- In 1006 Alzheimer disease patients, 62.3% were normal, 32.6% intermediate and 5.2% poor metabolizers; normal and intermediate metabolizers improved significantly in both anxiety and depression after one month of treatment, poor metabolizers only mildly and not significantly.
