# BE2-M17

BE(2)-M17 is a human neuroblastoma cell line with neuron-like properties.

## Connections

- Transduced with a lentiviral [[APP]] vector to model amyloid-driven toxicity and used to test [[Osthol]] and [[Zinc oxide]] nanoparticles. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
- Related neuroblastoma model: [[SH-SY5Y]]
- Expresses BACE2 at under 0.5 FPKM; Sleeping Beauty transposon overexpression of [[BACE2]] lowered secreted Aβ1-40 and Aβ1-42 in the medium at 72 hours. Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
