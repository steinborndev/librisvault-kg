# GABRA1

GABRA1 encodes the alpha-1 subunit of the GABA-A receptor, the main inhibitory ligand-gated chloride channel in brain.

## Connections

- Differentially expressed in excitatory neurons between early and late Alzheimer pathology, and a target of drugs in Alzheimer clinical trials, as are [[APP]] and [[TKT]]; the three overlapping genes were also tied to generalized epilepsy. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Isoform switch (differential transcript usage) in Alzheimer temporal lobe with synapse-related enrichment. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
