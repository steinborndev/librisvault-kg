# APOE rs440446

rs440446 is an exonic variant in a minor, prematurely terminated transcript of [[APOE]] (ENST00000434152), in mild linkage disequilibrium with the ε4-defining SNP [[APOE rs429358]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[APOE]]
- The ε4 C allele tracked higher expression of exon 2 measured at rs440446 but lower expression of exon 4 of the major transcript (ENST00000252486), so the opposite exon-level effects are explained by this linkage.
