# PRKACB

PRKACB encodes the beta catalytic subunit of cAMP-dependent protein kinase A (PKACb), a serine/threonine kinase reviewed as one of the main tau kinases in the brain.

## Connections

- Reviewed as a cardinal tau kinase: miR-200a-3p ([[MIR200A]]) represses PRKACB mRNA and so tau phosphorylation, and miR-200a-3p was found down in APP/PS1 and SAMP8 mice, in cultures and in patient blood, which would release the kinase. What lowers miR-200a-3p is unknown. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Process: [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Also one of nine genes flagged in a comparison of post-mortem prefrontal and frontal brain expression datasets for vascular disruption in Alzheimer disease, alongside [[ADCY1]] and [[GNAS]]. Study: [[Vascular gene expression in Alzheimer prefrontal and frontal brain GEO datasets]]
