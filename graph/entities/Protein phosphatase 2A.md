# Protein phosphatase 2A

Protein phosphatase 2A (PP2A) is a heterotrimeric serine/threonine phosphatase complex with scaffold (A), regulatory (B) and catalytic (C) subunits, and the main phosphatase that dephosphorylates tau in the human brain.

## Connections

- Its activity is reported to fall by 50% in Alzheimer brain, which goes with tau hyperphosphorylation and memory deficits in rat brain; subunits A and C are needed for activity. [[Prostaglandin A1]] binds Cys377 of subunit A and activates the complex. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
