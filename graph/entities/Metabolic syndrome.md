# Metabolic syndrome

A cluster of risk factors (abdominal obesity, insulin resistance, hypertension, high triglycerides and low HDL-cholesterol) that precedes diabetes and cardiovascular disease.

## Connections

- Modelled with Sirt3 deletion plus a calorie-rich diet, and combined with amyloid pathology in the [[APP-PS1-Sirt3 knockout mouse]]; lower brain [[SIRT3]] and [[IDE]] are proposed to connect it to [[Alzheimer disease]]. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
