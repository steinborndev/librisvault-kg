# Alsterpaullone

Alsterpaullone is a small-molecule ATP-competitive inhibitor of cyclin-dependent kinases that also inhibits [[GSK3B]].

## Connections

- One of four candidates the authors favour for Alzheimer disease repurposing: literature points to inhibition of [[GSK3B]], [[CDK5]] and ERK1/2, with less tau hyperphosphorylation and less NF-κB-driven inflammation, and moderate toxicity. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
