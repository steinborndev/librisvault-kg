# CYP46A1

CYP46A1 encodes cholesterol 24-hydroxylase, the neuronal enzyme that converts brain cholesterol to [[24S-Hydroxycholesterol]] so that it can leave the brain.

## Connections

- Described as one of three main brain cholesterol exporters with [[ABCA1]] and [[ABCG1]]; knockdown in mouse neurons raised cholesterol and amyloid-beta, and a polymorphism is linked to Alzheimer risk. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
