# APOE rs429358

rs429358 is one of the two [[APOE]] SNPs, with rs7412, whose combination defines the ε2, ε3 and ε4 isoforms; the C allele marks ε4, the major genetic risk allele for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[APOE]]
- Neighbouring variants at the locus: [[APOE rs769449]], [[TOMM40 rs2075650]], [[NECTIN2 rs6857]]
- Interacting variants in the two-locus model: [[TCF4 rs17594526]]; method: [[Generalized multifactor dimensionality reduction]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- TOMM40 RNA level in postmortem frontal lobe did not differ by ε4-defining C allele status; see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
- The ε4 SNP (with rs7412 for ε2) was kept separate from the 39-variant polygenic score used to stratify risk by APOE genotype. Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
- The proxy SNP was excluded from a 39-variant score so that APOE could be modelled as a separate predictor with its own prevalence. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- Genotyped with [[APOE rs7412]] to define APOE haplotypes in a statin pharmacogenetic cohort. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Fine-mapping gave it a 13.9% posterior probability of being causal at the APOE region (alongside [[NECTIN2 rs11668861]] and [[APOC1 rs56131196]] in neighbouring regions), and it showed allelic imbalance with fewer risk-allele transcripts, including in brain; see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].
- The C allele (ε4) lowered major-transcript APOE expression in prefrontal, posterior cingulate and caudate controls as well as across all four regions, and raised exon 2 expression tracked by [[APOE rs440446]]. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- The variant defining ε4: the C allele lowered CSF amyloid-beta 42 (Z = -36.29, p = 2.0e-288) and raised phosphorylated tau (Z = 18.31, p = 6.9e-75). The proxy [[APOC1 rs4420638]] stood in for amyloid-beta 42 replication and [[APOE rs769449]] for tau. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Used with rs7412 to genotype APOE ε3/ε3, ε3/ε4 and ε4/ε4 postmortem brains; ε3/ε4 brains had the strongest APOE-APOC1 chromatin contact and ε4/ε4 brains little extra looping. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
- Genotyped with [[APOE rs7412]] by PCR and Sanger sequencing to call ε2/ε3/ε4 in Polish patients and controls, all of whom carried ε4. Study: [[BCHE noncoding variants and APOE ε4 in Polish late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- The ε4-defining SNP was associated in healthy adults with higher total and LDL cholesterol and lower HDL (blunted in older groups, probably by statin use), higher plasma [[PLA2G7]] and [[CD28]], and higher levels of two [[Diacylglycerol]] species. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Genotyped with [[APOE rs7412]] to call APOE genotypes in a cohort assessed for anxiety and depression response to treatment. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
- Top HDL cholesterol SNP (P = 5.84e-256) at the APOE early-onset Alzheimer disease locus, whose own top SNP is [[APOE rs59007384]]. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
