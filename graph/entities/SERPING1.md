# SERPING1

SERPING1 encodes C1 inhibitor, a serpin that regulates the classical pathway by inactivating the C1 complex.

## Connections

- Plasma C1 inhibitor was higher in adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (150 versus 118 μg/ml, p < 0.001) and correlated positively with C1q; it is also reported higher in mothers carrying a fetus with Down syndrome. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
