# Subjective cognitive decline

A self-perceived worsening of cognition, with concern, in a person who passes objective cognitive testing. It is regarded as an early symptomatic expression of preclinical [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- In 330 such participants of a northern Han cohort, CSF Aβ42 was lower and p-tau/Aβ42 and t-tau/Aβ42 higher than in cognitively normal peers, and [[BIN1]] promoter methylation in blood was lower; methylation tracked CSF amyloid and tau markers only in this group. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
- Related stage: [[Mild cognitive impairment]]
- Risk gene: [[APOE]]
