# EPC1

EPC1 encodes enhancer of polycomb homolog 1, a chromatin-associated protein. In the mouse a circular RNA, circ-Epc1, is spliced from one exon of the gene (chr18:6448902-6450637) and can be packaged in exosomes.

## Connections

- circ-Epc1 was the only one of ten up-regulated circular RNAs significantly higher in exosomes from hypoxia-pretreated adipose-derived stem cells, and it bound [[MIR770]] in reporter assays, raising [[TREM2]] in [[BV2]] microglial cells. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Cargo of the exosomes described in [[Adipose-derived stem cell exosome therapy]]; molecular role: [[miRNA binding]]
