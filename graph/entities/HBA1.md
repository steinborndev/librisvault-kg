# HBA1

HBA1 encodes the alpha 1 chain of hemoglobin, the oxygen carrier of red blood cells.

## Connections

- RNA fell about 10-fold in Alzheimer caudate nucleus (305 vs 35 FPKM) and 7-fold in Parkinson caudate nucleus (305 vs 46 FPKM) against controls; hemoglobin subunit genes were the most significant hits overall (p about 1e-10), read as compromised oxygen transport.
