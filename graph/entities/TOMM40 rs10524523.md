# TOMM40 rs10524523

rs10524523 is a deoxythymidine homopolymer (poly-T) length polymorphism in intron 6 of [[TOMM40]], typed as short (S, 19 T or fewer), long (L, 20 to 29) or very long (VL, 30 or more). The L class travels with the [[APOE]] ε4 allele, and length has been linked to risk and age of onset in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[TOMM40]]
- In postmortem frontal lobe, S carriers had higher TOMM40 primary-transcript RNA than VL carriers (p < 0.02); see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
- Neighbouring variants: [[TOMM40 rs741780]], [[TOMM40 rs2075650]], [[APOE rs429358]]
- Cited as predicting response to multifactorial treatment: S/S carriers responded best, L/L worst, and L/L occurred only with APOE 4/4. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
