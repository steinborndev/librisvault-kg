# TRIM4

TRIM4 encodes a tripartite-motif protein of the E3 ubiquitin ligase family.

## Connections

- Allelic imbalance of TRIM4 was linked to rs1859788 in excitatory neurons of the prefrontal cortex, a situation judged to be driven by linked exonic SNPs rather than by the GWAS SNP.
