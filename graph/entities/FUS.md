# FUS

FUS encodes an RNA-binding protein whose mutations are a known cause of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis and, less often, [[Frontotemporal dementia]].

## Connections

- Three in-frame deletions, [[FUS p.S57del]], [[FUS p.229_231del]] and [[FUS p.166_167del]], were found in patients with Alzheimer disease, behavioral variant frontotemporal dementia and semantic variant primary progressive aphasia respectively, all of unknown pathogenicity.
