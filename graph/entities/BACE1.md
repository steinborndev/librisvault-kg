# BACE1

BACE1 encodes the β-secretase, the aspartyl protease that makes the first cut of [[APP]] in the amyloidogenic route, releasing sAPPβ and the C99 fragment that γ-secretase then converts to amyloid beta ([[Amyloid-beta formation]]). Cleavage mainly happens where APP and BACE1 meet in Rab5-positive early endosomes.

## Connections

- In [[Tg2576 mouse]] hippocampus and retina synaptic BACE1 was raised, and the tau antibody [[12A12 monoclonal antibody]] lowered it together with APP. Study: [[Tau antibody 12A12 and amyloid-beta in Tg2576 mouse hippocampus and retina]]
- Its sorting between endosomes is governed by [[BIN1]], [[RIN3]] and [[RAB5A]]; see [[Endocytosis]].
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Target of [[MIR29C]], an interaction confirmed in cerebrospinal fluid or blood samples; BACE1 itself has been studied as a cerebrospinal fluid marker. Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- Opioid receptor control: in amyloid-injured rat [[PC12]] cells, the δ-opioid receptor ([[OPRD1]]) agonist [[UFP-512]] lowered BACE1 activity (about 30%) and protein (31%) and DOR knock-down raised activity, while the μ-opioid receptor ([[OPRM1]]) agonist [[DAMGO]] raised BACE1 mRNA, protein and activity. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
- Reviewed as regulated by [[SIRT1]], which favours non-amyloidogenic processing. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Structural model of the human β-CTF in the BACE1 active site: [[APP p.G676R]] contacts glutamate 326 and [[APP p.F681Y]] hydrogen bonds asparagine 294, explaining why human APP is cut better than rodent APP (β-CTF to full-length APP ratio 4.8-fold higher in human than mouse brain, 7.8-fold higher than rat). Study: [[Humanized APP knock-in mouse and rat with PSEN1 M139T for Alzheimer disease]]
- Silenced by siRNA delivered in [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]: BACE1 mRNA and protein fell in [[Neuro2a]] and [[BV2]] cells and in hippocampus and cortex of [[FAD4T mouse]] animals, with Aβ1-42 down 52% and better memory; microglial BACE1 is tied to loss of the phagocytic state ([[Disease-associated microglia]]). Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Lower serum miR-29a-3p, miR-29c-3p and miR-191-5p in Aβ1-42-infused rats are proposed to de-repress BACE1 and add to Aβ production; BACE1 itself was not measured. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Target of the [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitors]]: [[FAH65]] (from a [[Phenytoin]] screening hit) blocked BACE1 cleavage of APP at about 0.01 μM while sparing [[NRG1]] and [[SELPLG]] cleavage (NRG1/APP ratio above 200, versus under 2.5 for [[Verubecestat]] and [[Lanabecestat]]), and spared [[BACE2]] and [[CTSD]]; it lowered brain βCTF and raised sAPPα in [[B254 mouse]] animals. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Enriched in [[Dystrophic neurite]] of Alzheimer patients and 5xFAD mice, which favours local Aβ production; the BACE1 inhibitor [[Umibecestat]] was tested with the vaccine [[CAD106]] in a prevention trial that stopped early, and miR-200a-3p ([[MIR200A]]) represses BACE1 mRNA. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Cortical BACE1 protein fell 46% in wild-type mice given [[Nicotinamide riboside]], which the authors tie to [[SIRT3]] activation and lower Aβ production, next to higher [[IDE]] and [[MME]]. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- Transcription: [[YY1]] binds its promoter and activates it in rat [[PC12]] cells, neurones and astrocytes; reactive astrocytes, not resting ones, express both proteins, including those beside plaques in Alzheimer brain. Study: [[YY1 activates the BACE1 promoter in rat cells and reactive astrocytes]]
- Palmitoylated APP is proposed to be a better BACE1 substrate in lipid rafts; hyperpalmitoylated APP in [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]] hippocampus coincided with more Aβ40 and Aβ42. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Higher mRNA in healthy men than healthy women in hippocampal CA1 and CA3 (among the top 200 upregulated genes of the male-versus-female control contrast; [[APP]] too in CA3), which the authors read as a possible higher baseline amyloid production in men; the sex ratio of the samples was unbalanced. See [[Sex differences in Alzheimer disease]]. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
- Its extracellular cleavage of APP generates Aβ, making it the target of the "cestat" inhibitors, several of which failed in Phase 3: [[Lanabecestat]], [[Verubecestat]] and [[Atabecestat]]. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- Promoter hypoacetylated in female hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load (log2 fold change -0.49, FDR 0.032); the authors note BACE1 also regulates myelination and oligodendrocytes can make Aβ. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Its mRNA was lower in mouse gut than brain, yet colonic BACE1 protein was significantly higher in wild-type mice given [[Fecal microbiota transplantation]] from 12-month-old APP/PS1 donors (P < 0.05), alongside more colonic Aβ42; brain BACE1 did not change. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- BACE-1 protein was raised in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and lowered by [[Magnolol]] (p < 0.01 at 20 mg/kg, p < 0.001 at 40 mg/kg) and by [[Donepezil]] (n = 3 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- The homologue [[BACE2]] has weaker β-secretase activity and is thought to act oppositely, via α-like and θ-secretase cleavage and Aβ degradation; BACE1 inhibitors cross-react with it, and the Swedish APP mutation used in rodent models is BACE1-preferring. Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
