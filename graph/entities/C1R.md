# C1R

C1R encodes complement component 1r, a serine protease of the classical complement pathway.

## Connections

- C1R was one of the differentially expressed genes shared by the Mayo and Mount Sinai temporal lobe datasets; its partner [[C1S]] behaved the same way. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
