# Bictegravir

Bictegravir is an HIV integrase strand-transfer inhibitor used in combination antiretroviral therapy.

## Connections

- Its simulated complex with [[ETV6]] was stable (RMSD 3.34 Å) but its predicted brain penetration was very low (logBB -1.105), making it a poor candidate for [[Alzheimer disease]]. Study: [[Lasmiditan prioritised for Alzheimer disease by regulator docking and dynamics]]
