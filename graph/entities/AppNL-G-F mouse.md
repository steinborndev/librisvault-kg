# AppNL-G-F mouse

AppNL-G-F is a knock-in mouse that carries the Swedish, Beyreuther/Iberian and Arctic mutations in the endogenous mouse App gene, so that [[APP]] is expressed at physiological level while amyloid beta accumulates and plaques build up with age.

## Connections

- Crossed onto an immunodeficient Rag2/Il2rg/human CSF1 background and used as the host for human iPSC-derived microglia, sampled at 12 months when amyloid pathology is extensive; see [[Human microglia xenotransplantation model]] and [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
- Related amyloid models: [[APP-PS1 mouse]], [[APP-PS45 mouse]], [[Tg2576 mouse]]
- Used as a positive control for amyloid histology next to the [[Humanized App mouse]], which supplies the matching humanized wild-type control that knock-in mutant lines lack. Study: [[Humanized APP knock-in mouse and rat with PSEN1 M139T for Alzheimer disease]]
- At 6 months, knock-in mice had higher cortical TREM2 and more IBA1 and P2RY12 staining than wild-type; four intraperitoneal doses of the TREM2 antibody [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]] over 10 days raised TREM2 further, lowered P2RY12, and reduced cortical plaque area and soluble (not insoluble) amyloid-beta. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
