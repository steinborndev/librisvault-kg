# Honokiol

A polyphenolic lignan from Magnolia grandiflora that activates [[SIRT3]].

## Connections

- In transgenic Alzheimer models it raised SIRT3, AMPK, CREB and PGC-1α, lowered reactive oxygen species and lipid peroxidation, inhibited β-secretase activity and lowered Aβ and sAPPβ. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Related: [[Sirtuin modulators]], [[BACE1]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
