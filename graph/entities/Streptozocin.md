# Streptozocin

Streptozocin is a glucosamine-nitrosourea toxic to pancreatic beta cells, used to induce [[Hyperglycemia]] and diabetes in rodents.

## Connections

- Four intraperitoneal doses of 100 mg/kg raised blood glucose to 220-314 mg/dl in 6-month-old [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals and worsened memory, hippocampal neuron marker loss, and Aβ and Tau staining. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
