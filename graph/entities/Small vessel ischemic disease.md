# Small vessel ischemic disease

Small vessel ischemic disease (SVID) is ischemic damage from disease of the small penetrating brain arteries, causing lacunar and subcortical infarcts and white matter lesions, and it often co-exists with [[Alzheimer disease]] as mixed dementia.

## Connections

- Early-onset cases screened for Alzheimer genes: the APOE ε4 allele was not enriched, loss-of-function variants clustered in amyloid-degradation genes, and [[EPHA1 p.V160A]], [[EPHA1 p.M900V]] and [[CD33 p.A14V]] were protective. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
