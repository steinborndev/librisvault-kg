# BCHE rs55781031

An intron 2 variant of [[BCHE]] (c.1518-121T>C) found in cis with [[BCHE rs1803274]] and [[BCHE rs1126680]]. Introns can carry splicing and expression regulators; no function has been shown for this one.

## Connections

- Gene: [[BCHE]]
- Companion variants: [[BCHE rs1803274]], [[BCHE rs1126680]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]; part of the combination with [[APOE]] ε4 found in 10 of 55 patients and no control. Study: [[BCHE noncoding variants and APOE ε4 in Polish late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
