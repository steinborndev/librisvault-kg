# CBL

CBL encodes an E3 ubiquitin ligase and adaptor that associates with [[PXN]] in focal adhesion signalling.

## Connections

- Y700 phosphorylation rose 60% in FAK-overexpressing 3xTg-AD hippocampus; with [[FYN]] and PXN it is placed upstream of [[CDK5]] activation. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; mouse model: [[3xTg-AD mouse]]
