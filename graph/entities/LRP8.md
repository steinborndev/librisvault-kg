# LRP8

LRP8 encodes ApoER2, a lipoprotein receptor that binds ApoE and Reelin and is present at synapses, where it regulates vesicle release and NMDA receptor activation.

## Connections

- Presynaptic activation of ApoER2 and [[VLDLR]] by [[RELN]] raises calcium and drives vesicle fusion; ApoE4 binding traps ApoER2 in postsynaptic recycling endosomes, blocking Reelin-dependent NMDA receptor activation and lowering calcium influx and long-term potentiation. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Ligand: [[APOE]]; disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Higher in the C2 than the C1 Alzheimer brain subtype defined by [[CDK5R1]] level; part of the [[Reelin signaling pathway]]. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
