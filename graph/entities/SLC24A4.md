# SLC24A4

SLC24A4 encodes a potassium-dependent sodium/calcium exchanger of the NCKX family, widely expressed in brain, and is a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- More than 30% of plasma [[Cholesteryl ester]] species showed a different association with the SLC24A4 risk SNP in Alzheimer cases than in controls (nominal, not corrected), alongside [[PICALM]] and [[SORL1]]; the authors point to its link with blood pressure as a possible vascular route.
