# CRH

CRH encodes corticotropin-releasing hormone, the hypothalamic peptide that starts the stress response of the HPA axis and is also made in neocortex, amygdala, hippocampus and by mast cells.

## Connections

- Reviewed as activating mast cells through CRH-R1 and CRH-R2, raising blood-brain barrier permeability and glial activation, and, through PKA, PKC, calcium and [[NF-kappaB signaling]], killing neurons; stress-driven CRH is said to raise Aβ formation and tau phosphorylation, and it has been proposed as a target to lower Alzheimer risk. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- Related: [[Psychological stress]], [[Mast cell activation]], [[Cortisol]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Downregulated in five of six post-mortem brain regions of Alzheimer disease (all but cerebellum) and a co-expression hub gene; the authors read it as a weakened stress and HPA-axis buffer in vulnerable regions. Study: [[Predictive gene expression signatures of Alzheimer disease in six brain regions]]
