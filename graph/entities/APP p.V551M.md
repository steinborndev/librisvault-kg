# APP p.V551M

A very rare missense change from valine to methionine at residue 551 of [[APP]] (c.1651G>A), in the E2 ectodomain that mediates APP self-dimerisation, away from the beta- and gamma-secretase cleavage sites. It was reported as novel in a Korean patient with [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] who also carried [[PSEN2 p.H169N]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[APP]]; pathway: [[Amyloid-beta formation]]
- Found heterozygous in the Korean patient; predicted to change the contact of residue 551 inside the E2 domain and so possibly APP dimerisation, with unknown effect on amyloid beta. Its allele frequency is about 0.00001 to 0.00003 in general databases. Study: [[Double APP and PSEN2 mutations in a Korean patient with early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Co-occurring variant: [[PSEN2 p.H169N]]
