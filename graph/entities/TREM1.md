# TREM1

TREM1 encodes a receptor on myeloid cells that amplifies inflammatory responses; it lies next to [[TREM2]] on chromosome 6 and is a candidate [[Alzheimer disease]] risk locus.

## Connections

- A TREM1 risk allele was linked to a lower TREM1/TREM2 ratio in monocytes. Study: [[CD33 modulates TREM2 in a monocyte protein QTL analysis of Alzheimer loci]]
- Partner gene: [[TREM2]]
- The TREM2 stalk antibody [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]] did not bind mouse TREM1, whose sequence differs in the epitope region. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
