# Cerulenin

Cerulenin is a natural fatty acid synthase inhibitor that is also used to block [[Protein palmitoylation]] in cells.

## Connections

- At 1 µM it improved the survival of NMDA-treated cortical neurons from [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]] to the level of wild-type neurons. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Related inhibitor: [[2-Bromopalmitate]]
