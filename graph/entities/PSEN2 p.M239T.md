# PSEN2 p.M239T

A missense change from methionine to threonine at residue 239 of [[PSEN2]], in the fifth transmembrane region, classed pathogenic under the ACMG criteria. It was found in a woman with familial early-onset Alzheimer disease whose presentation included dressing apraxia, dyscalculia and visual mislocalization; a second Chinese early-onset patient with the variant had been reported in 2021. In transfected [[HEK293]] cells it raised Aβ42 (144.7 against 71.5 pg/ml) and the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio (0.067 against 0.031) without changing Aβ40. M239V and M239I at the same residue are known causes, mostly in families of Italian ancestry.
