# CAPG

CAPG encodes macrophage-capping protein, an actin-capping protein that regulates actin filament ends in macrophages and other cells.

## Connections

- Up in CSF of symptomatic GRN mutation carriers and in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia medium, and the only panel protein significantly up in Grn knockout mouse microglia; one of four proteins up across species and models, with [[FUCA1]], [[FUCA2]] and [[HEXB]]. It did not differ between diagnostic groups in an Alzheimer cohort. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related panel members: [[FABP3]], [[MDH1]], [[GDI1]], [[CD44]], [[GPNMB]]
