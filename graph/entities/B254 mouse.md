# B254 mouse

B254 is a transgenic mouse model of amyloid pathology expressing human [[APP]] with the Swedish and Indiana mutations plus a D664A substitution, on a background of endogenous mouse tau; it shows plaques from about 6 months and memory impairment in novel location and novel object tests at 5-6 months.

## Connections

- Treated orally with [[FAH65]] for 10 or 26 days: brain βCTF fell in the pilot, and after 26 days novel location and novel object preference rose, brain sAPPα rose and the sAPPβ/sAPPα ratio fell. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Compared by earlier workers with the J20 (PDAPP) line; see [[Novel object recognition test]]. Other amyloid models: [[FAD4T mouse]], [[Tg2576 mouse]], [[APOE4-TR-5xFAD mouse]]
