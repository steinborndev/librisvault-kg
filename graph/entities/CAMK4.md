# CAMK4

CAMK4 encodes calcium/calmodulin-dependent protein kinase IV, a brain-enriched kinase that links calcium signalling to gene transcription.

## Connections

- Reported upregulated in Alzheimer brain across several microarray datasets; one of the five signature genes; docking predicted high-affinity binding by [[Antrocin]]. Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Full text: localised to the nucleoplasm; docking affinity for [[Antrocin]] -6.70 kcal/mol with three hydrogen bonds (THR291, HIS156, PRO220). Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
