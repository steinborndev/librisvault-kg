# D-ribose

D-ribose is the five-carbon sugar of RNA, ATP and acetyl coenzyme A, made in cells and taken in with the diet; excess intake is harmful in rodents and the sugar is reported to be higher in Alzheimer patients than in cognitively intact people.

## Connections

- Given to [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals and [[Neuro2a]] cells as an inducer of Alzheimer-like change: it lowered mitochondrial membrane potential, raised reactive oxygen species and cytochrome c release, suppressed PINK1, Parkin and LC3B, raised amyloid-beta and phospho-Tau, impaired learning and memory and caused anxiety-like behaviour, with higher PINK1 promoter methylation and [[DNMT1]]. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Reversed by: [[Berberine]]; process affected: [[Mitophagy]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
