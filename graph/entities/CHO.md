# CHO

CHO is a cell line from the ovary of the Chinese hamster, widely used for protein expression.

## Connections

- Showed no [[IQCK]] immunoreactivity; the authors note this may reflect antibodies raised against human and mouse protein rather than true absence. Study: [[IQCK protein is raised in Alzheimer hippocampus and found in amyloid plaques]]
- Compared with [[SH-SY5Y]], [[HeLa]] and [[HMC3]] in that experiment.
- CHO-7W cells (human wild-type APP) and CHO-7WSwe cells (Swedish-mutant APP) were the cell test bed: [[FAH65]] lowered sAPPβ and Aβ1-42 at 1 μM, and its active enantiomer had an EC50 of about 25 nM for sAPPβ. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
