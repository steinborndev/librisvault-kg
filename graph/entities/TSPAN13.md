# TSPAN13

TSPAN13 encodes tetraspanin 13, a membrane protein reported to be raised in microglia that accumulate lipid droplets in the ageing brain.

## Connections

- Raised in APOE4 and APOE-knockout human microglia relative to APOE2 and APOE3, one of few genes significant in one isoform but not the other; the authors take this to suggest that lipid dysregulation in APOE4 microglia follows from loss of APOE function.
