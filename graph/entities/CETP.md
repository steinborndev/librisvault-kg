# CETP

CETP encodes cholesteryl ester transfer protein, which moves cholesteryl esters from HDL to apolipoprotein B-containing lipoproteins in exchange for triglycerides and so takes part in reverse cholesterol transport.

## Connections

- Variants: [[CETP rs5882]], [[CETP rs708272]]
- CETP haplotypes carrying A at both SNPs gained most from statins, and rs5882 GG with rs708272 GG showed the strongest atorvastatin lowering of total and LDL cholesterol. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Drugs: [[Atorvastatin]], [[Simvastatin]]
