# Decreased amyloid beta 42 peptide CSF concentration

A low level of amyloid-beta 42 in cerebrospinal fluid, which tracks amyloid plaque burden in the brain and is a core laboratory marker of [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Genetic determinants: [[APOE]] ε4 ([[APOE rs429358]]) lowers it and ε2 ([[APOE rs7412]]) raises it; [[CR1]] was a further locus. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Companion marker: [[Increased CSF phosphorylated tau concentration]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Rare-variant determinants: gene-based tests in sequenced ADNI participants tied rare variants of [[APOE]], [[CBLC]], [[BCAM]] and [[RELB]] to CSF Aβ1-42 independently of APOE genotype. Study: [[Rare variants near APOE and CSF and neuroimaging biomarkers in ADNI]]
- Lower CSF Aβ42 was associated with lower blood [[BIN1]] promoter methylation (p = 0.0008) across 814 adults without objective impairment, in the subjective cognitive decline subgroup but not in cognitively normal participants. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
- BACE2 locus SNPs ([[BACE2 rs2837994]] in Alzheimer disease, p = 0.000183; [[BACE2 rs9980146]] and [[BACE2 rs2012050]] in mild cognitive impairment) associated with CSF Aβ1-42 only in APOE ε4 non-carriers and not in cognitively normal controls; the T/T genotype of rs2012050 went with higher CSF levels. Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
