# TREM2

TREM2 encodes a type I transmembrane receptor expressed mainly on myeloid cells, including brain microglia, which signals through the adaptor TYROBP (DAP12) to regulate phagocytosis and inflammatory responses. Rare loss-of-function variants such as p.Arg47His raise the risk of [[Alzheimer disease]]; the ectodomain is shed by [[ADAM10]] and [[ADAM17]] as soluble TREM2.

## Connections

- Its core promoter carries a conserved binding element for [[YY1]]; promoter mutation or YY1 knock-down lowers TREM2 reporter activity and protein in [[BV2]] and [[Neuro2a]] cells, and both fall after [[Lipopolysaccharide]] and in the [[APP-PS45 mouse]]. Study: [[TREM2 regulation by YY1 in microglia and Alzheimer disease models]]
- Method used to map the promoter: [[Promoter mapping and transcription factor binding assays]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- The protective [[CD33 p.A14V]] allele is linked to a CD33 isoform that is thought to relieve CD33 inhibition of TREM2 in microglia. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- TREM2 and ITGAX, markers of disease-associated microglia, did not differ between APOE3 and [[APOE p.R136S]] iPSC-derived microglia after amyloid beta 42. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Its alternative splicing involves [[CELF2]], a target of [[MIR375]]. Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- A rare TREM2 variant with odds ratio 2.46 raised the modelled probability of Alzheimer disease in the 65+ age group from about 0 to 0.13 at the lowest polygenic score, and from 0.6 to 0.66 at the highest; the abstract states a rise from 0.02 to 0.3. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- Soluble TREM2 in cerebrospinal fluid was associated with enlarged perivascular spaces in the basal ganglia of cognitively unimpaired adults, but only in models not corrected for amyloid-beta 40. Study: [[Perivascular spaces and CSF biomarkers in cognitively unimpaired Alzheimer continuum]]
- TREM2 expression correlated negatively with cortical glucose metabolism in all risk groups; the authors place it among immune genes linked to immunometabolism. Study: [[Brain metabolism and Alzheimer risk gene expression in normal elderly]]
- The TREM2 knockout and R47H mutant microglia of another study share genes with APOE4 microglia: [[ZNF248]] was up in the knockout, [[CHCHD2]] down in R47H. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
- The stop-gain [[TREM2 p.Q33X]], which in homozygous form causes [[Nasu-Hakola disease]], was heterozygous in 4 of 5,617 exome-sequenced Alzheimer cases and no controls; the authors note it is rarer than the R47H risk variant. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Post-transcriptional control: circ-Epc1 (from [[EPC1]]) raised TREM2 mRNA and protein in [[BV2]] cells by sponging [[MIR770]], whose binding site lies in the TREM2 3' untranslated region; TREM2 knockdown reversed the circ-Epc1 shift toward M2 microglia (see [[Microglial cell activation]]), but in the hippocampus of treated Alzheimer mice TREM2 was reported lower. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- With [[GRN]] it drove the 'immune' cluster of Alzheimer risk variants, which lowered CSF phosphorylated tau without changing amyloid-beta 42. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- One of five GWAS-list genes that are also targets of drugs in phase 1 to 3 Alzheimer trials, with [[CD33]], [[CHRNA2]], [[PRSS8]] and [[ACE]]. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Trem2 knockout mouse microglia and TREM2 knockout iPSC-derived microglia were locked in a homeostatic state, the opposite of GRN knockout; TREM2 protein rose in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia, and CSF [[GPNMB]] correlated with CSF TREM2 in GRN mutation carriers only. The authors recall soluble TREM2 rising up to 21 years before onset in familial Alzheimer disease. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Differentially expressed or usage-changed in at least one temporal lobe brain RNAseq dataset of Alzheimer cases. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
- [[TREM2 p.L211P]] (rs2234256) was heterozygous in a Thai early-onset Alzheimer patient who also carried MAPT p.P513A. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- In monocytes from 226 people the [[CD33]] risk allele led to greater TREM2 expression, CD33 suppression decreased it, a [[TREM1]] risk allele lowered the TREM1/TREM2 ratio, and cortical TREM2 mRNA was elevated with amyloid pathology. Study: [[CD33 modulates TREM2 in a monocyte protein QTL analysis of Alzheimer loci]]
- In mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture, amyloid-beta 1-42 raised TREM2 mRNA 5.2-fold, but not in microglia kept 16 days; young cells took up more amyloid-beta while aged cells with low TREM2 and high [[CD33]] left it outside the cell. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- In [[5xFAD mouse]] animals, Trem2 knockout worsened amyloid-beta pathology and neurodegeneration and lowered Iba1-positive cell numbers, none of which a Cd33 knockout rescued; the benefit of Cd33 knockout (less amyloid, better cognition, higher phagocytosis and acute-phase gene expression in microglia) needed Trem2, placing TREM2 downstream of [[CD33]]. Study: [[TREM2 acts downstream of CD33 in 5xFAD mouse microglia]]
- A stalk-binding antibody, [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]], stabilised full-length mouse TREM2 on the cell surface, lowered soluble TREM2, raised phospho-[[SYK]] signalling through [[TYROBP]], and in 6-month-old [[AppNL-G-F mouse]] animals raised microglial TREM2, lowered the homeostatic marker [[P2RY12]] and reduced plaque area and soluble amyloid-beta. In wild-type mice it engaged nearly all CSF soluble TREM2; see [[TREM2 ectodomain shedding]]. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- In human cortex microglia, TREM2 expression came before APOE along the trajectory to the amyloid-linked AD1 state, which the authors read as a response like the Trem2-dependent mouse phagocytic/activated state; 'cellular response to amyloid-beta' terms in AD1 included it. See [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
- Soluble TREM2, shed from microglia and raised in patient CSF, bound transgelin-2 ([[TAGLN2]]) on cultured mouse neurons and inactivated the RhoA-ROCK-GSK3B axis ([[RHOA]]); in tau P301S mice TREM2 overexpression or sTREM2 lowered tau phosphorylation and deficits, which the review reads as a microglia-mediated protection. See [[TREM2 ectodomain shedding]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Phosphatidylserine liposomes, a TREM2 ligand, gave a larger calcium release in mouse macrophages, primary microglia and iPSC-derived microglia carrying the protective [[PLCG2 p.P522R]] variant than in those with the common allele, placing the PLCγ2 hyperfunction downstream of TREM2. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
- In caudate nucleus RNA-seq, TREM2 differed from controls only when [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]] cases were pooled (FPKM 10-40 and 12-39), and not when analysed separately (6 and 3 cases). Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Described as affecting cholesterol, myelin and phospholipid metabolism and as being higher in CSF of familial carriers with white matter change; APOE ε4 microglia fail to clear myelin debris. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- The TREM2 region on 6p21.1 is genome-wide significant for early-onset Alzheimer disease ([[TREM2 rs75932628]], P = 1.66e-15) and also associated with [[Triglycerides]]. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
- Raised in the brains of Apoe knockout mice, one of 22 genes common to the Apoe-driven ivory module and human post-mortem microglial modules. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Lower methylation at its CpG island shore in Alzheimer blood, reported earlier in another Japanese group, was replicated by pyrosequencing in 96 further subjects. Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
- [[TREM2 p.R62H]] had an allele frequency of 4.9% among 102 Swedish patients with early-onset or familial dementia, against about 1% in population databases. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
- ADAM17 is described as its main sheddase; in human brain TREM2 mRNA showed only low to moderate correlation with ADAM17 mRNA. Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
