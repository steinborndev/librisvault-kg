# Ginkgolide A

Ginkgolide A is a platelet-activating-factor antagonist isolated from Ginkgo biloba leaves.

## Connections

- Favoured candidate: the literature links it to modulation of α-tubulin post-translational modification, NMDA and AMPA receptor inhibition, less JNK phosphorylation and more PI3K-AKT signalling, which would reduce [[GSK3B]]-driven tau phosphorylation. Ginkgolides have been trialled only in acute ischemic stroke. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]; method: [[Drug repurposing]]
