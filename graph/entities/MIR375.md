# MIR375

MIR375 encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-375, a small non-coding RNA that represses target messenger RNAs after transcription.

## Connections

- Its interactions with [[APH1B]] and [[CELF2]] were among four shared by the vesicle-miRNA and disease-database routes, making it a leading vesicle-linked candidate marker; the interactions were validated in a gastric carcinoma sample, and miR-375 was reported to differ in cerebrospinal fluid, though not in every study.
- Carried in [[Extracellular vesicle]] cargo.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see also [[MicroRNA-target interaction databases]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
