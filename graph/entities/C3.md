# C3

C3 encodes complement component 3, the central protein of the complement cascade, which is raised in Alzheimer brain and tags synapses and debris for microglial clearance.

## Connections

- Raised in the brain of APP/PS1 mice and significantly further in APP/PS1/Sirt3-knockout mice, the one inflammatory marker that rose beyond amyloid pathology alone with Sirt3 loss. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- Related: [[Neuroinflammation]], [[CCL3]]
- Serum C3 was raised (202, normal 90 to 180) with normal C4 in a Korean woman with Alzheimer disease, read as neuroinflammation. Study: [[Novel PSEN2 p.V226A in a Korean patient with atypical Alzheimer disease]]
- Plasma C3 was higher in adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (p < 0.05), tracked BMI (r = 0.26), and was lower in the 13 with dementia than in those without (p < 0.01), which the authors read as consumption by Alzheimer pathology; the activation fragment iC3b was also raised (p < 0.001) while C4 did not differ. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
