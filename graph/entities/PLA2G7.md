# PLA2G7

PLA2G7 encodes platelet-activating factor acetylhydrolase, a circulating enzyme carried on lipoproteins that is a cardiovascular risk marker with pro-inflammatory and oxidative activity and has been linked to [[Alzheimer disease]] and cognitive decline.

## Connections

- Plasma levels were higher in carriers of the ε4-defining SNP [[APOE rs429358]] among healthy adults. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
