# GNAS

GNAS is a complex imprinted locus that encodes the stimulatory G-protein alpha subunit and related products, which couple receptors to cyclic AMP signalling.

## Connections

- In an Alzheimer network analysis it was the most significantly hypermethylated gene and sat in the top module together with [[BIN1]]. Study: [[Network-based integration of Alzheimer GWAS and DNA methylation finds BIN1 and GNAS module]]
