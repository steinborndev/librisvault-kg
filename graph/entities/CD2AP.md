# CD2AP

CD2AP encodes an adaptor protein expressed in brain capillaries that binds cortactin and takes part in receptor-mediated endocytosis. It is a genome-wide association locus for Alzheimer disease in European-ancestry cohorts.

## Connections

- [[CD2AP rs9473117]] is the variant tested for early-onset disease and brain expression.
- [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] is the subgroup in which the variant was associated.
- In [[Tg2576 mouse]] hippocampal and retinal synapses CD2AP protein did not differ from wild-type and was unchanged by the tau antibody [[12A12 monoclonal antibody]], unlike [[BIN1]] and [[RIN3]]; see [[Endocytosis]].
- Studies: [[SNP association study of Alzheimer disease in southern Chinese population]], [[Tau antibody 12A12 and amyloid-beta in Tg2576 mouse hippocampus and retina]]
- No mammalian synaptic data yet; the Drosophila homologue Cindr is presynaptic and needed for synaptic maturation and vesicle recycling, acting with 14-3-3 on calcium channel and synapsin levels. In neurons a pool of CD2AP sits on dendritic early endosomes. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- A variant near CD2AP interacted with sex on three sphingomyelins and three plasmalogens in plasma of healthy adults. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Hemizygous mice ([[Cd2ap heterozygous mouse]]) did not lower Cd2ap expression (log2 fold change -0.07), had 34 differentially expressed genes, and shared a module with the Clu knockout and a similar pattern of correlation with human modules. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
