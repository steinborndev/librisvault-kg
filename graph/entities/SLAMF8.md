# SLAMF8

SLAMF8 encodes a member of the SLAM family of immune cell surface receptors that dampens reactive oxygen species and inflammation by negatively regulating NADPH oxidase (NOX) activity.

## Connections

- Plasma SLAMF8 was higher in carriers of the ε2-defining SNP [[APOE rs7412]], suggesting another antioxidant link to ε2. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
