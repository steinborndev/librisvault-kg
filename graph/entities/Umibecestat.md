# Umibecestat

Umibecestat is a [[BACE1]] inhibitor developed to lower production of amyloid-beta in people at genetic risk of Alzheimer disease.

## Connections

- Tested with the vaccine [[CAD106]] in the Alzheimer's Prevention Initiative Generation Study 1 (NCT02565511), a double-blind placebo-controlled trial in cognitively unimpaired 60 to 75-year-olds at genetic risk, which was terminated early. The review reports results only for the CAD106 cohort. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
