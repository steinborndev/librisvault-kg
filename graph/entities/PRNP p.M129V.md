# PRNP p.M129V

The common methionine or valine polymorphism at codon 129 of [[PRNP]], a documented risk factor for [[Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease]] and proposed as a modifier of Alzheimer disease.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PRNP]]; disease: [[Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease]]
- The patient with [[PSEN1 p.E184G]] was homozygous for it; the authors call for study of codon 129 genotypes together with that presenilin change. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
