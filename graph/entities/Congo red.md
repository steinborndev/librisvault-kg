# Congo red

Congo red is an amyloid-binding azo dye reported to reduce Aβ neurotoxicity, though it can also promote aggregation of full-length Tau in some cell systems.

## Connections

- Used as the comparator in the ΔK280 TauRD screen: at 10 μM it lowered thioflavin S signal, ROS and caspase 3 activity, restored neurite length, and in the cell-free thioflavin T assay cut ΔK280 TauRD aggregation from 16,856 to 4,774 units.
