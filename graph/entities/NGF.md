# NGF

NGF encodes nerve growth factor, a neurotrophin that also acts as a pain mediator released by mast cells.

## Connections

- Mast cells make NGF, which acts on mast cells and nerve endings through TrkA and triggers further histamine and NGF release; NGF is raised in painful inflammatory states, and NGF receptor antagonists are said to reduce inflammatory pain.
