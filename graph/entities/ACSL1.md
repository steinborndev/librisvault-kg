# ACSL1

ACSL1 encodes a long-chain fatty acid CoA ligase that activates fatty acids for lipid droplet biogenesis and has been linked to APOE4-driven lipid droplet accumulation in microglia.

## Connections

- Induced by amyloid beta 42 or by PSEN1 mutant neurons in APOE3 but not [[APOE p.R136S]] microglia, matching [[Lipid droplet]] formation. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Related: [[APOE]], [[Lipid peroxidation]]
