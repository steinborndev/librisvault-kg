# CYP2C19

CYP2C19 encodes a cytochrome P450 enzyme that metabolises many antidepressants, benzodiazepines and proton pump inhibitors. Loss-of-function alleles such as *2 make poor metabolizers; the *17 promoter allele makes ultra-rapid metabolizers.

## Connections
