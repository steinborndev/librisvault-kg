# SIRT1

SIRT1 encodes sirtuin 1, an NAD+-dependent histone and protein deacetylase (chromosome 10q21.3) that acts in chromatin silencing, DNA repair, circadian control and metabolism. It is the best studied sirtuin as a neuroprotective factor in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Reviewed as reduced in Alzheimer brain and serum (falling with Braak stage and with mild cognitive impairment), lowered by [[APOE]] ε4 expression, and protective in 3×Tg-AD mice when overexpressed; it favours non-amyloidogenic [[APP]] cleavage by raising [[ADAM10]] and restraining [[BACE1]], and deacetylates TFEB to help microglia clear fibrillar amyloid. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Activators in the review include [[Resveratrol]], [[Curcumin]], [[Citicoline]], [[Donepezil]] and [[Cilostazol]]; see [[Sirtuin modulators]].
- Related sirtuins: [[SIRT2]], [[SIRT3]], [[SIRT6]]
