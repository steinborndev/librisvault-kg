# PLCG2 p.P522R

A rare missense variant of [[PLCG2]] (rs72824905) that swaps proline 522 for arginine and is associated with protection from [[Alzheimer disease]]. In cell and mouse models it behaves as a hyperfunctional allele of phospholipase C gamma 2.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PLCG2]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Knock-in macrophages and microglia from [[Plcg2 R522 knock-in mouse]] and [[Kolf2 PLCG2 R522 hiPSC clones]] showed larger calcium release, deeper loss of [[Phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate]], less [[Phagocytosis]] and more [[Endocytosis]]. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
- A common PLCG2 variant with a protective association is [[PLCG2 rs3935877]].
