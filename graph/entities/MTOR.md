# MTOR

MTOR encodes the serine-threonine kinase mTOR, a growth and nutrient sensor whose activity suppresses [[Autophagy]].

## Connections

- Total and phosphorylated mTOR fell with both compounds in model mouse hippocampus, and isoacteoside was proposed to act through this axis. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Autophagy partners: [[SQSTM1]], [[MAP1LC3B]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Inhibited by [[Ouabain]], which activates [[TFEB]] and the autophagy-lysosome system, in the proposed mechanism of an Alzheimer repurposing candidate. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
