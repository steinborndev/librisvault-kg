# ALT001

ALT001 is a synthetic isoquinolinium compound with four free hydroxyl groups, made by removing all phenolic ether groups from [[Palmatine]] or [[Berberine]]. It induces [[Mitophagy]] through the [[ULK1]]-[[RAB9A]] [[Alternative mitophagy]] pathway without depolarising mitochondria.

## Connections

- About 25-fold more potent than palmatine in the mt-Keima assay, with no change in membrane potential, no OPA1 cleavage and no cell death, unlike [[Carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenyl hydrazone]]; given intranasally it restored hippocampal mitophagy, mitochondrial function, memory and long-term potentiation in Alzheimer mouse models. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]]
- Models: [[5xFAD mouse]], [[PS2APP mouse]], [[Tg2576 mouse]]
