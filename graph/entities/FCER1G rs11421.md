# FCER1G rs11421

rs11421 is an exonic variant in the 5' UTR of [[FCER1G]], used as a proxy to measure allelic expression of that gene.

## Connections

- Gene: [[FCER1G]]
- It was itself a significant allele-specific QTL of FCER1G in monocytes, in moderate linkage disequilibrium with [[ADAMTS4 rs4575098]]. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
