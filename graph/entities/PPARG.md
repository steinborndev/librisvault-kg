# PPARG

PPARG encodes a nuclear receptor involved in lipid and glucose metabolism.

## Connections

- Target of miR-128 (the microRNA gene has two copies, MIR128-1 and MIR128-2); upregulated miR-128 lowered PPARG in clinical Alzheimer samples and cell models, and PPARG polymorphisms have been tested for effects on risk and age at onset.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- In a reporter assay with the murine receptor, brown seaweed lipid extracts of Alaria esculenta, Ascophyllum nodosum, Fucus vesiculosus and Sargassum muticum activated PPARγ most, less than PPARα; the agonist [[Pioglitazone]] served as control. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Paralog: [[PPARA]]
- Reported target of [[Tomelukast]] and an agonist-binding site for [[Sulindac sulfide]], two drug candidates from an Alzheimer repurposing screen. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- In mice, SCD2 ([[SCD]]) is not required for PPARG expression in vivo, although it is required in vitro. Study: [[SCD2 in murine development, metabolism and disease review]]
- Its promoter carried low-energy [[EDR peptide]] docking sequences (a prediction only); the authors cite PPARG agonists raising BDNF and dendritic spine density after Aβ. Study: [[EDR and KED tripeptides restore dendritic spines in 5xFAD mice]]
