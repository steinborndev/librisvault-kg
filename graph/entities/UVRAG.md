# UVRAG

UVRAG encodes a regulator of several steps of [[Autophagy]], including the fusion of autophagosomes with lysosomes.

## Connections

- UVRAG mRNA was the most strongly reduced of the autophagy genes tested after TNF-α with Smac mimetic and Z-VAD-FMK in [[SH-SY5Y]] cells, through lost [[RELA]] binding to a predicted NF-κB site in its promoter. Its overexpression prevented p62 and LC3-II build-up and necroptosis in cells, and AAV-UVRAG in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] CA1 lowered p62 by 34.5% and phospho-MLKL by 36.5% and improved water-maze performance. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Downstream effectors: [[SQSTM1]], [[MLKL]]; process: [[Necroptosis]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
