# DLD

DLD encodes dihydrolipoamide dehydrogenase, the E3 subunit shared by the pyruvate dehydrogenase, 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase and branched-chain keto acid dehydrogenase complexes.

## Connections

- Reported as the 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase complex subunit whose expression correlated significantly with amyloid-beta, Tau or MMSE in Alzheimer brain. Study: [[TCA cycle enzyme genes in Alzheimer brain and blood transcriptomes]]
- Process: [[Tricarboxylic acid cycle]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
