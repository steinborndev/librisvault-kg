# PSEN1 p.P117L

A missense change of PSEN1 in [[PSEN1]] (proline 117 to leucine), seen in familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. A known pathogenic mutation found in one Taiwanese man whose memory decline began at 36, followed by myoclonic jerks; his father had poor memory before dying at 42 in an accident.
