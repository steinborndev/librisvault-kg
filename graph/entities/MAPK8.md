# MAPK8

MAPK8 encodes JNK1, a stress-activated mitogen-activated protein kinase of the c-Jun N-terminal kinase family. This page also stands for JNK as named in studies that do not say which family member (MAPK8, MAPK9, MAPK10) was meant.
