# Lysophosphatidylcholine

Lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC) is a phospholipid made of a glycerophosphocholine head group with a single fatty acyl chain; it is a carrier form in which fatty acids can be delivered to tissues, including the brain.

## Connections

- LPC containing c-9, t-11-CLA (CLA-LPC) was significantly raised in the brains of mice fed [[cis-9, trans-11-conjugated linoleic acid]], alongside the free fatty acid. Study: [[Dietary conjugated linoleic acid lowers amyloid-beta in Alzheimer mouse hippocampus]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- None of 24 plasma LPC species differed between Alzheimer cases and controls, and the LPC model classified less accurately than sphingomyelin, cholesteryl ester or triglyceride models. Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
