# Obesity

Obesity is a disorder of excess body fat; in midlife it raises the risk of later Alzheimer disease, whereas obesity in late life is associated with lower risk.

## Connections

- A cross-disorder meta-analysis of obesity and [[Alzheimer disease]] found [[IQCK]] with the same risk-allele direction for both, and a separate study confirmed IQCK as an obesity locus. See [[IQCK protein is raised in Alzheimer hippocampus and found in amyloid plaques]].
- Obesogenic diets strongly induce mouse stearoyl-CoA desaturase-2 ([[SCD]]) in adipose tissue; reviewed with obesity among its disease contexts. Study: [[SCD2 in murine development, metabolism and disease review]]
- Plasma C3, but not C4, correlated with BMI in adults with [[Down syndrome]], consistent with obesity-driven inflammation raising C3. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
