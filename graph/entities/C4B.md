# C4B

C4B encodes one of the two isotypes of complement component 4, part of the classical complement cascade.

## Connections

- Upregulated in brain, not blood, of Alzheimer cases in the APOE ε2/ε3 group and total sample (trending the opposite way in ε2/ε3 blood). It sits in the light green co-expression network and its brain expression was associated with all three vascular injury proteins (ICAM-1 P = 1.3 × 10^-5, SAA P = 1.6 × 10^-4, VCAM-1 P = 7.0 × 10^-6), which the authors link to complement-driven barrier dysfunction. Paralog: [[C4A]]. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
