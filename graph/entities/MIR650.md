# MIR650

MIR650 encodes the precursor of microRNA miR-650, a small non-coding RNA that represses messenger RNAs by binding their 3' untranslated regions; it is reported to be altered in several human cancers, where ING4, CDK1, AKT2 and EBF3 are among its targets.

## Connections

- Mature miR-650 was the most raised of 204 microRNAs in Alzheimer cortex while its primary and precursor transcripts were lower; it repressed reporters of the [[APOE]], [[PSEN1]] and [[CDK5]] 3' untranslated regions, and AAV-driven overexpression in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] hippocampus lowered CDK5 and amyloid plaques.
