# CHI3L1

CHI3L1 encodes chitinase-3-like protein 1 (YKL-40), a secreted glycoprotein made largely by astrocytes in the brain and measured in cerebrospinal fluid as a marker of astroglial and inflammatory activation in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Higher CSF YKL40 was associated with enlarged perivascular spaces in the basal ganglia of the whole sample when amyloid-beta 40 was not corrected for, but not in the centrum semiovale. Study: [[Perivascular spaces and CSF biomarkers in cognitively unimpaired Alzheimer continuum]]
- Related: [[Enlarged perivascular spaces]], [[Neuroinflammation]]
- CHI3L1 (YKL-40) was higher in CSF of Grn knockout mice, and in human CSF it correlated with [[FABP3]], [[MDH1]], [[GDI1]] and [[CD44]], including in amyloid-positive Alzheimer patients. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
