# SYT1

SYT1 encodes synaptotagmin-1, a calcium-sensing protein of synaptic vesicles that triggers fast neurotransmitter release.

## Connections

- Synaptotagmin-1 protein was lowered in the brains of [[TgCRND8 mouse]] animals and restored by [[Magnolol]] 40 mg/kg and by [[Donepezil]] (western blot, n = 3 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
