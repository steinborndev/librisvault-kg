# CUZD1

CUZD1 encodes a protein of pancreatic secretory granules thought to influence lipid-related metabolite levels, and lies at chromosome 10q26.13.

## Connections

- Top-ranked gene in the 10q26.13 region that shares genetic signal between [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] and total, [[LDL cholesterol]] and [[Non-HDL cholesterol]]; a cortex and hippocampus eQTL colocalizes with the Alzheimer signal. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
- Part of the zymogen activation pathway, which is enriched among the top 5% of genes in an Alzheimer meta-analysis, and its protein level is correlated with genetic risk of [[Migraine]].
