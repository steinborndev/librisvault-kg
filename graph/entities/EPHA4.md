# EPHA4

EPHA4 encodes an Eph receptor tyrosine kinase whose activation by ephrin-A ligands triggers dendritic spine retraction in the hippocampus; postsynaptic EphA4 is required for synaptic plasticity.

## Connections

- [[SORL1]] binds EphA4 and attenuates its activation by ephrinA1; SORL1 overexpression in mice reduced the Aβ-induced deficits in long-term potentiation and memory, so a lower SORL1 level could raise spine retraction even without Aβ. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Related Eph receptors: [[EPHB2]], [[EPHA1]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see [[Long-term potentiation]]
