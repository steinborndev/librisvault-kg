# ULK1

ULK1 encodes a serine/threonine kinase that starts autophagosome formation; its activation by phosphorylation drives [[Alternative mitophagy]] together with [[RAB9A]].

## Connections

- [[ALT001]] raised ULK1 phosphorylation at Ser555 and Ser317 without changing ULK1 protein level in cells and in mouse hippocampus, and shRNA knockdown of ULK1 suppressed ALT001-induced mitophagy; phospho-ULK1 was reduced in 5×FAD mouse hippocampus and restored by ALT001. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
