# CFHR4

CFHR4 encodes factor H-related protein 4, a plasma protein related to [[CFH]] that controls factor H activity.

## Connections

- Plasma FHR4 was higher in adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (p < 0.001) and higher in women than men with Down syndrome. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
