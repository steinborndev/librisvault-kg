# FRAS1

FRAS1 encodes an extracellular matrix protein of the Fraser complex that anchors epithelia to the basement membrane.

## Connections

- It was significantly downregulated in blood of Alzheimer cases in the APOE ε3/ε4 group (Z = -4.66, P = 3.2 × 10^-6) and not in brain; the authors cite a report that Fras1 knockdown mice show impaired memory and learning. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
