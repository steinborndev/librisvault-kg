# DLG4

DLG4 encodes PSD-95, the main scaffold of the postsynaptic density at excitatory synapses.

## Connections

- Lowered in APP-overexpressing [[BE2-M17]] cells and restored by [[Osthol]]-[[Zinc oxide]]. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]; related synaptic markers [[SYP]], [[SYN1]]
- PSD-95 is lost in APP-Swedish neurons, in AD mice and in human AD brain, which may underlie the synaptic removal of AMPA receptors. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- PSD-95 belongs to the postsynaptic density complex (over 1000 components) on which amyloid-beta oligomers have candidate binding sites, with [[TMEM97]] and PrPc. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Other scaffolds and receptors of the same structure were altered in Alzheimer brain: Shank2 up, Shank3 down, GluR2 and NMDA receptor 1 lost. Study: [[Shank proteins and glutamate receptors at the Alzheimer postsynaptic density]]
- PSD95 protein was lowered in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and restored by [[Magnolol]] at 40 mg/kg and by [[Donepezil]] (n = 3 per group); sibling scaffold [[DLG2]]. Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
