# FAD4T mouse

FAD4T is a transgenic mouse model of familial [[Alzheimer disease]]; at 8 months it shows Aβ plaques in hippocampus and cortex, activated microglia and astrocytes, anxiety-like behaviour and memory deficits.

## Connections

- Eight-month-old males received five intravenous doses of [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]; BACE1, Aβ1-42, plaques, glial activation and cytokine mRNA fell and memory tests improved. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Other amyloid models: [[Tg2576 mouse]], [[AppNL-G-F mouse]], [[APP-PS1 mouse]]
