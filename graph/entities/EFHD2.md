# EFHD2

EFHD2 encodes a calcium-binding EF-hand protein of the cytoskeleton that has been reported to co-localise with tau in Alzheimer brain.

## Connections

- The only gene downregulated among the top 200 in both CA1 and CA3 of both sexes with Alzheimer disease in postmortem hippocampus. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
