# Dense-core amyloid plaque

A senile plaque with a compact amyloid core, often ringed by a looser halo of [[Amyloid-beta]] and by [[Dystrophic neurite]], seen in [[Alzheimer disease]] brain and distinguished from diffuse plaques that lack a core.

## Connections

- [[TMCC2]] immunoreactivity marked the centre of dense cores in late-onset (APOE3 and APOE4 homozygous), familial ([[APP p.V717I]], [[APP p.V717G]]) and [[Down syndrome]] Alzheimer brain, but not the surrounding halo or diffuse plaques.
