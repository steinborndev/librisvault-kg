# CHCHD2

CHCHD2 encodes a small mitochondrial protein that promotes cell migration and is mutated in some familial forms of [[Parkinson disease]].

## Connections

- Both expression and promoter accessibility were sharply lower in APOE4 and APOE-knockout human microglia than in APOE2 or APOE3 microglia, read by the authors as a loss of protective function; it was lower in TREM2 R47H microglia in another study ([[TREM2]]). Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
