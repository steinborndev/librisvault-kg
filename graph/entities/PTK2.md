# PTK2

PTK2 encodes focal adhesion kinase (FAK), a non-receptor tyrosine kinase that relays integrin signals to the actin cytoskeleton and is highly expressed in developing brain, where it guides neurite outgrowth and axons. In adult brain it is enriched in cortex and hippocampus, in neuronal axons and dendrites and in astrocyte intermediate filaments. Its paralogue is [[PTK2B]].
