# Alprazolam

A benzodiazepine anxiolytic, a major substrate of CYP3A4 and a minor substrate of CYP1A1, CYP1A2, CYP2B6, CYP2C9, CYP2C19 and CYP2D6. Benzodiazepines in general can impair cognition and psychomotor function in dementia.

## Connections

- Given at 0.5 to 1.0 mg a day, or lorazepam at 1 to 2 mg, for one month to the 18% of Alzheimer disease patients with agitation or moderate to severe anxiety; at these low doses it relieved anxiety without clear cognitive or psychomotor disturbance.
