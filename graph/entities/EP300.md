# EP300

EP300 encodes p300, a histone acetyltransferase that acts as a transcriptional co-activator by acetylating histones and transcription factors.

## Connections

- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Flagged as a candidate master regulator of Alzheimer progression from neuronal transcriptional networks and found dysregulated in Alzheimer brain tissue. Study: [[Master regulators of Alzheimer progression from neuronal transcriptional networks]]
