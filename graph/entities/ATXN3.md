# ATXN3

ATXN3 encodes ataxin-3, a deubiquitinating enzyme whose expanded polyglutamine form causes spinocerebellar ataxia type 3.

## Connections

- A microglia-specific allele-specific association was detected for ATXN3 but not in the tissue-level analysis, probably because of its low whole-tissue expression. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
