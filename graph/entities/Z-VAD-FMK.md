# Z-VAD-FMK

Z-VAD-FMK is a cell-permeable pan-caspase inhibitor; by blocking [[CASP8]] it removes the brake on [[RIPK1]] and pushes TNF-stimulated cells from [[Apoptosis]] to [[Necroptosis]].

## Connections

- Combined with TNF-α and [[Smac mimetic]] (the TSZ condition) it blocked caspase-8 and caspase-3 activation and raised necrosis to about 51 to 54% of [[SH-SY5Y]], [[PC12]] and primary neuron cultures; on its own in primary neurons it provoked a small amount of cell death (4.9%). Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
