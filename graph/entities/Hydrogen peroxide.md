# Hydrogen peroxide

Hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) is a reactive oxygen species used in cell culture to impose oxidative stress and injure mitochondria.

## Connections

- At 500 µM for 24 hours it raised [[TOMM40]] RNA while lowering mitochondrial DNA copy number and membrane potential in four human cell lines, [[HepG2]], [[U-118 MG]], [[U-87 MG]] and [[LN-229]]; see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
