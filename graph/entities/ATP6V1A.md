# ATP6V1A

ATP6V1A encodes the catalytic A subunit of the V1 domain of the vacuolar proton-pumping ATPase, which acidifies intracellular compartments.

## Connections

- Reported upregulated in Alzheimer brain across several microarray datasets; one of the five signature genes of the study. Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Other V-ATPase subunits ([[ATP6V1D]], [[ATP6V1G2]]) were reported lower, not higher, in Alzheimer cortex and genetically protective. Study: [[Four synaptic vesicle cycle genes protective in Alzheimer disease by MR]]
- Full text: localised to cytosol and nucleoplasm; tightly clustered with other proteins in the interaction network; docking affinity for [[Antrocin]] -5.90 kcal/mol with one hydrogen bond (HIS96). Cited prior work links ATP6V1A to developmental encephalopathy with epilepsy. Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
