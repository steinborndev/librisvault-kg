# CREB1

CREB1 encodes CREB, a transcription factor that, once phosphorylated at serine 133, switches on memory and survival genes such as BDNF and BCL2.

## Connections

- CREB and phospho-CREB (S133) fell when ΔK280 TauRD was expressed in [[SH-SY5Y]] cells and were restored by [[LM-031]]; lentiviral shRNA against CREB1 lowered CREB mRNA modestly (81% to 67%, not significant) and LM-031 still rescued neurite length (46 to 51 μm). Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- In streptozocin-treated [[3xTg-AD mouse]] hippocampus phospho-CREB fell to 68% with total CREB unchanged, and LM-031 returned phospho-CREB to 110%; the authors conclude the lesion is in phosphorylation, not CREB amount. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Downstream targets measured: [[BDNF]], [[BCL2]], [[GADD45B]], [[AKT1]], [[MAPK1]], [[MAPK3]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Phospho-CREB (normalised to total CREB) fell in brain lysates of Aβ1-42-injected mice and was reverted by 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]] and by [[Morin]]; the pathway was not blocked experimentally. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
