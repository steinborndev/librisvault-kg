# U251

U251 is a human astroglioma (glioma-derived, astrocyte-like) cell line grown in RPMI 1640 medium with serum.

## Connections

- Used as an astrocyte stand-in: 24 hours of 5 µM oligomeric [[Amyloid-beta]] raised total [[Sphingomyelin]], [[Phosphatidylinositol]] and [[Triglycerides]] and the lyso-phosphatidylethanolamine to [[Phosphatidylethanolamine]] ratio, a partial echo of the plasma changes in Alzheimer disease.
