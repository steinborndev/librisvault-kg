# MIR124-1

MIR124-1 encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-124, a small non-coding RNA that represses gene expression after transcription.

## Connections

- miR-124 (written miR-124b in the study) was one of eight microRNAs selected from a screen; its plasma level was lower in the third patient than in the other three. The authors also cite earlier work in which altered miR-124 lowered BACE1 in APP/PS1 mice.
- Marker for [[Alzheimer disease]] on the nanoparticle kit; see [[Molecular beacon nanoparticle sensor]].
- Study: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]]
- Amyloid-beta 1-42 lowered miR-124 (0.6-fold) in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture; it was already about half as high in those kept 16 days and did not respond. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
