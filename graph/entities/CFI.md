# CFI

CFI encodes factor I, a serine protease that inactivates complement convertases by cleaving C3b and C4b; low levels linked to specific CFI variants are a risk factor for [[Age-related macular degeneration]].

## Connections

- Plasma factor I was lower in adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (p < 0.01) and lower again in those with dementia than without (p < 0.05), a pattern the authors read as over-activation of complement. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
