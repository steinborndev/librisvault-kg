# TFAM

TFAM encodes mitochondrial transcription factor A, which packages and drives transcription of mitochondrial DNA and is a downstream effector of [[Mitochondrial biogenesis]].

## Connections

- Raised with [[PPARGC1A]] and [[NRF1]] after [[ALT001]] treatment of cells and mouse hippocampus; its level was lower in 5×FAD hippocampus and restored by ALT001. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Related: [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]]
