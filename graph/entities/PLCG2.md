# PLCG2

PLCG2 encodes phospholipase C gamma 2, a signalling enzyme expressed in immune cells.

## Connections

- [[PLCG2 rs3935877]] is associated with lower risk of [[Alzheimer disease]], and a second common PLCG2 variant was confirmed in the same analysis. Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
- Listed with [[TREM2]], [[ABI3]] and [[SORL1]] as genes with rare variants that a polygenic score may not capture well. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- The rare protective missense variant [[PLCG2 p.P522R]] (rs72824905) made the enzyme hyperfunctional in knock-in mouse and human iPSC macrophages and microglia: larger calcium release, deeper depletion of [[Phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate]] and more diacylglycerol, less [[Phagocytosis]] and more [[Endocytosis]]. Models [[Plcg2 R522 knock-in mouse]] and [[Kolf2 PLCG2 R522 hiPSC clones]]. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
- Transcript was significantly higher in the caudate nucleus of both [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]] postmortem brain, read by the authors as reactive microgliosis (bulk tissue, so cell type was not resolved). Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
