# HMOX1

HMOX1 encodes heme oxygenase 1, a highly inducible enzyme that breaks down heme and has antioxidant properties; it has been tied to both neuroprotection and neurodegeneration.

## Connections

- Plasma HMOX1 was higher in carriers of the ε2-defining SNP [[APOE rs7412]], which the authors read as a possible antioxidant route to ε2 protection. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
