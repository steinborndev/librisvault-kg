# SYP

SYP encodes synaptophysin, a presynaptic vesicle protein used as a marker of synaptic integrity.

## Connections

- Lowered in APP-overexpressing [[BE2-M17]] cells and restored by [[Osthol]]-[[Zinc oxide]]. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]; related synaptic markers [[SYN1]], [[DLG4]]
- Synaptophysin is lost after AMPA receptor loss in APP-Swedish neurons, and in vitro Aβ blocks synaptophysin-VAMP2 complex formation. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Synaptophysin protein was lowered in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and restored by [[Magnolol]] at 40 mg/kg and by [[Donepezil]] (n = 3 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
