# PPM1B

PPM1B encodes a Mg2+/Mn2+-dependent serine/threonine protein phosphatase of the PPM family.

## Connections

- In mammalian cells it binds [[DYRK1A]] and dephosphorylates its Ser258, suppressing the kinase; this prevented tau oligomerisation, aggregation and toxicity in cell lines including DYRK1A-overexpressing H19-7 hippocampal progenitor cells. The review calls work on phosphatases that restrain tau kinases early-stage. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
