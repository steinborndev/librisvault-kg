# PSEN2 p.V226A

A heterozygous missense change from valine to alanine at residue 226 of [[PSEN2]], at the cytosolic end of the fifth transmembrane helix. It was found by exome sequencing in a 59-year-old Korean woman with rapidly progressive memory loss, visual hallucinations and amyloid-positive [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]; it is very rare in gnomAD (about 1.6 per million) and absent from 1000 Genomes, and its pathogenicity is unproven.
