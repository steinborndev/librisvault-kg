# Cortisol

Cortisol is the main glucocorticoid of the HPA axis in humans (corticosterone in rodents), released after [[CRH]] signalling during stress.

## Connections

- Raised in stress and in people with dementia and cognitive impairment; prolonged glucocorticoid exposure in chronic stress is said to promote Aβ and tau deposition, and Aβ in turn may drive HPA axis activity. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- Related: [[Psychological stress]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
