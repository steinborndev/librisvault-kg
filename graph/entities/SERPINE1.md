# SERPINE1

SERPINE1 encodes PAI-1, the main inhibitor of tissue plasminogen activator and a regulator of fibrinolysis that is also linked to cellular senescence.

## Connections

- Plasma PAI-1 fell in both wild-type and 5xFAD mice after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]]; the authors cite earlier work in which loss of PAI-1 lessened amyloid burden in Alzheimer mouse models. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- Related: [[Cellular senescence]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
