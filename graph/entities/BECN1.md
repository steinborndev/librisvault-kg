# BECN1

BECN1 encodes beclin 1, a protein of the early steps of [[Autophagy]] that is also recruited to phagosome membranes for LC3-associated phagocytosis; its level declines with age.

## Connections

- Beclin-1 protein rose 1.6-fold after amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture, was low in microglia kept 16 days, and rose only slightly and not significantly with the peptide there. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Readout partner: [[MAP1LC3B]]
