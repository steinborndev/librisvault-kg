# APP p.V717G

A familial missense change from valine to glycine at residue 717 of [[APP]], at the same transmembrane position as [[APP p.V717I]], and a cause of [[Familial Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[APP]]
- In one early-onset case, the temporal gyrus had abundant plaques; dense cores had [[Dystrophic neurite]] positive for both APP and [[TMCC2]], while other plaques had APP-positive dystrophies without TMCC2, as in the V717I cases. Study: [[TMCC2 in dense-core plaques and Down syndrome pathology in Alzheimer brain]]
