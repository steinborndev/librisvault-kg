# ACE Alu insertion-deletion

An Alu repeat insertion or deletion within intron 16 of [[ACE]]; the deletion allele raises serum angiotensin-converting enzyme and is linked to obesity and higher blood pressure.

## Connections

- Gene: [[ACE]]
- Insertion-allele carriers had lower total and LDL cholesterol regardless of statin use, and lower triglycerides on atorvastatin, while D/D carriers had lower triglycerides only on simvastatin. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Drugs: [[Simvastatin]], [[Atorvastatin]]
