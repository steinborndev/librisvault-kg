# MIR146B

MIR146B encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-146b, a small non-coding RNA that represses gene expression after transcription.

## Connections

- miR-146b was one of eight microRNAs selected from a screen and was measured in patient plasma; no stage-specific pattern was reported for it.
