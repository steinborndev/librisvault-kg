# Simvastatin

A lipophilic [[Statin]] that inhibits HMG-CoA reductase.

## Connections

- In Alzheimer disease outpatients it lowered total and LDL cholesterol and raised HDL cholesterol after one year; ACE insertion-allele carriers did better on total and LDL cholesterol with it than with atorvastatin. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Pharmacogenetic modifiers: [[ACE Alu insertion-deletion]], [[LDLR rs11669576]], [[CETP rs5882]]
- Compared with [[Atorvastatin]]
