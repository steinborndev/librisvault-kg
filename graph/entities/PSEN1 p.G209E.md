# PSEN1 p.G209E

A missense change of PSEN1 in [[PSEN1]] (glycine 209 to glutamate), seen in familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. A known pathogenic mutation found in one Taiwanese patient with onset at 60 and a positive family history.
