# P2rx4 knockout mouse

A mouse in which the first coding exon of P2rx4, the orthologue of [[P2RX4]], is replaced by a LacZ-neomycin cassette, so that the promoter drives beta-galactosidase. The line was backcrossed at least 20 generations onto C57BL/6 and carries a P2X7 passenger mutation from the 129 stem-cell background.

## Connections

- Crossed to [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] animals, it raised microglial ApoE, lowered soluble [[Amyloid-beta]] and reversed Hamlet-test and water-maze memory deficits at 12 months, without changing plaque load. Macrophages from it were the source of the ApoE accumulation in culture. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
