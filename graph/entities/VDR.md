# VDR

VDR encodes the vitamin D receptor, a ligand-inducible nuclear transcription factor that mediates the transcriptional effects of [[Vitamin D]].

## Connections

- Its binding motif was the top hit in regions with more open chromatin in APOE2 than in other APOE isoforms of xenografted human microglia, and monocyte VDR target genes were over-represented among genes higher in APOE2; the authors propose a route through [[IL10RA]]. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
- Isoform context: [[APOE]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
