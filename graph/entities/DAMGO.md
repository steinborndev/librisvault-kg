# DAMGO

DAMGO is a synthetic enkephalin analogue and a selective agonist of the μ-opioid receptor ([[OPRM1]]).

## Connections

- At 1 and 5 μM it raised [[BACE1]] mRNA, protein and activity, accelerated [[APP]] cleavage, raised Aβ42 and lowered viability in [[PC12]] and APPswe [[SH-SY5Y]] cells. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
