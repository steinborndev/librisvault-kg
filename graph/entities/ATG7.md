# ATG7

ATG7 encodes the E1-like enzyme of the LC3 and ATG12 conjugation systems needed for canonical autophagosome formation.

## Connections

- Mitophagy induced by [[ALT001]] persisted in ATG7 knockout [[HeLa]] cells expressing Parkin while [[Carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenyl hydrazone]]-induced mitophagy was abolished, placing ALT001 outside the canonical pathway.
