# BCAM

BCAM encodes the basal cell adhesion molecule, a cell-surface glycoprotein that carries the Lutheran blood group antigens and binds laminin; it lies in the chromosome 19 region around [[APOE]].

## Connections

- Rare variants in the gene stayed associated with CSF amyloid-beta 1-42 after adjustment for APOE genotype and multiple testing. Study: [[Rare variants near APOE and CSF and neuroimaging biomarkers in ADNI]]
- Phenotype: [[Decreased amyloid beta 42 peptide CSF concentration]]
- Neighbours in the same window: [[CBLC]], [[RELB]]
- Found in both European and African American cohorts among 37 genes whose predicted protein level associates with Alzheimer risk in a proteome-wide association study. Study: [[Penalized regression PWAS of Alzheimer disease in European and African American cohorts]]
