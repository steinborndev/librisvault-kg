# ABCA7

ABCA7 encodes a lipid-transporting ATP-binding cassette protein expressed in brain and immune cells and is a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Variant: [[ABCA7 p.G1527A]], nominally associated with small vessel ischemic disease risk.
- Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- ABCA7 was one of six genes with a microglia allele-specific association, overlapping the tissue-level signal. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- [[ABCA7 p.E1679X]] occurred in 4 cases and no controls in a case-only exome screen; the related transporter [[ABCD4]] carried a case-only haplotype. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Contributed to the 'amyloid' cluster of Alzheimer risk variants (lower CSF amyloid-beta 42 with higher phosphorylated tau), together with [[BIN1]], [[PICALM]] and [[CLU]]. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Lipid transporter with risk-raising and protective variants; knockout mice have altered brain lipids and impaired spatial memory (stronger in females), healthy carriers show impaired cortical connectivity, and a genetic interaction with [[APOE]] on memory is reported. Highest brain expression is in neurons; a direct synaptic mechanism is unshown. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Downregulation is reported to change cholesterol metabolism and cut amyloid-beta efflux in an in vitro blood-brain barrier model. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- The risk allele of [[ABCA7 p.G1527A]] lowered two [[Lactosylceramide]] species from early adulthood, supporting a lipid function, raised plasma [[DEFA1]], and interacted with sex on [[UBE2F]] in healthy adults. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Methylation at the ABCA7 CpG island shore did not differ between Japanese Alzheimer and control blood. Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
- The G allele of [[ABCA7 rs3764650]] moved the midpoint of cognitive decline about 1.75 years earlier in Han Chinese patients in Taiwan, an effect absent in ADNI, suggesting population-specific action. Study: [[Genetic effects on cognitive decline trajectories in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
- Differentially associated with more than half of plasma [[Diacylglycerol]] species (52.6%) and with phosphatidylinositols (57.1%, a figure the paper's discussion also assigns to MS4A6A) between Alzheimer cases and controls, with little effect on other lipid classes; nominal, not surviving correction. Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
