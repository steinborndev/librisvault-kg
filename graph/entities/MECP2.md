# MECP2

MECP2 encodes methyl-CpG-binding protein 2, a chromatin-associated protein that reads DNA methylation and regulates gene expression in neurons. Loss of function causes Rett syndrome.

## Connections

- In the hippocampus of the [[Ts65Dn mouse]] (mouse Mecp2), its level was rescued when the microRNAs [[MIR155]] and [[MIR802]] were sponged, marking it as a target of their overexpression. Study: [[miR-155 and miR-802 targets in the hippocampus of Ts65Dn Down syndrome mice]]
- Disease context: [[Down syndrome]]
