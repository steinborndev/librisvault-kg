# SORL1 p.D2065V

A missense change from aspartate to valine at residue 2065 of [[SORL1]], with a sample frequency of 0.46%.

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- Imputed in a GWAS it gave an odds ratio of 1.36 (95% CI 1.2-1.54), but in the exome sample it pointed the opposite way (OR 0.87, p = 0.18), a gap the authors attribute to limited power; carriers had a slightly earlier age at onset than wild-type cases.
