# DNM1

DNM1 encodes dynamin-1, a GTPase that cuts newly formed endocytic vesicles from the presynaptic membrane during synaptic vesicle recycling.

## Connections

- Depletion of dynamin-1 by overexpression of APP with the Swedish mutation, and calpain-driven degradation of dynamin-1 triggered by calcium influx from extracellular Aβ, are proposed to slow synaptic vesicle endocytosis in familial Alzheimer disease mouse models. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- [[BIN1]] binds dynamin through its SH3 domain, and CD2AP-family proteins bind dynamin in the kidney.
- Process: [[Synaptic vesicle cycle]]; disease: [[Familial Alzheimer disease]]
