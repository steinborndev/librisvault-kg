# APH1A

APH1A encodes one of the two alternative APH-1 subunits (the other is [[APH1B]]) of the [[Gamma-secretase complex]], which cleaves [[APP]] to release [[Amyloid-beta]].

## Connections

- Its mRNA did not clearly differ between mouse gut segments and brain at 3, 6 and 12 months. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- APH-1 protein was raised in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and lowered by [[Magnolol]] (p < 0.05 at 20 mg/kg, p < 0.001 at 40 mg/kg); an effect of donepezil on APH-1 was not reported (n = 3 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
