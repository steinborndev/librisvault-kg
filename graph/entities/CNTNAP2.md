# CNTNAP2

CNTNAP2 encodes contactin associated protein 2, a neuronal cell-adhesion protein tied to neurodevelopment.

## Connections

- Hub and bridge gene in the protein interaction network, with [[APOE]] and [[RELN]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- CTCF binding at CNTNAP2 was reduced in Alzheimer frontal cortex. Study: [[Diminished CTCF binding at synaptic genes in Alzheimer frontal cortex]]; regulator: [[CTCF]]
- Plasma CNTNAP2 was higher in carriers of the APOE ε2 SNP [[APOE rs7412]]. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
