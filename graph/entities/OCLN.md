# OCLN

OCLN encodes occludin, a transmembrane protein of the [[Tight junction]] linked inside the cell to the actin cytoskeleton.

## Connections

- Lower in rat hippocampus and frontal cortex after restraint stress, and lower in cultured brain endothelial cells exposed to mast cell tryptase; by contrast its expression is said to be raised in dementia and Alzheimer disease. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- Related: [[CLDN5]], [[TJP1]], [[Blood-brain barrier dysfunction]]
- Small-intestine Ocln mRNA was lower in untreated 5xFAD mice than in treated wild type and was no longer different after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]]; it rose in treated wild-type mice. See [[CLDN3]]. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
