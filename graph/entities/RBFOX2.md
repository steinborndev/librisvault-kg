# RBFOX2

RBFOX2 encodes an RNA-binding splicing regulator of the Fox family that binds GCAUG motifs in pre-mRNA.

## Connections

- RBFOX2 was among splicing-machinery genes with altered expression at high Braak stage and was lower in the perirhinal cortex of Alzheimer brains. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
