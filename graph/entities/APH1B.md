# APH1B

APH1B encodes one of the four core subunits of the gamma-secretase complex.

## Connections

- [[APH1B rs35408871]] is the intronic variant tested for early-onset disease.
- [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] is the subgroup in which the variant was associated.
