# APOE

APOE encodes apolipoprotein E, a lipid transport protein, and its ε4 allele is the strongest known genetic risk factor for late-onset Alzheimer disease.

## Connections

- [[Alzheimer disease]] risk is raised by the ε4 allele, and cases were stratified by carrier status.
- [[FERMT2 rs17125924]] showed a stronger association in ε4 carriers.
- [[TOMM40]] is the neighbouring gene; the APOE/TOMM40 locus was not the main driver of the link between Alzheimer genetic risk and [[Motor impairment]].
- APOE3 and APOE4 protein forms were two plasma markers on a fluorescent nanoparticle diagnostic kit for Alzheimer disease.
- Studies: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]], [[SNP association study of Alzheimer disease in southern Chinese population]], [[Alzheimer polygenic risk score and motor impairment in older adults]]
- In a Russian case-control cohort the ε4 allele, [[APOE rs429358]] and [[APOE rs769449]] stayed significant after multiple-testing correction, and [[APOE]] was the hub of the protein interaction network; see [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]].
- In postmortem frontal lobe, TOMM40 RNA was not associated with ε4 status, though it was raised in Alzheimer cases; the authors note APOE mRNA is also raised in Alzheimer brain and propose co-regulation within one topological domain; see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
- In 96 early-onset [[Small vessel ischemic disease]] patients only 29% carried ε4, no more than in controls, yet mouse Apoe transcripts rose up to 7-fold in hypoperfused hippocampus and striatum; the authors conclude APOE regulation, not the ε4 allele, may matter in ischemia. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- The rare protective variant [[APOE p.R136S]] (APOE3 Christchurch) made isogenic iPSC-derived microglia resistant to amyloid-beta-driven [[Lipid droplet]] formation, [[Lipid peroxidation]] and [[Ferroptosis]], preserving [[Phagocytosis]] and lowering phosphorylated Tau; an [[APOE mimetic peptide]] spanning residues 133-149 reproduced the effect in APOE3 cells. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- ε4 carriers were stratified by a 39-variant [[Polygenic risk score]]: the score separated age at onset only in ε4 carriers (4 years in heterozygotes, 5.5 years in homozygotes), and ε4 heterozygotes with the highest score resembled ε4 homozygotes. Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
- Each added ε4 allele raised risk about 3-fold in a sequencing sample, and shortened age at onset additively with [[SORL1]] truncating or high-priority missense variants (by 6 and 9 years in ε4-negative cases, 4 and 5.5 years in ε4/ε4 cases); interaction evidence was weak. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Modelled separately from the polygenic score because it changes disease prevalence and its ε4 frequency falls with age (0.18 at 55+, 0.05 at 85+): in the 65+ group ε4 raised the probability of Alzheimer disease from 0.07 at the lowest score up to 0.7 at the highest in ε4/ε4 carriers, and in the 85+ group the range was 0.3 to 0.85. The paper cites an odds ratio of 3.2 (allele frequency 0.14) and an onset of about 68 years in ε4/ε4 carriers. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- Among 322 cognitively unimpaired adults enriched for ε4 carriers, the number of ε4 alleles was not associated with [[Enlarged perivascular spaces]]. Study: [[Perivascular spaces and CSF biomarkers in cognitively unimpaired Alzheimer continuum]]
- In ROSMAP blood and brain RNA-seq stratified by genotype, 21 hallmark pathways were significant in at least one group; ten in ε3/ε4, and allograft rejection, interferon gamma response, peroxisome and TNFA signaling via NFKB were up in Alzheimer ε3/ε4 but down in ε2/ε3 and ε3/ε3. A brain co-expression network preserved in blood had higher expression in ε4 carriers, and its genes were associated with vascular injury proteins, pointing to an ε4 effect on [[Blood-brain barrier dysfunction]]. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
- In 189 Brazilian Alzheimer disease outpatients on statins (53.4% ε4 carriers), ε4 carriers gained more HDL cholesterol on [[Atorvastatin]] than non-carriers (corrected P = 0.011); the authors recommend stratifying lipid-drug studies by ε4 status. Haplotype SNP: [[APOE rs7412]]. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Across cortical regions of the Allen Human Brain Atlas, APOE expression had the strongest negative correlation with glucose metabolism of ten risk genes (r = -0.746 in cognitively normal elderly, more than two standard deviations beyond the whole-transcriptome distribution), strengthening from -0.733 in stable normals to -0.778 in those who later developed Alzheimer disease, and strongest in the module holding hippocampus, amygdala and entorhinal cortex. The correlation did not depend on participants' APOE genotype. Study: [[Brain metabolism and Alzheimer risk gene expression in normal elderly]]; methods: [[FDG PET]], [[Allen Human Brain Atlas]]
- Isoform-specific microglial regulation: in human iPSC-derived microglia xenografted into [[AppNL-G-F mouse]] brains, APOE4 versus APOE2 gave 1639 differentially expressed genes (APOE4 versus APOE3 332, APOE2 versus APOE3 470) and APOE expression was lower in APOE4 microglia. APOE4 microglia lowered migration, antigen-presentation and disease-associated programmes and, with the knockout, lowered [[CHCHD2]] and raised [[TSPAN13]] and [[ZNF248]] and raised cytokines ([[CCL3]], [[CCL3L1]], [[CCL4L2]]), with an [[STAT2]] motif in open chromatin, whereas APOE2 microglia migrated and phagocytosed more and showed a [[VDR]] motif and raised [[IL10RA]]. Genes lowered in APOE4 and the knockout were enriched for Alzheimer GWAS risk, supporting partial loss of function. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]; model: [[Human microglia xenotransplantation model]]
- Plasma APOE measured by [[Multiple reaction monitoring mass spectrometry]] in 331 Moscow participants was part of the 13-protein universal panel for Alzheimer disease versus controls (ROC-AUC 0.90) and for progressing versus stable [[Mild cognitive impairment]] (0.81), and was one of the most replicated plasma candidates. The ε2 allele was associated with higher plasma APOE (beta 0.37) and ε4 with lower (beta -0.22), both q = 6.3 x 10^-3. Study: [[Plasma protein panel for Alzheimer disease risk by MRM mass spectrometry]]
- In 1086 patients with Alzheimer disease, APOE ε4 carriers (especially 2/4 and 4/4) had earlier onset and 3/3 carriers were the best and ε4 carriers the worst responders to a one-year multifactorial treatment; ε4 carriers also accumulated among SIRT2 T/T and C/T carriers ([[SIRT2 rs10410544]]) and among CYP2D6 poor metabolizers ([[CYP2D6]]), and APOE4 expression is reported to lower [[SIRT1]]. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Fine-mapping of the extended locus found risk haplotypes in the [[NECTIN2]] and [[APOC1]] regions that act independently of ε4 and ε2; they raised brain APOE transcript (most clearly in non-carriers of ε4), while ε4 itself lowered APOE, [[TOMM40]] and APOC1 transcript in brain, and APOE transcript rose with age in blood and brain. Long-range haplotypes joining the three regions and carrying ε4 were enriched in patients. Study: [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]]
- In allele-specific analysis of four brain regions, the ε4 allele lowered expression of the major APOE transcript in every region, and the effect persisted in unaffected controls only, so it is not caused by disease status; opposite exon-level effects reflected linkage of ε4 with [[APOE rs440446]] in a minor transcript. Astrocytes are proposed as the main source. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- Expression rose in [[CCF-STTG1]] astrocytoma cells treated with lipid extracts of seven brown seaweeds, together with the efflux genes [[ABCA1]] and [[ABCG1]]; the authors propose this supports secretion of APOE-containing particles that feed neurons. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- In a 13,116-person meta-analysis of CSF markers it was the strongest locus for both: ε4 ([[APOE rs429358]]) lowered amyloid-beta 42 (p = 2e-288) and raised phosphorylated tau, ε2 ([[APOE rs7412]]) did the reverse, and the signal colocalized with a helper T cell eQTL for [[NKPD1]], judged a likely false positive. Phenotypes: [[Decreased amyloid beta 42 peptide CSF concentration]], [[Increased CSF phosphorylated tau concentration]]. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- The protective ε2 isoform was tested in isogenic hiPSC neural cultures from three [[Familial Alzheimer disease]] lines: converting APOE3 to APOE2 lowered secreted amyloid-beta 42 and 40 in all three, lowered the Aβ42/40 ratio and phosphorylated tau only in a [[PSEN1 p.A246E]] line, and left a non-demented control unchanged. The effect was partly astrocyte-dependent and was explained by a shift of APP processing toward non-amyloidogenic cleavage more than by Aβ uptake. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]; model: [[Isogenic hiPSC neural culture model]]
- Apoe protein rose in Grn knockout mouse microglia and CSF, and APOE rose at mRNA and protein level in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Reviewed for synaptic effects independent of Aβ: ApoE4 misfolds, recycles poorly, is hypolipidated and moves cholesterol less efficiently; APOE4 knock-in and APOE knockout mice have fewer, shorter spines, weaker plasticity and higher calcineurin ([[PPP3CA]]) activity; ApoE4 traps [[LRP8]] in postsynaptic recycling endosomes. Correcting APOE4 to APOE3 in patient iPSC neurons reduced synaptic release and density without rescuing secreted Aβ. Alleles ε3, ε2 and ε4 have frequencies of 77.9%, 8.4% and 13.7%. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Mice homozygous for human APOE ε4 crossed with 5xFAD mice ([[APOE4-TR-5xFAD mouse]]) were used to measure brain exposure and sAPPβ lowering of [[FAH65]]; the authors suggest BACE1 inhibition may suit ε4 carriers before symptoms. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Genotyped post-mortem cortex showed that ε4 raised synaptic clusterin ([[CLU]]) in Alzheimer cases, though not total cortical clusterin, and the loss of synapses near plaques and the share of synapses holding oligomeric [[Amyloid-beta]] were greater in ε4 than ε3 Alzheimer cases. Study: [[Clusterin and amyloid-beta accumulate in synapses of APOE4 carriers in Alzheimer disease brain]]; method: [[Array tomography]]
- Chromatin architecture: the APOE exon 4 CpG island loops to the [[APOC1]] promoter in every cell line and brain sample tested, and the APOE promoter loops to [[TOMM40]] introns 2 to 3 in astrocyte-like and microglia-like but not neuron-like cells. Contact strength was higher in Alzheimer frontal lobe and in ε3/ε4 carriers, while ε4/ε4 brains showed little looping despite high APOC1 mRNA; see [[Chromatin looping]]. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
- In the pseudotime trajectory of human cortex microglia towards the amyloid-linked AD1 state, APOE expression followed [[TREM2]]. See [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
- In 757 sequenced ADNI participants, rare variants in APOE itself were associated with CSF amyloid-beta 1-42 after correction for multiple testing and with APOE genotype controlled, alongside [[CBLC]], [[BCAM]] and [[RELB]] in the surrounding 312 kb window. Study: [[Rare variants near APOE and CSF and neuroimaging biomarkers in ADNI]]
- In a penalized-regression proteome-wide association study APOE was one of 37 genes associated with Alzheimer risk and was found in both European and African American cohorts, alongside [[BCAM]]. Study: [[Penalized regression PWAS of Alzheimer disease in European and African American cohorts]]; method: [[Proteome-wide association study]]
- ApoE was detergent-insoluble in demented but not control human amygdala. Study: [[Detergent-insoluble proteins in amygdala of cognitively impaired elderly]]
- ApoE-binding partner [[TMCC2]]: in 15 human brains stratified by APOE3/3 versus APOE4/4, total TMCC2 did not differ, but the cerebellum-versus-temporal gyrus shift in TMCC2 western blot band ratio was seen in APOE3/3 and absent in APOE4/4 AD, and TMCC2-APP co-staining of plaque-adjacent dystrophies was more common in APOE3 brains; APOE4/4 cases were at later Braak stages. Study: [[TMCC2 in dense-core plaques and Down syndrome pathology in Alzheimer brain]]
- The APOE genotypes of five affected members of an Italian autosomal dominant late-onset family were ε3ε3 (three), ε2ε4 and ε3ε4, so ε4 did not explain the pedigree. Study: [[Exome sequencing finds GRIN2C p.A1072V in an Italian late-onset Alzheimer family]]
- In region-wise logistic models of post-mortem brain, e3/e4 raised the odds of Alzheimer status over e2/e3 in dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (OR 9.58) and cerebellum (OR 27.9); in temporal cortex genotype separated cases perfectly in only 23 samples. Study: [[Predictive gene expression signatures of Alzheimer disease in six brain regions]]
- Reviewed as a driver of white matter injury and cholesterol dysmetabolism: ε4 impairs astrocyte-to-oligodendrocyte cholesterol transfer, kills cultured oligodendrocytes when lipidated, and worsens white matter change from early adulthood, while APOE2 carriers have robust white matter. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- All 55 patients and 18 controls in a Polish study carried ε4, and ten patients (18%) also carried three [[BCHE]] variants ([[BCHE rs1803274]], [[BCHE rs1126680]], [[BCHE rs55781031]]) that no control had; the K variant alone gave no ε4-dependent signal. Study: [[BCHE noncoding variants and APOE ε4 in Polish late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- In plasma of 2831 healthy adults aged 18 to 90, the ε4 SNP raised total and LDL cholesterol, lowered HDL, and raised [[PLA2G7]], [[CD28]] and two [[Diacylglycerol]] species, while the ε2 SNP lowered LDL cholesterol and raised six proteins ([[LDLR]], [[HMOX1]], [[SLAMF8]], [[RNF31]], [[CNTNAP2]], [[SRP14]]); an APOE-free polygenic score showed no blood association. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Upregulated in astrocytes of aged cynomolgus monkey entorhinal cortex in a single-nucleus comparison of young and aged animals. Study: [[Single-nucleus atlas of aging monkey entorhinal cortex and Alzheimer disease]]
- Described as the most potent single-gene risk modifier of late-onset disease whose genotype effect is still unexplained, also linked to clinical heterogeneity and to risk and onset age in Down syndrome and familial disease; solving it is called a high-priority genetics goal. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- In 1006 Alzheimer disease patients, APOE 3/3 and 3/4 carriers were the best responders in anxiety and depression after one month of multifactorial treatment, and 4/4 carriers (3.5%) had the highest baseline depression; the authors summarise that APOE-4 carriers respond worst. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
- Used as a covariate (ε4 carrier status) in PSEN1 progression models; cytoplasmic PSEN1 carriers had a higher share of ε4 carriers than transmembrane carriers, and the authors note a consistent ε4 effect on progression has not been seen in dominantly inherited disease. Study: [[PSEN1 transmembrane versus cytoplasmic variants and progression in DIAN]]
- In 814 adults without objective impairment, adding an APOE ε4 by [[BIN1]] promoter methylation interaction left the methylation links to CSF Aβ42 and the tau ratios unchanged; the interaction modestly affected CSF p-tau (p = 0.0195), and effects looked larger in the 122 ε4 carriers. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
- Predicted by TargetScan as a [[MIR650]] target; miR-650 lowered an APOE 3' untranslated region reporter in cultured cells, and deleting the site removed the effect (not tested in mice). Study: [[MicroRNA-650 targets CDK5 and lessens amyloid pathology in APP-PSEN1 mice]]
- The APOE region on 19q13.32 was the strongest early-onset Alzheimer disease GWAS locus (top SNP [[APOE rs59007384]], P = 5.73e-311) and was significant for all five blood lipid traits tested, with [[APOE rs429358]] topping HDL and [[APOE rs7412]] topping LDL, non-HDL and total cholesterol. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
- In 63 adults with [[Down syndrome]], the 16 ε4 heterozygotes (2 with dementia) did not differ from non-carriers in any of 14 plasma complement proteins; the authors flag low power. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
- Stratifying by ε4 dose revealed a nominal [[BACE2]] locus association with Alzheimer disease, with CSF Aβ1-42 and with BACE2 expression only in ε4 non-carriers (1599 autopsy-verified subjects; ADNI for CSF), suggesting BACE2 acts in an APOE-dependent way. Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
- In 61 genotyped patients with amnestic [[Mild cognitive impairment]] due to Alzheimer disease (72.6% ε4 carriers), ε4 carriage was not associated with informant-rated [[Mild behavioral impairment]] severity (B = 0.52, p = 0.846), its domain scores or depressive symptoms, nor was the APOE-by-[[BDNF p.V66M]] interaction (p = 0.948); the authors suggest earlier positive ε4 findings in unselected non-demented samples may include undetected preclinical Alzheimer disease. Study: [[Mild behavioral impairment with APOE and BDNF in early Alzheimer disease]]
- Mouse Apoe loss ([[Apoe knockout mouse]]) and astrocyte-driven human APOE ε4 ([[GFAP-APOE4 mouse]]) gave the most similar whole-brain transcriptomes of five risk-gene mice; the knockout drove an inflammation and microglia module that matched human post-mortem microglial modules and correlated positively with human immune modules. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Methylation of the APOE CpG island shore did not differ between Alzheimer and control blood or in other dementias, but was somewhat higher in ε4 homozygotes; CLU shore methylation added to ε4 allele number for classifying patients (AUC 0.80 to 0.85 versus 0.73 to 0.78). Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
- The top-scoring [[EDR peptide]] DNA sequence occurred twice in its promoter by docking (a prediction only); the authors cite fewer dendritic spines with the ε4 allele in mice and in human Alzheimer brain. Study: [[EDR and KED tripeptides restore dendritic spines in 5xFAD mice]]
- The APOE ε4 allele frequency was 54% in 102 Swedish patients with early-onset or familial dementia (57% in 77 Alzheimer cases), and [[APOE p.R269G]], earlier linked to familial hypercholesterolemia, was found in one early-onset patient. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
- Each ε4 copy brought the midpoint age of cognitive decline forward by 2.39 years (95% credible interval -4.12 to -0.64) and raised the rate of decline in 299 Han Chinese Alzheimer and MCI patients, and shifted the midpoint in the ADNI sample too; with [[SORL1 rs3737529]] it formed the best-fitting of eight models. Study: [[Genetic effects on cognitive decline trajectories in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
- In mouse macrophages and microglia, ApoE bound the ATP-gated channel [[P2RX4]] in lysosomes, which promoted [[CTSB]]-dependent ApoE degradation (P2X4-null cells held several-fold more ApoE with unchanged mRNA); in 12-month [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] animals lacking P2X4 microglial ApoE rose, soluble [[Amyloid-beta]] fell and memory recovered, while ApoE and P2X4 co-localised in plaque-associated microglia of human Alzheimer cortex. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
