# Cd2ap heterozygous mouse

A mouse with one deleted copy of Cd2ap, the mouse orthologue of [[CD2AP]], kept on a [[C57BL-6 mouse]] background by crossing heterozygotes to wild type because homozygous loss is lethal.

## Connections

- At 6 months, whole-brain RNA-seq of females showed 34 differentially expressed genes (down genes enriched for [[Endocytosis]], phagosome and autoimmune terms) and no change in Cd2ap expression itself (log2 fold change -0.07, p = 0.7). It shared the steelblue module with the [[Clu knockout mouse]] and correlated with human modules like that knockout, which the authors tentatively link to blood-brain barrier function. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Grouped with the [[Bin1 heterozygous mouse]] in principal component analysis; see [[Mouse-human module comparison]]
