# BIN1 rs6733839

rs6733839 is a late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] GWAS SNP at the [[BIN1]] locus.

## Connections

- Gene: [[BIN1]]
- Its association with BIN1 allelic expression reached significance only by combining weak evidence from genotype-level and allele-specific expression in the hierarchical Poisson mixed model, and BIN1 was also an allele-specific QTL in microglia. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- The most significant variant of the Alzheimer GWAS at BIN1, tagging the first of two independent signals; the CSF phosphorylated tau signal colocalized with it (posterior probability 0.84). Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Overlaps, with rs4663105, an H3K27ac peak enriched in microglia and oligodendrocyte-enriched glia but not neurons, so BIN1 effects should be tested in oligodendrocytes too. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
