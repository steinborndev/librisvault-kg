# Endosome

Endosomes are the membrane compartments that sort cargo taken up by [[Endocytosis]] before it is recycled or passed to the [[Lysosome]]. Swollen endosomes are an early cellular change in familial [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Endosomes and lysosomes were larger in wild-type mouse neurons with inefficient gamma-secretase, in line with swelling reported in patient iPSC-derived neurons with familial mutations. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Related: [[Gamma-secretase complex]], [[Amyloid-beta formation]], [[Retrograde transport from endosome to Golgi]]
