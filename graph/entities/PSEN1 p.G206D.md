# PSEN1 p.G206D

A missense change of PSEN1 in [[PSEN1]] (glycine 206 to aspartate), seen in familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. A known pathogenic mutation found in one Taiwanese woman, an adopted child, with memory impairment from age 33 and later seizures; her biological father died before 65 with dementia-like features.
