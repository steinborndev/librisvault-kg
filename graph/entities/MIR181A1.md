# MIR181A1

MIR181A1 encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-181a, a small non-coding RNA that represses gene expression after transcription.

## Connections

- miR-181a was one of eight microRNAs selected from a screen and was measured in patient plasma; no stage-specific pattern was reported for it.
