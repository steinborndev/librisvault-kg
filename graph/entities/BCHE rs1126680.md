# BCHE rs1126680

A G-to-A change 32 nucleotides upstream of the known start codon of [[BCHE]] (c.-32G>A, formerly called -116A), reported to lower butyrylcholinesterase activity and found in cis with the K variant [[BCHE rs1803274]]. In serum mass spectrometry it came with an in-frame upstream translation start that adds 41 amino acids to the signal peptide, and it changes residue -11 of the extended peptide from cysteine to tyrosine (p.C-11Y).
