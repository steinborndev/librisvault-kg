# CCF-STTG1

CCF-STTG1 is a human astrocytoma cell line used as an astrocyte-like model of brain cholesterol handling.

## Connections

- Main expression model: all seaweed lipid extracts raised [[ABCG1]], [[ABCA1]] and [[APOE]] and lowered cholesterol synthesis genes ([[DHCR7]], [[DHCR24]], [[HMGCR]], [[SREBF2]]), fatty acid synthesis genes ([[SREBF1]], [[ACACA]], [[SCD]], [[FASN]]) and [[GFAP]]. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Related cell lines in the same study: [[HEK293]], [[SH-SY5Y]], [[CHME3]]
