# C5

C5 encodes complement component 5, which is cleaved to release C5a and the membrane attack complex precursor C5b.

## Connections

- It was stably selected for the Alzheimer versus control classifier, listed by the authors with both the coagulation and the complement clusters, and overlapped with the panel of an earlier study; it is not among the 13 universal panel proteins.
