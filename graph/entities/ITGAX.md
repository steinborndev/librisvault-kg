# ITGAX

ITGAX encodes integrin alpha X (CD11c), a cell-surface marker of myeloid cells that is also a marker of disease-associated microglia.

## Connections

- Marker of AD1 microglia in human cortex; in tissue from 29 donors, ITGAX and [[P2RY12]] immunoreactivity in microglia were inversely correlated, in line with the gene expression shift along the homeostatic-to-AD1 trajectory. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
- ITGAX and [[TREM2]] did not differ between APOE3 and [[APOE p.R136S]] iPSC-derived microglia after amyloid beta 42. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Related: [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]], [[Disease-associated microglia]]
- Induced about fivefold in APP/PS1 mouse brain, with no further rise on Sirt3 deletion. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
