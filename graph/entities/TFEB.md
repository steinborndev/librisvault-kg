# TFEB

TFEB encodes a transcription factor that is the master regulator of lysosomal biogenesis and the autophagy-lysosome system, repressed by [[MTOR]].

## Connections

- Reported to be activated by [[Ouabain]], which inhibits mTOR; raising TFEB increases lysosomal degradation of APP in Alzheimer mice. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Process: [[Autophagy]]; disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
