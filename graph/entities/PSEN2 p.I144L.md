# PSEN2 p.I144L

A missense change of [[PSEN2]] (isoleucine 144 to leucine) not reported before the Swedish memory-clinic screen, where it was found in an [[Alzheimer disease]] patient with a family history of dementia (male, onset 65 years, APOE ε3/ε3). Pathogenicity unknown: population frequency 0.0000080, SIFT 0.01, PolyPhen-2 0.257.
