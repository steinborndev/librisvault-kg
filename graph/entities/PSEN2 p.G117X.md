# PSEN2 p.G117X

A stop-gain change (a premature stop codon at residue 117 (glycine in the reference sequence)) in [[PSEN2]], located in the extracellular loop between the first and second transmembrane regions; a stop-gain variant of uncertain significance found in a 63-year-old man who began with memory loss, lack of initiative and restlessness at 56 and had bilateral parietal atrophy on MRI. In transfected [[HEK293]] cells it did not change Aβ42, Aβ40 or the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio relative to wild-type PSEN2, which the authors say does not by itself show that it is harmless.
