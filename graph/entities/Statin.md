# Statin

Statins are the most commonly prescribed lipid-lowering drugs; they competitively inhibit [[HMGCR]], block cholesterol synthesis and upregulate [[LDLR]], and are also credited with antioxidant, antithrombotic, antiamyloid and vasodilating effects.

## Connections

- Members: [[Atorvastatin]], [[Simvastatin]] (rosuvastatin was used by only two patients)
- Over a year of use in Alzheimer disease outpatients statins lowered total and LDL cholesterol; response differed by variants in [[ACE]], [[APOE]], [[LDLR]] and [[CETP]]. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Study type: [[Pharmacogenetics]]
- Reviewed as lowering the risk of developing Alzheimer disease though it is disputed whether they alter its course. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
