# APP p.R684H

The third rodent-to-human substitution in the amyloid beta sequence of [[APP]] (residue 684, APP770 numbering, arginine to histidine). Unlike the other two it was not tied to a specific new contact with [[BACE1]] in the structural model, but it completes the humanized Aβ sequence.

## Connections

- Gene: [[APP]]
- Introduced with [[APP p.G676R]] and [[APP p.F681Y]] in the [[Humanized App mouse]] and [[Humanized App Psen1 M139T rat]]
- Study: [[Humanized APP knock-in mouse and rat with PSEN1 M139T for Alzheimer disease]]
