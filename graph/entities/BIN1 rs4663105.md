# BIN1 rs4663105

rs4663105 is a late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] GWAS SNP at the [[BIN1]] locus.

## Connections

- Gene: [[BIN1]]
- With rs6733839 it overlaps an H3K27ac peak enriched in both microglia and oligodendrocyte-enriched glia but not neurons (log2 fold change microglia 0.21, oligodendrocyte-enriched glia 0.53), so its effect on BIN1 should be tested in oligodendrocytes as well as myeloid cells. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
