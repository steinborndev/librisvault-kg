# ADAM17 p.R215I

A rare missense change (arginine to isoleucine at residue 215, c.G644T, rs142946965) in [[ADAM17]], next to the pro-protein convertase cleavage site. It impairs maturation of the enzyme in cells and was found in a family with [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]] and in five further patients.
