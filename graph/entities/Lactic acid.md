# Lactic acid

Lactic acid (as the anion L-lactate in tissue) is the end product of glycolysis when pyruvate is not fully oxidized in mitochondria; high levels mark glycolysis running ahead of oxidative phosphorylation. Interstitial and tissue lactate correlates with amyloid beta load in Alzheimer disease models, and it is raised in the CSF of patients.
