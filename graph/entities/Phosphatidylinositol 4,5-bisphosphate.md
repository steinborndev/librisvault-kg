# Phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate

A minor phospholipid of the inner plasma membrane (PIP2, PI(4,5)P2) that anchors actin dynamics and endocytic machinery. Phospholipase C gamma enzymes cut it into inositol trisphosphate, which releases calcium from stores, and diacylglycerol, which stays in the membrane.

## Connections

- Substrate of [[PLCG2]]. In macrophages and microglia carrying [[PLCG2 p.P522R]] it fell further after Fc-receptor, [[Lipopolysaccharide]] or [[Amyloid-beta]] stimulation, recovered more slowly, and was about 60% lower in microglia of young knock-in mice; diacylglycerol rose correspondingly, while PI(3,4)P2 and PIP3 did not differ. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
- Supplying extra PIP2 raised uptake of bacterial and yeast particles ([[Phagocytosis]]) but not of amyloid oligomers or dextran ([[Endocytosis]]) in the same study.
- Downstream signal: [[Calcium signalling]]
