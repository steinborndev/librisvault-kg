# Naltrindole

Naltrindole is a selective antagonist of the δ-opioid receptor ([[OPRD1]]).

## Connections

- Given alone or with [[UFP-512]] it raised [[BACE1]] activity and Aβ42 and worsened viability in [[PC12]] cells, and reversed the effects of UFP-512 on BACE1 under amyloid injury. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
