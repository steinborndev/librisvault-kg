# CHRFAM7A

CHRFAM7A is a fusion gene, found only in humans, that joins part of [[CHRNA7]] to the duplicated FAM7A (ULK4-derived) sequence; its product can join the alpha7 nicotinic receptor pentamer and acts as a negative regulator of the receptor's ion-channel function. Laboratory animals do not carry it, whereas over 99% of human samples do.

## Connections

- In isogenic induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) microglia-like cells it lowered alpha7-dependent [[Amyloid-beta]] uptake, yet strengthened and prolonged NF-κB activation and cytokine output after Aβ or [[Lipopolysaccharide]]; the authors read this as a switch of alpha7 from an anti-inflammatory to a proinflammatory transducer.
