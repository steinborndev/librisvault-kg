# CYP27A1

CYP27A1 encodes sterol 27-hydroxylase, which makes [[27-Hydroxycholesterol]] from cholesterol.

## Connections

- Its cellular distribution shifts in Alzheimer brain (lower in neurons, higher in oligodendrocytes, present around plaques). Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Related: [[CYP46A1]], [[Cholesterol]]
