# CYP2D6 rs3892097

The splicing-defect variant (1846G>A) defining the CYP2D6 *4 allele, the commonest null allele in people of European ancestry; two null alleles give a poor metabolizer.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CYP2D6]]
- Among the most frequent CYP2D6 alleles in 1006 Alzheimer disease patients, alongside *3, *5, *6, *41 and gene duplications; carriers of two null alleles (poor metabolizers, 5.6%) had higher baseline anxiety and did not improve significantly after treatment.
