# APP-PS1-Sirt3 knockout mouse

A mouse made by deleting Sirt3 globally in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals, so that amyloid pathology is combined with a metabolic-syndrome-like state; it serves as a comorbid Alzheimer model. Wild-type 129 Sv and C57BL/6 backgrounds and single Sirt3-knockout animals served as comparators.

## Connections

- Earlier work cited in the study reports more plaques, microglial activation and insulin resistance than in APP/PS1 alone; brain RNA-seq at 8 months showed altered metabolic, inflammatory, amyloid and insulin signalling pathways and lower [[IDE]]. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
