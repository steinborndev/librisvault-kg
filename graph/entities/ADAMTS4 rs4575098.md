# ADAMTS4 rs4575098

rs4575098 is a late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] GWAS SNP in the UTR of [[ADAMTS4]], in moderate linkage disequilibrium (r = 0.59) with [[FCER1G rs11421]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[ADAMTS4]]
- Associated with allelic expression of ADAMTS4, B4GALT3 and, via the linked FCER1G variant, FCER1G; direction of effect for FCER1G agreed between brain regions, microglia and monocytes. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
