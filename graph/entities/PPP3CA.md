# PPP3CA

PPP3CA encodes the catalytic subunit of calcineurin, a calcium-dependent phosphatase that dephosphorylates AMPA receptors and so promotes their removal from synapses and long-term depression.

## Connections

- Calcineurin activity is raised in APOE4 knock-in mice, a possible route by which [[APOE]] ε4 lowers AMPA receptor availability at synapses. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Process: [[AMPA receptor trafficking]]; disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
