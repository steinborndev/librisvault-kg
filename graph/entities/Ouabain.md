# Ouabain

Ouabain is a cardiotonic steroid and inhibitor of the Na+/K+-ATPase sodium pump.

## Connections

- Favoured candidate: reported to inhibit [[MTOR]] and activate [[TFEB]], the master regulator of the autophagy-lysosome system, which could speed degradation of APP and phosphorylated tau; effects on inflammation depend on dose. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see [[Autophagy]]; method: [[Drug repurposing]]
