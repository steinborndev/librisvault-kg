# TIPRL

TIPRL encodes TIP41-like protein, a regulator of the TOR signalling pathway and of protein phosphatase 2A-family catalytic subunits.

## Connections

- Reported upregulated in Alzheimer brain across several microarray datasets; one of the five signature genes; docking predicted high-affinity binding by [[Antrocin]] (the abstract spells it "T1PRL"). Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Full text: localised to vesicles and cytosol; docking affinity for [[Antrocin]] -6.60 kcal/mol with three hydrogen bonds (THR208, SER173, PHE254). Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
