# GI254023X

GI254023X is a hydroxamate inhibitor selective for the metalloprotease [[ADAM10]] over [[ADAM17]], used as a tool to block α-secretase cleavage of [[APP]].

## Connections

- Blocked the [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]]-driven increase in intracellular and secreted sAPPα in 293sw cells and mouse astrocytes, raised basal secreted Aβ, and raised intracellular Aβ when combined with the compound.
- Study: [[DAPG lowers beta-amyloid secretion in Alzheimer disease models]]
