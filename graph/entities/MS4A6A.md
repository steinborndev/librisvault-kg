# MS4A6A

MS4A6A encodes a member of the membrane-spanning 4-domains family, clustered on chromosome 11 with other MS4A genes expressed in myeloid cells, and is a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Its coding sequence carried the most variants per kilobase of the screened GWAS loci in small vessel ischemic disease patients, but none was associated with the disease. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- In the mouse model of hypoperfusion, eight Ms4a-family transcripts (Ms4a4a, Ms4a4c, Ms4a6c, Ms4a6d, Ms4a14, Ms4a4b, Ms4a6b, Ms4a7) rose as microglia entered the infarct; see [[BCCAS mouse model]].
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]], [[Small vessel ischemic disease]]
- The GWAS SNP rs2081545 was an allele-specific QTL of MS4A6A in classical monocytes and in the head of the caudate nucleus, with the same direction of effect, at two exonic SNPs. Related family gene: [[MS4A7]]. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- Top-scoring gene of the 11q12.1-q12.2 region showing local genetic covariance between early-onset Alzheimer disease and [[Non-HDL cholesterol]] (P = 1.60e-6): gene-based P = 4.07e-10, colocalization H4 = 0.96 with brain eQTLs, methylation linked to amyloid and tau load, and differential expression in human Alzheimer brain single-cell data (P = 3.74e-20). Variant: [[MS4A6A rs610932]]; neighbour [[MS4A4E]]. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
- Its SNP showed differential associations with plasma lipids in all nine lipid classes between Alzheimer cases and controls (nominal, not surviving correction); the authors link this to phosphatidylinositol and brain atrophy.
