# MIR770

MIR770 encodes the precursor of microRNA miR-770, whose 3p strand represses target messenger RNAs after transcription; it is reported to rise with ageing and to promote apoptosis by driving inflammation.

## Connections

- In mouse reporter assays the miR-770-3p mimic bound the circular RNA circ-Epc1 (see [[EPC1]]) and the 3' untranslated region of [[TREM2]]; in [[BV2]] cells circ-Epc1 lowered it and its mimic reversed the circ-Epc1 rise in TREM2 and the shift toward M2 microglia. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Molecular role: [[miRNA binding]]; disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
