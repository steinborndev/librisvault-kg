# RAB5A

RAB5A encodes a small GTPase that organises early endosomes and is the key regulator of the compartment where [[APP]] and [[BACE1]] meet. Both over-activation and loss of Rab5 activity have been reported to raise amyloid beta production. The antibody used in the study below recognises Rab5 generally and does not separate the A, B and C isoforms.
