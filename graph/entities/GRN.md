# GRN

GRN encodes progranulin, a secreted lysosomal and immune-related protein linked to frontotemporal dementia as well as to [[Alzheimer disease]] risk.

## Connections

- With [[TREM2]] it was the largest contributor to the "immune" cluster of Alzheimer risk variants, which lowered CSF phosphorylated tau without changing amyloid-beta 42. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Related dementia gene: [[MAPT]]
- Knockout of GRN made microglia hyperactivated in mice and in iPSC-derived microglia, and CSF from 11 symptomatic heterozygous mutation carriers with [[Frontotemporal dementia]] showed lower GRN, higher [[NEFL]] and six raised microglial activity proteins ([[FABP3]], [[MDH1]], [[GDI1]], [[CAPG]], [[CD44]], [[GPNMB]]). Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Cited as the opposite case to TREM2: absence of progranulin arrests microglia in a disease-associated state, TREM2 loss in a homeostatic one, and both cause similar brain-wide changes in energy metabolism on FDG-PET. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- [[GRN p.A324V]], a rare missense variant of unknown pathogenicity, was found in an Alzheimer patient with family history among 102 Swedish memory-clinic patients. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
