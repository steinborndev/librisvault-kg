# Crenezumab

Crenezumab (RG7412) is a Roche monoclonal antibody against amyloid-beta that failed two Phase-3 trials in prodromal or mild [[Alzheimer disease]] in 2019.

## Connections

- It is being tested in an ongoing double-blind Phase-2 prevention trial (NCT01998841) in presymptomatic members of a large Colombian kindred, randomising carriers of [[PSEN1 p.E280A]] to crenezumab or placebo while mutation-negative relatives all receive placebo, with at least five years of follow-up and an estimated completion in February 2022. The author notes that a wrong antibody specificity could make this trial fail even if the amyloid hypothesis holds for familial disease.
