# FOXN3

FOXN3 encodes forkhead box N3, a transcription factor.

## Connections

- Its regulon was the main driver of age-related expression change in microglia of monkey entorhinal cortex, with targets in lipid metabolism and immune processes, found with [[SCENIC regulon analysis]]. Study: [[Single-nucleus atlas of aging monkey entorhinal cortex and Alzheimer disease]]
