# MAPT p.R741W

A missense change from arginine to tryptophan at residue 741 of [[MAPT]], the tau gene.

## Connections

- Gene: [[MAPT]]
- Found in 4 Alzheimer cases and no controls, confirming a previous association. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
