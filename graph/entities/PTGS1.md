# PTGS1

PTGS1 encodes cyclooxygenase 1, the constitutive prostaglandin-endoperoxide synthase, which in brain is expressed largely by microglia.

## Connections

- Raised in the brains of [[Apoe knockout mouse]] animals, one of 22 genes shared between the Apoe-driven ivory module and the microglial modules of human post-mortem brain. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Related: [[Neuroinflammation]], [[AMP-AD consensus clusters]]
- A high-degree target in the network of every one of seven heat-clearing herbs (degree 15 for Phellodendron bark), read by the authors as arachidonic acid driven inflammation (database prediction only). Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
