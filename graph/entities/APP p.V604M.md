# APP p.V604M

A missense change from valine to methionine at residue 604 of [[APP]], in exon 14, outside the amyloid beta region. It was reported as possibly novel because no database lists it.

## Connections

- Gene: [[APP]]; disease: [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Found in a Thai patient (#6) with progressive non-fluent aphasia from age 55 and a CSF profile of amyloid beta 42 555 pg/mL, total tau 285 pg/mL and phosphorylated tau 37 pg/mL, who also carried [[CSF1R p.L536V]]. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
