# ABCA7 p.G1527A

A missense change from glycine to alanine at residue 1527 of [[ABCA7]] (rs3752246), a late-onset Alzheimer disease association signal.

## Connections

- Gene: [[ABCA7]]
- Nominally associated with lower risk of [[Small vessel ischemic disease]], not significant after correction. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- In healthy adults the risk allele (odds ratio 1.15) went with lower levels of two [[Lactosylceramide]] species from the youngest age group and higher plasma [[DEFA1]], and interacted with sex on [[UBE2F]]. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
