# IL4

IL4 encodes interleukin-4, an anti-inflammatory cytokine that pushes microglia toward a repair-type (M2) state.

## Connections

- IL-4 rose in amyloid-beta-challenged [[BV2]] cells treated with ajugol or isoacteoside. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Cytokine partners: [[IL1B]], [[IFNG]], [[CSF3]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- IL-4 and IL-13 signaling was the top-ranked Reactome pathway among targets shared by heat-clearing herbs and Alzheimer disease; the authors cite IL-4 and IL-13 activating microglia to degrade amyloid-beta. Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
