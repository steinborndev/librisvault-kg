# CELF1

CELF1 (formerly CUGBP1) encodes an RNA-binding protein that regulates splicing and mRNA stability, and lies at a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Its regional brain expression, with that of [[MAPT]], was the only one of ten risk genes not significantly correlated with cortical glucose metabolism. Study: [[Brain metabolism and Alzheimer risk gene expression in normal elderly]]
