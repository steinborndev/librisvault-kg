# Memantine

Memantine is an NMDA receptor modulator licensed for moderate to severe [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- It appeared among significant drugs for the GWAS, excitatory-neuron and oligodendrocyte risk-gene lists, though not near the top, which the authors cite as partial recovery of a licensed drug. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Compare: [[Donepezil]], [[Galantamine]]
- Reported to lower GluN2C expression and GRIN2C mRNA in prefrontal cortex, cited in a study of the NMDA subunit variant [[GRIN2C p.A1072V]] in a late-onset Alzheimer family. Study: [[Exome sequencing finds GRIN2C p.A1072V in an Italian late-onset Alzheimer family]]
