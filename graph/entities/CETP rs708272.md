# CETP rs708272

A [[CETP]] SNP whose A allele is tied to lower transfer protein activity and lower coronary heart disease risk, and which may explain up to 10% of HDL cholesterol variation.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CETP]]
- A-allele carriers had larger HDL cholesterol rises on atorvastatin, GG carriers lower HDL. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
