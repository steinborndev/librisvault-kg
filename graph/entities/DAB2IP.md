# DAB2IP

DAB2IP encodes a member of the Ras GTPase-activating protein family that restrains cell proliferation, metastasis and apoptosis signalling.

## Connections

- Higher in the C2 than the C1 subtype of Alzheimer brain samples; its expression correlated positively with follicular helper T cells and negatively with M1 macrophages and activated dendritic cells. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
- Pathway: [[Reelin signaling pathway]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
