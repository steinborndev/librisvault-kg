# Pirinixic acid

Pirinixic acid (WY-14643) is a synthetic agonist of [[PPARA]].

## Connections

- PPARα positive control (50 µM); it raised [[GFAP]] expression in [[CCF-STTG1]] cells where the seaweed extracts lowered it. The introduction cites it as reducing Aβ aggregation, tau phosphorylation and gliosis in Alzheimer mouse models. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Target: [[PPARA]]
