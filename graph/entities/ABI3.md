# ABI3

ABI3 encodes ABI family member 3, a protein expressed in microglia, and rare variants in it are among those that raise the risk of [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Named with [[TREM2]], [[PLCG2]] and [[SORL1]] as a gene with rare variants whose effect a polygenic score may not capture; its effect size was used to illustrate rare-variant probability curves. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
