# Humanized App mouse

A C57Bl6J mouse whose endogenous App gene encodes the human amyloid beta sequence (G676R, F681Y, R684H), expressed from the native promoter. It makes about 2.5-fold more β-CTF and about 2.3-fold more Aβ40 than wild type, and is meant as the proper control for App knock-in mutants such as [[AppNL-G-F mouse]].
