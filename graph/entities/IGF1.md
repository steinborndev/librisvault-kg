# IGF1

IGF1 encodes insulin-like growth factor 1, a hormone that regulates insulin sensitivity, cell survival and hippocampal neurogenesis.

## Connections

- Target of [[MIR206]]. Serum IGF1 is lower in Alzheimer disease and tracks cognitive decline in diabetic patients; only one IGF1 polymorphism has been associated with risk, in a Chinese Han population.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- Neighbour of both [[ESR1]] and [[ESR2]] among Alzheimer susceptibility genes; the authors point to an estrogen, IGF1, AKT and MAPK neuroprotective axis. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
