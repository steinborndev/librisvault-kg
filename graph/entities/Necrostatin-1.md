# Necrostatin-1

Necrostatin-1 is a small-molecule inhibitor of the kinase activity of [[RIPK1]] and a standard tool for blocking [[Necroptosis]].

## Connections

- Suppressed [[RIPK3]] and [[MLKL]] activation and cell death caused by TNF-α, with or without Smac mimetic and Z-VAD-FMK, in [[SH-SY5Y]] cells and primary cortical neurons. The authors note earlier work in which it blocked Aβ-induced death in APP/PS1 mice but with limited efficacy at early stages. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
