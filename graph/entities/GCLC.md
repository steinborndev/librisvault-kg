# GCLC

GCLC encodes the catalytic subunit of glutamate-cysteine ligase, the rate-limiting enzyme of glutathione synthesis and an [[NFE2L2]] target gene.

## Connections

- GCLC protein rose from 78% to 120% with [[LM-031]] (1 μM) in ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed [[SH-SY5Y]] cells, part of the NRF2/NQO1/GCLC antioxidant response. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
