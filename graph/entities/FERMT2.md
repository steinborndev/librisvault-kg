# FERMT2

FERMT2 (kindlin-2) encodes an integrin-activating adaptor protein involved in cell-cell adhesion. Silencing it raises amyloid-beta production, and it is a genome-wide association locus for Alzheimer disease.

## Connections

- [[FERMT2 rs17125924]] is the intronic variant tested in the southern Chinese case-control study.
- [[Alzheimer disease]] is the disease whose risk the locus is tied to.
- [[APOE]] ε4 status modifies the association.
- Study: [[SNP association study of Alzheimer disease in southern Chinese population]]
- FERMT2 expression (called PLEKHC1 in the paper) correlated negatively with cortical glucose metabolism, more strongly as risk group rose. Study: [[Brain metabolism and Alzheimer risk gene expression in normal elderly]]
- Promoter hypoacetylated in female hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load (-0.47, FDR 0.03); its sentinel SNP lies in an oligodendrocyte-enriched peak. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Its SNP showed differential associations with plasma lipids in all nine lipid classes between Alzheimer cases and controls (nominal, not surviving correction), with the highest proportion in [[Phosphatidylinositol]]; the authors suggest lipid-raft composition could mediate its effect on amyloid precursor protein handling. Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
