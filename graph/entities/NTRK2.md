# NTRK2

NTRK2 encodes TrkB, the receptor of brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), a growth factor supporting neuronal survival and synaptic plasticity.

## Connections

- [[SORL1]] acts as a sorting factor that moves TrkB between the synaptic plasma membrane, postsynaptic densities and the cell soma. Intracellular Aβ disturbs the endosomal sorting of TrkB for lysosomal degradation, which could disturb synapses. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; process: [[Endocytosis]]
- TrkB Y702 phosphorylation rose 532% when active [[PTK2]] was added to 3xTg-AD hippocampal lysate. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Named as the receptor through which [[BDNF]] activates [[AKT1]] and ERK in the proposed [[LM-031]] mechanism; receptor levels were not measured. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
