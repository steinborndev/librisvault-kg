# SORL1 p.R1490C

A missense change from arginine to cysteine at residue 1490 in the CR cluster of [[SORL1]], giving an odd number of cysteines in the domain.

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- Found in five cases and two controls among unrelated individuals, one of the recurrent cysteine-changing variants that together gave a 4.9-fold risk of [[Alzheimer disease]]. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
