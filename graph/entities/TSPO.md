# TSPO

TSPO encodes the 18 kDa translocator protein of the outer mitochondrial membrane, whose density rises with microglial activation and is used as the imaging marker of [[Neuroinflammation]] (for example with the ligand PK11195).

## Connections

- [3H]PK11195 binding rose with age in cortex of wild-type and [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] animals but more in transgenic mice (frontal at 12 and 24 months, parietal at 18 and 24 months), preceded the fall in [[SLC6A4]] binding, and was inversely correlated with it in parietal cortex. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
