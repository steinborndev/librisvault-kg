# LDLR rs11669576

A SNP in the epidermal growth factor precursor homology domain of [[LDLR]], linked to dysfunctional cholesterol metabolism; the G allele has been tied to an abnormal receptor that impairs apolipoprotein E internalisation.

## Connections

- Gene: [[LDLR]]
- G-allele carriers had lower total and LDL cholesterol on simvastatin and lower total cholesterol and triglycerides on atorvastatin; low genotype variability left some haplotypes under-represented. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Neighbouring SNP: [[LDLR rs5930]]
