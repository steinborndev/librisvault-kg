# DNM1L

DNM1L encodes Drp1, a GTPase that drives mitochondrial fission.

## Connections

- In oligodendrocytes of Alzheimer mice, Drp1 activation inhibits [[HK1]] and triggers [[NLRP3]] inflammation; knockdown prevented myelin loss. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
