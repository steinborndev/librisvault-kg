# CTSB

CTSB encodes the lysosomal cysteine protease cathepsin B, which can cleave amyloid beta and [[APP]].

## Connections

- Mouse Ctsb was among the five amyloid-degradation transcripts raised during hypoperfusion; see [[BCCAS mouse model]]. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Related clearance genes: [[A2M]], [[PLAT]], [[CTSD]], [[KLK6]]
- Ctsb rose in Grn knockout mouse microglia and CSF, and CTSB rose in lysate and medium of GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Cathepsin B activity (Z-RR-AMC and Magic Red) was lower in P2X4-null macrophages with equal protein, and its inhibition by [[Z-Phe-Ala-FMK]] raised ApoE in wild-type but not P2X4-null cells; cathepsin B-deficient macrophages also held more [[APOE]], making it the protease behind P2X4-dependent ApoE degradation. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
