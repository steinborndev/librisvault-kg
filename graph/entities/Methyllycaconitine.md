# Methyllycaconitine

Methyllycaconitine (MLA) is a plant alkaloid that selectively blocks the alpha7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor ([[CHRNA7]]) and is used to probe its function.

## Connections

- In iPSC microglia-like cells lacking [[CHRFAM7A]], MLA raised [[Amyloid-beta]] uptake and NF-κB target transcription, read as release of the tonic anti-inflammatory brake; in carriers its effect was small and in the opposite direction on transcription. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
- Related: [[Nicotine]], [[Cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway]]
