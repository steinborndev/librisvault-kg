# MIR132

MIR132 encodes microRNA miR-132, a neuronal microRNA that regulates gene expression after transcription. Its prefrontal cortex level was linked to the Alzheimer polygenic risk score, to parkinsonism and to cognitive decline.

## Connections

- Direct upstream node of the parkinsonism score in a Bayesian network, so a candidate driver of [[Parkinsonism]] in people with high genetic risk of [[Alzheimer disease]].
- Reported as dysregulated in brains of both Alzheimer disease and [[Parkinson disease]].
- Study: [[Alzheimer polygenic risk score and motor impairment in older adults]]
- Lower in APP-overexpressing [[BE2-M17]] cells and raised by [[Osthol]] conjugated to [[Zinc oxide]] nanoparticles; an anti-miR-132 inhibitor lowered synapsin-1 ([[SYN1]]), and the authors propose miR-132 acts through the [[Wnt signaling pathway]] and [[GSK3B]]. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
