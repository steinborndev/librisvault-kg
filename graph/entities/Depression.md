# Depression

Persistent low mood and loss of interest, measured in dementia studies with scales such as the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale. In Alzheimer disease its prevalence ranges from about 5% to over 40%, it is the second most common psychiatric symptom after apathy, and depressive symptoms accompany faster memory decline.
