# SORL1

SORL1 encodes a sorting receptor that directs the amyloid precursor protein away from amyloidogenic processing, and its loss of function raises risk of [[Alzheimer disease]]. The protein has 2,214 residues in repeated domains (VPS10p β-propeller, 10CC, YWTD-EGF, 11 CR domains, six 3Fn domains and a cytoplasmic tail) and is encoded by 48 exons.

## Connections

- SORL1 was a protein marker on the nanoparticle kit and was measured in patient plasma.
- Study: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]]
- A hub gene in the protein interaction network built around [[APOE]] in a Russian cohort; SORL1 rs11218343 failed genotyping quality control. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- Two rare VPS10-domain variants, [[SORL1 p.E270K]] and [[SORL1 p.A528T]], occurred in 2 of 96 [[Small vessel ischemic disease]] patients, a carrier rate (2%) like that in late-onset Alzheimer cohorts (2.4%). Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Truncating variants carry a 17.2-fold risk of [[Alzheimer disease]] (35.3-fold for [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]); 107 high-priority missense variants chosen by [[Domain mapping of disease mutations]] carry 6.1-fold risk, with median age at onset 62 years for truncations and 64 years for those missense variants against 72 in wild-type cases. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- High-priority missense variants: [[SORL1 p.Y391C]] (VPS10p loop L1), [[SORL1 p.R953H]] (YWTD domain), [[SORL1 p.D1108N]] and [[SORL1 p.R1490C]] (CR cluster), [[SORL1 p.G1732A]] and [[SORL1 p.Y1816C]] (3Fn domain); truncating [[SORL1 p.R744X]] and [[SORL1 p.R866X]]; the common [[SORL1 p.D2065V]] had no clear effect.
- Cargo-binding partner of the retromer complex in [[Retrograde transport from endosome to Golgi]]; binds [[APP]] and amyloid beta. Age at onset in carriers is shifted further by [[APOE]] genotype.
- A rare SORL1 variant with odds ratio 7.2 raised the modelled probability of Alzheimer disease in the 65+ age group from about 0 to 0.4 at the lowest polygenic score and from 0.6 to 0.76 at the highest. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- SORL1 expression had the strongest positive correlation with cortical glucose metabolism of ten risk genes (r = 0.710, z = 2.228, more than two standard deviations beyond the whole-transcriptome distribution), the opposite direction to APOE; expression was low in the early-affected parahippocampal and entorhinal cortex. The authors read this as support for a protective role. Study: [[Brain metabolism and Alzheimer risk gene expression in normal elderly]]
- [[SORL1 p.Y391C]] was seen in 5 cases and no controls in a European-ancestry exome case-only screen, confirming an earlier association. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Synaptic roles reviewed: sorting factor for [[NTRK2]] (TrkB), directs phosphorylated synapsin to calpain degradation through 14-3-3, and restrains [[EPHA4]] activation by ephrinA1; expression is lower in Alzheimer and mild cognitive impairment brain. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Transcript was higher in the caudate nucleus of [[Alzheimer disease]] but not of [[Parkinson disease]]; of ten Alzheimer risk genes checked, only SORL1 and [[PLCG2]] differed from controls. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Promoter hyperacetylated in prefrontal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load (0.52, FDR 0.048); the transcript rise in Alzheimer oligodendrocytes was not significant. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- [[SORL1 rs3737529]] T allele delayed the midpoint of cognitive decline by 2.6 years and slowed its rate in Han Chinese patients in Taiwan, independent of APOE; [[SORL1 rs1784933]] and [[SORL1 rs2298813]], earlier tied to disease risk there, did not affect decline, and none replicated in ADNI. Study: [[Genetic effects on cognitive decline trajectories in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
- More than 30% of plasma [[Cholesteryl ester]] species associated differently with the SORL1 risk SNP in Alzheimer cases than in controls, together with [[PICALM]] and [[SLC24A4]] (nominal, not surviving correction).
