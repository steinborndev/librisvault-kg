# Docosahexaenoic acid

Docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) is an omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acid long linked to brain health and cognition.

## Connections

- DHA was the one polyunsaturated fatty acid whose plasma level showed a sex-specific effect of the protective [[PICALM rs3851179]] allele, alongside five long-chain fatty acids. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
