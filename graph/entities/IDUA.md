# IDUA

IDUA encodes alpha-L-iduronidase, a lysosomal enzyme that degrades glycosaminoglycans.

## Connections

- Plasma IDUA was lower in carriers of the protective [[INPP5D rs10933431]] allele; the authors found no earlier report of this link. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Genotype link: [[INPP5D]]
