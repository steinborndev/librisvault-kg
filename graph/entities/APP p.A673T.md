# APP p.A673T

A missense change from alanine to threonine at residue 673 of [[APP]], at the P2' position of the [[BACE1]] cleavage site (the Icelandic variant); it lowers BACE1 cleavage of APP by roughly 30% over a lifetime and protects against [[Alzheimer disease]] and age-related cognitive decline.

## Connections

- Gene: [[APP]]; pathway: [[Amyloid-beta formation]]
- Cited as human genetic support for BACE1 cleavage as a causal step and as precedent that a modest, lifelong reduction in cleavage is tolerated, which underlies the case for partial inhibition by [[FAH65]]. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
