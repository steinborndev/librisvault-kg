# FYN

FYN encodes Fyn, a Src-family intracellular tyrosine kinase whose SH3 domain binds Pro-xx-Pro motifs in tau.

## Connections

- Fyn phosphorylates tau at Tyr18; in mouse hippocampal neurons in vitro Tyr18-phosphorylated tau increased NMDA receptor-mediated calcium influx, so the review presents this route as feeding [[Excitotoxicity]] and neurodegeneration rather than tangle formation. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Process: [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Y213/Y214 phosphorylation rose 112% in hippocampus of 3xTg-AD mice overexpressing [[PTK2]]; FYN is placed upstream of CDK5 activation and inhibitory GSK3B phosphorylation. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Higher in the C2 than the C1 Alzheimer brain subtype defined by [[CDK5R1]] level (subtype AUC 0.826), with correlations to several immune cell fractions. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
- Palmitoylation of FYN was 25.2% higher and its membrane fraction 15.5% higher in hippocampus of mice carrying [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]], with more phosphorylated [[GRIN2B]] and more NMDA excitotoxicity that [[2-Bromopalmitate]] and [[Cerulenin]] reversed. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
