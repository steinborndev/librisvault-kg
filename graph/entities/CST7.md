# CST7

CST7 encodes cystatin F, a cysteine-protease inhibitor made mainly by immune cells.

## Connections

- The most induced gene named in the mouse RNA-seq: about 50-fold higher with amyloid deposition in APP/PS1 brain, equal in APP/PS1/Sirt3-knockout brain. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
