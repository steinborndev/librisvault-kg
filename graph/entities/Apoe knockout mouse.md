# Apoe knockout mouse

A mouse with homozygous deletion of Apoe, the mouse orthologue of [[APOE]], on a [[C57BL-6 mouse]] background. It is a loss-of-function model and the background on which the [[GFAP-APOE4 mouse]] is built.

## Connections

- At 6 months, whole-brain RNA-seq of females showed 219 differentially expressed genes, inflammation and immune-response enrichment, and the ivory co-expression module, which overlapped the microglial modules of human post-mortem brain (consensus cluster B). Nine microglial genes ([[TREM2]], [[CSF1R]], [[C1QA]], [[C1QB]], [[C1QC]], [[PTGS1]], [[AIF1]], [[LAPTM5]], [[LY86]]) were raised. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Its transcriptome was closest to that of the GFAP-APOE4 mouse and correlated positively with human immune modules; see [[Mouse-human module comparison]]
- Related models: [[Clu knockout mouse]], [[APP-PS1 mouse]]
