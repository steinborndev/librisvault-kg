# NNT

NNT encodes nicotinamide nucleotide transhydrogenase, an inner mitochondrial membrane enzyme that regenerates [[NADPH]] and so maintains the redox balance and antioxidant capacity of mitochondria.

## Connections

- Found down-regulated in an Alzheimer mouse model; its deficiency alone produced synapse loss and cognitive deficits and sensitised neurons to amyloid-beta toxicity. Study: [[NNT deficiency impairs synapses and cognition in Alzheimer mouse models]]
- Disease and phenotypes: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]], [[Synapse loss]], [[Cognitive impairment]]
- Process: [[Oxidative stress]]
- Detergent-insoluble in demented human amygdala, with inclusion body-like staining in cognitively impaired subjects, smears on western blots, and a nominal rare-variant association with Alzheimer-type dementia risk. Study: [[Detergent-insoluble proteins in amygdala of cognitively impaired elderly]]
