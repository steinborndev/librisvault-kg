# DEFA1

DEFA1 encodes defensin alpha 1, an antimicrobial peptide of neutrophils; higher levels have been reported in cerebrospinal fluid and serum of patients with [[Alzheimer disease]] compared with controls.

## Connections

- Plasma DEFA1 was higher in carriers of the [[ABCA7 p.G1527A]] risk allele, a possible link between [[ABCA7]] and an inflammatory route to Alzheimer disease. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
