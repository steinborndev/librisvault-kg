# Obsessive-compulsive disorder

Obsessive-compulsive disorder is a psychiatric condition marked by intrusive thoughts and repetitive behaviours, and is described as a factor that raises the risk of developing Alzheimer disease.

## Connections

- A gene-level association of [[IQCK]] with this disorder has been reported, in addition to its association with [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Obesity]]. See [[IQCK protein is raised in Alzheimer hippocampus and found in amyloid plaques]].
