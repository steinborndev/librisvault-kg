# SYK

SYK encodes a cytoplasmic tyrosine kinase that relays signals from immune receptors; in microglia it is activated by phosphorylation downstream of the [[TREM2]]-[[TYROBP]] complex, and phospho-SYK is the usual read-out of TREM2 signalling.

## Connections

- Phospho-SYK (Tyr525/526) rose dose-dependently in HEK293 cells expressing mouse TREM2 and TYROBP when the bivalent antibody [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]] was added, but not with its monovalent Fab, and the antibody amplified the signal from anionic liposomes 2.3-fold.
