# HEXB

HEXB encodes the beta subunit of the lysosomal enzyme beta-hexosaminidase, which breaks down gangliosides and other glycoconjugates.

## Connections

- One of four proteins (with [[FUCA1]], [[FUCA2]] and [[CAPG]]) raised in CSF of symptomatic GRN mutation carriers, in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia medium and in Grn knockout mouse microglia; it was also raised in Grn knockout mouse CSF. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
