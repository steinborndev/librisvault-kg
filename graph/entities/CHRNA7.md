# CHRNA7

CHRNA7 encodes the alpha7 subunit of the nicotinic acetylcholine receptor, a calcium-permeable ion channel on neurons.

## Connections

- Aβ can bind alpha7-nicotinic receptors to induce presynaptic calcium entry, one proposed route for its physiological role in boosting vesicle recycling and long-term potentiation at picomolar concentrations. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Process: [[Synaptic vesicle cycle]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- In blood mononuclear cells of patients with dementia with Lewy bodies or Alzheimer disease, mRNA was reported as not different from controls or between the diseases, though a figure legend gives sign-test p values of 0.002 and 0.021 against controls; in Alzheimer disease it correlated negatively with [[CHRNA3]] and [[CHRNA4]]. Study: [[PBMC nicotinic receptor and cytokine expression in DLB and Alzheimer disease]]
- In human iPSC microglia-like cells the alpha7 receptor lowered inflammation and Aβ uptake was alpha7-dependent only when the human fusion gene [[CHRFAM7A]] was absent; with CHRFAM7A the receptor behaved as a hypomorphic, proinflammatory transducer. Alpha7 colocalised with internalised Aβ1-42 (Manders 0.575). Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
