# PCDH11X rs5984894

rs5984894 is a single nucleotide polymorphism at Xq21.3 in [[PCDH11X]], associated with [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]] in a two-stage genome-wide study of European-descent individuals from the United States, with a combined P value of 3.9 x 10(-12). An independent dataset of 889 cases and 850 controls did not replicate the association.
