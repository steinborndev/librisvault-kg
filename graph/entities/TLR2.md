# TLR2

TLR2 encodes toll-like receptor 2, a pattern-recognition receptor of the innate immune system.

## Connections

- Target of [[MIR146A]]. TLR2 activation induced neurodegeneration and cognitive deficit in Alzheimer mouse models, and TLR2 polymorphisms were associated with the disease in Asian populations.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- mRNA rose 2.4-fold with amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture but not in those kept 16 days (age by treatment interaction p < 0.05), alongside [[TLR4]] and [[HMGB1]], its proposed ligand. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
