# CYP2C9 rs1057910

The reduced-function variant defining the CYP2C9 *3 allele (c.1075A>C); one copy gives an intermediate and two copies a poor metabolizer.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CYP2C9]]; companion variant: [[CYP2C9 rs1799853]]
- Genotyped in 1006 Alzheimer disease patients; *1A/*3A carriers were counted as intermediate and *3A/*3A as poor metabolizers, who improved only mildly in anxiety and depression after treatment. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
- Concept: [[Cytochrome P450 metabolizer phenotypes]]
