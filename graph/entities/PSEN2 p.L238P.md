# PSEN2 p.L238P

A missense change from leucine to proline at residue 238 of [[PSEN2]], in the middle of the fifth transmembrane helix, where proline is predicted to kink the helix. It was reported in a German patient with memory and language impairment and parietal atrophy, and in two late-onset cases (onset at 63 and 80) in the Alzheimer's Disease Sequencing Project; gnomAD suggests reduced penetrance.
