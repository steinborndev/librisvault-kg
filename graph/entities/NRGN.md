# NRGN

NRGN encodes neurogranin, a postsynaptic calmodulin-binding protein of neurons whose level in cerebrospinal fluid is used as a marker of synaptic dysfunction in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Higher CSF neurogranin was associated with enlarged perivascular spaces in the centrum semiovale of amyloid-positive, cognitively unimpaired adults, and the association survived correction for CSF amyloid-beta 40. Study: [[Perivascular spaces and CSF biomarkers in cognitively unimpaired Alzheimer continuum]]
- Related phenotype: [[Enlarged perivascular spaces]]
