# NOS2

NOS2 encodes inducible nitric oxide synthase, which makes nitric oxide in activated macrophages and microglia and serves as an M1 marker.

## Connections

- Its mRNA rose 4.7-fold after amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture and 5.4-fold in those kept 16 days, while MHC class II rose only in the young cells. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- See [[Microglial cell activation]]; counterpart M2 marker [[ARG1]]
- iNOS staining was higher in cortex and hippocampus of mice transplanted with old APP/PS1 microbiota ([[Fecal microbiota transplantation]]), marking microglial activation before any plaque. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
