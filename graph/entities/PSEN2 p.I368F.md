# PSEN2 p.I368F

A missense change from isoleucine to phenylalanine at residue 368 of [[PSEN2]], in the seventh transmembrane region, classed likely pathogenic under the ACMG criteria. No causative change at residue 368 had been reported before; it was found in a man with familial early-onset Alzheimer disease and the lowest MMSE score of the three validated carriers. In transfected [[HEK293]] cells it gave the largest rise of the three validated variants in Aβ42 (380.9 against 71.5 pg/ml) and in the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio (0.156 against 0.031), with Aβ40 unchanged.
