# CLDN5

CLDN5 encodes claudin-5, the predominant claudin of brain endothelial [[Tight junction]] complexes.

## Connections

- Lower in hippocampus and frontal cortex of rats after restraint stress and after chronic social stress; mast cell [[TNF]] and tryptase also lowered it in endothelial cells, and it is listed among junction proteins implicated in Alzheimer pathogenesis. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
