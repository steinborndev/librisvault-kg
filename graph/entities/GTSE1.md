# GTSE1

GTSE1 encodes a protein that regulates the G1/S cell cycle transition and microtubule stability and is placed by the authors in neurodegeneration pathways.

## Connections

- Two missense variants ([[GTSE1 p.A219V]] and a synonymous p.A293A) sit with [[CELSR1]] variants on a rare 12-SNP haplotype in 8 Alzheimer cases, one of them homozygous for all four variants. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
