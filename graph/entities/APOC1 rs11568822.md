# APOC1 rs11568822

rs11568822 is a common CGTT insertion/deletion polymorphism in the promoter of [[APOC1]] on chromosome 19, beside [[APOE]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[APOC1]]
- In 25 postmortem frontal lobes the genotype changed the strength of the APOE-APOC1 chromatin contact (highest in insertion/deletion heterozygotes), with a non-significant matching trend in APOC1 mRNA; the authors infer strong linkage with the ε4 allele. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
- A gel-shift assay showed that the region around the indel binds nuclear protein. Study: [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]]
- Neighbouring variants: [[APOE rs429358]], [[TOMM40 rs2075650]]
