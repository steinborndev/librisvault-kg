# Anxiety

A state of excessive worry and tension, measured in dementia studies with scales such as the Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale. It occurs in 30 to 40% of people with dementia and is also a risk factor for it.

## Connections

- Present in nearly 60% of 1006 Alzheimer disease patients, more often and more severely in women; improved after one month of multifactorial treatment, best in normal and intermediate metabolizers of [[CYP2D6]], [[CYP2C19]] and [[CYP2C9]] and in [[APOE]] 3/3 and 3/4 carriers. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
- Drugs used: [[Alprazolam]]; related symptom: [[Depression]]; concept: [[Neuropsychiatric symptoms of Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Self-rated anxiety on the Beck Anxiety Inventory did not differ between amnestic [[Mild cognitive impairment]] patients due to Alzheimer disease and controls, but [[BDNF p.V66M]] Met carriers scored higher than ε4-negative, Met-negative patients after adjusting for [[APOE]] ε4 and the interaction (B = 10.73, p = 0.012, uncorrected). Study: [[Mild behavioral impairment with APOE and BDNF in early Alzheimer disease]]
- D-ribose induced anxiety-like behaviour (more time at the edge of the arena) in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals, which [[Berberine]] did not relieve; see [[Open field test]]. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
