# Diacylglycerol

Diacylglycerols (DGs) are glycerol lipids with two fatty acid chains and a precursor of triacylglycerol; non-targeted metabolomics has reported raised DG and monoglyceride levels in blood and brain of people with mild cognitive impairment and [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Two DG species were higher in carriers of both the ε4 SNP [[APOE rs429358]] and the ε2 SNP [[APOE rs7412]], despite the opposite effects of those alleles on circulating cholesterol; ε2 carriers also had a monoglyceride and further DG species raised. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Related lipid: [[Cholesterol]]
- The only lipid class whose summed plasma level differed between 40 Alzheimer cases and 42 controls (higher in cases, p = 0.009); most of the 50 species were higher, and over half of them associated differently with the [[ABCA7]] risk SNP in cases and controls. Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
