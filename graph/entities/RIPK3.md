# RIPK3

RIPK3 encodes the kinase that binds [[RIPK1]] in the necrosome and phosphorylates [[MLKL]] to carry out [[Necroptosis]].

## Connections

- Phosphorylated on Ser227 (human) or Thr231/Ser232 (mouse) in neuronal cultures under TNF-α, strongly so with Smac mimetic plus Z-VAD-FMK, and co-precipitated with p62 and RIPK1; Necrostatin-1 and RIPK1 knockdown suppressed it. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
