# F12

F12 encodes coagulation factor XII (Hageman factor), a plasma serine protease that starts the contact pathway of coagulation.

## Connections

- It was one of five proteins added to the universal panel through the mild cognitive impairment progression comparison, and the authors list it in the coagulation and protease regulatory cluster that links coagulation, vascular inflammation and complement activation.
