# STX1A

STX1A encodes syntaxin-1a, a SNARE protein of the presynaptic plasma membrane that forms the fusion complex with VAMP2 and SNAP-25 for neurotransmitter release.

## Connections

- In vitro, Aβ oligomers bind syntaxin-1a directly and inhibit assembly of the SNARE fusion complex, a route by which cytosolic Aβ could block vesicle release. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]

- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
