# Migraine

Migraine is a recurrent headache disorder in which neurogenic inflammation of the meninges plays a part.

## Connections

- Stress is the second most common headache trigger, which the review attributes to activation of dural mast cells; migraine is said to worsen with stress and to raise blood-brain barrier permeability. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- Related: [[Mast cell activation]], [[Psychological stress]], [[Pain]], [[Blood-brain barrier dysfunction]]
- [[Lasmiditan]], approved for acute migraine as a 5-HT1F agonist without vasoconstriction, was proposed for repurposing in [[Alzheimer disease]] by docking to [[ETV6]]. Study: [[Lasmiditan prioritised for Alzheimer disease by regulator docking and dynamics]]
- CUZD1 protein levels are correlated with genetic risk of migraine, and ANKDD1B was named among top genes connecting migraine and major depression; both were prioritised genes in an early-onset Alzheimer lipid-covariance study. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]; genes: [[CUZD1]], [[ANKDD1B]]
