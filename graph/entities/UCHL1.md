# UCHL1

UCHL1 encodes a neuron-abundant deubiquitinating enzyme of the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway.

## Connections

- UCHL1 was one of the protein markers screened and placed on the nanoparticle kit for early detection of [[Alzheimer disease]], and was measured in patient plasma.
