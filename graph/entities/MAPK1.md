# MAPK1

MAPK1 encodes ERK2, a mitogen-activated protein kinase of the extracellular signal-regulated kinase pathway. This page also stands for ERK as named in studies that do not say whether ERK1 or ERK2 was meant.

## Connections

- The ERK inhibitor [[U0126]] raised Dicer1 protein beyond amyloid oligomer treatment alone and lowered phospho-Dicer1 in neurons, so ERK activity was taken to keep [[DICER1]] phosphorylated. Study: [[Amyloid oligomers rapidly raise neuronal Dicer1 by reducing phosphorylation]]
- Process: [[Protein phosphorylation]]
- Co-expressed in brain with the five upregulated genes [[ATP6V1A]], [[BNIP3]], [[CAMK4]], [[TIPRL]] and [[TOMM70]]. Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
- Phospho-ERK1/2 (T202/Y204) was restored by [[LM-031]] (90% to 117%) in ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed [[SH-SY5Y]] cells with total ERK unchanged (see also [[MAPK3]]). Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Neighbour of both [[ESR1]] and [[ESR2]] in the interaction network of Alzheimer susceptibility genes; MAPK signaling was enriched in both sexes in hippocampal CA1 and CA3 and is proposed to carry estrogen-mediated neuroprotection.
