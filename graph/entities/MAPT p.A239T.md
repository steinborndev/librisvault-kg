# MAPT p.A239T

A rare missense change of [[MAPT]] (alanine 239 to threonine) of unknown pathogenicity, previously described in a frontotemporal dementia patient who carried a pathogenic GRN deletion. Found in a [[Mild cognitive impairment]] patient with a family history (male, onset 61 years, APOE ε3/ε4). Population frequency 0.00063, SIFT 0.27, PolyPhen-2 0.079.
