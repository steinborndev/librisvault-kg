# CYP2C9 rs1799853

The reduced-function variant defining the CYP2C9 *2 allele (c.430C>T); with [[CYP2C9 rs1057910]] it defines the intermediate and poor metabolizer classes.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CYP2C9]]
- Genotyped in 1006 Alzheimer disease patients; *1A/*2A carriers were counted with intermediate metabolizers (32.6% with *3 carriers) and *2A/*2A with poor metabolizers (5.2% with other poor genotypes). Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
