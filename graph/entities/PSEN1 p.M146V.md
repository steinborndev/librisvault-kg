# PSEN1 p.M146V

A missense change of [[PSEN1]] (methionine 146 to valine) that causes early-onset [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]], with onset typically near 40 years. A Swedish memory-clinic patient (male, onset 34 years, APOE ε3/ε3) had memory and spatial problems from about 30, later myoclonic epileptic seizures and gait difficulty, and widespread parietal hypometabolism on FDG-PET; no family history was reported. SIFT 0.01, PolyPhen-2 0.985, absent from gnomAD.
