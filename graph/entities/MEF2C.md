# MEF2C

MEF2C encodes a MADS-box transcription factor, myocyte enhancer factor 2C, expressed in neurons and microglia, and is an immune-related risk gene for [[Alzheimer disease]] and a gene associated with immunometabolism.

## Connections

- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Its regional brain expression correlated positively with cortical glucose metabolism, as SORL1 did. Study: [[Brain metabolism and Alzheimer risk gene expression in normal elderly]]
- Method: [[Allen Human Brain Atlas]]
- One of two MEF2 family regulators (with MEF2A) called differentially active in myeloid cells of Alzheimer prefrontal cortex, generally higher with age. Study: [[Lasmiditan prioritised for Alzheimer disease by regulator docking and dynamics]]
