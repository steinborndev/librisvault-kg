# TF

TF encodes transferrin, the plasma iron-transport glycoprotein.

## Connections

- It was one of the six proteins closest to significance between controls and mild cognitive impairment, was added to the universal panel because L1-regularization repeatedly selected it for separating progressing from stable mild cognitive impairment, and was replicated in three or more earlier cohorts; the authors place it with proteins of iron and mineral transport.
