# Neuronal loss

Neuronal loss is the death and disappearance of neurons from brain tissue, a pathological hallmark of [[Alzheimer disease]] that follows [[Synapse loss]].

## Connections

- TUNEL-positive (apoptotic) NeuN-labelled neurons were more frequent in hippocampal CA1 of 9-month-old [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]] animals, and cultured mutant neurons died more after NMDA exposure ([[Excitotoxicity]]). Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Process: [[Apoptosis]]
- Nissl-stained and NeuN-positive neurons in CA1 and cortex fell after Aβ1-42 injection in mice and were restored by 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]] and [[Morin]]. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- MAP2-positive cells in the dentate gyrus were more numerous in [[APP-PS1 mouse]] hippocampus overexpressing [[MIR650]] than on the control-virus side (p < 0.05, n = 3). Study: [[MicroRNA-650 targets CDK5 and lessens amyloid pathology in APP-PSEN1 mice]]
- D-ribose caused disordered neurons with pyknotic nuclei, vacuolization and uneven Nissl staining in cortex and hippocampus of [[APP-PS1 mouse]] animals; [[Berberine]] improved morphology unless [[Mdivi-1]] was added. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Intraventricular TNF-α caused about 13.75% neuronal loss in 10-month-old wild-type mouse hippocampus, consistent with [[Necroptosis]] activation, which was raised in Alzheimer cortex and in transgenic mice. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
