# DAB1

DAB1 encodes an intracellular adaptor that binds the cytoplasmic tails of [[LRP8]] and VLDLR and relays the [[Reelin signaling pathway]] signal in neurons.

## Connections

- Higher in the C2 than the C1 subtype of Alzheimer brain samples, and negatively correlated with activated dendritic cells. Study: [[CDK5R1-related subtypes and immune infiltrates in Alzheimer disease]]
- Ligand: [[RELN]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
