# RTN4

RTN4 encodes reticulon 4, whose longest isoform Nogo-A is a neurite-growth inhibitor of the myelin and endoplasmic reticulum.

## Connections

- Nogo-A was a protein marker on the nanoparticle kit; in the brains of the APP/PS/ApoE knockdown mouse model it co-localised with amyloid beta 1-42, and it was measured in patient plasma.
