# CTSD

CTSD encodes the lysosomal aspartyl protease cathepsin D, which degrades amyloid beta and [[APP]] fragments.

## Connections

- Mouse Ctsd rose in striatum and hippocampus 7 days after carotid stenosis, with other lysosomal genes; see [[BCCAS mouse model]]. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Related clearance genes: [[A2M]], [[PLAT]], [[CTSB]], [[KLK6]]
- Ctsd rose in Grn knockout mouse CSF, and CTSD rose at mRNA and protein level and in the medium of GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Counter-screen for [[BACE1]] inhibitors: [[FAH65]] inhibited cathepsin D only at about 47 μM. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
