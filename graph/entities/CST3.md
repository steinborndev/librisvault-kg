# CST3

CST3 encodes cystatin C, a secreted cysteine protease inhibitor that binds amyloid beta, and its L68Q mutation causes hereditary cerebral haemorrhage with amyloidosis, Icelandic type.

## Connections

- Variant: [[CST3 p.A25T]], whose homozygous carriers were over-represented in a small vessel ischemic disease cohort. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Disease: [[Cerebral amyloid angiopathy]], [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
