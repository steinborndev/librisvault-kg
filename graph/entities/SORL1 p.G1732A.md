# SORL1 p.G1732A

A missense change from glycine to alanine at residue 1732, at a conserved glycine of the second 3Fn domain of [[SORL1]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- One case with age at onset 70 years; the authors note its pathogenicity is also supported by earlier pedigree work (Thonberg et al.) in a family with [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]], and suggest it impairs 3Fn folding. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
