# TJP1

TJP1 encodes zonula occludens-1 (ZO-1), the scaffold protein that anchors claudins and occludin of the [[Tight junction]] to the cytoskeleton.

## Connections

- Mast cell [[TNF]] is said to down-regulate it with [[OCLN]], [[CLDN5]] and VE-cadherin; ZO-1 is among barrier proteins implicated in Alzheimer pathogenesis. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
