# SLC6A4

SLC6A4 encodes the serotonin transporter (SERT), a membrane protein on serotonergic nerve terminals that clears [[Serotonin]] from the synapse and is the main target of [[Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors]].

## Connections

- In [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] cortex, SERT density (DASB binding) fell in frontal cortex at 24 months and parietal cortex at 18 to 24 months and serotonin uptake Vmax fell at 20 months, while brainstem Slc6a4 mRNA stayed unchanged. Synthetic Aβ40, not Aβ42, cut uptake Vmax by about 20% in wild-type membranes without displacing the DASB ligand. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
- Related: [[TSPO]], [[APP]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
