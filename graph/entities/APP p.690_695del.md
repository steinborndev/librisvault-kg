# APP p.690_695del

An 18-base-pair in-frame deletion in APP exon 17 removing six residues (690 to 695) of the amyloid-beta sequence, named the Uppsala mutation. It is described as the first dominantly inherited Alzheimer disease caused by a large deletion within amyloid-beta; it raises amyloid-beta generation by altering alpha- and beta-secretase cleavage of [[APP]], and the mutant peptide forms unique polymorphs that speed fibril and plaque formation. In the Swedish memory-clinic screen it was found in two siblings (onset 44 and 40 years, APOE ε3/ε3) and a cousin with aggressive early-onset disease in a family with dominant inheritance and onset at 40 to 50 years in earlier generations; more than 500 Swedish patients, older relatives and controls carried no other case. The sequence is in GenBank (MW892394).
