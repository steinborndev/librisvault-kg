# GRID2

GRID2 encodes the delta-2 glutamate receptor subunit, a synapse-organising protein best known from cerebellar Purkinje neurons.

## Connections

- The most enriched gene of AD2 microglia, the tau-associated profile in human cortex; GRID2-positive cells with microglia-like morphology were abundant in [[Alzheimer disease]] tissue with tau pathology, rare in controls and amyloid-only tissue, and sat in tau-rich regions and neuritic plaque-like structures.
