# CD33 p.A14V

A missense change from alanine to valine at residue 14 of [[CD33]] (rs12459419), in strong linkage disequilibrium with the protective late-onset Alzheimer disease allele rs3865444-A and linked to an isoform lacking exon 2.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CD33]]
- Associated with lower risk of [[Small vessel ischemic disease]] in a 96-patient cohort. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Disease context: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]; the shorter isoform is thought to relieve CD33 inhibition of [[TREM2]] in microglia
- Its exon 2 position (rs12459419, in almost complete linkage with [[CD33 rs3865444]]) was one of two exonic loci where exon 2 allele-specific expression was significant in monocytes and brain, so the promoter SNP and this exonic variant cannot be separated statistically. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
