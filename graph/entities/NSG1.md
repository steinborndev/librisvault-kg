# NSG1

NSG1 encodes a neuronal endosomal protein that regulates the trafficking of AMPA receptors and of [[APP]], and through them synaptic transmission, plasticity and amyloid-beta production.

## Connections

- NSG1 showed an isoform switch in Alzheimer temporal lobe without a change in total expression, and was assigned to GABAergic neurons only. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
