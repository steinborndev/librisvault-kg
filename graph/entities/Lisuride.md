# Lisuride

Lisuride is an ergot-derived dopamine agonist used in Parkinson disease; it is not approved in the United States.

## Connections

- Docked well to [[ETV6]] and was predicted to cross the blood-brain barrier without efflux, but was ranked below [[Lasmiditan]] because of ergot-related cardiac and pulmonary fibrosis risk and poor tolerability; an earlier randomised trial in [[Alzheimer disease]] showed no strong effect beyond some verbal learning gain. Study: [[Lasmiditan prioritised for Alzheimer disease by regulator docking and dynamics]]
