# PTK2B

PTK2B encodes a cytoplasmic protein tyrosine kinase highly expressed in the central nervous system, which interacts with hyperphosphorylated and oligomeric tau.

## Connections

- [[PTK2B rs73223431]] is the variant tested for early-onset disease and brain expression.
- [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] is the subgroup in which the variant was associated.
- Study: [[SNP association study of Alzheimer disease in southern Chinese population]]
- The [[NME8]] locus influenced PTK2B protein levels in monocytes. Study: [[CD33 modulates TREM2 in a monocyte protein QTL analysis of Alzheimer loci]]
- Its paralogue [[PTK2]] (FAK) was overexpressed in 3xTg-AD hippocampus; the knockdown shRNA was shown by immunoblot in HEK293T cells to spare Pyk2. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
