# Increased CSF total tau concentration

A raised level of total tau in cerebrospinal fluid, read as a marker of neuronal injury (the N of the [[ATN framework]]) in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Lower [[BIN1]] promoter methylation in blood went with higher CSF total tau (p = 0.0130) only in participants with [[Subjective cognitive decline]], independent of CSF Aβ42. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
- Related protein: [[MAPT]]
- Companion markers: [[Increased CSF phosphorylated tau concentration]], [[Decreased amyloid beta 42 peptide CSF concentration]]
