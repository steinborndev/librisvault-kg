# U0126

U0126 is a small-molecule inhibitor of MEK1 and MEK2, the kinases that activate ERK ([[MAPK1]]), used in cell experiments as an ERK pathway inhibitor.

## Connections

- Added to neurons with amyloid oligomers it raised [[DICER1]] protein further and lowered phospho-Dicer1. Study: [[Amyloid oligomers rapidly raise neuronal Dicer1 by reducing phosphorylation]]
