# Aducanumab

Aducanumab (Aduhelm, BIIB037) is a monoclonal antibody against aggregated forms of [[Amyloid-beta]], developed by Biogen and Eisai, that clears plaque on amyloid PET and was given conditional accelerated approval by the US Food and Drug Administration in June 2021 for [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Reported as targeting oligomeric Aβ, it failed a futility analysis midway through two Phase-3 trials in 2019 and was dropped; a 2020 reanalysis of the high-dose subgroups suggested benefit in only one of the two trials. The independent advisory panel (eleven members) voted against approval, three members resigned after it was granted, and approval rested on the amyloid-PET surrogate with a required Phase-4 trial and a safety concern about [[Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities]] (ARIA-E). The listed annual price was about $56,000. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
