# RBFOX3

RBFOX3 encodes the RNA-binding protein recognised by the NeuN antibody, the standard immunohistochemical marker of mature neuronal nuclei.

## Connections

- Streptozocin lowered NeuN staining in the dentate gyrus, CA1 and CA3 of 6-month-old [[3xTg-AD mouse]] hippocampus to 89-91% of control; [[LM-031]] limited the loss to 95-97%, though only the dentate gyrus change was significant. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- NeuN-positive cells in hippocampus and cortex fell in Aβ1-42-injected mice and were restored by 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]] and by [[Morin]], matching Nissl-stained cell counts. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- Acetylation near RBFOX3 was the neuron-specific control for sorting NeuN-positive nuclei (log2 fold change 1.99, FDR 4.9e-107). Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
