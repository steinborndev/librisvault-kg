# 27-Hydroxycholesterol

27-Hydroxycholesterol is an oxysterol made by [[CYP27A1]] that, unlike cholesterol, crosses the blood-brain barrier into the brain.

## Connections

- Proposed to carry the effect of hypercholesterolemia on the brain, promoting amyloid-beta accumulation and lowering glucose uptake. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Related: [[24S-Hydroxycholesterol]], [[Cholesterol]]
