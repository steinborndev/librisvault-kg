# PST-001

PST-001 is an orally active, brain-penetrant, ATP-site small-molecule inhibitor of [[DYRK1A]] built on a 5-methoxybenzothiazole scaffold with an acetamidopyridine extension (molecular weight 299.35 g/mol). It is inactive against [[GSK3B]] and affects other DYRK family members and CLK2 as its main off-targets.

## Connections

- In flies overexpressing human [[MAPT]] tau, [[Amyloid-beta]] 42 or the DYRK1A orthologue minibrain, 334 μM PST-001 cut tau phosphorylation at S262 by more than 50% and corrected eye degeneration, lifespan, climbing, sleep and memory to varying degree without affecting control flies. Study: [[PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome]]
- Earlier work cited there found the same compound reversed associative memory deficits in [[Ts65Dn mouse]] animals.
