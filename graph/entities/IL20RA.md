# IL20RA

IL20RA encodes the alpha subunit of the interleukin-20 receptor family, which pairs with [[IL20RB]] to form the receptor for [[IL19]].

## Connections

- With IL20RB, expressed in the nervous system only by microglia, according to the abstract. Study: [[Interleukin-19 as an autocrine brake on activated microglia]]
- Related: [[Microglial cell activation]]
