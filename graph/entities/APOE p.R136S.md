# APOE p.R136S

A rare missense change from arginine to serine at residue 136 of [[APOE]], called APOE Christchurch. Found in homozygous form in a carrier of the [[PSEN1 p.E280A]] mutation who showed delayed onset of cognitive impairment and limited Tau pathology despite heavy amyloid plaque burden; it is thought to act partly through reduced binding of APOE to heparan sulfate proteoglycans and other receptors.

## Connections

- Gene: [[APOE]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- In isogenic iPSC-derived microglia the variant resisted amyloid-beta-driven [[Lipid droplet]] formation, [[Lipid peroxidation]] and [[Ferroptosis]], kept [[Phagocytosis]] active and lowered phosphorylated [[MAPT]] in neurons and PSEN1 mutant organoids. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- An [[APOE mimetic peptide]] blocking receptor binding reproduced the protection in APOE3 microglia.
