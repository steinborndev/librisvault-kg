# TBXAS1

TBXAS1 encodes thromboxane A synthase, the cytochrome P450 enzyme that converts prostaglandin H2 to thromboxane A2 in platelets and myeloid cells.

## Connections

- Higher genetically predicted expression went with slightly lower Alzheimer risk (estimate 0.965, the weakest protective signal), with expression largely restricted to macrophages. Study: [[Single-nucleus sequencing and Mendelian randomization of Alzheimer risk genes]]
