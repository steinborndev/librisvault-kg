# CA12

CA12 encodes carbonic anhydrase 12, a membrane-bound zinc enzyme that interconverts carbon dioxide and bicarbonate.

## Connections

- A new allele-specific association between the GWAS SNP [[APH1B rs117618017]] and CA12 expression was found in the head of the caudate nucleus, not reported in earlier brain eQTL work. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
