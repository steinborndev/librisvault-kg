# CERS2

CERS2 encodes ceramide synthase 2, which makes the very long chain ceramides that are precursors of myelin galactosylceramide and [[Sulfatide]].

## Connections

- Enzyme activity falls early (Braak stage I/II in temporal cortex), before cortical tangles. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Related concept: [[White matter injury]]
