# CYP2D6

CYP2D6 encodes a cytochrome P450 enzyme that metabolises many drugs, including cholinesterase inhibitors used in [[Alzheimer disease]]. Carriers are classed as poor, intermediate, extensive or ultra-rapid metabolizers.

## Connections

- In 1086 Alzheimer patients, poor metabolizers (5.4%) had the lowest baseline cognition and the worst response to a multifactorial treatment, extensive metabolizers (59.5%) the best, and [[APOE]] 3/4 and 4/4 carriers were over-represented among poor metabolizers. Combined with [[SIRT2 rs10410544]], C/T extensive metabolizers responded best. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Design context: [[Pharmacogenetics]]; drug in the regimen's class: [[Donepezil]]
- In 1006 Alzheimer disease patients, normal (57.7%) and intermediate (30.6%) metabolizers improved significantly in anxiety and depression after one month of treatment, while poor (5.6%) and ultra-rapid (6.1%) metabolizers did not; poor metabolizers had higher baseline anxiety.
