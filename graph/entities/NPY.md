# NPY

NPY encodes neuropeptide Y, a neuropeptide expressed in neurons.

## Connections

- Listed as a co-risk gene for [[Alzheimer disease]] and memory disorders in an aging-disease gene set and abnormally expressed in both neurons and oligodendrocytes of aged monkey entorhinal cortex. Study: [[Single-nucleus atlas of aging monkey entorhinal cortex and Alzheimer disease]]
