# HP

HP encodes haptoglobin, an acute-phase plasma protein that binds free haemoglobin.

## Connections

- It was a significantly different protein in the combined cohort, replicated as a candidate marker in three or more earlier cohorts, stably selected for the Alzheimer versus control classifier, and included in the universal panel; the authors count it among the complement and innate immune components.
