# LEP

LEP encodes leptin, an adipose-derived hormone that signals satiety and energy status to the brain and is discussed as having pro-cognitive effects.

## Connections

- Plasma leptin rose in both wild-type and 5xFAD mice after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]]; the authors note no earlier report of such an effect. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
