# Mild behavioral impairment

A neurobehavioral syndrome of new-onset, persistent and impactful neuropsychiatric symptoms in older adults (decreased motivation, affective dysregulation, impulse dyscontrol, social inappropriateness, abnormal perception and thought content), regarded as a high-risk state for cognitive decline and progression to dementia that may reflect neurodegeneration.

## Connections

- Present in 48.4% of 62 patients with amnestic [[Mild cognitive impairment]] due to Alzheimer disease and in none of 50 cognitively normal controls at the validated cutoffs (6% of controls at a lower cutoff); dominated by affective dysregulation, [[Apathy]] and impulse dyscontrol. Severity was not associated with [[APOE]] ε4 or [[BDNF p.V66M]] Met carriage. Study: [[Mild behavioral impairment with APOE and BDNF in early Alzheimer disease]]
