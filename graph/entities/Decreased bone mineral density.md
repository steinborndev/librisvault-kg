# Decreased bone mineral density

Decreased bone mineral density is a lower than normal mineral content of bone, a laboratory and imaging finding that precedes or accompanies fragile bone.

## Connections

- Found in SCD2 knockout mice fed a high-fat diet. See [[SCD]]. Study: [[SCD2 in murine development, metabolism and disease review]]
