# LBP

LBP encodes lipopolysaccharide-binding protein, a plasma lipid-transfer protein that presents bacterial lipopolysaccharide to the innate immune system.

## Connections

- It was grouped with the lipid transport proteins that mediate cholesterol and phospholipid transport and inflammation, stably selected for the Alzheimer versus control classifier, and included in the universal panel.
