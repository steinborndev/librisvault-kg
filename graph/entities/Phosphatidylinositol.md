# Phosphatidylinositol

Phosphatidylinositols (PIs) are glycerophospholipids with an inositol head group, present in nearly all cell membranes and the parent of the phosphoinositide signalling lipids such as [[Phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate]].

## Connections

- Most plasma PI species were higher in Alzheimer cases than controls, unlike [[Phosphatidylcholine]] and [[Phosphatidylethanolamine]]; PI totals also rose in [[U251]] cells given oligomeric [[Amyloid-beta]], and 57.1% of PI species associated differently with a risk SNP in cases and controls (named as [[ABCA7]] in the results; the discussion attributes this to [[MS4A6A]]).
