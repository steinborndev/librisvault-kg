# Myoclonus

Myoclonus is a sudden, brief, involuntary muscle jerk. It is commoner in familial than in sporadic [[Alzheimer disease]], especially with PSEN1 mutations.

## Connections

- Disease: [[Familial Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: 3 of 9 index patients with [[PSEN1 p.M146I]] had myoclonus (33.3%), close to the 47% cited for PSEN1 carriers in general. See [[Familial Alzheimer disease mutations in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
