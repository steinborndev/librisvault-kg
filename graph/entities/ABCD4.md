# ABCD4

ABCD4 encodes an ATP-binding cassette transporter of the same family as [[ABCA7]]; its mutations cause an inborn error of vitamin B12 metabolism.

## Connections

- Three missense variants, [[ABCD4 p.G59R]], [[ABCD4 p.V172I]] and a third (rs57773157), sit on one rare 8-SNP haplotype of 12.9 kb carried by 10 Alzheimer cases and no controls. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
