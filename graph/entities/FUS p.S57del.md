# FUS p.S57del

A single-residue deletion in [[FUS]] (serine 57) previously reported as likely pathogenic in sporadic amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. A Swedish memory-clinic [[Alzheimer disease]] patient with a family history (male, onset 59 years, APOE ε4/ε4) carried it and had memory problems but no typical ALS symptoms, unlike the earlier reported cases. Population frequency 0.00017.
