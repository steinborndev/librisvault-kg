# SOX4

SOX4 encodes an SRY-box transcription factor of the SOX family, several members of which drive central nervous system [[Myelination]] and oligodendrocyte development.

## Connections

- A peak annotated to SOX4 lies in the hypoacetylated set from female hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia, whose peaks were enriched for Sox-family motifs; because Sox motifs are near-identical the data cannot say which family member is the master regulator.
