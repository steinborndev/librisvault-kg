# MIR193B

MIR193B encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-193b, a small non-coding RNA that represses target messenger RNAs after transcription.

## Connections

- Its interaction with [[APP]] was validated in an Alzheimer mouse model and in patient samples, was also seen in vesicles, and miR-193b has been proposed as an early diagnostic circulating marker.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see also [[MicroRNA-target interaction databases]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- Predicted to link the lncRNA XIST to [[NSF]] in a regulatory network built from miRNA target databases. Study: [[Four synaptic vesicle cycle genes protective in Alzheimer disease by MR]]
