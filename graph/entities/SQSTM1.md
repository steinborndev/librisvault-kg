# SQSTM1

SQSTM1 encodes p62, a cargo adaptor that links ubiquitinated proteins to autophagosomes and is itself degraded by [[Autophagy]].

## Connections

- p62 protein rose with both compounds, most strongly with isoacteoside, which the authors read as part of an [[MTOR]]-linked pro-autophagic effect; proteomics also correlated it with Alloprevotella in the raw-root group. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Autophagy partners: [[MAP1LC3B]], [[MTOR]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- RNA rose in the caudate nucleus of both [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]], and was linked by the authors to NF-κB signalling. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- p62 was cleared and LC3B rose after [[Berberine]] in D-ribose-treated cells and mice, read as activation of PINK1-Parkin mitophagy. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- p62 accumulated after TNF-α in neuronal cultures, in Braak VI cortex and in APP/PS1 CA1; it bound [[RIPK1]], [[RIPK3]] and [[MLKL]] through its ZZ domain (residues 122 to 167) and promoted [[Necroptosis]], and p62 shRNA lowered phospho-MLKL by 47% in APP/PS1 mice. Its build-up followed [[UVRAG]] loss and stalled autophagic flux. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
