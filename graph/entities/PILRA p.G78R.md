# PILRA p.G78R

A missense change from glycine to arginine at residue 78 of [[PILRA]] (rs1859788), the leading candidate causal variant at a late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] locus; it reduces binding of endogenous ligands and has been linked to herpes simplex virus 1 infectivity and antibody titre.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PILRA]]
- Used in place of the index SNP [[NYAP1 rs12539172]], it gave stronger associations with lower plasma [[PILRB]] (FDR p = 3.6 x 10^-52) and PILRA (1.4 x 10^-22), with genotype differences in all age groups. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Also the GWAS SNP examined for allelic imbalance at PILRB in brain cells. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
