# SOD2

SOD2 encodes the mitochondrial manganese superoxide dismutase, which detoxifies superoxide produced by the respiratory chain.

## Connections

- Docking placed low-energy [[EDR peptide]] binding sequences in its promoter; the authors link oxidative stress and falling superoxide dismutase levels in postmortem synaptosomes to synaptic loss. Study: [[EDR and KED tripeptides restore dendritic spines in 5xFAD mice]]

- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
