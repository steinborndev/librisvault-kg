# TGFB1

TGFB1 encodes transforming growth factor beta 1, a cytokine with neuroprotective and anti-inflammatory effects that is used as an M2 marker in microglia.

## Connections

- Its mRNA (written TGFβ in the study) rose about 2.4-fold after amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture and 2.3-fold in those kept 16 days, one of few responses shared by both ages. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
