# IL18

IL18 encodes interleukin-18, a pro-inflammatory cytokine made as an inactive precursor that is cleaved by caspase-1 in inflammasome complexes.

## Connections

- Its mRNA rose 2.1-fold after amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture and less in those kept 16 days, although [[NLRP3]] mRNA did not change; the authors suggest the NLRP1 inflammasome or NLRP3-independent routes, without testing them. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Related: [[IL1B]], [[HMGB1]]
- Suggested as a proinflammatory marker of cerebral white matter injury. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
