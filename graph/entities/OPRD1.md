# OPRD1

OPRD1 encodes the δ-opioid receptor (DOR), a G protein-coupled receptor widely expressed in cortex and hippocampus that is activated by endogenous enkephalins and by synthetic ligands such as [[UFP-512]] and [[DADLE]], and blocked by [[Naltrindole]].

## Connections

- In rat [[PC12]] and Swedish-mutant [[SH-SY5Y]] cells, DOR activation lowered [[BACE1]] activity and protein and Aβ42 under amyloid injury, while DOR blockade or knock-down raised them; the authors cite reduced DOR density in Alzheimer brain. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
- Opposed in this setting by [[OPRM1]]; context: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Amyloid-beta formation]].
