# Atorvastatin

A lipophilic [[Statin]] that inhibits HMG-CoA reductase.

## Connections

- In Alzheimer disease outpatients it gave smaller triglyceride variations than [[Simvastatin]], and a larger HDL cholesterol rise in APOE ε4 carriers. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Pharmacogenetic modifiers: [[APOE]], [[ACE Alu insertion-deletion]], [[LDLR rs11669576]], [[CETP rs5882]], [[CETP rs708272]]
