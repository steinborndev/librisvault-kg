# DADLE

DADLE is an enkephalin analogue that activates the δ-opioid receptor ([[OPRD1]]) but also binds the μ-opioid receptor ([[OPRM1]]).

## Connections

- Unlike [[UFP-512]], it did not reliably lower [[BACE1]] in [[PC12]] cells (a slight rise at 5 μM, a small fall at 1 μM), which the authors attribute to MOR binding; earlier work using DADLE in [[HEK293]] cells reported that DOR activation promoted amyloid processing. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
