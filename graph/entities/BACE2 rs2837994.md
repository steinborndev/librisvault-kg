# BACE2 rs2837994

rs2837994 is a common [[BACE2]] SNP that was the strongest variant for CSF amyloid-beta 1-42 in ADNI participants with Alzheimer disease (uncorrected p = 0.000183), in [[APOE]] ε4 non-carriers only.
