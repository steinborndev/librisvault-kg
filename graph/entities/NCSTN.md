# NCSTN

NCSTN encodes nicastrin, a subunit of the [[Gamma-secretase complex]] together with [[PSEN1]] or [[PSEN2]], [[APH1A]] or [[APH1B]] and PEN-2. The complex makes the final cut of [[APP]] that releases [[Amyloid-beta]].

## Connections

- In mouse gut and brain its mRNA did not clearly differ between tissues, and colonic nicastrin protein did not change between 3 and 12 months in wild-type mice. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
