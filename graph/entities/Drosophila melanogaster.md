# Drosophila melanogaster

The fruit fly, a short-lived genetic model organism in which human genes can be overexpressed in chosen neurons with the GAL4/UAS system and tested in rapid assays of degeneration, lifespan, movement, sleep and memory.

## Connections

- Human tau 0N4R, secreted tandem [[Amyloid-beta]] 42 or the [[DYRK1A]] orthologue minibrain (mnb, 82% amino acid identity) overexpressed in fly eye, neurons, clock neurons or mushroom body caused photoreceptor loss, shorter life, poor climbing, sleep loss and weak 1 h memory, and the inhibitor [[PST-001]] corrected most of them.
