# PCDH11X rs5941047

rs5941047 is a polymorphism in the X-linked gene [[PCDH11X]], one of four reported by Carrasquillo et al. (2009) as associated with [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]. In an independent dataset of 889 cases and 850 controls it showed no association. Study: [[PCDH11X polymorphisms fail to replicate in late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
