# Acetylcysteine

Acetylcysteine (N-acetylcysteine) is a cysteine derivative used as an antioxidant and glutathione precursor, and as a mucolytic and paracetamol antidote.

## Connections

- In cultured neurons it reversed the fall of [[NFE2L2]] and [[DICER1]] caused by chronic [[Amyloid-beta]] 42 exposure, placing [[Oxidative stress]] upstream of the Dicer1 loss. Study: [[Dicer1 loss in APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse brain via amyloid-beta 42 repression of Nrf2]]
- At 2 mM it blocked mitophagy induced by [[Carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenyl hydrazone]] but not by [[ALT001]], consistent with ALT001 not acting through oxidative damage. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
