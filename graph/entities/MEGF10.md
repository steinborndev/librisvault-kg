# MEGF10

MEGF10 encodes a phagocytic receptor of astrocytes that helps them engulf debris.

## Connections

- In the hippocampus of APP/PS1 mice, which hold both dystrophic neurites and amyloid-beta oligomers, amyloid-beta lowered Megf10 and Mertk ([[MERTK]]) on reactive astrocytes and so cut their phagocytic capacity, preserving oligomers and [[Dystrophic neurite]] and feeding a proposed vicious cycle. See [[Astrocyte activation]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
