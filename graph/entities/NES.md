# NES

NES encodes nestin, an intermediate filament protein that marks neural stem cells during neurogenesis.

## Connections

- The top [[EDR peptide]] DNA sequence from docking was found in its promoter; the authors note that nestin-deficient mice show raised dentate gyrus neurogenesis but impaired long-term memory. Study: [[EDR and KED tripeptides restore dendritic spines in 5xFAD mice]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
