# Fucosterol

Fucosterol is the main sterol of brown algae and the precursor of [[Saringosterol]]; it crosses the blood-brain barrier less well than saringosterol and is thought to activate liver X receptors indirectly by raising endogenous desmosterol.

## Connections

- Highest in crude Fucus vesiculosus, Sargassum muticum, Himanthalia elongata and Ascophyllum nodosum, and in extracts of F. vesiculosus, S. muticum and A. nodosum. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Targets: [[NR1H3]], [[NR1H2]]
