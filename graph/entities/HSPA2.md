# HSPA2

HSPA2 encodes a member of the Hsp70 chaperone family.

## Connections

- Moderately upregulated (P < 10^-3) in brain but not blood of Alzheimer cases in the APOE ε2/ε3 group and total sample, alongside [[C4A]] and [[C4B]]. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Showed an upward trend in the caudate nucleus of [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]], read as a proteostatic stress response, but it was not significant when each disease was compared with controls. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
