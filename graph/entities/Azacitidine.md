# Azacitidine

Azacitidine is a cytidine analogue that inhibits DNA methyltransferases and lowers [[DNA methylation]]; it is used clinically in myeloid malignancies and in the lab as a demethylating agent.

## Connections

- Used as the positive control for demethylation: with [[D-ribose]] it lowered PINK1 promoter methylation and [[DNMT1]] and raised PINK1 as much as [[Berberine]] did in [[Neuro2a]] cells and [[APP-PS1 mouse]] hippocampus. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Gene affected: [[PINK1]]
