# TREM2 p.R62H

A missense change of [[TREM2]] (arginine 62 to histidine) associated with increased risk of [[Alzheimer disease]], though one early study found no association and an 85,133-subject study found a strong one (allele frequency 1.4% in cases, 0.9% in controls). In the Swedish memory-clinic screen it was carried by eight Alzheimer and two [[Mild cognitive impairment]] patients, an allele frequency of 4.9% against about 1% in European databases, including both patients with the Uppsala APP deletion [[APP p.690_695del]].
