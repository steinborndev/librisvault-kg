# MIR34A

MIR34A encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-34a, a small non-coding RNA that represses gene expression after transcription.

## Connections

- miR-34a was one of eight microRNAs selected from a screen; its plasma level, with those of miR-124b and miR-153, was lower in the third patient than in the other three.
- Marker for [[Alzheimer disease]] on the nanoparticle kit; see [[Molecular beacon nanoparticle sensor]].
- Study: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]]
- It was the only microRNA of 169 disease-database candidates seen in all four evidence categories (patient samples, Alzheimer models, non-Alzheimer models, in silico prediction). Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- hsa-miR-34a-5p was one of the miRNAs predicted to regulate [[CLTB]] or [[NSF]], and is already proposed as an Alzheimer marker. Study: [[Four synaptic vesicle cycle genes protective in Alzheimer disease by MR]]
