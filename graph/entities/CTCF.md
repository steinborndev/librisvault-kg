# CTCF

CTCF encodes CCCTC-binding factor, a zinc-finger DNA-binding protein and master organiser of genome architecture that anchors [[Chromatin looping]] and so shapes how distant regulatory elements reach their target genes.

## Connections

- CTCF binding was reduced at many synaptic, cell-adhesion and cytoskeletal genes in Alzheimer frontal cortex. Study: [[Diminished CTCF binding at synaptic genes in Alzheimer frontal cortex]]
