# TREM2 p.L211P

A missense change from leucine to proline at residue 211 of [[TREM2]], in the cytoplasmic domain, in the shorter transcript form; the source's discussion writes it as P211L. A recent report linked it to Alzheimer disease risk in African Americans.

## Connections

- Gene: [[TREM2]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Heterozygous in the Thai patient #2 who also carried [[MAPT p.P513A]]; the authors suggest linkage disequilibrium and mention lower soluble TREM2 in cerebrospinal fluid. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
