# Hereditary diffuse leukoencephalopathy with spheroids

Hereditary diffuse leukoencephalopathy with spheroids is a white-matter disease starting in the 40s and 50s with dementia, psychiatric signs, parkinsonism and seizures, caused by kinase-domain mutations of [[CSF1R]].

## Connections

- Two [[CSF1R]] variants in Thai Alzheimer patients lay outside the kinase domain and were not read as causing it: [[CSF1R p.P54Q]], [[CSF1R p.L536V]]. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
