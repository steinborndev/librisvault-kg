# MIR200A

MIR200A encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-200a, a small non-coding RNA that represses gene expression after transcription.

## Connections

- miR-200a was one of eight microRNAs selected from a screen; its target sensor gave a fluorescence signal in HT22 cells treated with amyloid beta oligomer, and it was measured in patient plasma.
- Marker for [[Alzheimer disease]] on the nanoparticle kit; see [[Molecular beacon nanoparticle sensor]].
- Study: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]]
- miR-200a-3p was found down in APP/PS1 and SAMP8 mice, cultures and patient blood; it blocks neuronal apoptosis through the Bax/caspase-3 axis and represses [[BACE1]] and [[PRKACB]] mRNAs, lowering Aβ42 and tau phosphorylation. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
