# Insulin resistance

A state in which tissues respond poorly to insulin, a core feature of [[Metabolic syndrome]].

## Connections

- Peripheral and central insulin resistance worsened in APP/PS1/Sirt3-knockout compared with APP/PS1 mice (earlier work cited), and lower [[IDE]] is proposed to add to it. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- Related: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Obesity]]
- Hippocampal [[INSR]] fell 47% and inhibitory [[IRS1]] serine phosphorylation rose in 3xTg-AD mice overexpressing [[PTK2]]; pathway analysis called raised insulin resistance. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Untreated 5xFAD mice had lower plasma [[INS]], higher [[GCG]], a lower insulin to glucagon ratio and low hippocampal phospho-PI3K and phospho-Akt; the insulin sensitiser [[D-Pinitol]] normalised most of these. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
