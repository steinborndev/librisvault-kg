# PPARGC1A

PPARGC1A encodes PGC-1α, a transcriptional coactivator that is a master regulator of [[Mitochondrial biogenesis]].

## Connections

- Induced with [[TFAM]] and [[NRF1]] by [[ALT001]] in cells and in mouse hippocampus, and reduced in 5×FAD hippocampus until ALT001 raised it. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Related: [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
