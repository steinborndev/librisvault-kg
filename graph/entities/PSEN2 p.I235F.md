# PSEN2 p.I235F

A missense change from isoleucine to phenylalanine at residue 235 of [[PSEN2]], in the fifth transmembrane helix. It was found in a Caribbean Hispanic family with [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]], although two unaffected relatives also carried it, and in mutant neuroblastoma cells it doubled the amyloid beta 42 to 40 ratio.
