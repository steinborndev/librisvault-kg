# LINGO1

LINGO1 encodes LINGO-1, a transmembrane protein that negatively regulates oligodendrocyte differentiation and myelination.

## Connections

- Raised in Alzheimer brain; an anti-LINGO-1 antibody increased oligodendrocytes and myelin and improved memory in APP/PS1 and 5xFAD mice. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
