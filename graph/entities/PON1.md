# PON1

PON1 encodes paraoxonase 1, an HDL-associated serum enzyme with antioxidant activity.

## Connections

- It was significant in the Mental Health Research Center cohort alone, one of the proteins closest to significance for control versus mild cognitive impairment, and a lipid transport protein in the authors' functional grouping (antioxidant defence). It was stably selected for the classifier and overlapped with an earlier panel, but is not among the 13 universal panel proteins.
