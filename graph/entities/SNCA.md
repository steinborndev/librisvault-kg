# SNCA

SNCA encodes α-synuclein, a presynaptic protein that aggregates into the Lewy bodies of [[Parkinson disease]].

## Connections

- Its toxicity is reported to be mitigated by SCD1 inhibition or deficiency; see [[SCD]]. Study: [[SCD1 as a therapeutic target in neurological disorders review]]
- Alpha-synuclein was detergent-insoluble in demented but not control human amygdala. Study: [[Detergent-insoluble proteins in amygdala of cognitively impaired elderly]]
