# SORL1 p.E270K

A rare missense change from glutamate to lysine at residue 270 in the VPS10 domain of [[SORL1]], reported in Alzheimer disease families.

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- Found in one of two [[Small vessel ischemic disease]] patients carrying a VPS10 variant. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Common in the sequencing sample (frequency 1.9%); imputed in a GWAS it gave an odds ratio of 1.02 (95% CI 0.95-1.09), no clear effect on [[Alzheimer disease]] risk. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
