# IL1B

IL1B encodes interleukin-1 beta, a pro-inflammatory cytokine and common readout of microglial activation.

## Connections

- More strongly induced in APOE3 than [[APOE p.R136S]] microglia by PSEN1 mutant neurons, Tau oligomers and Alzheimer synaptosomes. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Fell in all ajugol- and isoacteoside-treated groups of amyloid-beta-challenged [[BV2]] microglia; inflammasome partners [[NLRP3]], [[CASP1]]. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Brain mRNA fell in [[FAD4T mouse]] animals treated with [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Not induced in rat astrocytes by Aβ1-42 alone, induced by LPS, and induced (p = 0.05) when [[MIR146A]] was blocked first. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- IL-1β protein by ELISA fell in Alzheimer mouse brain after ADSC exosomes (most with circ-Epc1-containing exosomes) and in LPS-challenged [[BV2]] cells overexpressing circ-Epc1; [[MIR770]] mimic or TREM2 knockdown partly reversed the cell effect. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- IL-1β protein was higher in [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]] cortex from 12 months, rising most at 20 months, before serotonin transporter density and uptake fell; no direct effect on transporter binding was seen, and tissue levels were at least tenfold below those of earlier in vitro studies. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
- mRNA rose with amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture and, less, in those kept 16 days, whose baseline was about 10-fold lower; the authors treat IL-1β and [[TNF]] as targets for stage-specific tuning. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Part of the IL-1β / [[IL1RN]] axis whose regulation differs with Cd33 and Trem2 status in 5xFAD mouse microglia. Study: [[TREM2 acts downstream of CD33 in 5xFAD mouse microglia]]
- mRNA in blood mononuclear cells was higher than in controls in both dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer disease, and higher in Lewy body dementia than in Alzheimer disease (p = 0.008). Study: [[PBMC nicotinic receptor and cytokine expression in DLB and Alzheimer disease]]
- Brain IL-1β measured by ELISA rose in Aβ1-42-injected mice and was lowered by 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]]. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- IL-1β was raised in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and lowered by [[Magnolol]] at 20 and 40 mg/kg (p < 0.001) and by [[Donepezil]] (ELISA, n = 6 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- IL1B transcript rose after Aβ1-42 only in [[CHRFAM7A]] carrier iPSC microglia-like cells; the IL-1β protein response to LPS was below the antibody array's detection limit but seen by RT-qPCR and ELISA. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
