# Genistein

Genistein is a soy isoflavone and tyrosine kinase inhibitor that, at millimolar doses in cell work, blocks caveolin-mediated endocytosis.

## Connections

- At 1 mmol/L it lowered uptake of [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]] in [[Neuro2a]] as much as [[Chlorpromazine]] did, and was one of only two inhibitors that acted in [[BV2]] microglia. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
