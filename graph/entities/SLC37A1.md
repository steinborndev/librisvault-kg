# SLC37A1

SLC37A1 encodes a glucose-6-phosphate transporter of the endoplasmic reticulum membrane.

## Connections

- Changed in the caudate nucleus of [[Alzheimer disease]] but not clearly of [[Parkinson disease]]; the discussion lists it with [[SLC25A6]] as a reduced solute carrier. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
