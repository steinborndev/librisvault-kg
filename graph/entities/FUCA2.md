# FUCA2

FUCA2 encodes plasma alpha-L-fucosidase, a secreted enzyme related to the lysosomal tissue fucosidase [[FUCA1]].

## Connections

- One of four proteins (with [[FUCA1]], [[HEXB]] and [[CAPG]]) raised in CSF of symptomatic GRN mutation carriers, in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia medium and in Grn knockout mouse microglia. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related: [[GRN]], [[Frontotemporal dementia]]
