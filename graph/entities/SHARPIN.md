# SHARPIN

SHARPIN encodes SHANK associated RH domain interactor, a protein whose function feeds the NF-κB pathway of inflammatory and immune signalling.

## Connections

- Two common missense variants, [[SHARPIN p.S17F]] and [[SHARPIN p.P294S]], raise the risk of [[Alzheimer disease]]; a rare variant, p.Gly186Arg, had earlier mislocalised the protein and weakened NF-κB activation.
