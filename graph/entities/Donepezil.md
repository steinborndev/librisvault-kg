# Donepezil

A cholinesterase inhibitor and the most prescribed drug for [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Beyond acetylcholinesterase inhibition, the review notes it raises [[SIRT1]] activity and reduces reactive oxygen species. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Response to cholinesterase inhibitors depends on [[CYP2D6]] metabolizer status; see [[Pharmacogenetics]] and [[Sirtuin modulators]].
- Named with rivastigmine, galantamine and memantine in a repurposing screen as the licensed Alzheimer drugs to look for in the ranked lists; [[Memantine]] and [[Galantamine]] were found, donepezil was not reported. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Given at 5 mg a day to about 5% of patients in a cohort assessed for anxiety and depression response; the authors note most cholinesterase inhibitors except rivastigmine are cleared through CYP enzymes. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
- Positive control at 5 mg/kg by gavage for four months in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] animals: it improved maze performance, lowered Aβ42, cytokines and glial density, and restored synaptic proteins; [[Magnolol]] at 40 mg/kg was judged stronger by the authors, who also cite an oral mouse LD50 of 45.2 mg/kg for donepezil against about 2200 mg/kg for magnolol. Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
