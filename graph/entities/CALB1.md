# CALB1

CALB1 encodes calbindin D28k, a calcium-binding protein that marks a subset of GABAergic interneurons.

## Connections

- CALB1 was among the differentially expressed genes shared by the Mayo and Mount Sinai temporal lobe datasets and was assigned to GABAergic neurons only. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
