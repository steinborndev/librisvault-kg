# EPHA1 p.V160A

A missense change from valine to alanine at residue 160 of [[EPHA1]] (rs4725617, G allele), lying about 12 kb from the late-onset Alzheimer disease signal rs11767557.

## Connections

- Gene: [[EPHA1]]
