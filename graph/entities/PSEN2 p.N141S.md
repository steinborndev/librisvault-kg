# PSEN2 p.N141S

A missense change from asparagine to serine at residue 141 of [[PSEN2]], in the second transmembrane region. It was newly found in a Chinese woman with familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] and classed pathogenic under the ACMG criteria. In transfected [[HEK293]] cells it raised Aβ42 (140.3 against 71.5 pg/ml for wild-type PSEN2) and the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio (0.067 against 0.031) without changing Aβ40. Other changes at the same residue (N141I, N141D, N141Y) had been reported before.
