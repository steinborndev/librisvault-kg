# PDHB

PDHB encodes the E1 beta subunit of the pyruvate dehydrogenase complex, which turns pyruvate into acetyl-CoA and so feeds the TCA cycle.

## Connections

- Correlated with amyloid-beta, Tau and MMSE in Alzheimer brain data and down-regulated in both brain and peripheral blood cells, a candidate blood biomarker. Study: [[TCA cycle enzyme genes in Alzheimer brain and blood transcriptomes]]
- Process: [[Tricarboxylic acid cycle]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
