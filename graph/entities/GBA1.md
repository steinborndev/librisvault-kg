# GBA1

GBA1 encodes the lysosomal enzyme glucosylceramidase beta 1 (glucocerebrosidase), and variants in it are an established risk factor for [[Parkinson disease]]. Proposed mechanisms include lysosomal dysfunction, impaired autophagy and disturbed calcium balance.

## Connections

- Variant: [[GBA1 p.I20V]]
- A network search linked GBA1 to [[PSEN2]] through text mining and co-expression, and the authors suggest the two could meet through calcium signalling and Wnt/beta-catenin signalling. Study: [[Novel PSEN2 p.V226A in a Korean patient with atypical Alzheimer disease]]
