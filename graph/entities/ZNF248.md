# ZNF248

ZNF248 encodes a zinc finger protein of poorly characterised function.

## Connections

- Expression and nearby chromatin accessibility were higher in APOE4 and APOE-knockout human microglia than in APOE2 or APOE3 microglia, which the authors read as a possible toxic gain of function; in another study it was raised in TREM2 knockout microglia ([[TREM2]]).
