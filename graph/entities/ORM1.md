# ORM1

ORM1 encodes orosomucoid (alpha-1-acid glycoprotein 1), an abundant acute-phase plasma protein.

## Connections

- It was one of the 16 proteins selected in every L1-regularized run for separating Alzheimer disease from controls, one of the 13 proteins of the universal panel, and among the proteins replicated in three or more earlier cohorts; the authors group it with complement and innate immune components. It also overlapped with the panel of an earlier study on one of the two cohorts. Study: [[Plasma protein panel for Alzheimer disease risk by MRM mass spectrometry]]
