# MT-CYB

MT-CYB is the mitochondrial gene for cytochrome b, a core subunit of complex III (the cytochrome bc1 complex) of the respiratory chain.

## Connections

- Appeared on the first principal component in the separate Alzheimer and Parkinson analyses of caudate nucleus RNA-seq, taken as a sign of mitochondrial involvement in both. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Related: [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]].
