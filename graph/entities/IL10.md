# IL10

IL10 encodes interleukin-10, an anti-inflammatory cytokine and the founding member of the interleukin-10 family, which also includes [[IL19]]. It signals through a receptor containing [[IL10RA]].

## Connections

- The number of IL-10-expressing astrocytes rose in the hippocampus of Alzheimer model mice fed [[cis-9, trans-11-conjugated linoleic acid]], without a change in total astrocyte number. Study: [[Dietary conjugated linoleic acid lowers amyloid-beta in Alzheimer mouse hippocampus]]
- Related: [[Astrocyte activation]], [[Neuroinflammation]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- IL-10 was lowered in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and raised by [[Magnolol]] at 20 and 40 mg/kg (p < 0.001) and by [[Donepezil]] (ELISA, n = 6 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- IL-10 rose after LPS in the isogenic [[CHRFAM7A]] carrier line, which the authors found unexpected next to falling TNF. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
