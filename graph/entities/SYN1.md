# SYN1

SYN1 encodes synapsin I, a presynaptic phosphoprotein that tethers synaptic vesicles.

## Connections

- Lowered in APP-overexpressing [[BE2-M17]] cells, lowered further by an anti-miR-132 inhibitor, and restored by [[Osthol]]-[[Zinc oxide]]. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
- Regulator proposed: [[MIR132]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; related markers [[SYP]], [[DLG4]]
- Synapsin-1 protein was lowered in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain; [[Magnolol]] at 20 and 40 mg/kg and [[Donepezil]] restored it (n = 3 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
