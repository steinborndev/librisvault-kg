# TARDBP

TARDBP encodes TDP-43, an RNA- and DNA-binding nuclear protein whose cytoplasmic, aggregated form marks frontotemporal dementia and amyotrophic lateral sclerosis and is common in other dementias.

## Connections

- TDP-43 was among the known pathological proteins recovered in the detergent-insoluble fraction of demented but not control amygdala, and TDP-43 inclusions partly colocalized with FTL and phospho-PRKDC. Study: [[Detergent-insoluble proteins in amygdala of cognitively impaired elderly]]
- Disease and phenotype: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Cognitive impairment]]
