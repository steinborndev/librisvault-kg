# Palmatine

Palmatine is a plant protoberberine alkaloid with an isoquinolium core, found in medicinal herbs.

## Connections

- One of four isoquinolium compounds (with [[Berberine]], epiberberine and coptisine) that induced mitophagy in a 61-compound screen; stripping its ether groups gave [[ALT001]], about 25 times more active. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Pathway: [[Mitophagy]]
- Second-ranked ingredient (degree 9) in the predicted Alzheimer target network of Coptis chinensis. Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
