# CD163

CD163 encodes a scavenger receptor of macrophages for the haemoglobin-haptoglobin complex; in brain it marks CNS-associated macrophages and a subset of amyloid-associated microglia.

## Connections

- Marker of CNS-associated macrophages in human cortex nuclei, and also significantly differentially expressed along the AD1 microglia trajectory, where it was confined to nuclei at the end; the authors link this to a report that CD163 is nearly exclusive to amyloid-associated microglia. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
- Related: [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- One of 16 genes in the top 200 upregulated in both hippocampal CA1 and CA3 of both women and men with Alzheimer disease, read as amyloid-responsive microglia present in both sexes. Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]
