# CACNA2D3

CACNA2D3 encodes the alpha-2/delta-3 auxiliary subunit of voltage-gated calcium channels, which helps traffic the pore subunit to the membrane and tunes its currents in neurons.

## Connections

- In a Mendelian randomization screen of macrophage-derived candidates, higher genetically predicted expression went with lower Alzheimer risk (estimate 0.938); unlike the other six genes it was expressed mainly in glutamatergic neurons.
