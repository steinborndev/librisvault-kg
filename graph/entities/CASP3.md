# CASP3

CASP3 encodes caspase-3, the main executioner protease of [[Apoptosis]].

## Connections

- Caspase-3 fell with ajugol or isoacteoside in model mouse hippocampus and in [[BV2]] cells, and ajugol was reported to have the stronger effect on this axis. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Apoptosis axis: [[CYCS]], [[BAX]], [[BCL2]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Caspase 3 activity raised by misfolded ΔK280 TauRD fell with [[LM-031]] and [[Licochalcone A]] (121% to 75-83% in [[HEK293]]; 117% to 90-102% in [[SH-SY5Y]]); cleaved CASP3 fell from 116-123% to 82-87% in CREB1-knockdown cells. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- One of five hub genes by network degree among 95 ginger-compound and Alzheimer targets, and the only hub carried into docking: [[6-Gingerol]] scored -6.0 kcal/mol, the weakest of the four simulated complexes, though the 100 ns run stayed stable (complex RMSD 0.3-0.4 nm, up to four hydrogen bonds). Other hubs: [[ALB]], [[ACTB]], [[GAPDH]], [[CAT]]. Study: [[6-Gingerol from ginger screened for Alzheimer disease by docking and dynamics]]
- The top-scoring [[EDR peptide]] DNA sequence was found in its promoter by docking (a prediction only); the authors cite caspase-3 control of spine density and raised synaptic caspase-3 in Alzheimer brain. Study: [[EDR and KED tripeptides restore dendritic spines in 5xFAD mice]]
- Activated with caspase-8 in neuronal cultures by TNF-α with Smac mimetic and blocked by [[Z-VAD-FMK]], which moved cells to [[Necroptosis]]. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Degree 26 in the interaction network of 34 targets shared by heat-clearing herbs and Alzheimer disease, and a target of all seven herbs; the authors cite reduced CASP3 lowering LPS-induced inflammation in astrocytes (prediction only). Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
