# Dementia with Lewy bodies

Dementia with Lewy bodies is a progressive dementia marked by alpha-synuclein deposits (Lewy bodies) in the brain, and one of the common dementias to be told apart from [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Frontotemporal dementia]].

## Connections

- Three CSF proteins were differentially expressed and validated across three cohorts, and CSF immune-related processes were altered; the glycolytic enzymes raised in Alzheimer disease ([[ALDOA]], [[LDHA]], [[MDH1]], [[PGAM1]]) were not. Study: [[CSF proteomics of Alzheimer, Lewy body and frontotemporal dementia]]
- Related gene: [[SNCA]]

Study: [[PBMC nicotinic receptor and cytokine expression in DLB and Alzheimer disease]]
