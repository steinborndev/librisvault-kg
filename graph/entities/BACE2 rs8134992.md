# BACE2 rs8134992

rs8134992 is a common [[BACE2]] SNP on chromosome 21; it was the most significant variant in the all-subject case-control scan of the locus in a neuropathology-confirmed [[Alzheimer disease]] cohort (uncorrected p = 0.00136).
