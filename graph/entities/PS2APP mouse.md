# PS2APP mouse

A double transgenic mouse expressing human [[PSEN2]] N141I and [[APP]] with the Swedish mutation under a neuron-specific enolase promoter, a model of familial Alzheimer amyloid pathology.

## Connections

- At 10 months it spent less time and travelled less in the target quadrant of the [[Morris water maze]] and had raised hippocampal mitochondrial ROS, lower membrane potential and lower ATP; four weeks of intranasal [[ALT001]] reversed all of these. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Related models: [[5xFAD mouse]], [[Tg2576 mouse]]
