# Early-onset Alzheimer disease

Early-onset Alzheimer disease (EOAD) is [[Alzheimer disease]] with onset before 65 years. About 10% of cases are familial, from autosomal dominant variants in the genes for amyloid precursor protein, presenilin 1 and presenilin 2; the rest are thought to be mainly polygenic.

- Variants tested against it: [[HLA-DRB1 rs9271058]], [[CD2AP rs9473117]], [[PTK2B rs73223431]], [[APH1B rs35408871]].

- The 39-variant common-variant risk score was associated with onset before 65 years (OR 1.58 per standard deviation), so polygenic risk contributes to early-onset as well as late-onset disease. Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
- Truncating [[SORL1]] variants carried a 35.3-fold risk and high-priority missense variants a 9.9-fold risk of early-onset disease; their median age at onset of 62 to 64 years is later than for typical presenilin or APP mutations. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Early-onset and late-onset age groups were given separate probability curves in a polygenic-score framework. Study: [[Probability of Alzheimer disease from polygenic score, APOE and rare variants]]
- Digenic case: a Korean woman with onset at about 61 carried rare variants in both [[APP]] ([[APP p.V551M]]) and [[PSEN2]] ([[PSEN2 p.H169N]]); the authors propose a synergistic effect and an East Asian screening panel. Study: [[Double APP and PSEN2 mutations in a Korean patient with early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Mutations in PSEN1 or APP explained 17 of 77 (22%) Taiwanese familial or early-onset cases, with earlier onset (46.2 against 52.7 years) and less white matter hyperintensity than in non-carriers; see [[Familial Alzheimer disease]]. Study: [[Familial Alzheimer disease mutations in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
- Thai cohort: in eight patients only two carried classical mutations ([[PSEN1 p.E184G]], [[APP p.V604M]]); variants in Parkinson, frontotemporal, prion and CSF1R genes were also found, and the authors argue for panel testing. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- Chinese hospital cohort: pathogenic or likely pathogenic [[PSEN2]] variants were found in 0.8% (3/367) of early-onset and 1.0% (3/306) of family-history patients and in none of the late-onset or sporadic ones; the three carriers had familial early-onset disease with memory decline and mental symptoms. Study: [[PSEN2 mutation spectrum and functionally validated variants in a Chinese Alzheimer cohort]]
- A 59-year-old Korean woman with rapid memory loss and visual hallucinations and a novel [[PSEN2 p.V226A]] was diagnosed with early-onset disease on amyloid PET, a blood oligomer assay and elevated C3. Study: [[Novel PSEN2 p.V226A in a Korean patient with atypical Alzheimer disease]]
- Cited as the rare (1% or less of all Alzheimer cases), fully penetrant monogenic form whose mechanism was assumed to apply to common late-onset disease; people with [[Down syndrome]] who survive to middle age are at high risk of it. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- Promoters of the familial genes APP, PSEN1 and PSEN2 were hypoacetylated in hippocampal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load, suggesting shared oligodendrocyte regulation with late-onset risk genes. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Genetic overlap with blood lipids: five GWAS loci ([[APOE]], [[TREM2]], [[MS4A4E]], [[LILRA5]], [[LRRC25]]) are also significant for a lipid trait, and local genetic covariance with total, LDL and non-HDL cholesterol identified 5q13.3 ([[ANKDD1B]]), 10q26.13 ([[CUZD1]]) and 11q12.1-q12.2 ([[MS4A6A]]) as candidate loci. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
- The early-onset [[APP-PS1 mouse]] transcriptome clustered apart from five late-onset risk-gene mice, though some neuroimmune change was common; the authors question whether early- and late-onset cases share mechanisms. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- A screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish patients with onset before 65 or family history found pathogenic [[PSEN1 p.P264L]], [[PSEN1 p.M146V]] and [[APP p.690_695del]]. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
