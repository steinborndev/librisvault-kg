# MDH1

MDH1 encodes cytosolic malate dehydrogenase, an enzyme of the malate-aspartate shuttle that links glycolysis to the TCA cycle.

## Connections

- Up in CSF of symptomatic GRN mutation carriers and in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia medium, but down in Grn knockout mouse microglia and CSF. In an Alzheimer cohort it rose in mild cognitive impairment and Alzheimer disease, separated amyloid-positive from amyloid-negative groups in both (the only panel protein to do so within Alzheimer disease), and gave an AUC of 0.77 for amyloid-positive Alzheimer disease versus amyloid-negative controls. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related panel members: [[FABP3]], [[GDI1]], [[CAPG]], [[CD44]], [[GPNMB]]

- Together with [[ALDOA]], [[LDHA]] and [[PGAM1]] it was raised in CSF in Alzheimer disease and amyloid-positive mild cognitive impairment across several cohorts, unchanged in dementia with Lewy bodies and frontotemporal dementia. Study: [[CSF proteomics of Alzheimer, Lewy body and frontotemporal dementia]]
- Its transcript was lower in Alzheimer brain and peripheral blood cells and correlated with amyloid-beta, Tau or MMSE, a candidate blood biomarker; see [[Tricarboxylic acid cycle]]. Study: [[TCA cycle enzyme genes in Alzheimer brain and blood transcriptomes]]
