# ZDHHC21

ZDHHC21 (chromosome 9) encodes a DHHC-family palmitoyl acyltransferase that attaches palmitate to cysteines of substrates such as [[FYN]] and [[APP]] ([[Protein palmitoylation]]).

## Connections

- The novel heterozygous missense variant [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]] was found in a Han Chinese family with [[Familial Alzheimer disease]]; in knock-in mice it raised palmitoylation of FYN and APP without changing ZDHHC21 mRNA or protein level. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Substrates: [[FYN]], [[APP]]; downstream pathway: [[GRIN2B]]
