# INPP5D rs10933431

An intronic SNP in [[INPP5D]] associated with lower risk of late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] in genome-wide meta-analysis.

## Connections

- Gene: [[INPP5D]]
- Carriers had lower plasma [[IDUA]] in healthy adults, an association not previously reported. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Overlaps an H3K27ac peak active only in microglia, not in neurons or oligodendrocyte-enriched glia, pointing to myeloid regulation at INPP5D. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
