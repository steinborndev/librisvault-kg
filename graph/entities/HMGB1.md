# HMGB1

HMGB1 encodes a nuclear protein that binds DNA and helps gene transcription; released outside the cell it acts as an alarmin and pro-inflammatory cytokine that signals through [[TLR2]] and [[TLR4]].

## Connections

- Its mRNA rose 1.6-fold in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture after amyloid-beta 1-42, together with [[TLR2]], [[TLR4]] and [[IL18]], but not in microglia kept 16 days, where baseline levels were already low. The authors recall that HMGB1 drives neurite degeneration in Alzheimer disease. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
