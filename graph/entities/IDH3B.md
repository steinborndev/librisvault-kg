# IDH3B

IDH3B encodes the beta subunit of NAD-dependent isocitrate dehydrogenase 3, a TCA cycle enzyme that converts isocitrate to 2-oxoglutarate.

## Connections

- One of the TCA cycle genes whose expression correlated significantly with amyloid-beta, Tau or MMSE in Alzheimer brain. Study: [[TCA cycle enzyme genes in Alzheimer brain and blood transcriptomes]]
