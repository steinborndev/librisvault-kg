# NADPH

NADPH is the reduced form of nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate, the main electron donor for biosynthesis and for regenerating antioxidants such as glutathione.

## Connections

- Regenerated in mitochondria by [[NNT]]; loss of that supply in an Alzheimer mouse model pushed neurons into a pro-oxidant state. Study: [[NNT deficiency impairs synapses and cognition in Alzheimer mouse models]]
- Process: [[Oxidative stress]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
