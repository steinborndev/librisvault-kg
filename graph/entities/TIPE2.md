# TIPE2

TIPE2 (formerly TNFAIP8L2) encodes a TNF-alpha-induced protein that restrains inflammatory signalling.

## Connections

- Higher in the caudate nucleus of [[Alzheimer disease]] alone, not of [[Parkinson disease]], among TNF-related genes tested. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
