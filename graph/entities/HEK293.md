# HEK293

HEK293 is a human embryonic kidney cell line widely used for transfection. A line stably expressing Swedish-mutant [[APP]] (293sw) secretes large amounts of amyloid beta and serves as a screening model for compounds that lower it.

## Connections

- 293sw cells were the first-line screen for phloroglucinol derivatives and the main mechanistic model for [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]]; wild-type HEK293, which make little Aβ, served for clearance and degradation assays with added Aβ1-40.
- Study: [[DAPG lowers beta-amyloid secretion in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Also used as a second cell background for [[TREM2]] promoter deletion reporters, where it reproduced the activity pattern seen in [[BV2]]. Study: [[TREM2 regulation by YY1 in microglia and Alzheimer disease models]]
- Host cells for the LXR and PPAR luciferase reporter assay of brown seaweed lipid extracts, where all extracts activated liver X receptors. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]; method: [[Dual luciferase nuclear receptor reporter assay]]
- Host cells (written HEK293T in the legends) for dual-luciferase reporters of circ-Epc1 and the [[TREM2]] 3' untranslated region with the [[MIR770]] mimic. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]; see [[miRNA binding]]
- Host for alkaline-phosphatase-tagged [[NRG1]] and [[SELPLG]] cleavage assays with [[BACE1]], and (as HEK293T) for GFP-tagged full-length APP, where FAH65E(-) at 1 μM left more APP on the cells. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Flp-In HEK293 lines stably expressing mouse TREM2 with its adaptor [[TYROBP]] were the screening system for antibodies that raise membrane TREM2 and lower soluble TREM2 (with [[GM6001]] as control), and for phospho-[[SYK]] read-outs of [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]]. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- Host cells carrying Swedish-mutant APP, transfected with wild-type or mutant [[PSEN2]] to read Aβ40 and Aβ42 in the medium by ELISA. Study: [[PSEN2 mutation spectrum and functionally validated variants in a Chinese Alzheimer cohort]]; method: [[Functional validation of presenilin variants in transfected cells]]
- The Tet-On ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed 293 line was used to screen [[Licochalcone A]] and five derivatives for lowering Tau misfolding, ROS and caspase 3 activity. See [[TauRD-DsRed cell model]]. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Host cells (293FT for 3' untranslated region cloning) for dual-luciferase reporters of the [[APOE]], [[PSEN1]] and [[CDK5]] 3' untranslated regions with the [[MIR650]] duplex and a miR-572 negative control; see [[miRNA binding]]. Study: [[MicroRNA-650 targets CDK5 and lessens amyloid pathology in APP-PSEN1 mice]]
- Used to repeat ULK1 and Rab9 knockdowns (mt-Keima and western blot) and to count EGFP-LC3 puncta, which did not rise with [[ALT001]]. See [[Alternative mitophagy]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
