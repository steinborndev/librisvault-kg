# BNIP3

BNIP3 encodes a BCL2-family protein of the outer mitochondrial membrane that takes part in cell death and in the removal of damaged mitochondria.

## Connections

- Reported upregulated in Alzheimer brain across several microarray datasets; one of the five signature genes of the study. Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Full text: localised to the cytosol; docking affinity for [[Antrocin]] was the lowest of the five (-5.80 kcal/mol) with no hydrogen bonds. Study: [[Multiomic analysis of Alzheimer brain finds five upregulated genes and antrocin]]
