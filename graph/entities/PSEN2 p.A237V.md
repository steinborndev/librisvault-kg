# PSEN2 p.A237V

A missense change from alanine to valine at residue 237 of [[PSEN2]], in the fifth transmembrane helix. It was seen in an Italian patient with [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]] and no family history, with neuropathology consistent with Alzheimer disease. Structure prediction showed little disturbance of the helix.
