# NOTCH3 p.A284T

A missense change from alanine to threonine at residue 284 of [[NOTCH3]], in the EGF-like calcium-binding repeats between EGF10 and EGF11 (exon 6), close to the [[JAG1]] binding site.

## Connections

- Gene: [[NOTCH3]]; comparator [[NOTCH3 p.R332C]] in the same exon
- Present in 10 Alzheimer cases and no controls of the Alzheimer's Disease Sequencing Project (allele frequency 0.0005 in Europeans), confirmed by Sanger sequencing in 8, with a shared 5-SNP haplotype suggesting a distant common ancestor; 8 of 10 carriers clustered with Ashkenazi-type mitochondrial haplogroups. Whole-genome data added one case and one person with [[Mild cognitive impairment]], no controls. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Modelling predicts new hydrogen bonds that strengthen ligand contact; carriers had Alzheimer, not [[CADASIL]], features. See [[Alzheimer disease]]
