# INPP5D

INPP5D encodes SHIP1, an inositol polyphosphate 5-phosphatase that restrains PI3K signalling in myeloid cells, including microglia and macrophages. It lies in a known Alzheimer GWAS region.

## Connections

- Higher genetically predicted expression went with lower Alzheimer risk, the largest protective estimate of the seven (0.844), with expression confined largely to macrophages. Microglial receptor partner: [[TREM2]]. Study: [[Single-nucleus sequencing and Mendelian randomization of Alzheimer risk genes]]
- Method: [[Mendelian randomization]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Nominally upregulated in both blood and brain of Alzheimer cases in ROSMAP with the same direction of effect; in blood it was most expressed in dendritic cells and monocytes, and in brain it was the only gene of its group expressed, in microglia. It belongs to the light green co-expression network (Fc gamma R-mediated phagocytosis and B cell receptor signaling pathways). Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
- Its mouse form, Ship1, was a target rescued by sponging [[MIR155]] and [[MIR802]] in the hippocampus of the [[Ts65Dn mouse]]. Study: [[miR-155 and miR-802 targets in the hippocampus of Ts65Dn Down syndrome mice]]
- The protective intronic SNP [[INPP5D rs10933431]] was associated with lower plasma [[IDUA]] in healthy adults; INPP5D encodes the lipid phosphatase SHIP1, a negative regulator of immune signalling expressed in microglia. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- The sentinel SNP [[INPP5D rs10933431]] overlaps a peak acetylated only in microglia (log2 fold change 1.61 versus -0.75 in neurons and -0.86 in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia). Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
