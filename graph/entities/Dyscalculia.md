# Dyscalculia

Dyscalculia is a loss or impairment of the ability to calculate. It appears among the early cognitive signs of some Alzheimer patients.

## Connections

- The woman with [[PSEN2 p.M239T]] had difficulty with visual and auditory calculation, and a man with the stop-gain variant [[PSEN2 p.G117X]] became unable to calculate; dyscalculia was also among the initial symptoms of some carriers of uncertain-significance variants.
