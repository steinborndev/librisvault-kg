# Neuro2a

Neuro2a (N2A) is a mouse neuroblastoma cell line, easy to transfect and used as a neuron-like model; it expresses endogenous [[TREM2]].

## Connections

- [[YY1]] shRNA lowered the TREM2 reporter and, by more than 70%, both YY1 and TREM2 protein, a stronger knock-down than in [[BV2]]. Study: [[TREM2 regulation by YY1 in microglia and Alzheimer disease models]]
- Used as the neuron-like cell in a [[bEnd.3]] Transwell barrier model: [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]] took up better than the non-targeted carrier, lowered [[BACE1]] mRNA and protein more than free siRNA or the liposome reagent, and uptake was reduced by [[Chlorpromazine]] and [[Genistein]]. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Treated with [[D-ribose]], [[Berberine]], [[Mdivi-1]] and PINK1 siRNA to test mitophagy, membrane potential, reactive oxygen species and apoptosis; D-ribose lowered PINK1, Parkin and LC3B and raised [[DNMT1]]. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
