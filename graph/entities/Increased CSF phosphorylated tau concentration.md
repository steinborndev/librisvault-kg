# Increased CSF phosphorylated tau concentration

A raised level of phosphorylated tau in cerebrospinal fluid, which follows tau pathology in the brain and is a core laboratory marker of [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Genetic determinants: [[APOE]] ε4 ([[APOE rs429358]]) raises it and ε2 ([[APOE rs7412]]) lowers it; [[GMNC]], [[C16orf95]] and [[BIN1]] were further loci. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Companion marker: [[Decreased amyloid beta 42 peptide CSF concentration]]
- Related protein: [[MAPT]]
- CSF phospho-tau 181 (immunoassay) and the mass spectrometry pT217/T217 ratio were higher in transmembrane than cytoplasmic PSEN1 carriers, while pT181, pT202 and pT205 ratios were not. Study: [[PSEN1 transmembrane versus cytoplasmic variants and progression in DIAN]]
- Higher CSF p-tau was associated with lower blood [[BIN1]] promoter methylation (p = 0.0028) only in the [[Subjective cognitive decline]] subgroup, independent of Aβ42. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
