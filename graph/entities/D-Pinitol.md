# D-Pinitol

D-Pinitol is 3-O-methyl-D-chiro-inositol, a methylated inositol found in carob pods and some legumes, taken as an insulin sensitiser. In an earlier Wistar rat study it was reported to lower tau phosphorylation by reducing CDK5 activity and p35, not through GSK-3β.

## Connections

- Given at 200 mg/kg/day in drinking water for 18 weeks to [[5xFAD mouse]] animals it lowered hippocampal Aβ plaques, raised [[IDE]], restored insulin and glucagon, activated hippocampal PI3K and Akt signalling, lowered AT8 tau phosphorylation and the p35 to [[CDK5]] ratio, lowered plasma [[Lipopolysaccharide]] and intestinal [[TLR4]], and shifted the [[Gut microbiota]].
