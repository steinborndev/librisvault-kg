# PRNP p.E219K

A glutamate to lysine change at residue 219 of [[PRNP]], documented in studies of [[Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[PRNP]]; disease: [[Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease]]
- Found with [[PRNP p.M129V]] in Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients; the authors ask whether such common variants are benign. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
