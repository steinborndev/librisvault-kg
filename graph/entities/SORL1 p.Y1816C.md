# SORL1 p.Y1816C

A missense change from tyrosine to cysteine at residue 1816 of [[SORL1]], in the 'tyrosine corner' of the third 3Fn domain, which stabilises dimerisation.

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- Six unrelated cases (mean age at onset 60.2 years) and affected members of three pedigrees; the authors cite functional work indicating it matures normally but fails to form the dimer-dependent complex with retromer, consistent with haploinsufficiency, and a carrier had reduced soluble SORL1.
