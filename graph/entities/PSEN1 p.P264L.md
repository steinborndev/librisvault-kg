# PSEN1 p.P264L

A missense change of [[PSEN1]] (proline 264 to leucine) that causes autosomal dominant [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. Reported earlier with onset around 45 years and abundant cotton wool plaques at autopsy. In a Swedish memory-clinic screen it was found in two siblings (onset 50 and 42 years, both APOE ε3/ε4) from a family where the father and another sibling were also affected; they had global cognitive impairment, early personality change and posterior temporo-parietal hypometabolism on FDG-PET. Population frequency 0.0000040, SIFT 0, PolyPhen-2 1.
