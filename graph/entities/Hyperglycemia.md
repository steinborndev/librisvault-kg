# Hyperglycemia

Hyperglycemia is an abnormally high concentration of glucose in the blood, a hallmark of diabetes and a proposed accelerator of Alzheimer disease progression.

## Connections

- Induced in 6-month-old [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals with [[Streptozocin]] to accelerate Aβ, Tau and memory phenotypes; [[LM-031]] partly lowered it. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
