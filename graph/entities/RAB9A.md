# RAB9A

RAB9A encodes a small GTPase of late endosome to Golgi transport; Rab9-decorated, Golgi-derived autophagosomes engulf mitochondria in [[Alternative mitophagy]].

## Connections

- [[ALT001]] increased YFP-Rab9 puncta and Rab9 rings around mitochondria in human cells; Rab9 knockdown abolished ALT001-induced mitophagy and its rescue of APP-mutant mitochondrial dysfunction. In mouse hippocampus, AAV Rab9 shRNA (mouse Rab9) blunted ALT001-induced mitophagy and abolished its rescue of long-term potentiation in 5×FAD mice, without changing baseline mitophagy. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
