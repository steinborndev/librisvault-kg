# SORL1 p.Y391C

A missense change from tyrosine to cysteine at residue 391, gaining a cysteine in loop L1 of the VPS10p domain of [[SORL1]]. It is a high-priority variant in the domain-mapping scheme.

## Connections

- Gene: [[SORL1]]
- Seen in 12 unrelated cases and no controls, with a median age at onset of 67.5 years (range 60-86), later than truncating variants; the authors speculate it impairs ligand binding and lysosomal delivery of amyloid beta. Study: [[Domain mapping of SORL1 missense variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Also found in 5 Alzheimer cases and no controls among 10,211 exome-sequenced European-ancestry participants. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
