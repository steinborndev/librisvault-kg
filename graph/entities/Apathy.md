# Apathy

A loss of motivation and goal-directed behaviour, one of the commonest neuropsychiatric symptoms of Alzheimer disease and the "decreased motivation" domain of [[Mild behavioral impairment]].

## Connections

- Higher in patients with amnestic [[Mild cognitive impairment]] due to Alzheimer disease than in cognitively normal controls (decreased motivation domain, B = 0.88, p < 0.001), together with affective dysregulation and impulse dyscontrol. Study: [[Mild behavioral impairment with APOE and BDNF in early Alzheimer disease]]
- Related symptoms: [[Depression]], [[Anxiety]]; concept: [[Neuropsychiatric symptoms of Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
