# CETP rs5882

A [[CETP]] SNP whose G allele goes with lower serum cholesteryl ester transfer protein levels; with rs708272 it forms the haplotypes analysed for statin response.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CETP]]
- GG carriers had lower LDL cholesterol on atorvastatin than AA carriers, and lower triglycerides on atorvastatin than simvastatin. Study: [[Statin lipid response and cholesterol gene variants in Alzheimer disease]]
- Partner SNP: [[CETP rs708272]]; drug: [[Atorvastatin]]
