# SSH2

SSH2 encodes slingshot protein phosphatase 2, which dephosphorylates and activates cofilin and so controls actin filament turnover.

## Connections

- Higher genetically predicted expression went with higher Alzheimer risk (estimate 1.049); expression was highest in macrophages with moderate signal in glutamatergic neurons. The paper notes an earlier blood-methylation study that also linked SSH2 to Alzheimer risk. Study: [[Single-nucleus sequencing and Mendelian randomization of Alzheimer risk genes]]
