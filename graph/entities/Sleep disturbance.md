# Sleep disturbance

Sleep disturbance is any abnormality of the amount, timing or quality of sleep. It is common in [[Alzheimer disease]] (night waking, nocturnal wandering) and in [[Down syndrome]] (including sleep apnoea).

## Connections

- Fly clock neurons overexpressing human tau, secreted [[Amyloid-beta]] 42 or the [[DYRK1A]] orthologue minibrain slept less in total, by day and by night; [[PST-001]] restored sleep in tau flies (night only) and minibrain flies (day and night) but not in Aβ42 flies. Study: [[PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome]]
- The paper's discussion links sleep to disease course, since interstitial and cerebrospinal Aβ42 and tau levels rise by day and fall during sleep, and sleep deprivation increases tau seeding and spread (cited from earlier work).
