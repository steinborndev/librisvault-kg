# NKPD1

NKPD1 lies next to [[APOE]] on chromosome 19. An expression quantitative trait locus for NKPD1 in a subset of helper T cells colocalized with the APOE signal for CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau; the authors judge this likely a false positive because of the known coding mechanism of APOE.
