# NYAP1 rs12539172

A SNP in the 3' region of [[NYAP1]] on chromosome 7 whose minor allele is protective against late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] (odds ratio 0.92) and is in linkage disequilibrium (r2 = 0.77) with the [[PILRA]] missense variant [[PILRA p.G78R]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[NYAP1]]
- The minor allele lowered plasma [[PILRB]] and [[PILRA]] in a roughly dose-dependent way (FDR p = 2.2 x 10^-33 and 2.3 x 10^-17), the most significant association in the study. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
