# TAGLN2

TAGLN2 encodes transgelin-2, an actin-binding protein found on neuronal membranes that is reviewed as a neuronal receptor for soluble TREM2.

## Connections

- In cultured mouse neurons soluble [[TREM2]] bound transgelin-2 and induced phosphorylation of [[RHOA]] at Ser188, inactivating the RhoA-ROCK axis, which inhibited [[GSK3B]] and tau phosphorylation. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
