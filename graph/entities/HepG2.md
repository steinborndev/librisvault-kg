# HepG2

HepG2 is a human liver cancer cell line (described in the study as hepatocytoma).

## Connections

- Used to detect TOMM40 pseudogene RNAs and, under hydrogen peroxide stress, to relate [[TOMM40]] RNA to mitochondrial DNA copy number and membrane potential; see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
- Related cell lines in the same study: [[U-87 MG]], [[U-118 MG]], [[LN-229]], [[SH-SY5Y]]
- Stress agent: [[Hydrogen peroxide]]
- Hepatocyte-like model in which the APOE exon 4 CpG island loops to the APOC1 promoter; contact strength and APOC1 mRNA were compared across four lines. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
