# Bafilomycin A1

Bafilomycin A1 inhibits the vacuolar H+-ATPase, blocking acidification of endo-lysosomes and autophagosome-lysosome fusion.

## Connections

- At 100 nM for 5 h it shortened the time to loss of membrane integrity after [[4,4'-Dithiodipyridine]] in cultured mouse cortical neurons, in all three experiments. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Related: [[Chloroquine]], [[LLOMe]], [[Lysosome]], [[Autophagy]]
- At 100 nM it abolished the rescue by [[ALT001]] of mitochondrial ROS and ATP in APP Swe/Ind [[SH-SY5Y]] cells, showing the benefit needs autophagosome-lysosome fusion. It was also co-applied with ALT001 in [[BEAS-2B]] cells for the electron microscopy count of autophagosomes containing mitochondria. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
