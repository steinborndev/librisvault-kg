# GCG

GCG encodes the precursor of glucagon, the hormone that opposes insulin by raising hepatic glucose output; a low insulin to glucagon ratio favours gluconeogenesis.

## Connections

- Plasma glucagon was higher in untreated 5xFAD mice, giving a lower insulin to glucagon ratio; [[D-Pinitol]] left treated 5xFAD mice no different from wild type. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
