# HLA-DQA2

HLA-DQA2 encodes an alpha chain of the HLA-DQ class II antigen-presenting molecule family.

- rs6931277 was a genotype-level cis-eQTL of HLA-DQA2 in microglia, with a stronger p value after adding microglia-specific samples, and was also associated with its allelic expression in microglia. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
