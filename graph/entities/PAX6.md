# PAX6

PAX6 encodes a paired-box transcription factor that is the master regulator of eye development and retinal patterning; mutations cause aniridia.

## Connections

- A neuronal regulator differentially active between Alzheimer and control prefrontal cortex in Brodmann area 10, followed up partly because of the eye changes described in Alzheimer disease; its downstream target NFIB was co-expressed with it in controls and early disease but not late disease. It was one of five regulators docked and simulated. Study: [[Lasmiditan prioritised for Alzheimer disease by regulator docking and dynamics]]
