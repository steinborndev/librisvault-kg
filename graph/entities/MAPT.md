# MAPT

MAPT encodes the microtubule-associated protein tau, whose hyperphosphorylated aggregates form [[Neurofibrillary tangle]] in [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Tau was a protein marker on the nanoparticle kit; its plasma level rose with disease severity in four patients, and it was stained in the brains of the mouse model but not of controls.
- A cleavage-specific antibody, [[12A12 monoclonal antibody]], neutralizes the toxic N-terminal 20-22 kDa tau fragment; in [[Tg2576 mouse]] mice this was followed by lower amyloid-pathway proteins and restored [[BIN1]], which binds the proline-rich region of tau.
- Studies: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]], [[Tau antibody 12A12 and amyloid-beta in Tg2576 mouse hippocampus and retina]]
- Phosphorylated Tau (AT8) rose in neurons challenged with Alzheimer postmortem synaptosomes or Tau oligomers and in PSEN1 mutant organoids; microglia carrying [[APOE p.R136S]] limited the rise through [[Phagocytosis]], and cytochalasin D abolished that protection. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- CSF phosphorylated tau and total tau were associated with enlarged perivascular spaces in the centrum semiovale of amyloid-positive, cognitively unimpaired adults, and stayed so after correcting for amyloid-beta 40; see [[Enlarged perivascular spaces]]. Study: [[Perivascular spaces and CSF biomarkers in cognitively unimpaired Alzheimer continuum]]
- MAPT expression, with that of [[CELF1]], was the only one of ten risk genes whose regional brain expression did not correlate significantly with cortical glucose metabolism. Study: [[Brain metabolism and Alzheimer risk gene expression in normal elderly]]
- Tau hyperphosphorylation at Ser202, Ser396, Ser404, Thr205 and Thr181 is described as the basis of tangles; [[GSK3B]] is the kinase whose lowering by [[Osthol]]-[[Zinc oxide]] nanoparticles is proposed to protect cells. Study: [[ZnO-osthol nanoparticles protect APP-overexpressing neuroblastoma cells]]
- Rat brain expresses 3R tau (barely detectable in mouse) but the 3R to 4R ratio is about 1:13, against 1:1 in human; knock-in rats up to 2 years old, even with an Aβ42-shifting Psen1 mutation, showed AT100 phosphorylation with age but no tangles. Study: [[Humanized APP knock-in mouse and rat with PSEN1 M139T for Alzheimer disease]]
- [[MAPT p.R741W]] occurred in 4 cases and no controls in a case-only exome screen. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Stress raised tau hyperphosphorylation and tangle formation in animals, and stress-driven [[CRH]] is said to promote it; see [[Psychological stress]] and [[Neurofibrillary tangle]]. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- Its risk variant sat in the same immune-labelled cluster as [[GRN]] and [[TREM2]] (lower CSF phosphorylated tau, no amyloid-beta 42 effect), grouped with genes tied to other dementias. Phenotype: [[Increased CSF phosphorylated tau concentration]]. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
- Phospho-tau (Thr231) relative to total tau fell with APOE2 only in the isogenic [[PSEN1 p.A246E]] hiPSC pair where the Aβ42/40 ratio also fell, and was unchanged by removing astrocytes, which the authors read as tau tracking the Aβ42/40 ratio and not total Aβ. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]
- [[MAPT p.P513A]], a rare variant first reported in a Chinese family with progressive non-fluent aphasia, was found in a Thai early-onset Alzheimer patient. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- Phospho-tau load (AT8 staining) correlated with the abundance of AD2 microglia, a tau-associated state marked by [[GRID2]], in occipitotemporal cortex of Alzheimer donors; the amyloid-linked AD1 state correlated with amyloid-beta load in tau-free tissue. See [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
- Reviewed as the microtubule-stabilising protein whose hyperphosphorylation, by kinases such as [[PRKACB]], [[FYN]], [[GSK3B]], [[CDK5]] and [[DYRK1A]] and weakened dephosphorylation by [[Protein phosphatase 2A]], frees it to form filaments and tangles; see [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]] and [[Tau propagation]]. The vaccine [[AADvac1]] targets pathological tau, and genetic ablation of MAPT reversed the cognitive harm of a high-salt diet in mice. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Tau was detergent-insoluble in demented but not control amygdala, and tau inclusion bodies partly colocalized with FTL and phosphorylated PRKDC; see [[FTL]] and [[PRKDC]]. Study: [[Detergent-insoluble proteins in amygdala of cognitively impaired elderly]]
- Tau was a tyrosine phosphorylation target in the in-lysate [[PTK2]] assay, and serine/threonine tau phosphosites (S396, S721, S739, T522) rose in FAK-overexpressing 3xTg-AD hippocampus (array signal, tau not stained). Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- The pro-aggregant [[MAPT p.K280del]] repeat domain is the basis of the [[TauRD-DsRed cell model]]; Tau immunoreactivity (area 197-202% of control) rose in streptozocin-treated [[3xTg-AD mouse]] hippocampus and cortex and [[LM-031]] cut it to 113-141%. See [[Tau aggregation]]. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Phosphorylation of tau at Thr181, Thr217, Thr231 and Ser396 was higher in hippocampus of mice carrying [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]]. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Expression per cell rose in most cell types of aged monkey entorhinal cortex without a change in the share of MAPT-positive cells, together with proteins that co-localise with [[Neurofibrillary tangle]]s. Study: [[Single-nucleus atlas of aging monkey entorhinal cortex and Alzheimer disease]]
- Dominant mutations cause [[Frontotemporal dementia]] with parkinsonism and tauopathy, not Alzheimer disease, which the author argues helped the field overlook tangles; the antibody [[Gosuranemab]] against extracellular tau fragments failed in Phase 2. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- Three distal peaks were hyperacetylated in prefrontal oligodendrocyte-enriched glia with high amyloid load (0.39 to 0.67), though oligodendrocyte transcript was nonsignificantly lower. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Human tau 0N4R overexpressed in fly neurons caused the largest eye loss (about 60%), 38% shorter life, poor climbing, sleep loss and weak memory; [[PST-001]] lowered its phosphorylation at S262 by over 50% (not S356 or T231, total tau unchanged) and extended life by 30%. See [[Drosophila models of Alzheimer disease and Down syndrome]]. Study: [[PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome]]
- Mouse Mapt (tau) mRNA was lower in gut than brain, in contrast to [[APP]], which the authors read as APP-derived Aβ rather than tau mattering most in the periphery. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- In 8-month-old 5xFAD hippocampus tau phosphorylated at Ser202/Thr205 (AT8) was not higher than in wild type, yet fell with [[D-Pinitol]]; phospho-Thr212/Ser214 (AT100) and total tau were unchanged. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- Two rare missense variants, [[MAPT p.V224G]] and [[MAPT p.A239T]], of unknown pathogenicity were found among 102 Swedish memory-clinic patients. Study: [[Targeted exome screen of eleven dementia genes in 102 Swedish memory clinic patients]]
