# JAG1

JAG1 encodes Jagged-1, a cell-surface ligand that binds Notch receptors, including [[NOTCH3]], and triggers [[Notch signaling]].

## Connections

- Homology modelling placed [[NOTCH3 p.A284T]] near the Jagged-1 binding site of the NOTCH3 EGF repeats and predicted stronger hydrogen bonding with a JAG1 arginine. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Its JAG1-Notch signalling, cleaved by [[BACE1]], is cited as shaping hippocampal neurogenesis and astrogenesis. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
- Pathway: [[Notch signaling]]
