# PSEN2 p.H169N

A missense change from histidine to asparagine at residue 169 of [[PSEN2]], in transmembrane domain III, predicted to twist the helix. It has been seen only in East Asian patients with [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] (one in Korea, four in China before the Korean case described below) and is more frequent in Korean population databases (about 0.4%) than in general ones.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PSEN2]]; pathway: [[Amyloid-beta formation]]
- The sixth East Asian carrier, a Korean woman who also carried [[APP p.V551M]], is described in [[Double APP and PSEN2 mutations in a Korean patient with early-onset Alzheimer disease]]; the authors suggest a founder mutation and recommend including it in East Asian screening panels.
- Same domain as other Alzheimer-linked PSEN2 changes (Met174Ile, Met174Val, Ser175Cys, Ser175Phe, Phe183Ser).
