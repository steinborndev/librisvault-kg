# C9

C9 encodes complement component 9, the last protein added to the [[Membrane attack complex]] and essential for terminal pathway activity.

## Connections

- Plasma C9 was higher in adults with [[Down syndrome]] than in controls (p < 0.05) and did not differ with APOE ε4 status. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
