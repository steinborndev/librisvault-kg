# FUCA1

FUCA1 encodes the lysosomal enzyme tissue alpha-L-fucosidase, which removes fucose from glycoproteins and glycolipids.

## Connections

- One of four proteins (with [[FUCA2]], [[HEXB]] and [[CAPG]]) raised in CSF of symptomatic GRN mutation carriers, in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia medium and in Grn knockout mouse microglia. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related: [[GRN]], [[Frontotemporal dementia]]
