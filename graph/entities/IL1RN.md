# IL1RN

IL1RN encodes the interleukin-1 receptor antagonist, a secreted protein that blocks the receptor for [[IL1B]] and so dampens IL-1 signalling.

## Connections

- The IL-1β / IL-1RN axis is part of the crosstalk between Cd33 and Trem2 in 5xFAD mouse microglia. Study: [[TREM2 acts downstream of CD33 in 5xFAD mouse microglia]]
- Partner: [[IL1B]]; receptors in play: [[CD33]], [[TREM2]]
