# Triglycerides

Triglycerides are the main storage and transport fat of the blood, measured as plasma triglyceride (TG) in lipid panels.

## Connections

- Shares the [[TREM2]] and [[LILRA5]] loci with early-onset Alzheimer disease in a direct GWAS comparison, but showed no significant local genetic covariance with it. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
- Related: [[Cholesterol]], [[Dyslipidemia]]
- About 30% of 382 plasma triglyceride species were higher and about 18% lower in Alzheimer cases (long-chain polyunsaturated ones mostly lower) though the class sum did not differ; a TG model classified cases at over 80% AUC, and oligomeric amyloid-beta raised TG in [[U251]] cells. Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
