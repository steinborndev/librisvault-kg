# C16orf95

C16orf95 is an uncharacterised open reading frame on chromosome 16q24.2. Intronic variants in it are associated with phosphorylated tau levels in cerebrospinal fluid and with [[Lateral ventricular volume]].

## Connections

- Lead variant: [[C16orf95 rs4843559]]
- Phenotypes: [[Increased CSF phosphorylated tau concentration]], [[Lateral ventricular volume]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Its associated CSF proteins were enriched for glycosaminoglycan metabolism and extracellular matrix organisation. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
