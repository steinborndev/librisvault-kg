# Vascular dementia

Vascular dementia is cognitive decline caused by cerebrovascular disease, and the most common companion of [[Alzheimer disease]] in mixed dementia.

## Connections

- A mouse carotid stenosis model of it is described in [[BCCAS mouse model]]. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
- Related: [[Small vessel ischemic disease]], [[KLK6]]
- Blood methylation at the CLU and APOE CpG island shores did not differ from controls in 40 patients. Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
