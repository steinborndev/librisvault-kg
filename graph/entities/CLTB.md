# CLTB

CLTB encodes clathrin light chain B, a regulatory subunit of the clathrin coat that forms vesicles at the plasma membrane and endosomes.

## Connections

- Lower in Alzheimer cortex in three expression datasets, with the highest expression in excitatory neurons; genetically predicted higher expression went with lower Alzheimer risk (odds ratio 0.934). Study: [[Four synaptic vesicle cycle genes protective in Alzheimer disease by MR]]
