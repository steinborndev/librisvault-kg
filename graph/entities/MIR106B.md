# MIR106B

MIR106B encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-106b, a small non-coding RNA that represses gene expression after transcription.

## Connections

- In plasma of four patients, miR-106b showed a clear expression pattern that tracked disease stage, and the authors propose it as an early diagnostic marker; it was also altered by amyloid beta oligomer in HT22 cells.
- Marker for [[Alzheimer disease]] on the nanoparticle kit; see [[Molecular beacon nanoparticle sensor]].
