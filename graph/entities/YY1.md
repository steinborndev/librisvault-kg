# YY1

YY1 (Yin Yang 1) is a ubiquitously expressed zinc-finger transcription factor that can activate or repress its targets depending on modifications, cofactors, chromatin context and the target gene. In Alzheimer disease brain it is reported to be lower, with more proteolytic fragments, and in neurons it may influence amyloid beta production.

## Connections

- Binds a conserved element (CCATCTG, -254 to -248) in the core promoter of [[TREM2]] and is needed for its expression in [[BV2]] and [[Neuro2a]] cells; YY1 protein is lower after [[Lipopolysaccharide]] and in the [[APP-PS45 mouse]]. Study: [[TREM2 regulation by YY1 in microglia and Alzheimer disease models]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Binds a putative recognition sequence in the [[BACE1]] promoter and stimulates its activity in rat [[PC12]] cells and primary neurones and astrocytes; YY1 and BACE1 are co-expressed in chronically activated rat astrocytes and in reactive astrocytes near plaques in Alzheimer brain, but rarely in neurones. Study: [[YY1 activates the BACE1 promoter in rat cells and reactive astrocytes]]
- Among three top candidate master regulators (with [[EP300]] and [[ZMYM3]]) inferred from neuronal transcriptional networks and shown to be biochemically and histopathologically dysregulated in Alzheimer brain tissue.
