# GM6001

GM6001 (ilomastat) is a broad-spectrum hydroxamate inhibitor of matrix metalloproteases and ADAM family proteases, used in cell culture to block shedding of membrane proteins such as [[TREM2]].

## Connections

- At 25 μM it raised membrane TREM2 and lowered soluble TREM2 in HEK293 cells expressing mouse TREM2, the benchmark that the antibody [[4D9 monoclonal antibody]] matched. The authors argue that such general ADAM inhibition would be counterproductive in Alzheimer disease because [[ADAM10]] also cuts [[APP]] in the non-amyloidogenic direction. Study: [[Dual function TREM2 stalk antibody 4D9 in mouse microglia and APP knock-in mice]]
- Mechanism: [[TREM2 ectodomain shedding]]
