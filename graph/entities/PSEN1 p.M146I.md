# PSEN1 p.M146I

A missense change of PSEN1 in [[PSEN1]] (methionine 146 to isoleucine), seen in familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. The most frequent mutation in the Taiwanese cohort: found in 9 of 16 index patients with a PSEN1 mutation, all unrelated by their own pedigrees, 6 of them with a modified Goldman score of 1. Met146 sits in a conserved hydrophobic putative transmembrane helix, and replacing it with another hydrophobic residue (isoleucine or leucine) is classed as pathogenic. Earlier reports are Danish, British and Swedish families with onset in the 30s to 40s. Clinical comparison with other PSEN1 mutations (onset age, education, myoclonus, seizures, extrapyramidal signs) showed no significant difference. Two large four-generation pedigrees are shown, onset in the 40s.
