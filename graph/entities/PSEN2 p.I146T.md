# PSEN2 p.I146T

A missense change from isoleucine to threonine at residue 146 of [[PSEN2]], in a transmembrane region; a novel variant of uncertain significance that was not tested in cells. It was not among the eight variants tested in transfected [[HEK293]] cells.
