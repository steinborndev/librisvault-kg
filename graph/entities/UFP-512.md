# UFP-512

UFP-512 is a potent, highly selective peptidomimetic agonist of the δ-opioid receptor ([[OPRD1]]).

## Connections

- At 5 μM it lowered [[BACE1]] activity and protein and cut APP cleavage in amyloid-injured [[PC12]] cells, and lowered Aβ42 in lysate of APPswe [[SH-SY5Y]] cells; [[Naltrindole]] reversed the effect, and it had little effect in uninjured cells. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
- Contrast with the less selective agonist [[DADLE]].
