# IL6

IL6 encodes interleukin-6, a cytokine that mediates inflammation and also supports neuronal growth and differentiation.

## Connections

- Target of [[MIR107]]; the pair was shared by both routes. IL6 is raised in Alzheimer brain tissue, and variants in its receptor gene appear in genome-wide studies.
- Variant: [[IL6 rs1800796]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- Brain mRNA fell in [[FAD4T mouse]] animals treated with [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Not induced in rat astrocytes by Aβ1-42 alone, induced by LPS, and induced (p = 0.003) when miR-146a-5p was blocked first. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Brain levels fell after ADSC exosomes, more after hypoxia-pretreated ones and most after circ-Epc1-containing ones, and circ-Epc1 lowered it in LPS-challenged [[BV2]] cells (ELISA). Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Cortical IL-6 rose with age in transgenic and wild-type mice alike, so it did not explain the transgenic fall in serotonin transporter density. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
- mRNA rose with amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture but not in those kept 16 days, whose baseline was about 10-fold lower. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- IL-6 signalling genes were higher in microglia of 5xFAD;CD33-/- mice and lower in 5xFAD;TREM2-/- mice (pathway named in the abstract, not the transcript). Study: [[TREM2 acts downstream of CD33 in 5xFAD mouse microglia]]
- Higher in activated mouse microglia lacking [[IL19]] and lowered by added IL-19, which acts as an autocrine brake on microglia. Study: [[Interleukin-19 as an autocrine brake on activated microglia]]
- mRNA in blood mononuclear cells was higher than in controls in both dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer disease, with no difference between the two (p = 0.785). Study: [[PBMC nicotinic receptor and cytokine expression in DLB and Alzheimer disease]]
- IL-6 was raised in [[TgCRND8 mouse]] brain and lowered by [[Magnolol]] at 20 and 40 mg/kg (p < 0.001) and by [[Donepezil]] (ELISA, n = 6 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Plasma IL-6 fell only in wild-type mice given [[D-Pinitol]], not in 5xFAD mice. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- IL-6 transcript and secreted protein rose after LPS in iPSC microglia-like cells, most strongly in lines carrying [[CHRFAM7A]], and [[Nicotine]] enhanced this in carriers but dampened it in non-carriers. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
- IL6 had the highest degree (29) in the interaction network of 34 targets shared by at least 5 heat-clearing herbs and Alzheimer disease, with the authors citing raised IL-6 in patients. Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
