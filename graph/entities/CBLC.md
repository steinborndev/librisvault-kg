# CBLC

CBLC encodes Cbl proto-oncogene C, an E3 ubiquitin ligase of the CBL family, and lies within the chromosome 19 region around [[APOE]].

## Connections

- Rare variants in the gene stayed associated with CSF amyloid-beta 1-42 after adjustment for APOE genotype, and were linked to temporal lobe (including entorhinal cortex) and frontal cortical clusters on whole-brain MRI analysis. Study: [[Rare variants near APOE and CSF and neuroimaging biomarkers in ADNI]]
- Phenotype: [[Decreased amyloid beta 42 peptide CSF concentration]]
- Neighbours in the same window: [[BCAM]], [[RELB]]
