# Postsynaptic density

The postsynaptic density is the protein-dense specialisation beneath the postsynaptic membrane of excitatory synapses, where scaffolds such as [[DLG4]] and the Shank proteins organise glutamate receptors and signalling molecules.

## Connections

- Glutamate receptor depletion (GluR2 down to 40%) and altered Shank2 and Shank3 levels at this structure were reported in Alzheimer brain, as a possible basis of its destruction. Study: [[Shank proteins and glutamate receptors at the Alzheimer postsynaptic density]]
- Components: [[DLG4]], [[SHANK2]], [[SHANK3]], [[GRIN1]], [[GRIA2]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; phenotype: [[Synapse loss]]
