# A2M

A2M encodes alpha-2-macroglobulin, a plasma protease inhibitor that binds and carries amyloid beta for clearance.

## Connections

- Mouse A2m was the one amyloid-degradation transcript raised in prefrontal cortex, hippocampus and striatum at both 2 and 7 days after carotid stenosis; see [[BCCAS mouse model]]. Study: [[Alzheimer disease genes in small vessel ischemic disease and mouse hypoperfusion]]
