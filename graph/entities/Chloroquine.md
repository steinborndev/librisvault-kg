# Chloroquine

Chloroquine is a weak base that accumulates in acidic compartments, raises endo-lysosomal pH and impairs autophagosome-lysosome fusion; it is used experimentally to disturb the endo-lysosomal system.

## Connections

- At 100 μM for 5 h it made cultured mouse cortical neurons turn propidium iodide positive faster after the oxidant [[4,4'-Dithiodipyridine]], placing endo-lysosomal disturbance upstream of neuronal vulnerability. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Related: [[Bafilomycin A1]], [[LLOMe]], [[Lysosome]], [[Autophagy]]
- At the autophagic-flux level it raised p62 and LC3-II and enhanced RIPK1 and MLKL activation and necrosis after TNF-α or TSZ in [[SH-SY5Y]] cells and primary neurons, supporting a role for impaired flux in [[Necroptosis]]. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
