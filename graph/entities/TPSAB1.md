# TPSAB1

TPSAB1 encodes tryptase alpha/beta 1, the main protease stored in mast cell granules and released on degranulation.

## Connections

- Acts on nerve endings through PAR-2 to raise substance P and CGRP release, correlates with severe pain when high, and lowered occludin and claudin-5 in cultured brain endothelial cells. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
