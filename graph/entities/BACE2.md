# BACE2

BACE2 encodes beta-secretase 2, an aspartyl protease closely related to [[BACE1]] that serves as a counter-screen for BACE1 inhibitors and, unlike BACE1, can limit amyloid-beta by cleaving it or APP within the Aβ domain.

## Connections

- [[FAH65]] did not inhibit it (IC50 above 1 μM) while inhibiting BACE1 at about 0.01 μM, whereas BACE-IV did. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Beyond its weak β-site cleavage of [[APP]], it cuts near the α site and at a θ-secretase site inside the Aβ domain, and can degrade [[Amyloid-beta]] (reported efficiency second only to [[IDE]]). Common SNPs across the locus ([[BACE2 rs8134992]], [[BACE2 rs2898441]], [[BACE2 rs9978431]]) were nominally associated with Alzheimer risk only in [[APOE]] ε4 non-carriers, [[BACE2 rs2837994]] and [[BACE2 rs9980146]] with CSF Aβ1-42 in ADNI, and [[BACE2 rs2012050]] with cortex BACE2 expression; overexpressing BACE2 in [[BE2-M17]] cells lowered secreted Aβ1-40 and Aβ1-42.
