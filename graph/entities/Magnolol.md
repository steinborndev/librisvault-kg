# Magnolol

A neolignan polyphenol and the main active component of the bark of Magnolia officinalis (about 1.0 to 1.25% of the bark), used in Chinese medicine for inflammatory conditions; reported to cross the blood-brain barrier after oral dosing.

## Connections

- Given by gavage at 20 and 40 mg/kg daily for four months to male [[TgCRND8 mouse]] animals, it improved recognition and radial-arm-maze performance, lowered brain Aβ40 and Aβ42 and inflammatory cytokines, and restored synaptic proteins, with the higher dose generally stronger and compared with [[Donepezil]]. Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Earlier work cited in that study: protection of cells against [[Amyloid-beta]] toxicity, prevention of scopolamine-induced deficits through acetylcholinesterase inhibition, and preserved cholinergic function in [[SAMP8 mouse]] animals.
- Targets implicated: [[RELA]] and [[NF-kappaB signaling]], [[AKT1]], [[GSK3B]]; disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
