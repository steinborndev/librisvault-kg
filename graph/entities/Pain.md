# Pain

Pain is an unpleasant sensory and emotional experience tied to actual or potential tissue damage. In neurodegenerative disease it is common, mixed in origin (nociceptive, neuropathic or both) and often under-treated.

## Connections

- A review quotes 38 to 75% of people with [[Alzheimer disease]] and 40 to 86% of people with [[Parkinson disease]] as having painful symptoms, and about 45 to 83% of nursing-home residents with dementia; [[Mast cell activation]] mediators ([[Histamine]], [[TPSAB1]] tryptase, [[NGF]]) act on nociceptors.
