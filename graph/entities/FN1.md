# FN1

FN1 encodes fibronectin, an extracellular matrix and plasma glycoprotein involved in cell adhesion.

## Connections

- It was one of only five proteins significant in the Alzheimer versus control comparison of the Mental Health Research Center cohort alone, and one of six proteins near q of about 0.20 for control versus mild cognitive impairment. It was stably selected for the classifier and is in the universal panel, and replicated in three or more earlier cohorts.
