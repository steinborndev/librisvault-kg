# CCL7

CCL7 encodes C-C motif chemokine ligand 7 (monocyte chemotactic protein 3), a secreted chemokine that attracts monocytes.

## Connections

- Plasma level was lowered, dose-dependently, in carriers of the [[APOC1]] gamma risk haplotype; see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].
