# SAMP8 mouse

SAMP8 is the senescence-accelerated mouse prone-8 strain, used as a model of accelerated brain ageing and of features of [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Hippocampal and cortical gene expression after five traditional Chinese medicine decoctions was profiled by microarray in this strain. Study: [[Common nodes of five Chinese medicine decoctions in SAMP8 mouse brain subnetworks]]
- Related models: [[APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse]], [[3xTg-AD mouse]]; see also [[Traditional Chinese medicine decoctions]]
- Earlier work cited for [[Magnolol]]: it preserved cholinergic function and learning and memory in SAMP8 mice, and modulated Akt activation there. Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
