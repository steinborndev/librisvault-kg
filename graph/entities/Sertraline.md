# Sertraline

A selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor antidepressant. It is a major substrate of CYP2D6 and CYP2C19, a moderate inhibitor of CYP2C19, CYP2D6 and CYP3A4, and is linked to 31 pharmacogenes.

## Connections

- Given at 50 mg a day as one of three SSRIs to Alzheimer disease patients with moderate to severe depression; the authors also cite meta-analyses that found antidepressants, sertraline among them, mostly ineffective against placebo for depression in Alzheimer disease.
