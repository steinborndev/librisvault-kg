# TKT

TKT encodes transketolase, a thiamine-dependent enzyme of the pentose phosphate pathway.

## Connections

- Differentially expressed in excitatory neurons between early and late Alzheimer pathology, and a target of drugs in Alzheimer clinical trials, as are [[APP]] and [[GABRA1]]. Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
