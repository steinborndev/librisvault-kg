# Familial Alzheimer disease

Familial Alzheimer disease is [[Alzheimer disease]] that runs in families. Its autosomal dominant form (ADAD) is caused by mutations in [[PSEN1]], [[PSEN2]] or [[APP]], usually with onset before 65 years, and is the genetic core of [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Genes: [[PSEN1]] (most often mutated, youngest onset), [[APP]], [[PSEN2]]
- Related disease pages: [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]], [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: in a referral series of 77 Taiwanese patients, 17 (22%) carried a PSEN1 or APP mutation and no one carried a PSEN2 mutation; [[PSEN1 p.M146I]] was the commonest. See [[Familial Alzheimer disease mutations in Han Chinese in Taiwan]]
- Korean digenic case: [[Double APP and PSEN2 mutations in a Korean patient with early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Founder kindred: [[PSEN1 p.E280A]]
- Neural cultures from hiPSC lines with [[APP p.V717I]], an APP duplication or [[PSEN1 p.A246E]] showed raised Aβ that APOE2 editing lowered, giving a human model of the protective ε2 allele. Study: [[APOE2 lowers amyloid-beta in isogenic hiPSC models of familial Alzheimer disease]]
- Rare (about 1% of patients); Aβ-triggered synaptic mechanisms (endosomal Aβ, dynamin-1 depletion, AMPA receptor endocytosis) are reviewed from eFAD models. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- In the APPswe/PS1dE9 familial model, serotonin transporter density and uptake fell in aged cortex after neuroinflammation began. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
- Compared with the British and Danish familial dementias caused by [[ITM2B]] mutations, which resemble it in pathology. Study: [[BRI2 in familial British and Danish dementia and Alzheimer disease review]]
- In 702 Chinese patients, 4.0% carried rare variants in APP (7), PSEN1 (12) or PSEN2 (9); three PSEN2 variants ([[PSEN2 p.N141S]], [[PSEN2 p.M239T]], [[PSEN2 p.I368F]]) were functionally validated. Study: [[PSEN2 mutation spectrum and functionally validated variants in a Chinese Alzheimer cohort]]
- Cases with APP Val717 mutations ([[APP p.V717I]], [[APP p.V717G]]) showed [[TMCC2]] in plaque cores but little TMCC2-APP co-staining in dystrophic neurites, unlike late-onset disease. Study: [[TMCC2 in dense-core plaques and Down syndrome pathology in Alzheimer brain]]
- A late-onset autosomal dominant Italian family with no APP, PSEN1 or PSEN2 mutation (exome and NeuroX array negative) segregated the rare missense variant [[GRIN2C p.A1072V]]. Study: [[Exome sequencing finds GRIN2C p.A1072V in an Italian late-onset Alzheimer family]]
- Best evidence that white matter abnormalities precede plaques and tangles comes from carriers, because age at onset is fairly consistent. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- A Han Chinese pedigree with no APP, PSEN1 or PSEN2 mutation segregated the novel [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]]; knock-in mice supported a palmitoylation-based mechanism. The Chinese network it came from found PSEN/APP missense changes in 13.12% of 404 pedigrees ([[Chinese Familial Alzheimer's Disease Network]]). Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- In cultured wild-type mouse cortical neurons, about 20% (those with the lowest gamma-secretase activity) showed familial-type phenotypes without any mutation: a higher Aβ42/38 ratio, enlarged endo-lysosomes and quicker death after glutamate or oxidant; the authors propose this as a link between familial and sporadic disease. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
- Its mutations in APP, PSEN1 and PSEN2 all raise production of amyloidogenic Aβ, which is the genetic root of the amyloid hypothesis; the crenezumab prevention trial in a Colombian [[PSEN1 p.E280A]] kindred is the main test of anti-amyloid therapy in this form ([[Crenezumab]]). Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
- In the Dominantly Inherited Alzheimer Network, PSEN1 variants in transmembrane regions went with faster cognitive and hippocampal decline than cytoplasmic variants; time axis and cohort in [[Expected years to symptom onset]] and [[Dominantly Inherited Alzheimer Network]]. Study: [[PSEN1 transmembrane versus cytoplasmic variants and progression in DIAN]]
- A late-onset autosomal dominant family negative for APP, PSEN1 and PSEN2 mutations carried [[ADAM17 p.R215I]] in all four affected members (maximal LOD 1.81). Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
