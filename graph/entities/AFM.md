# AFM

AFM encodes afamin, a plasma vitamin E-binding glycoprotein of the albumin family.

## Connections

- It was one of five proteins significant in the Alzheimer versus control comparison of the Mental Health Research Center cohort alone, and the only one to pass a 5% false discovery rate cutoff in the authors' earlier smaller analysis. It was replicated in three or more earlier cohorts and stably selected for the classifier, but it is not among the 13 universal panel proteins.
