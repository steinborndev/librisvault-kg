# QNZ46

QNZ46 is a research antagonist of NMDA receptors containing the GluN2C or GluN2D subunit ([[GRIN2C]]), used to isolate the contribution of those subunits to NMDA-evoked currents.

## Connections

- Removed a larger share of the NMDA current in rat neurons overexpressing [[GRIN2C p.A1072V]] than in wild-type GluN2C neurons, read as evidence that the extra current comes from the mutant subunit. Study: [[Exome sequencing finds GRIN2C p.A1072V in an Italian late-onset Alzheimer family]]
- Compare: [[Memantine]], a clinical NMDA receptor modulator that is not subunit-selective
