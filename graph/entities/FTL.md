# FTL

FTL encodes the light chain of ferritin, the iron-storage protein complex that sequesters intracellular iron.

## Connections

- Its protein was detergent-insoluble in demented amygdala, appeared in inclusion body-like structures, and colocalized partially with TDP-43 and tau inclusion bodies. Study: [[Detergent-insoluble proteins in amygdala of cognitively impaired elderly]]
- Disease and phenotype: [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Cognitive impairment]]
- Co-inclusion proteins: [[TARDBP]], [[MAPT]]
