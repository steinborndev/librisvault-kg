# Alzheimer disease

Alzheimer disease is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder with memory loss and behavioural change, and the cause of about two-thirds of dementia cases. Roughly 95% of cases are sporadic, with a mix of genetic and environmental risk factors.

Beyond cognition, Alzheimer disease is accompanied by late-life [[Motor impairment]], and a [[Polygenic risk score]] for it predicts gait, rigidity, dexterity and hand-strength deficits in older adults, largely independently of the [[APOE]] and [[TOMM40]] region and mainly in those with dementia.

A proof-of-concept blood test uses quantum-dot nanoparticle probes to read microRNA and protein markers of amyloid, tau and inflammation, and ranked four patient plasma samples by severity in line with clinical diagnosis; see [[Molecular beacon nanoparticle sensor]].

In a Russian case-control cohort only chromosome 19 markers around [[APOE]] and [[TOMM40]] survived multiple-testing correction for late-onset disease, while interaction analysis added protective genotype combinations involving [[TCF4]] and [[CLU]].

Amyloid beta, the main component of the plaques, is a drug target: in cell and mouse models the small compound [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]] lowered secreted amyloid beta by shifting [[APP]] cleavage toward [[ADAM10]] and altering Golgi trafficking, but it does not cross the blood-brain barrier after systemic dosing.

In postmortem frontal lobe, [[TOMM40]] RNA measured without interference from [[TOMM40 pseudogene RNAs]] was about 2.5-fold higher in Alzheimer cases, and in cell models its rise tracked [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]].

In early-onset [[Small vessel ischemic disease]], which often co-exists with Alzheimer disease as mixed dementia, the [[APOE]] ε4 allele was not enriched but loss-of-function variants clustered in amyloid-degradation genes, and mouse brain hypoperfusion raised degradation-gene transcripts and produced amyloid beta oligomers, suggesting the vascular and amyloid-cascade hypotheses complement each other.

Tau-directed immunotherapy has an amyloid effect too: in the [[Tg2576 mouse]] model the cleavage-specific antibody [[12A12 monoclonal antibody]] lowered amyloid-pathway proteins in hippocampus and retina, alongside restored endosomal adaptors ([[BIN1]], [[RIN3]]) and normalized lactate.

On the innate-immune side, expression of the risk gene [[TREM2]] in microglia depends on the transcription factor [[YY1]]; both proteins were lower in an amyloid mouse model and after inflammatory challenge with [[Lipopolysaccharide]], suggesting that maintaining microglial YY1 could preserve TREM2 function.

In human iPSC co-cultures, microglia carrying the rare protective [[APOE p.R136S]] variant resisted amyloid-beta-induced lipid droplets, lipid peroxidation and [[Ferroptosis]], kept clearing synapses and Tau, and shielded neurons from phospho-Tau accumulation and network failure, pointing to microglial lipid metabolism as a therapeutic target.

In single-nucleus data from prefrontal cortex, macrophages were more abundant in Alzheimer brains than in controls, and Mendelian randomization on the macrophage-altered genes gave four apparently protective ([[CACNA2D3]], [[INPP5D]], [[RBM47]], [[TBXAS1]]) and three risk-raising ([[EPB41L2]], [[MYO1F]], [[SSH2]]) genes; see [[Single-nucleus sequencing and Mendelian randomization of Alzheimer risk genes]].

BACE1 expression and activity are raised in the Alzheimer brain, and [[YY1]] has been proposed as an activator of the [[BACE1]] promoter in reactive astrocytes beside plaques; see [[YY1 activates the BACE1 promoter in rat cells and reactive astrocytes]].

Women carry about two-thirds of cases; a sex-split reanalysis of postmortem hippocampal expression found the [[Estrogen signaling pathway]] upregulated and the GABAergic synapse downregulated in men only, the dopaminergic synapse downregulated in women only, and the IL-17 pathway upregulated in both ([[Sex differences in Alzheimer disease]]). Study: [[Sex-specific hippocampal transcriptomes in Alzheimer disease from GEO datasets]]

- White matter injury, cholesterol dysmetabolism and amyloid-beta dysmetabolism proposed as an interacting triad ([[White matter injury]], [[Cholesterol dysmetabolism]]), with risk modifiers [[Hypertension]] and [[Type 2 diabetes mellitus]]. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Known risk variants already alter blood lipids and immune proteins in cognitively normal adults from early adulthood, and the APOE ε2 and ε4 SNPs have largely opposite lipid effects; see [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]].
- In a single-nucleus atlas of aged monkey entorhinal cortex, Alzheimer gene-set scores rose in all cell types, 166 genes overlapped with human Alzheimer entorhinal differential expression (mostly neuronal), and an excitatory neuron subpopulation tracked Alzheimer bulk expression; see [[Entorhinal cortex]]. Study: [[Single-nucleus atlas of aging monkey entorhinal cortex and Alzheimer disease]]
- A 2021 perspective on why amyloid-targeted Phase-3 trials failed, and what a network view of pathogenesis (inflammation, infection, cerebrovascular disease, RNA modification) and valid models would need: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]; drugs [[Aducanumab]], [[Crenezumab]], [[Solanezumab]], [[Bapineuzumab]], [[Gosuranemab]], [[Levetiracetam]]; concepts [[Mouse model validity for Alzheimer disease]], [[Heterogeneity of late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Anxiety (nearly 60%) and depression (almost 70%) in 1006 patients were more frequent in women and their treatment response depended on [[APOE]] and on [[CYP2D6]], [[CYP2C19]] and [[CYP2C9]] metabolizer class; see [[Neuropsychiatric symptoms of Alzheimer disease]]. Study: [[Pharmacogenetics of anxiety and depression in Alzheimer disease patients]]
- Food-derived compound angle: ginger constituents were screened in silico against Alzheimer disease genes, singling out [[6-Gingerol]] and the targets [[ACHE]], [[MMP2]], [[PTGS2]] and [[CASP3]]; plant [[Zingiber officinale]], hub genes [[ALB]], [[ACTB]], [[GAPDH]], [[CAT]]. Study: [[6-Gingerol from ginger screened for Alzheimer disease by docking and dynamics]]
- Sorted-nucleus H3K27ac maps of hippocampus and prefrontal cortex place common risk variants in microglia regulatory elements but put most amyloid-associated acetylation change in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia, near APP, PSEN1, PSEN2, BACE1 and myelination genes. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- DYRK1A inhibition as a therapeutic idea: in fly models of tau, Aβ42 and DYRK1A-orthologue toxicity, [[PST-001]] improved degeneration, lifespan, movement, sleep and memory, with the strongest case for people with [[Down syndrome]] who have Alzheimer disease. Study: [[PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome]]
- In a cohort without objective impairment, lower blood [[BIN1]] promoter methylation marked preclinical disease (subjective cognitive decline with amyloid positivity) and CSF amyloid and tau changes, but with weak discrimination (AUROC 0.59 to 0.64); see [[Subjective cognitive decline]]. Study: [[BIN1 promoter hypomethylation in blood and preclinical Alzheimer disease]]
- Mature [[MIR650]] was the most raised of 204 microRNAs in Alzheimer cortex (16 controls, 18 cases, p = 0.00236) while its primary and precursor forms were lower; overexpressing it in an amyloid mouse lowered [[CDK5]] and plaque load. See [[miRNA processing]]. Study: [[MicroRNA-650 targets CDK5 and lessens amyloid pathology in APP-PSEN1 mice]]
- Mouse evidence that gut-derived Aβ and the [[Gut microbiota]] of aged APP/PS1 mice contribute to early microglial activation and memory deficits without plaques, via the [[Gut-brain axis]]. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- In Down syndrome (early-onset, genetically determined Alzheimer disease), plasma C3 and factor I were lower with dementia, which may aid diagnosis alongside Alzheimer-specific markers; see [[Complement activation]]. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
- Common variants at the [[BACE2]] locus were nominally associated with risk in 1599 autopsy-verified subjects, only in [[APOE]] ε4 non-carriers. Study: [[Common BACE2 polymorphisms and CSF amyloid in APOE ε4 non-carriers]]
- Preclinical mitophagy therapy: the isoquinolinium compound [[ALT001]] restored impaired hippocampal [[Mitophagy]], mitochondrial function, spatial memory and synaptic plasticity in 5×FAD, PS2APP and Tg2576 mice through [[Alternative mitophagy]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Plasma lipidomics of 40 cases and 42 controls found 420 of 778 lipid species different, with sphingomyelin, cholesteryl ester and triglyceride models classifying at over 80% AUC and risk-gene SNPs associating differently with lipids by diagnosis. Study: [[Plasma lipidome of Alzheimer disease and associations with risk gene SNPs]]
- Neuronal necroptosis in Alzheimer brain was traced to TNF-α/TNFR1 signalling, [[UVRAG]] loss, stalled autophagy and p62-[[RIPK1]] binding, with UVRAG overexpression or p62 knockdown lowering phospho-[[MLKL]] in APP/PS1 mice. See [[Necroptosis]]. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
- Four genes of the [[Synaptic vesicle cycle]] ([[ATP6V1D]], [[ATP6V1G2]], [[CLTB]], [[NSF]]) were lower in Alzheimer cortex and, by Mendelian randomization, genetically predicted higher expression of each went with lower risk, with a four-gene nomogram reaching an area under the curve of 0.799 in its training set; see [[Four synaptic vesicle cycle genes protective in Alzheimer disease by MR]].
- Microglial purinergic signalling: the ATP-gated channel [[P2RX4]] drives lysosomal degradation of [[APOE]] in plaque-associated microglia, and its deletion in amyloid mice lowers soluble [[Amyloid-beta]] and restores memory; see [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]].
- Seven heat-clearing traditional Chinese herbs were screened in silico for Alzheimer disease; their 34 shared targets centred on [[IL6]], [[CASP3]] and [[VEGFA]] and pointed to anti-[[Neuroinflammation]]; see [[Heat-clearing and detoxifying traditional Chinese medicine]]. Study: [[Network pharmacology of seven heat-clearing herbs for Alzheimer disease]]
