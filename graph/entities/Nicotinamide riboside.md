# Nicotinamide riboside

A precursor of NAD+, the cofactor that sirtuins consume.

## Connections

- In 3×TgAD/Polβ+/- mice it lessened phosphorylated tau pathology without changing Aβ accumulation, reduced DNA damage, neuroinflammation and hippocampal neuronal apoptosis, and raised brain [[SIRT3]] activity. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- Related: [[Sirtuin modulators]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- At 0.4% in chow for 3 months in wild-type mice it raised cortical [[IDE]] (65%, activity 84%), [[MME]] (47%) and [[SIRT3]] (72%) and lowered [[BACE1]] (46%); in [[BV2]] cells 0.5-2 mM raised SIRT3 and IDE at 24 h but little at 48 h. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
- Cited with urolithin A as a natural mitophagy enhancer that improves Alzheimer model deficits, the precedent for [[ALT001]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
