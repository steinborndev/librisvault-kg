# MAFB

MAFB encodes a basic leucine zipper transcription factor that directs monocyte and macrophage differentiation.

## Connections

- Differentially expressed in Alzheimer blood monocytes and, with [[HES4]] and [[FOSL1]], a signature regulator of the M4 transcription factor module; named with [[ATF3]], [[HES4]] and [[KLF4]] as a candidate regulator of [[TNF]], [[CCR1]] and [[APP]] under exercise. Study: [[Exercise-regulated monocyte transcription network in Alzheimer disease]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
