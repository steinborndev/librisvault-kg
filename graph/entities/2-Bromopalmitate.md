# 2-Bromopalmitate

2-Bromopalmitate (2-BP) is a brominated analogue of palmitate used as a broad inhibitor of [[Protein palmitoylation]] in cell experiments.

## Connections

- At 1 µM it restored the NMDA survival of cortical neurons from [[ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse]] to wild-type levels and brought their calcium transients close to wild-type. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Related inhibitor: [[Cerulenin]]
