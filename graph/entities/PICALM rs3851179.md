# PICALM rs3851179

A SNP near [[PICALM]] whose minor allele is protective against late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]], with a stronger effect in men than in women.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PICALM]]
- Its minor allele went with higher levels of 30 correlated plasma proteins in men and lower levels in women, and with a sex-specific effect on five long-chain fatty acids and [[Docosahexaenoic acid]]. In 12,324 cases and 11,453 controls of the Alzheimer's Disease Genetics Consortium the allele lowered risk in both sexes, more strongly in men. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- In tight linkage with rs10792832; both overlap microglia and oligodendrocyte-enriched H3K27ac peaks. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- Genotype was unrelated to methylation at the PICALM CpG island shore in blood (148 genotyped Japanese samples). Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
