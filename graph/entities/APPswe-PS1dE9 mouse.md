# APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse

APPswe-PS1dE9 is a double-transgenic amyloid mouse expressing a chimeric mouse/human [[APP]] with the Swedish mutation and a human presenilin-1 lacking exon 9 ([[PSEN1]]); it forms Aβ deposits from about 6 months and abundant plaques by 9 months.

## Connections

- Serum of 15-month-old mice had lower miR-125b-5p, miR-29a-3p, miR-29c-3p and miR-191-5p, as in Aβ1-42-infused rats, but no change in miR-146a-5p at 4 or 15 months. Study: [[Rat intrahippocampal amyloid-beta model identifies miR-146a-5p as blood marker]]
- Related models: [[APP-PS1 mouse]], [[Tg2576 mouse]]
- Called APP/PS1 double transgenic (Jackson Laboratory stock 004462) in a stem cell exosome study: 2-month-old males given monthly exosome injections for two months were tested in the [[Morris water maze]] and by hippocampal TUNEL, cytokine and microglial staining. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- Cortical serotonin transporter binding fell at 18 to 24 months and uptake Vmax at 20 months, after TSPO and IL-1β had risen, with brainstem Slc6a4 mRNA unchanged; soluble Aβ40 share rose at 20 months. Study: [[SERT loss with neuroinflammation and amyloid-beta 40 in APPswe-PS1dE9 mice]]
- Hippocampal Dicer1 protein and mRNA were lower than in littermates, and adenovirus-delivered [[DICER1]] injected into the hippocampus improved spatial learning (written APPswe/PSEN1dE9 in the paper). Study: [[Dicer1 loss in APPswe-PS1dE9 mouse brain via amyloid-beta 42 repression of Nrf2]]
- Crossed to a [[P2rx4 knockout mouse]], 12-month animals (the Tg(APPswe,PSEN1dE9)85Dbo line, JAX 034832) showed P2X4 only in plaque-associated microglia, and lost their Hamlet-test and water-maze memory deficit without a change in plaque load. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
