# RVG29 peptide

RVG29 is a 29-residue peptide taken from the rabies virus glycoprotein that binds nicotinic acetylcholine receptors on brain capillary endothelium and neurons, and is used as a brain-targeting ligand on nanocarriers.

## Connections

- Coupled through polyethylene glycol to [[Polyamidoamine dendrimer]] to make the carrier of [[PPR@siBACE1 nanomedicine]]; it raised brain accumulation about 1.8-fold at 8 h, increased uptake by [[bEnd.3]] and [[Neuro2a]] cells, and as free peptide lowered carrier uptake in bEnd.3 but not in [[BV2]]. Study: [[Brain-targeted BACE1 siRNA nanomedicine in Alzheimer disease mice]]
