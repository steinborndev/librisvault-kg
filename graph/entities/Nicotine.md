# Nicotine

Nicotine is the tobacco alkaloid that activates nicotinic acetylcholine receptors, including the alpha7 subtype encoded by [[CHRNA7]].

## Connections

- At 10 μM overnight it strengthened the anti-inflammatory tone of alpha7 in iPSC microglia-like cells lacking [[CHRFAM7A]], but raised the [[IL6]] response to [[Lipopolysaccharide]] in CHRFAM7A carriers. Study: [[CHRFAM7A proinflammatory switch of alpha7 nAChR in iPSC microglia]]
- Related: [[Methyllycaconitine]], [[Cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway]]
