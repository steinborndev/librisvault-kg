# PRKDC

PRKDC encodes the catalytic subunit of DNA-dependent protein kinase (DNA-PKcs), a nuclear kinase central to repair of DNA double-strand breaks by non-homologous end joining.

## Connections

- It gave a high-molecular-weight smear on western blots of insoluble amygdala fractions, its phosphorylated form colocalized partly with TDP-43 or tau inclusions, and rare variants showed a nominal association with Alzheimer-type dementia risk.
