# TOMM40

TOMM40 encodes the channel-forming subunit of the mitochondrial outer membrane protein import machinery. It sits next to [[APOE]] on chromosome 19, and the two genes are treated together as the APOE/TOMM40 locus, the strongest common genetic risk locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

Its RNA is hard to measure because the genome holds several intronless copies, the [[TOMM40 pseudogene RNAs]], that conventional assays co-amplify. With an intron-targeted [[Primary transcript RT-qPCR assay]] that excludes them, TOMM40 RNA was about 2.5-fold higher in [[Alzheimer disease]] frontal lobe than in control, whereas a commercial-style assay showed no difference. In cultured cells, oxidative stress raised TOMM40 RNA as [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]] developed.

- Neighbouring gene and shared locus: [[APOE]]
- Locus removed from, or tested alone in, the [[Polygenic risk score]] for Alzheimer risk.
- Study: [[Alzheimer polygenic risk score and motor impairment in older adults]]
- In a Russian cohort [[TOMM40 rs2075650]] was associated with [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]] after correction, with a G allele frequency of 28% against 16% in other Europeans; see [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]].
- Expression and regulation: RNA level differed by allele at [[TOMM40 rs10524523]] and [[TOMM40 rs741780]] but not by [[TOMM40 rs2075650]] or [[APOE rs429358]]; pseudogenes [[TOMM40P1]], [[TOMM40P2]], [[TOMM40P3]], [[TOMM40P4]]; stress agent [[Hydrogen peroxide]]; see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
- Earlier pharmacogenetic work cited in a sirtuin study found poly-T length ([[TOMM40 rs10524523]]) predicted treatment response, with S/S carriers best and L/L (always on APOE 4/4) worst. Study: [[SIRT2 genotype with APOE and CYP2D6 in Alzheimer disease treatment response]]
- APOE ε4 lowered brain TOMM40 transcript (effect -0.370) in a study of the extended locus, and the locus's regions contacted each other in brain Hi-C data (the NECTIN2-TOMM40 region met the APOC1 haplotype region); see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].
- The APOE promoter contacts a region spanning TOMM40 introns 2 to 3 (enhancer mark H3K4me1, contains [[TOMM40 rs2075650]]) in astrocyte-like U-87 and microglia-like HMC3 cells but not neuron-like SH-SY5Y cells, supporting coregulation of TOMM40, APOE and APOC1 through [[Chromatin looping]].
