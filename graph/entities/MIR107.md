# MIR107

MIR107 encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-107, a small non-coding RNA that represses target messenger RNAs after transcription.

## Connections

- Its interactions with [[CDC42SE2]] and [[IL6]] were shared by both routes; miR-107 was lower in an Alzheimer cell model and in plasma of patients, though some studies found no difference in blood or cerebrospinal fluid.
- Carried in [[Extracellular vesicle]] cargo.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see also [[MicroRNA-target interaction databases]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- The discussion of a nanoparticle-osthol cell study credits overexpression of miR-107 (with miR-377) with rescuing amyloid beta 1-42-induced synapse loss through [[GSK3B]] and [[CTNNB1]] signalling, a claim not tested in its own results.
