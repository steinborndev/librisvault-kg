# BDNF

BDNF encodes brain-derived neurotrophic factor, which supports neuronal survival and differentiation.

## Connections

- Target of [[MIR613]]. BDNF is lower in Alzheimer brain and falls in serum at early stages, and BDNF polymorphisms have been associated with the disease.
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Circulating microRNA biomarkers for Alzheimer disease by database mining]]
- Chronic restraint and social defeat stress lower BDNF in rodents, and pre-clinical Alzheimer patients show lower pro-BDNF and BDNF; see [[Psychological stress]]. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- BDNF protein (14 kDa form 82% to 155%) rose with [[LM-031]] in ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed [[SH-SY5Y]] cells, as a CREB-dependent target alongside [[BCL2]]; the pathway runs through [[NTRK2]], [[AKT1]] and ERK. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- BDNF protein fell in the brain of Aβ1-42-injected mice and was restored by 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]], alongside phospho-[[CREB1]]; the authors read this as CREB/BDNF-mediated neuroprotection. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- The Val66Met polymorphism ([[BDNF p.V66M]]) was not associated with mild behavioral impairment severity in amnestic mild cognitive impairment due to Alzheimer disease, but Met carriers reported more [[Anxiety]] once [[APOE]] ε4 and the interaction were adjusted for (p = 0.012, uncorrected). Study: [[Mild behavioral impairment with APOE and BDNF in early Alzheimer disease]]
