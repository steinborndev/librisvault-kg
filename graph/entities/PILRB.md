# PILRB

PILRB encodes paired immunoglobin-like type 2 receptor beta, a member of the PILR receptor family on immune cells, and lies at a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Allelic imbalance of PILRB was linked to the GWAS SNP rs1859788 in excitatory and inhibitory neurons, astrocytes and oligodendrocytes, but the two exonic proxy SNPs explained the signal better than rs1859788 and the SNP was not a genotype-level eQTL, so the effect is judged to come from the exonic variants. The signal also appeared in the region-specific analysis and showed exon-dependent opposite effects. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- Method: [[Hierarchical Poisson mixed model]]
- Plasma PILRB was lowered by the minor alleles of [[NYAP1 rs12539172]] (FDR p = 2.2 x 10^-33) and of the missense [[PILRA p.G78R]] (3.6 x 10^-52) in healthy adults, roughly dose-dependently; its partner is [[PILRA]]. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
