# Visual mislocalization

Visual mislocalization is a disorder of visually guided reaching: a person who can name and identify an object cannot reach to it correctly and reaches in the wrong direction.

## Connections

- The authors describe it for the first time in an Alzheimer patient with a PSEN2 mutation, the woman carrying [[PSEN2 p.M239T]], and relate it to her left-predominant parietal atrophy, since the parietal cortex integrates sensory input with motor signals. Study: [[PSEN2 mutation spectrum and functionally validated variants in a Chinese Alzheimer cohort]]
- Disease: [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
