# TOMM40 rs741780

rs741780 is a single nucleotide polymorphism in intron 8 of [[TOMM40]], close to the intron 6 poly-T marker [[TOMM40 rs10524523]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[TOMM40]]
- Its alleles differed in TOMM40 primary-transcript RNA level in postmortem frontal lobe (p < 0.01), suggesting regulatory sequence between introns 6 and 8; see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
