# CLU rs11136000

rs11136000 is a [[CLU]] variant from [[Alzheimer disease]] genome-wide association studies; the minor T allele is associated with better cognitive function.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CLU]]
- It was not associated with plasma clusterin in adults with [[Down syndrome]]. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
- Genotype was unrelated to methylation at the CLU CpG island shore in blood (148 genotyped Japanese samples). Study: [[CR1, CLU and PICALM CpG island shore hypomethylation in Japanese Alzheimer blood]]
