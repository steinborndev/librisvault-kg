# CR1 rs6656401

rs6656401 is a [[CR1]] variant long associated with higher [[Alzheimer disease]] risk; it is in linkage disequilibrium (R2 = 0.88) with the lead CSF amyloid-beta 42 variant at the locus.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CR1]]
- Phenotype: [[Decreased amyloid beta 42 peptide CSF concentration]]
- Studies: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]; [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- Minor-allele (A) carriers had higher plasma soluble CR1 than GG homozygotes among adults with [[Down syndrome]], replicating the general-population finding; the allele is linked to the long CR1 form and faster cognitive decline. Study: [[Plasma complement dysregulation in Down syndrome with and without dementia]]
