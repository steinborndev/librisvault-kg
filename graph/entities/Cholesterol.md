# Cholesterol

Cholesterol is a sterol lipid that makes up about a quarter of myelin lipids and, in the brain, is made mainly by astrocytes in adults.

## Connections

- Its release from injured myelin is central to the white matter and amyloid cycle proposed in [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Metabolites: [[27-Hydroxycholesterol]], [[24S-Hydroxycholesterol]]; processes: [[Cholesterol dysmetabolism]], [[Cholesterol efflux]]; transport and synthesis genes: [[APOE]], [[LRP1]], [[ABCA1]], [[ABCG1]], [[CYP46A1]], [[HMGCR]]
- Circulating total and LDL cholesterol were higher and HDL lower in carriers of the APOE ε4 SNP, and LDL cholesterol lower in ε2 carriers, in healthy adults across ages 18 to 90. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Total cholesterol showed local genetic covariance with early-onset Alzheimer disease at 5q13.3 (P = 5.5e-10) and 10q26.13 (P = 3.78e-9); fractions: [[LDL cholesterol]], [[HDL cholesterol]], [[Non-HDL cholesterol]]. Study: [[Local genetic covariance of early-onset Alzheimer disease with lipid traits]]
