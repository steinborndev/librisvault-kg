# Morin

Morin is a plant flavonol used in research as a reference inhibitor of Aβ aggregation.

## Connections

- Positive control (10 mg/kg/day orally; also in the thioflavin T assay) that restored neuron counts, phospho-[[CREB1]] and [[BDNF]] and lowered inflammatory markers in Aβ1-42-injected mice, with an effect comparable to 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]]. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- Target peptide: [[Amyloid-beta]]
- Model: [[Intracerebroventricular amyloid-beta injection mouse model]]
