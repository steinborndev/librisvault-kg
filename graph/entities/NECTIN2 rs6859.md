# NECTIN2 rs6859

rs6859 is a non-coding variant in the untranslated region of [[NECTIN2]] (PVRL2) on chromosome 19; the minor allele is A.

## Connections

- Gene: [[NECTIN2]]
- A causal variant of the NECTIN2 risk haplotypes, with allelic imbalance against its minor-allele transcripts, a strong blood eQTL and a predicted [[MIR595]] binding site at the major allele. Related variant: [[NECTIN2 rs6857]]; see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
