# SPI1

SPI1 encodes PU.1, a transcription factor that governs myeloid and microglial cell identity and lies at a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- SPI1 was among the immune-related genes with an allele-specific association with a GWAS SNP in classical monocytes. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- A variant in SPI1 interacted with sex on plasma [[SMOC2]], a protein involved in microgliosis. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Acetylation near SPI1 (Pu.1) was the microglia-specific control for sorting Pu.1-positive nuclei (log2 fold change 1.8, FDR 8.2e-58). Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
- SPI1 (PU.1) target genes were enriched in the two Apoe-driven co-expression modules of mouse brain, by iRegulon prediction only. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
