# NOTCH3 p.R332C

A missense change from arginine to cysteine at residue 332 of [[NOTCH3]] (exon 6) that causes [[CADASIL]]; the extra cysteine is predicted to disrupt disulfide bonding and overall protein structure.

## Connections

- Gene: [[NOTCH3]]; disease [[CADASIL]]
- Used as a structural comparator: it lies next to [[NOTCH3 p.A284T]], but where that change alters hydrogen bonding quantitatively, this one alters folding qualitatively. Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
