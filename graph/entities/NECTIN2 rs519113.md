# NECTIN2 rs519113

rs519113 is a non-coding variant in the [[NECTIN2]] (PVRL2) region on chromosome 19, one of the variants that define its risk haplotypes.

## Connections

- Gene: [[NECTIN2]]
- Showed a distal association with APOE transcript level in brain tissue (meta-p 1.3 x 10^-5); see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].
