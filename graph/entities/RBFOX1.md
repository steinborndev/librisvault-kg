# RBFOX1

RBFOX1 encodes a neuron-enriched RNA-binding splicing regulator of the Fox family; lower RBFOX1 has been linked to inclusion of [[APP]] exon 7, which carries the Kunitz protease inhibitor domain.

## Connections

- RBFOX1 was among splicing-machinery genes with altered expression at high Braak stage and was lower in the perirhinal cortex of Alzheimer brains. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
