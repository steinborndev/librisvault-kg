# PSEN1 p.E280K

A missense change of PSEN1 in [[PSEN1]] (glutamate 280 to lysine), seen in familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. A known pathogenic mutation found in one Taiwanese patient with onset at 55. It changes the same residue as the Colombian founder mutation [[PSEN1 p.E280A]] but to lysine rather than alanine.
