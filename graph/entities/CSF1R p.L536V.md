# CSF1R p.L536V

A missense change from leucine to valine at residue 536 of [[CSF1R]], in exon 11 near the intracellular tyrosine kinase domain. Both PolyPhen-2 (HumDiv 1.000, HumVar 0.996) and SIFT predict damage.

## Connections

- Gene: [[CSF1R]]; disease: [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Absent from KCDC, present in ExAC at 0.00009984; found in the Thai patient who also carried [[APP p.V604M]]. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
