# APP-PS45 mouse

APP-PS45 is a double-transgenic amyloid mouse model made by crossing APP23 mice (human [[APP]]) with PS45 mice (presenilin); by 3-4 months of age it has abundant neuritic plaques in cortex and hippocampus.

## Connections

- Brain lysates showed [[YY1]] about 39% and [[TREM2]] about 49% lower than in wild-type mice. Study: [[TREM2 regulation by YY1 in microglia and Alzheimer disease models]]
- Disease modelled: [[Alzheimer disease]]; a comparable amyloid line is the [[Tg2576 mouse]].
