# LDHA

LDHA encodes the A (muscle) subunit of lactate dehydrogenase, which converts pyruvate to lactate at the end of glycolysis.

## Connections

- Raised in CSF in Alzheimer disease and amyloid-positive mild cognitive impairment, but not in dementia with Lewy bodies or frontotemporal dementia. Study: [[CSF proteomics of Alzheimer, Lewy body and frontotemporal dementia]]
- Related glycolytic CSF markers: [[ALDOA]], [[MDH1]], [[PGAM1]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Process: [[Glycolysis]]
