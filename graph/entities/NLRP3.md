# NLRP3

NLRP3 encodes the sensor protein of the NLRP3 inflammasome, a driver of interleukin-1 beta maturation in innate immunity.

## Connections

- NLRP3 was an inflammation-related marker on the nanoparticle kit; it stained strongly in brain tissue of the APP/PS/ApoE knockdown mouse model and not in controls, and was measured in patient plasma.
- Study: [[Fluorescent nanoparticle kit for early Alzheimer disease diagnosis]]
- Ajugol and isoacteoside lowered NLRP3 protein in the hippocampus of a mouse Alzheimer model and in amyloid-beta-challenged [[BV2]] cells, and isoacteoside docked to it with -9.668 kcal/mol; pathway partners [[TLR4]], [[CASP1]]. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- mRNA did not change with amyloid-beta 1-42 in mouse microglia kept 2 or 16 days in culture, although [[IL18]] and [[HMGB1]] rose, so the authors do not support NLRP3 activation in this model. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
