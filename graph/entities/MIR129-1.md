# MIR129-1

MIR129-1 encodes a precursor of microRNA miR-129, which gives rise to the mature forms miR-129-5p and miR-129-3p; a second precursor gene, MIR129-2, produces the same mature microRNAs. Prefrontal cortex levels of miR-129-5p and miR-129-3p were linked to dexterity in older adults.

## Connections

- miR-129-5p was a direct upstream node of the dexterity score in a Bayesian network, a candidate mediator of [[Motor impairment]] in people with high genetic risk of [[Alzheimer disease]].
