# APP p.D678H

A missense change of APP in [[APP]] (aspartate 678 to histidine), seen in familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. The only APP mutation in the Taiwanese cohort, in a woman with progressive dementia from age 50 whose mother and maternal aunt had memory impairment in their seventies. Asp678 is encoded by APP exon 16, where pathogenic APP mutations cluster; the same change was reported in another Taiwanese family in 2012 and two more families were found in the literature, so the authors judge it the commonest APP mutation in Taiwan. Asp678Asn is known from Japan.
