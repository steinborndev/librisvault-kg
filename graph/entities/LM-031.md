# LM-031

LM-031 (3-benzoyl-5-hydroxychromen-2-one) is a synthetic coumarin-type derivative of [[Licochalcone A]], proposed as a multi-target compound for [[Alzheimer disease]] and other tauopathies.

## Connections

- In ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed cells it lowered Tau misfolding, ROS and caspase 3 activity and raised [[HSPB1]], [[NFE2L2]], [[CREB1]] and their downstream genes; it was the least cytotoxic derivative in HEK293 cells (IC50 about 56 μM) and had PAMPA-BBB permeability of 4.80 × 10-6 cm/s. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- In streptozocin-treated [[3xTg-AD mouse]] animals (40 mg/kg i.p.) it improved Y-maze and water-maze performance, partly lowered glucose, and reduced Aβ and Tau staining. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Compared with: [[Congo red]], [[Licochalcone A]]
