# Plcg2 R522 knock-in mouse

A C57BL/6J mouse line carrying the protective arginine allele at residue 522 of phospholipase C gamma 2, made by CRISPR/Cas9 editing (a C-to-G change at nucleotide 1,565 that also disrupts the PAM site). The edit was first made on an Apoe4 knock-in background, which was bred away over two generations; littermates of the founders serve as P522 controls, and the line is held at the Jackson Laboratory (stock 29598). Expression of Plcg2 itself is normal.

## Connections

- Models the human variant [[PLCG2 p.P522R]] in the mouse orthologue of [[PLCG2]].
- Source of conditionally immortalized (Hoxb8) macrophage precursors, primary cortical and hippocampal microglia, and young-animal brain sections in which microglial [[Phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate]] was about 60% lower than in controls. Study: [[PLCG2 P522R variant raises PIP2 depletion and endocytosis in microglia]]
