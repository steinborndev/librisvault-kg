# PTGS2

PTGS2 encodes cyclooxygenase 2 (COX-2), the inducible rate-limiting enzyme of prostaglandin synthesis, raised in inflammation and discussed as a contributor to [[Alzheimer disease]] through [[Neuroinflammation]] and [[Oxidative stress]].
