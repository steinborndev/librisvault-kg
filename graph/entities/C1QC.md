# C1QC

C1QC encodes the C chain of C1q, the recognition subunit of the classical complement pathway, which is assembled from the [[C1QA]], [[C1QB]] and C1QC chains and is expressed by microglia in brain.

## Connections

- Raised in the brains of [[Apoe knockout mouse]] animals, one of 22 genes shared between the Apoe-driven ivory module and the microglial modules of human post-mortem brain. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Related: [[Complement activation]], [[AMP-AD consensus clusters]]
