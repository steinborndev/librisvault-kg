# AKT1

AKT1 encodes the PI3K-activated kinase AKT, which inactivates [[GSK3B]] by serine-9 phosphorylation and mediates insulin signalling.

## Connections

- Expression was 62% higher in FAK-overexpressing 3xTg-AD hippocampus, together with higher [[PDPK1]]. Study: [[FAK overexpression raises amyloid load and impairs memory in 3xTg-AD mice]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; mouse model: [[3xTg-AD mouse]]
