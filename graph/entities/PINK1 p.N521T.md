# PINK1 p.N521T

A missense change, asparagine to threonine at residue 521, of [[PINK1]]. It was absent from KRGDB, ExAC and 1000 Genomes and so reported as novel.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PINK1]]; disease: [[Parkinson disease]] (recessive early-onset form)
- Heterozygous in Thai patients with early-onset Alzheimer disease (found in patients #6 and #1). Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
