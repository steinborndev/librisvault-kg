# GRIN2C p.A1072V

A heterozygous missense change in the cytoplasmic C-terminal domain of [[GRIN2C]] (exon 13, c.3215C>T), next to the 14-3-3 binding motif and the PKB phosphorylation site Ser1081. It is very rare in the general population (allele frequency 2.64e-05 in gnomAD v4.0, 15 heterozygous alleles) and is predicted damaging by CADD and Metadome.

## Connections

- Present in all 6 affected and none of 9 unaffected tested members of an Italian family with autosomal dominant [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]] (LOD 2.07); in rat neurons it raised NMDA currents and GluN2C surface expression and lowered colocalization with [[14-3-3 proteins]].
