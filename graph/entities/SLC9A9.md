# SLC9A9

SLC9A9 encodes NHE9, a sodium/proton exchanger of endosomes that sets endosomal pH and so affects vesicle trafficking and receptor recycling.

## Connections

- Altered in the same direction in the caudate nucleus of [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]]; the discussion reads it as raised, a possible compensation for weaker [[GLP1R]] signalling and mitochondrial dysfunction.
