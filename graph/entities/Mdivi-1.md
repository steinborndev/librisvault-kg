# Mdivi-1

Mdivi-1 is a small-molecule inhibitor of mitochondrial division that is used in cell and animal work as a blocker of [[Mitophagy]].

## Connections

- Added to the berberine group, it removed berberine's rescue of mitochondrial membrane potential, reactive oxygen species, amyloid-beta and phospho-Tau levels, neuron morphology and water-maze and avoidance performance, which the authors read as evidence that berberine works through mitophagy. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
- Counteracted compound: [[Berberine]]; models: [[Neuro2a]], [[APP-PS1 mouse]]
