# ABCA1

ABCA1 encodes a membrane transporter that exports cholesterol and phospholipids to apolipoprotein acceptors and is a classic target gene of [[NR1H3]] and [[NR1H2]].

## Connections

- Expression rose in [[CCF-STTG1]] astrocytoma cells after treatment with each of the seaweed lipid extracts. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Process: [[Cholesterol efflux]]
- Partner genes: [[ABCG1]], [[APOE]]
- One of the differentially expressed genes shared by the Mayo and Mount Sinai temporal lobe datasets. Study: [[Differential transcript usage in Alzheimer disease brain RNAseq by lobe]]
- Rare damaging ABCA1 variants (with ATP8B4) from an earlier exome study are cited as precedent for rare-variant discovery in Alzheimer disease. Study: [[Exome sequencing finds GRIN2C p.A1072V in an Italian late-onset Alzheimer family]]
- Cholesterol transport to CSF through ABCA1 is reported 73% lower in Alzheimer disease; deficiency raises plaque load in APP23 mice. Study: [[White matter injury, cholesterol and amyloid in Alzheimer disease review]]
