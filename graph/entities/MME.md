# MME

MME encodes neprilysin, a membrane-bound zinc metallopeptidase and one of the main enzymes that degrade amyloid-beta in brain.

## Connections

- Cortical neprilysin protein rose 47% in wild-type mice fed [[Nicotinamide riboside]], alongside higher [[IDE]] and lower [[BACE1]]. Study: [[SIRT3 loss lowers insulin-degrading enzyme in comorbid Alzheimer mice]]
