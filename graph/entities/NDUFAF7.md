# NDUFAF7

NDUFAF7 encodes an assembly factor of mitochondrial respiratory complex I, and lies next to [[PRKD3]].

- The lead variant [[PRKD3 rs876461]] sits between NDUFAF7 and PRKD3 at a new [[Alzheimer disease]] risk locus. Study: [[Meta-GWAS of Alzheimer disease finds six new loci and a polygenic risk score]]
