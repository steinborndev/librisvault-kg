# EPHB2

EPHB2 encodes a receptor tyrosine kinase of the Eph family that binds membrane-bound ephrin-B ligands and is needed for spine formation and for recruitment and function of NMDA receptors at excitatory synapses.

## Connections

- Aβ oligomers bind EphB2 and increase its degradation, which lowers NMDA receptor currents and long-term plasticity in the dentate gyrus and impairs memory in mice. Study: [[Endocytic trafficking genes and synapse dysfunction in Alzheimer disease]]
- Related Eph receptors: [[EPHA4]], [[EPHA1]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]; see [[Synapse loss]]
