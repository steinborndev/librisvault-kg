# APOC1 rs4420638

rs4420638 is a non-coding variant in the [[APOC1]] region on chromosome 19.

## Connections

- Gene: [[APOC1]]
- A fine-mapped candidate causal variant that stayed genome-wide significant after adjusting for APOE ε4 in trans-ethnic data; see [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]].
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- The proxy for ε4 used to replicate the CSF amyloid-beta 42 association (G allele, Z = -25.51, p = 1.6e-143) because rs429358 was missing from the replication cohorts. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
