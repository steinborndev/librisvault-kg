# SH-SY5Y

SH-SY5Y is a human neuroblastoma cell line widely used as a neuron-like model.

## Connections

- Used to detect [[TOMM40 pseudogene RNAs]] and to validate the [[Primary transcript RT-qPCR assay]]; see [[TOMM40 RNA upregulation in Alzheimer disease brain and mitochondrial dysfunction]].
- Related cell lines in the same study: [[HepG2]], [[U-87 MG]], [[U-118 MG]], [[LN-229]]
- A Swedish-mutant APP (APPswe) version with endogenous DOR and BACE1 confirmed that the δ-opioid agonist [[UFP-512]] lowers lysate Aβ42, [[DAMGO]] raises it, and DOR siRNA raises it by 53.1%. Study: [[Opposite roles of delta and mu opioid receptors in BACE1 regulation]]
- In the PPAR reporter assay of brown seaweed lipid extracts the relative increase in PPARα and PPARγ activation was largest in this line. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Stained positive for [[IQCK]] in nucleus and cytoplasm, suggesting neuronal expression of the Alzheimer risk gene. Study: [[IQCK protein is raised in Alzheimer hippocampus and found in amyloid plaques]]
- Neuron-like line that lacked the APOE promoter to TOMM40 contact seen in glia-like lines, and served as the baseline (APOC1 mRNA set to 1) for APOC1 expression. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
- Retinoic-acid-differentiated ΔK280 TauRD-DsRed Tet-On cells shortened neurites (63 to 56 μm); [[LM-031]] and [[Licochalcone A]] rescued this and the signalling changes in [[HSPB1]], [[NFE2L2]] and [[CREB1]] pathways. See [[TauRD-DsRed cell model]]. Study: [[LM-031 targets HSPB1, NRF2 and CREB in Tau cell and 3xTg-AD mouse models]]
- Cells expressing APP Swedish/Indiana mutant [[APP]] had more mitochondrial ROS, less ATP and respiration and more damaged mitochondria; [[ALT001]] (15 μM, 12 h) reversed these, and [[Bafilomycin A1]] or Rab9 knockdown abolished the effect. See [[Mitochondrial dysfunction]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Stable lines overexpressing wild-type, p.E406A (catalytically dead) or [[ADAM17 p.R215I]] ADAM17, with or without Swedish-mutant APP695, showed that the R215I form fails to mature and, unlike wild type, does not lower APP expression or secreted Aβ40 and Aβ42. Study: [[ADAM17 p.R215I loss-of-function variant in a late-onset Alzheimer family]]
- Used with [[PC12]] and primary neurons as a TNF-α necroptosis model: TNF-α, [[Smac mimetic]] and [[Z-VAD-FMK]] gave about 51% necrosis, and stable [[UVRAG]] overexpression, [[RIPK1]] or p62 knockdown and [[Necrostatin-1]] changed the outcome. See [[Necroptosis]]. Study: [[TNF-alpha neuronal necroptosis via p62-RIPK1 and UVRAG in Alzheimer models]]
