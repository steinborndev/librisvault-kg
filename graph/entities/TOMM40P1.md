# TOMM40P1

TOMM40P1 is one of the intronless, processed pseudogenes of [[TOMM40]] scattered across the human genome. Its RNA is transcribed in human cell lines; it sits in repeat-rich sequence that prevented amplification of its genomic region, and its RNA primers also amplified TOMM40 cDNA, reflecting high homology.
