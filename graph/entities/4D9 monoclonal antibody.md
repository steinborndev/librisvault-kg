# 4D9 monoclonal antibody

4D9 is a rat IgG2a monoclonal antibody against the stalk region of mouse [[TREM2]], binding next to the ADAM10/17 cleavage site. It stabilises full-length TREM2 on the cell surface and activates its signalling, and it does not bind human TREM2 or mouse [[TREM1]]. It was also used as a human IgG1 chimera with Fc effector function removed.

## Connections

- Bound mouse TREM2 with a monovalent K_D of 68 pM, lowered soluble TREM2 and raised membrane TREM2 in cells, raised phospho-[[SYK]], promoted macrophage survival and microglial uptake of myelin and amyloid-beta, engaged nearly all CSF soluble TREM2 in mice, and lowered soluble amyloid-beta and plaque area in [[AppNL-G-F mouse]] animals.
