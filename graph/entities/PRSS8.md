# PRSS8

PRSS8 encodes prostasin, a membrane-anchored serine protease.

## Connections

- One of five late-onset Alzheimer GWAS-list genes that are also targets of drugs in Alzheimer clinical trials (with [[TREM2]], [[CD33]], [[CHRNA2]] and [[ACE]]). Study: [[Gene2Drug repurposing candidates for Alzheimer disease risk genes]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
