# AIF1

AIF1 encodes allograft inflammatory factor 1, better known as Iba-1, a calcium-binding protein expressed in microglia and macrophages and used as the standard immunohistochemical marker of microglial activation.

## Connections

- Iba-1-positive cells rose in hippocampus and cortex of Aβ1-42-injected mice and fell with 150 mg/kg [[Ficus erecta leaf ethanol extract]]. Study: [[Ficus erecta leaf extract improves memory in amyloid-beta injected mice]]
- Process: [[Microglial cell activation]], [[Neuroinflammation]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Iba1 immunoreactivity was higher in cortex and hippocampus of young wild-type mice given faecal microbiota from old [[APP-PS1 mouse]] donors, and gut-injected oligomeric human Aβ42 activated cortical microglia. Study: [[Gut-derived amyloid-beta and gut microbiota in Alzheimer disease mice]]
- Iba-1-positive microglial density was raised in hippocampus and cortex of [[TgCRND8 mouse]] animals and reduced by [[Magnolol]] and [[Donepezil]] (n = 4 per group). Study: [[Magnolol improves cognition and neuropathology in TgCRND8 Alzheimer mice]]
- Raised in Apoe knockout mouse brain, one of 22 genes common to the Apoe-driven ivory module and human post-mortem microglial modules. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
- Iba1 staining marked microglia clustering around plaques in APP/PS1 mice and human Alzheimer cortex, where [[P2RX4]] was co-localised; the Iba1 area around plaques was equal with and without P2X4. Study: [[P2X4 deletion raises microglial ApoE and rescues memory in APP-PS1 mice]]
