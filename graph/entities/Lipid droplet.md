# Lipid droplet

Lipid droplets are cytoplasmic stores of neutral lipid. Droplet-laden microglia are a dysfunctional, pro-inflammatory state in the aging brain.

## Connections

- Amyloid beta 42 and medium from PSEN1 mutant neurons raised droplet number per cell in APOE3 but not [[APOE p.R136S]] microglia, with [[ACSL1]] induction; an [[APOE mimetic peptide]] reduced droplets in APOE3 cells. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- Downstream: [[Lipid peroxidation]], [[Ferroptosis]]
