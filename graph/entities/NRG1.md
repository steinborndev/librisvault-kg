# NRG1

NRG1 encodes neuregulin 1, a signalling protein cut by [[BACE1]] that supports myelination and synaptic function; blocking its cleavage is a feared side effect of BACE1 inhibitors.

## Connections

- Its BACE1 cleavage was the main off-target readout in an alkaline-phosphatase-fusion assay in [[HEK293]] cells: [[FAH65]] and FAH17 left it untouched (EC50 above 10 μM) while [[Verubecestat]], [[Lanabecestat]] and BACE-IV blocked it at nanomolar levels.
