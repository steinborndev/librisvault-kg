# GPNMB

GPNMB encodes glycoprotein NMB, a transmembrane glycoprotein highly expressed in lipid-laden and plaque-associated microglia, whose ectodomain is shed in soluble form.

## Connections

- Up in CSF of symptomatic GRN mutation carriers and in GRN knockout iPSC-derived microglia lysate and medium, with CSF levels that correlated with CSF [[TREM2]] in carriers but not in controls; it did not differ between diagnostic groups in an Alzheimer cohort. The authors cite it as part of a disease-associated microglia signature in amyloid and tau mouse models. Study: [[Microglial activity state marker panel in FTD-GRN and Alzheimer disease CSF]]
- Related panel members: [[FABP3]], [[MDH1]], [[GDI1]], [[CAPG]], [[CD44]]; process: [[Disease-associated microglia]]
- One of the genes that define the amyloid-linked AD1 microglia in human cortex, listed under the 'cell migration' term. See [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
