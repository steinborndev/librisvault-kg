# ABCD4 p.G59R

A missense change from glycine to arginine at residue 59 of [[ABCD4]].

## Connections

- Gene: [[ABCD4]]
- One of three ABCD4 missense variants on a rare 8-SNP, 12.9 kb haplotype in 10 Alzheimer cases and no controls (with [[ABCD4 p.V172I]]). Study: [[Case-only rare variant search in Alzheimer Disease Sequencing Project exomes]]
