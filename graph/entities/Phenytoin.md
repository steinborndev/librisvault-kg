# Phenytoin

Phenytoin is a hydantoin anticonvulsant used in epilepsy. In a screen of a compound library it came up as a weak inhibitor of BACE1 cleavage of an APP substrate and became the starting scaffold for the aminohydantoin series that gave [[FAH65]].

## Connections

- Screening hit in the maltose-binding-protein-APPC125 assay; changes to its phenyl rings gave no gain, while conversion to an aminohydantoin did. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Target: [[BACE1]]; class: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitors]]
