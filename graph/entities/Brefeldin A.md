# Brefeldin A

Brefeldin A is a fungal macrolide that blocks endoplasmic reticulum to Golgi transport by inhibiting ADP-ribosylation factor guanine nucleotide exchange factors (GBF1, BIG1 and BIG2).

## Connections

- Alone it lowered secreted and intracellular Aβ in 293sw cells and astrocytes. Together with [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]] it was the only trafficking inhibitor that reversed the compound's lowering of secreted Aβ in 293sw cells, and it raised intracellular Aβ; in astrocytes [[Golgicide A]] did the same.
- Study: [[DAPG lowers beta-amyloid secretion in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Blocked mitophagy induced by [[ALT001]] and restored mitochondrial protein levels (1 μg/ml, 12 h), the evidence for Golgi-derived autophagosomes in [[Alternative mitophagy]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
