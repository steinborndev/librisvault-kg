# T0901317

T0901317 is a synthetic agonist of liver X receptors alpha and beta that protects against cognitive decline in Alzheimer mouse models but induces lipogenesis, causing hepatic steatosis and hypertriglyceridemia.

## Connections

- Positive control (1 µM) in the reporter and expression assays: it raised the fatty acid synthesis genes and [[GFAP]] in [[CCF-STTG1]] cells, whereas the seaweed extracts lowered them. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Targets: [[NR1H3]], [[NR1H2]]
