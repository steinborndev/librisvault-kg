# CLDN3

CLDN3 encodes claudin-3, a [[Tight junction]] protein of the intestinal epithelium that regulates paracellular permeability.

## Connections

- Small-intestine Cldn3 mRNA was higher in untreated 5xFAD mice than wild type and no longer differed significantly after 18 weeks of [[D-Pinitol]]; the discussion treats excess claudin-3 as loosening the barrier.
