# 4,4'-Dithiodipyridine

4,4'-Dithiodipyridine (DTDP) is a thiol-oxidising reagent used to impose oxidative stress on cells.

## Connections

- At 100 μM it shortened the time to propidium iodide uptake in cultured mouse cortical neurons, most in those with low gamma-secretase activity, and more so after [[DAPT]], [[Chloroquine]] or [[Bafilomycin A1]]. Study: [[A wild-type neuron subpopulation with familial Alzheimer-like phenotypes]]
