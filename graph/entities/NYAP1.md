# NYAP1

NYAP1 encodes a neuronal adaptor of phosphoinositide-3-kinase signalling and sits on chromosome 7 in a linkage disequilibrium block with ZCWPW1, [[PILRA]] and [[PILRB]], a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- Variant: [[NYAP1 rs12539172]], in the 3' region of the gene, whose minor allele lowered plasma PILRA and PILRB. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Its sentinel SNP overlaps an H3K27ac peak significantly enriched in oligodendrocyte-enriched glia. Study: [[Cell-type H3K27ac profiling of Alzheimer hippocampus and prefrontal cortex]]
