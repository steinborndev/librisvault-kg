# CX3CR1

CX3CR1 encodes the receptor for fractalkine (CX3CL1) and is a main microglial receptor in the brain, where it restrains microglial activation.

## Connections

- Its mRNA rose 2.6-fold in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture after amyloid-beta 1-42 and did not rise in microglia kept 16 days. The authors call its role in Alzheimer disease controversial, citing mouse work in which its loss raised cytokines and tau pathology but also protofibrillar amyloid phagocytosis. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
- Related: [[Microglial cell activation]], [[Neuroinflammation]]
- Homeostatic marker of the control-enriched microglia subclusters in human cortex, and kept in tau-linked AD2 microglia; its expression fell along the trajectory to the amyloid-linked AD1 state. See [[AD1 and AD2 microglia]]. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau-associated microglia profiles in Alzheimer brain snRNAseq]]
