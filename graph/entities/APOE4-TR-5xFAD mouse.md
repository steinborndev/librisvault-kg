# APOE4-TR-5xFAD mouse

A cross of mice with human [[APOE]] ε4 targeted-replaced into the mouse gene (homozygous) with the 5xFAD line, which carries five familial Alzheimer mutations (APP K670N/M671L, I716V, V717I and PSEN1 M146L, L286V) under a neuron-specific promoter; mice are hemizygous for the transgenes.

## Connections

- Used for a pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic study of [[FAH65]]'s active enantiomer: oral 30 mg/kg gave a brain Cmax of 188 nM at 1 h and a fall in brain sAPPβ at 2 h. Study: [[APP-selective BACE1 inhibitor FAH65 from a phenytoin screening hit]]
- Carries [[APP]] and [[PSEN1]] variants; related models: [[B254 mouse]], [[FAD4T mouse]], [[APP-PS1 mouse]]
