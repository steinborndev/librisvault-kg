# MFGE8

MFGE8 encodes a secreted glycoprotein that bridges phosphatidylserine on dying cells to integrin receptors on phagocytes, so promoting [[Phagocytosis]] of apoptotic cells such as neurons.

## Connections

- Its mRNA fell more than 2-fold in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture after amyloid-beta 1-42, and was already low in microglia kept 16 days and stayed low with the peptide; the authors say they are the first to show this decrease with age and amyloid-beta in primary microglia. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
