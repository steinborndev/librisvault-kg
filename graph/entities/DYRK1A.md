# DYRK1A

DYRK1A encodes a dual-specificity kinase on chromosome 21 that phosphorylates several serine and threonine residues of tau.

## Connections

- It is overexpressed in [[Down syndrome]], so DYRK1A-driven tau phosphorylation is expected to be stronger there; the review doubts it is a main tau kinase in other patients, since tau is not a specific substrate and overexpression is not general. [[PPM1B]] dephosphorylates its autophosphorylation site Ser258 and so blocks its phosphorylation of tau at Thr212. Study: [[Amyloid-beta and tau interplay in Alzheimer disease review]]
- Process: [[Tau hyperphosphorylation]]
- Its fly orthologue minibrain (mnb, 82% amino acid identity) overexpressed in neurons shrank the eye by about 25%, shortened life by 13% and lowered climbing, sleep and 1 h memory; the inhibitor [[PST-001]] cut tau phospho-S262 by more than 50% in tau flies and corrected most of these phenotypes, fully so for the mnb eye, climbing and sleep. See [[Drosophila models of Alzheimer disease and Down syndrome]]. Study: [[PST-001 DYRK1A inhibitor rescues fly models of Alzheimer and Down syndrome]]
