# SERPINF2

SERPINF2 encodes alpha-2-antiplasmin, the main plasma inhibitor of plasmin and so a brake on fibrinolysis.

## Connections

- It was the first of six proteins nearest to significance for control versus mild cognitive impairment (q about 0.20), is in the coagulation and protease regulatory cluster, was stably selected for the classifier, and overlapped with an earlier panel; it is not among the 13 universal panel proteins.
