# GMNC

GMNC encodes geminin coiled-coil domain containing, a protein of the geminin family. Its chromosome 3q28 locus is an established genetic determinant of phosphorylated tau levels in cerebrospinal fluid and also overlaps the signal for [[Lateral ventricular volume]].

## Connections

- Lead variant: [[GMNC rs9877502]]
- Phenotypes: [[Increased CSF phosphorylated tau concentration]], [[Lateral ventricular volume]]
- Disease: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Its associated CSF proteins were enriched for axon guidance and ephrin signalling. Study: [[Genome-wide meta-analysis of CSF amyloid-beta 42 and phosphorylated tau]]
