# PINK1

PINK1 encodes a mitochondrial kinase that works with [[PRKN]] to flag damaged mitochondria; loss-of-function variants cause recessive early-onset [[Parkinson disease]].

## Connections

- Three heterozygous variants, [[PINK1 p.Y253D]], [[PINK1 p.A340P]] and [[PINK1 p.N521T]], were reported as novel in Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients; ClueGO placed the gene with synaptic transmission and oxidative stress regulation. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- Not required for mitophagy induced by [[ALT001]]: PINK1 knockdown left it intact while [[Carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenyl hydrazone]]-induced mitophagy was lost, and ALT001 caused no PINK1 accumulation, so it acts through [[Alternative mitophagy]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
- Protein fell with [[D-ribose]] in [[Neuro2a]] cells and [[APP-PS1 mouse]] hippocampus alongside a more methylated promoter and higher [[DNMT1]]; [[Berberine]] or [[Azacitidine]] lowered promoter methylation and restored PINK1, Parkin and mitophagy, and PINK1 siRNA abolished the berberine rescue of membrane potential, reactive oxygen species and apoptosis. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
