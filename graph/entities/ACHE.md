# ACHE

ACHE encodes acetylcholinesterase, the enzyme that hydrolyses the neurotransmitter acetylcholine at cholinergic synapses and is the target of cholinesterase inhibitors used in dementia.

## Connections

- mRNA in blood mononuclear cells was higher in dementia with Lewy bodies than in Alzheimer disease (median fold change 2.0 against 0.4, p = 0.002) and raised against controls in Lewy body dementia but not in Alzheimer disease; the patients had not received cholinesterase inhibitors.
- Study: [[PBMC nicotinic receptor and cytokine expression in DLB and Alzheimer disease]]
- Cholinergic receptor genes: [[CHRNA7]], [[CHRNA4]]
- Diseases: [[Dementia with Lewy bodies]], [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Brain neurons express N-terminally extended membrane forms of acetylcholinesterase made by alternative splicing, which may play a part in apoptosis; cited as an analogy for the extended [[BCHE]] signal peptide formed by an alternative translation start.
