# PIGHP1

PIGHP1 is a pseudogene related to PIGH, the gene for a subunit of the GPI-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase complex.

## Connections

- It was the one transcriptome-wide significant gene in the APOE ε2/ε3 group: upregulated in Alzheimer cases in combined blood and brain data (Z = 4.67, P = 3.1 × 10^-6), driven mainly by brain. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
