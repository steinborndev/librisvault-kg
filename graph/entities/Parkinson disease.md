# Parkinson disease

Parkinson disease is a neurodegenerative disorder with loss of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra, causing bradykinesia, rigidity, tremor and gait disturbance.

## Connections

- Shares motor signs with [[Parkinsonism]], which also occurs in older adults without the disease.
- A [[Polygenic risk score]] for it was moderately associated with the clinical diagnosis but not with parkinsonism scores in older adults; the Alzheimer risk score was not associated with it.
- Genes: [[DRD2]], [[PRKN]]; [[VGF]] and [[MIR132]] are reported as dysregulated in both this disease and [[Alzheimer disease]].
- Study: [[Alzheimer polygenic risk score and motor impairment in older adults]]
- The Parkinson gene [[CHCHD2]] was sharply lower in APOE4 and knockout human microglia in expression and promoter accessibility. Study: [[APOE isoforms shape transcriptome and chromatin of xenografted human microglia]]
- Painful symptoms are quoted in 40 to 86% of patients, and stress is said to alter barrier endothelium and astrocytic end feet in the disease; see [[Pain]]. Study: [[Stress, mast cells and blood-brain barrier disruption in Alzheimer disease]]
- Twelve variants in [[PINK1]], [[PRKN]] and [[LRRK2]] were found, all heterozygous, in Thai patients diagnosed with early-onset Alzheimer disease, suggesting overlap between the genetics of the two diseases. Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
- SCD1 inhibition or deficiency is reported to protect against neurodegeneration by mitigating [[SNCA]] toxicity; see [[SCD]]. Study: [[SCD1 as a therapeutic target in neurological disorders review]]
- Mouse stearoyl-CoA desaturase-2 ([[SCD]]), the main SCD isoform in brain, is reviewed as relevant to this disease. Study: [[SCD2 in murine development, metabolism and disease review]]
- Caudate nucleus RNA-seq of 3 Parkinson cases against 5 controls (with 6 [[Alzheimer disease]] cases) showed higher [[NFKB1]], [[NFE2L2]], [[PLCG2]] and [[SQSTM1]] and lower [[HBA1]] (7-fold), with altered [[GLP1R]], [[SLC25A6]] and [[SLC9A9]]; TNF signalling via NF-κB ranked first or second. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
- Parkinson-related gene-set scores rose in neurons of aged monkey entorhinal cortex; [[MAP2]] was a Parkinson-specific risk gene dysregulated in oligodendrocytes. Study: [[Single-nucleus atlas of aging monkey entorhinal cortex and Alzheimer disease]]
