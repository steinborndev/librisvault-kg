# SLC25A6

SLC25A6 encodes a mitochondrial ADP/ATP carrier (adenine nucleotide translocase 3) that moves ATP out of and ADP into the mitochondrial matrix.

## Connections

- Transcript was altered in the caudate nucleus of both [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]]; the abstract and discussion read it as reduced, together with lower [[GLP1R]], pointing to impaired mitochondrial energy supply. Study: [[NFKB1 and GLP1R transcripts in caudate nucleus in Alzheimer and Parkinson]]
