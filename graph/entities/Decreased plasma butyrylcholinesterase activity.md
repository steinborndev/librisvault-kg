# Decreased plasma butyrylcholinesterase activity

Lower enzymatic activity of [[BCHE]] in blood plasma, measured with the Ellman assay using butyrylthiocholine as substrate. It is a recognised effect of some BCHE alleles.

## Connections

- Reported for [[BCHE rs1126680]]; plasma from people carrying all three variants [[BCHE rs1126680]], [[BCHE rs55781031]] and [[BCHE rs1803274]] had lower activity than predicted from the Kozak score of the upstream start. Study: [[BCHE noncoding variants and APOE ε4 in Polish late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Related: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]], [[Translational initiation]]
