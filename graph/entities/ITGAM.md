# ITGAM

ITGAM encodes the alpha M integrin subunit (CD11b) of complement receptor 3, a marker of myeloid cells including microglia.

## Connections

- A microglia-specific allele-specific association was detected for ITGAM in the pooled microglia single-nucleus and sorted-cell data; it was not found in the tissue-level analysis, probably because ITGAM is weakly expressed in whole tissue. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
- CD11b-positive cells, taken as M1 microglia, fell when circ-Epc1 was overexpressed in LPS-challenged [[BV2]] cells and in the hippocampus of Alzheimer mice given circ-Epc1-containing exosomes. See [[Microglial cell activation]]. Study: [[Hypoxic ADSC exosomes deliver circ-Epc1 and shift microglia in Alzheimer mice]]
- CD11b-positive cells were fewer in mouse microglia kept 16 days than 2 days in culture, and amyloid-beta 1-42 lowered them at both ages; the authors tie the loss of the αMβ2 integrin to poorer phagocytosis and migration. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
