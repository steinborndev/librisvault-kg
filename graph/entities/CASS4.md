# CASS4

CASS4 encodes a scaffold protein of the Cas family involved in focal adhesion and kinase signalling, and is a genome-wide association locus for late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]].

## Connections

- A new association between the GWAS SNP [[CASS4 rs6014724]] and CASS4 allelic expression was found in the head of the caudate nucleus. Study: [[Allele-specific eQTLs of Alzheimer disease variants by brain region and cell type]]
