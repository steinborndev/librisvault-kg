# PSEN1 p.E280A

A missense change from glutamate to alanine at residue 280 of [[PSEN1]], the founder mutation of a large Colombian (Paisa) kindred with autosomal dominant [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]]. It disturbs gamma-secretase cleavage of [[APP]] and raises the amyloid beta 42 to 40 ratio.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PSEN1]]; pathway: [[Amyloid-beta formation]]
- Engineered into iPSCs, it made neurons that release more amyloid beta 42 and carry more phosphorylated [[MAPT]] in organoids; APOE3 microglia lost phagocytic capacity next to these neurons, while [[APOE p.R136S]] microglia did not. Study: [[APOE Christchurch microglia protect neurons from Tau in iPSC models]]
- A homozygous carrier of [[APOE p.R136S]] with this mutation had delayed onset, which motivated the study.
- The Colombian kindred carrying it is enrolled in the double-blind prevention trial of [[Crenezumab]] (NCT01998841), which randomises carriers to antibody or placebo with at least five years of follow-up. Study: [[Genetics and failed Alzheimer disease therapeutics perspective]]
