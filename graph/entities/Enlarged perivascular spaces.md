# Enlarged perivascular spaces

Perivascular (Virchow-Robin) spaces are fluid-filled channels around small brain vessels that carry cerebrospinal and interstitial fluid and help clear metabolic waste and amyloid-beta; when they are visibly enlarged on MRI they are read as a marker of impaired drainage and of small-vessel disease.

## Connections

- In cognitively unimpaired adults enlargement rose with age in both the basal ganglia and the centrum semiovale, and in the centrum semiovale of amyloid-positive people it was associated with CSF phosphorylated tau, total tau and neurogranin.
