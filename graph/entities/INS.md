# INS

INS encodes preproinsulin, the precursor of insulin, the pancreatic hormone that drives PI3K-Akt signalling and glucose uptake and that competes with amyloid-beta for [[IDE]].

## Connections

- Plasma insulin was lower in untreated 5xFAD than wild-type mice and returned to wild-type level with [[D-Pinitol]]. Study: [[D-Pinitol improves cognition, insulin signalling and gut barrier in 5xFAD mice]]
- Related: [[GCG]], [[Insulin resistance]], [[INSR]], [[Type 2 diabetes mellitus]]
