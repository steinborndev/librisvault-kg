# ZDHHC21 T209S knock-in mouse

A C57BL/6J line carrying the c.999A>T point change in the endogenous mouse Zdhhc21 gene, made by CRISPR/Cas9 with a 224-nucleotide donor oligonucleotide (guide 5'-TCAAAGGTAGTCAAACAGTATGG-3') at the Shanghai Model Organisms Center. The change models the human variant [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S]]; expression of Zdhhc21 is unchanged.

## Connections

- Homozygous animals showed poorer water-maze learning and memory, hyperpalmitoylated [[FYN]] and [[APP]], raised [[Amyloid-beta]] and phospho-tau, fewer synapses, more apoptotic neurons and lower [[Long-term potentiation]], but no amyloid plaques. Study: [[ZDHHC21 p.T209S in a Chinese Alzheimer family and knock-in mice]]
- Gene: [[ZDHHC21]]; test: [[Morris water maze]]
