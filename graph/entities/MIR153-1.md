# MIR153-1

MIR153-1 encodes a precursor of the microRNA miR-153, a small non-coding RNA that represses gene expression after transcription.

## Connections

- In plasma of four patients, miR-153 tracked disease stage and is proposed as an early diagnostic marker; it was also among the three microRNAs with the largest change after amyloid beta oligomer treatment of HT22 cells, and was lower in the third patient than in the other three.
- Marker for [[Alzheimer disease]] on the nanoparticle kit; see [[Molecular beacon nanoparticle sensor]].
