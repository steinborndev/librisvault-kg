# SCD

SCD encodes stearoyl-CoA desaturase 1, which introduces the first double bond into saturated fatty acids.

## Connections

- Expression fell in [[CCF-STTG1]] cells treated with the seaweed lipid extracts. Study: [[Brown seaweed lipid extracts activate LXR and PPAR in human cell lines]]
- Regulator: [[SREBF1]]; related: [[ACACA]], [[FASN]]
- Reviewed as a therapeutic target in the brain: inhibition or deficiency suppresses [[Neuroinflammation]] and protects against neurodegeneration in [[Multiple sclerosis]], [[Alzheimer disease]] and [[Parkinson disease]], including less [[SNCA]] toxicity, but rerouting of fatty acids through it helps in [[X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy]]. Study: [[SCD1 as a therapeutic target in neurological disorders review]]
- Mouse isoform SCD2 (filed here as the human orthologue) makes the monounsaturated fatty acids [[Palmitoleoyl-CoA]] and [[Oleoyl-CoA]], is the main SCD isoform in brain, and is induced in adipose tissue, lung and kidney by obesogenic diets; reviewed in [[Obesity]], [[Chronic kidney disease]], [[Alzheimer disease]], [[Multiple sclerosis]] and [[Parkinson disease]]. In knockout mice on a high-fat diet bone mineral density is lower ([[Decreased bone mineral density]]), and in vivo it is not needed for [[Fat cell differentiation]] or [[PPARG]] expression. Study: [[SCD2 in murine development, metabolism and disease review]]
