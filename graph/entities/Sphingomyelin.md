# Sphingomyelin

Sphingomyelins (SMs) are sphingolipids made of a ceramide with a phosphocholine head group; they are abundant in cell membranes and lipoproteins and sit in lipid rafts.

## Connections

- Most individual SM species were lower in plasma of Alzheimer patients than controls although the class sum did not differ; the SM class gave more than 80% classification AUC, and 43.4% of SM species associated differently with the [[CLU]] risk SNP in cases and controls.
