# TTR

TTR encodes transthyretin, a plasma transport protein for thyroxine and retinol-binding protein.

## Connections

- It was one of five proteins added to the universal panel because L1-regularization repeatedly selected it for separating progressing from stable mild cognitive impairment. Study: [[Plasma protein panel for Alzheimer disease risk by MRM mass spectrometry]]
