# ATP6V1D

ATP6V1D encodes the D subunit of the V1 domain of the vacuolar proton-pumping ATPase (V-ATPase), which acidifies lysosomes and vesicles.

## Connections

- Lower in Alzheimer cortex in three expression datasets, and genetically predicted higher expression went with lower Alzheimer risk (odds ratio 0.926, the most significant of four genes); spread across all cell types, with lower levels in Alzheimer excitatory neurons.
