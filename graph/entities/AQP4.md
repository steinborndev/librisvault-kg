# AQP4

AQP4 encodes aquaporin-4, a water-channel protein that is the major aquaporin of the mammalian brain and is concentrated on astrocytic endfeet, where it transports water.

## Connections

- Reviewed as altered in distribution and expression in Alzheimer disease patients and animal models, and as contributing to removal of amyloid-beta from the brain by lymphatic clearance, transcytotic delivery and glial degradation, and to synaptic function.
