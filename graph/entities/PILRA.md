# PILRA

PILRA encodes the inhibitory member of the paired immunoglobulin-like type 2 receptor pair on innate immune cells, partner of the activating receptor [[PILRB]]; it recognises certain O-glycosylated proteins and regulates acute inflammation. Its missense variant is the leading candidate for the causal signal at a late-onset [[Alzheimer disease]] locus.

## Connections

- Variant: [[PILRA p.G78R]] (G78R), which reduces ligand binding and has been linked to herpes simplex virus 1 infectivity.
- Plasma PILRA was lowered by the minor allele of [[NYAP1 rs12539172]] (FDR p = 2.3 x 10^-17), and more strongly by the missense SNP (1.4 x 10^-22), in 2831 healthy adults. Study: [[Blood multiomic associations of Alzheimer disease risk variants in healthy adults]]
- Neighbouring genes at the locus: [[NYAP1]], [[PILRB]]
