# PINK1 p.Y253D

A missense change, tyrosine to aspartate at residue 253, of [[PINK1]]. It was absent from KRGDB, ExAC and 1000 Genomes and so reported as novel.

## Connections

- Gene: [[PINK1]]; disease: [[Parkinson disease]] (recessive early-onset form)
- Heterozygous in Thai patients with early-onset Alzheimer disease (also in patient #6). Study: [[Fifty-gene neurodegeneration panel in eight Thai early-onset Alzheimer patients]]
