# Motor impairment

Motor impairment in older adults is a decline in strength, dexterity, gait and balance, measured here as a global motor score from ten performance tests and a global parkinsonism score from rated parkinsonian signs.

## Connections

- Predicted by the Alzheimer [[Polygenic risk score]], mainly in people with [[Alzheimer disease]] dementia, and not mainly through [[APOE]] and [[TOMM40]].
- Includes [[Parkinsonism]]; candidate mediators are [[Neurofibrillary tangle]] density, [[MIR132]], [[MIR129-1]], [[IGFBP5]], [[VGF]] and [[SYT12]].
- Study: [[Alzheimer polygenic risk score and motor impairment in older adults]]
