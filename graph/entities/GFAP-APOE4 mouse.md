# GFAP-APOE4 mouse

A transgenic mouse expressing human [[APOE]] ε4 from the astrocyte promoter of [[GFAP]] on an Apoe-null background (see [[Apoe knockout mouse]]).

## Connections

- At 6 months, whole-brain RNA-seq of females gave 120 differentially expressed genes with no pathway enrichment, and a transcriptome closest to the Apoe knockout; with that knockout it drove the skyblue3 module, and it correlated positively with human immune modules. The authors note that astrocyte-only expression may not capture microglial APOE effects. Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer risk gene mouse models mapped to human brain modules]]
