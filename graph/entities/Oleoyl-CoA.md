# Oleoyl-CoA

Oleoyl-CoA is the coenzyme A thioester of oleic acid (18:1n9), a monounsaturated fatty acid used as an energy source and as a signalling molecule.

## Connections

- A major product of the mouse stearoyl-CoA desaturase-2 ([[SCD]]). Study: [[SCD2 in murine development, metabolism and disease review]]
- Companion product: [[Palmitoleoyl-CoA]]
