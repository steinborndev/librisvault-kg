# APOC1

APOC1 encodes apolipoprotein C1, a lipoprotein component encoded next to [[APOE]] on chromosome 19.

## Connections

- Neighbouring genes at the same locus: [[APOE]], [[TOMM40]], [[NECTIN2]]
- Disease: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease]]
- Study: [[Late-onset Alzheimer disease association study in the Russian population]]
- The APOC1 gamma haplotype and the APOC1-region variants [[APOC1 rs56131196]], [[APOC1 rs157592]], [[APOC1 rs12721051]], [[APOC1 rs4420638]] and [[APOC1 rs60049679]] carried APOE ε4-independent risk, gamma raised brain APOE and APOC1 transcript and was linked to lower plasma free Aβ1-40 and [[CCL7]]. Method: [[Statistical fine-mapping]]. Study: [[Non-coding risk haplotypes at the APOE locus in Alzheimer disease]]
- Its promoter is the target of a loop from the APOE exon 4 CpG island, seen in four cell lines and all 25 postmortem frontal lobes; stronger looping went with higher APOC1 mRNA across cell lines and was higher in Alzheimer brain (mRNA p < 0.005), highest in ε3/ε4 carriers, with the highest mRNA in ε4/ε4. The promoter indel [[APOC1 rs11568822]] changed contact strength. Methods: [[Chromosome conformation capture assay]], [[CIS-dPCR chromatin interaction strength assay]]. Study: [[3D chromatin interactions at the APOE locus in Alzheimer brain and cell lines]]
