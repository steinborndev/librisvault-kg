# Fluoxetine

A selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor antidepressant. It is a major substrate of CYP2D6 and CYP2C9, a strong inhibitor of CYP2D6 and a moderate inhibitor of CYP1A2 and CYP2C19, and is linked to 28 pharmacogenes.

## Connections

- Given at 20 mg a day, with paroxetine or sertraline, to the roughly 25% of 1006 Alzheimer disease patients with moderate to severe depression; over 80% of those on any of the three improved, though the authors credit the whole regimen.
