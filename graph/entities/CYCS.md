# CYCS

CYCS encodes somatic cytochrome c, an electron carrier of the respiratory chain that, once released from mitochondria, triggers caspase activation in [[Apoptosis]].

## Connections

- Cytochrome C protein was lowered by ajugol and isoacteoside in mouse hippocampus and [[BV2]] cells. Study: [[Ajugol and isoacteoside as anti-Alzheimer components of Rehmannia root]]
- Apoptosis axis: [[CASP3]], [[BAX]], [[BCL2]]
- Disease context: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Cytochrome c in the cytoplasm of [[Neuro2a]] cells rose with [[D-ribose]], read as mitochondrial release, and fell with [[Berberine]] unless mitophagy was blocked. Study: [[Berberine rescues D-ribose-induced Alzheimer pathology via mitophagy]]
