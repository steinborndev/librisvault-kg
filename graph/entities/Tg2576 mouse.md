# Tg2576 mouse

Tg2576 is a transgenic mouse line overexpressing human [[APP]] 695 with the Swedish K670N/M671L mutation, which raises amyloid beta production; it is a standard model of familial [[Early-onset Alzheimer disease]] amyloid pathology, with changes in retina as well as brain.

## Connections

- Treated at 6 months with [[12A12 monoclonal antibody]] to study endocytic and glucose-metabolism changes. Study: [[Tau antibody 12A12 and amyloid-beta in Tg2576 mouse hippocampus and retina]]
- Disease modelled: [[Alzheimer disease]]
- Hippocampal long-term potentiation was also restored in Tg2576 mice (treated from 9 months) by four weeks of intranasal [[ALT001]]. Study: [[ALT001 induces Rab9-dependent alternative mitophagy in Alzheimer disease models]]
