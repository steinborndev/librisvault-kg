# C4A

C4A encodes the other isotype of complement component 4 and neighbours [[C4B]] in the major histocompatibility complex region.

## Connections

- Moderately upregulated (P < 10^-3) in brain but not blood of Alzheimer cases in the APOE ε2/ε3 group and total sample. Study: [[Blood and brain transcriptome of Alzheimer disease by APOE genotype]]
