# ARG1

ARG1 encodes arginase 1, an enzyme of the urea cycle that is used in microglia as a marker of the repair-type M2a state.

## Connections

- Its mRNA rose 2.6-fold after amyloid-beta 1-42 only in mouse microglia kept 2 days in culture; in those kept 16 days it stayed below 40% of the young level. Study: [[Aged cultured microglia respond less to amyloid-beta than young microglia]]
