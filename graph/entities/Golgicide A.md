# Golgicide A

Golgicide A is a synthetic small molecule that inhibits the Golgi guanine nucleotide exchange factor GBF1 (but not BIG1 or BIG2), blocking endoplasmic reticulum to Golgi trafficking more selectively than [[Brefeldin A]].

## Connections

- Alone it lowered secreted Aβ in 293sw cells and astrocytes. With [[2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol]] it reversed the Aβ reduction in astrocytes but not in 293sw cells, evidence the authors use for a cell-type-specific trafficking route.
- Study: [[DAPG lowers beta-amyloid secretion in Alzheimer disease models]]
