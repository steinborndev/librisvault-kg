# BACE2 rs9980146

rs9980146 is a common [[BACE2]] SNP that was the strongest variant for CSF amyloid-beta 1-42 in ADNI participants with [[Mild cognitive impairment]] (uncorrected p = 0.0207), in [[APOE]] ε4 non-carriers only.
